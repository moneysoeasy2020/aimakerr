## 2026 Q4 Irving Market Analysis

Irving, TX (population ~256,000 across 67 square miles, Dallas County) sits at the center of the DFW Metroplex with a median household income of roughly $71,000 and approximately 38% of housing stock built before 1990 — meaning a steady pipeline of repair, remodel, and maintenance demand. As of Q4 2026, the Irving handyman market supports **billing rates of $65–$110/hour** for general handyman work, **$85–$150/hour** for licensed specialty trades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC), and **project-based pricing of $1,800–$12,500** for mid-size remodel jobs. Startup capital to launch lean (tools, insurance, licensing, initial marketing) runs **$8,500–$22,000**, with most operators reaching break-even in **4–7 months** at 25–35 billable jobs/month.

A critical 2026 Q4 note: **Texas does not require a statewide handyman license**, but Irving enforces local contractor registration for work over $600, and any electrical, plumbing, or HVAC work above $200 in labor requires a licensed trade professional under Texas Occupations Code. General liability insurance for a solo operator runs **$1,200–$2,400/year**; a $1M/$2M policy is the practical minimum for commercial and multi-family clients. Expect to compete with roughly **140–180 active handyman/contractor listings** across Google Business Profile and Angi in the Irving–Las Colinas–Valley Ranch corridor.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Entity:** File Texas LLC via SOSDirect ($300 filing fee). Register with Irving's Finance Department for local contractor registration if doing jobs >$600.
2. **Insurance:** Secure $1M/$2M general liability ($1,200–$2,400/yr) + commercial auto. Required for most property managers.
3. **Licensing:** Confirm trade boundaries — sub out electrical/plumbing/HVAC >$200 labor to licensed partners (build a 3–5 person referral bench in Irving).
4. **EIN, business bank account, and merchant processor** (Stripe/Square at 2.6–2.9% + $0.10).

### Phase 2 — Digital GEO Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
5. **Domain + lean website** ($1,200–$2,800 freelance): service pages for *handyman, drywall repair, fence repair, deck building, painting, TV mounting, furniture assembly, bathroom remodel* — each with Irving-specific schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ).
6. **Google Business Profile:** Claim, verify (postcard/video — allow 5–14 days), add 10+ geotagged project photos, set service area to Irving + Las Colinas, Valley Ranch, Hackberry Creek, and adjacent ZIPs (75038, 75039, 75060, 75061, 75062, 75063).
7. **Programmatic SEO layer:** 40–80 pages targeting "handyman in [Irving neighborhood/ZIP]" and "[service] contractor Irving TX" — $900–$2,200 freelance build, then $300–$600/mo maintenance.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
8. **Google Local Services Ads (LSA):** Highest-intent channel in DFW trades. Budget $1,200–$2,500/mo; expect $18–$45 per qualified lead in Irving.
9. **Review engine:** Automate post-job SMS review requests (target 25+ Google reviews by day 90 — map-pack threshold in Irving is roughly 15–20 reviews).
10. **Referral bench:** Sign 3–5 Irving property managers and 2 real estate agents (Las Colinas corridor has heavy rental turnover — 40%+ renter-occupied units).

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 3–6)
11. **Track cost-per-booked-job** weekly; kill channels above $120 CPBJ. Reinvest into GBP posts, before/after project galleries, and neighborhood-specific landing pages.
12. **Add a second crew/van** once you sustain 30+ billable jobs/month at $85+ average ticket — the Irving volume supports a 2-van operation by month 6–9.
