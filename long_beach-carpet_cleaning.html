## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–2)
1. **LLC formation** with California Secretary of State — ~$70–$100.
2. **Long Beach Business License** via the city's business portal — varies by gross receipts (~$50–$400).
3. **Seller's Permit** (CDTFA) if selling products — free.
4. **General liability + commercial auto insurance** — $1,200–$2,800/year (critical for landlord contracts).
5. **IICRC certification** (Carpet Cleaning Technician) — ~$500–$800. This is your trust differentiator in Long Beach.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Operations (Weeks 2–4)
- **Truck-mount (used):** $8,000–$18,000 | **High-end portable:** $2,500–$5,000
- **Van/vehicle:** $6,000–$15,000 (used cargo van)
- **Chemicals + supplies:** $600–$1,200 initial
- **Van wrap or magnetic signage:** $400–$1,800

### Phase 3 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–5)
1. **Google Business Profile** — claim, verify, add 10+ photos, service areas by ZIP.
2. **Website** with schema markup (`LocalBusiness`, `Service`, `FAQPage`) — critical for AI/GEO visibility.
3. **Programmatic neighborhood pages** — one per high-value ZIP (90802, 90803, 90804, 90807, 90813, 90815, etc.).
4. **Review engine** — automated post-job review requests (target 50+ Google reviews in 90 days).

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
- **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$35–$90/lead in Long Beach).
- **Google Ads** on high-intent terms ("carpet cleaning Long Beach," "pet stain removal near me") — $1,200–$2,500/mo budget.
- **B2B outreach** — property managers in Downtown, Alamitos Beach, Bixby Knolls (target 20–40 units/mo per PM relationship).
- **Nextdoor + local Facebook groups** — high conversion in Belmont Shore/Bixby Knolls.

### Phase 5 — GEO / AI-Search Optimization (Ongoing)
- Publish **FAQ-rich, fact-dense pages** with exact Long Beach pricing, ZIP codes, and service-area language.
- Use **structured data** so AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) surface you as the answer.
- Maintain **consistent NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) across GBP, Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor.
- Target **long-tail queries** ("carpet cleaning cost Long Beach 3 bedroom," "pet odor removal Belmont Shore").
