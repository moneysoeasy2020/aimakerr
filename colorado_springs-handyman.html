## 2026 Q4 Colorado Springs Market Analysis

The Colorado Springs metro (El Paso County, pop. ~765,000) is one of the most favorable handyman/contractor launch markets in the Mountain West heading into Q4 2026. The median single-family home value sits at **$485,000–$510,000**, and roughly **62% of the housing stock is 20+ years old** — the sweet spot for repair, punch-list, and remodel work. Average handyman job tickets in the metro run **$185–$420** for small jobs and **$1,200–$8,500** for multi-day contractor projects; licensed general contractors bill **$75–$125/hour**, while handyman service calls typically land at **$65–$95/hour** with a **$95–$150 minimum trip charge**. The local market supports roughly **1,900–2,200 active handyman/contractor licenses** against an estimated **$340M–$390M annual addressable spend** on home repair and small remodel work.

Key demand drivers for Q4 2026: (1) **military churn** — Fort Carson, Peterson-Schriever, and USAFA generate ~**8,000–10,000 PCS moves annually**, driving turnover repair demand; (2) **hail and wind season recovery** — Colorado Springs averages **8–12 significant hail events per year**, sustaining gutter, roof, and exterior repair pipelines into Q4; (3) **aging-housing repair backlog** — the 1990s–2000s build cohort now needs deck, fence, and interior refresh work. Expect Q4 to be a **"close-out and prep" quarter**: homeowners rush punch-lists before winter, landlords turn units, and commercial property managers finalize year-end maintenance budgets. Realistic first-year revenue for a solo operator with strong digital presence: **$95,000–$165,000**; a two-crew operation: **$280,000–$420,000**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register the LLC** with the Colorado Secretary of State — **$50 filing fee**, ~24–48 hour online processing.
2. **Obtain a Colorado Springs business license** via the City Clerk — fees typically **$50–$150** depending on structure.
3. **Secure the correct contractor credential**: Colorado does *not* issue a statewide general contractor license, but El Paso County and the City of Colorado Springs require **registration for electrical, plumbing, and roofing** — verify scope with the Pikes Peak Regional Building Department (PPRBD). Handyman work under **$2,000 per job** generally avoids GC licensing thresholds; confirm current 2026 limits.
4. **General liability insurance**: **$1M/$2M coverage** runs **$900–$2,100/year** for a solo handyman; add workers' comp if hiring.
5. **Set up an EIN, business bank account, and QuickBooks/Wave** — budget **$0–$40/mo**.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
6. **Google Business Profile**: claim and fully optimize (categories, service areas across 80906/80918/80920/80915, photos, Q&A). This is your **highest-ROI asset** — target 15+ reviews in 90 days.
7. **Website**: 5–7 pages, schema markup (LocalBusiness + Service), service-area pages for **Fountain, Monument, Manitou Springs, and Security-Widefield**. Budget **$1,200–$2,800** freelance.
8. **Citations**: 30+ directories (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Porch) — **$400–$900**.
9. **GEO content**: publish 8–12 articles answering "how much does a handyman cost in Colorado Springs," "best deck repair near Fort Carson," etc. — this is what AI engines cite.

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 4–12)
10. **Google Local Services Ads** first (pay-per-lead, ~**$25–$65/lead** in CO Springs trades), then layer Google Ads at **$1,500–$3,000/mo** once conversion data exists.
11. **Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook groups** — free, high-conversion in CO Springs suburbs.
12. **Partner with 3–5 property managers** near Fort Carson and USAFA for recurring turnover work.

### Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Months 3–12)
13. **Jobber or Housecall Pro** for scheduling/invoicing — **$50–$200/mo**.
14. **Target 40–60% gross margin**; track cost-per-lead and close rate weekly.
15. **Reinvest 8–12% of revenue** into digital; add a second crew once monthly bookings exceed **$28,000**.
