## 2026 Q4 Baton Rouge Market Analysis

Baton Rouge's handyman and contractor market in Q4 2026 is defined by three hard numbers: an **East Baton Rouge Parish population of roughly 450,000** (metro ~880,000), a **homeownership rate near 60%**, and a housing stock where **more than 55% of owner-occupied units were built before 1990** — meaning a deep, recurring backlog of repair, remodel, and deferred-maintenance work. Post-hurricane-season demand (Ida-era repairs still cycling through insurance claims) plus steady industrial-corridor relocation keeps call volume high. Typical 2026 Q4 rate ranges in Baton Rouge: **handyman labor $65–$110/hour**, **flat-fee small jobs $150–$450**, **bathroom remodel $9,000–$22,000**, **deck build $4,500–$14,000**, and **drywall/paint packages $2.50–$6.00/sq ft**. Louisiana requires a **State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC) license for residential projects of $75,000+ or commercial work of $50,000+**; below those thresholds, a registered LLC plus local permits cover most handyman scopes. Expect startup capital of **$8,000–$25,000** (tools, truck branding, insurance, licensing, digital assets) and a realistic path to **$12,000–$28,000/month gross** within 6–9 months on 3–5 jobs/week at average tickets of $600–$1,800.

Demand is concentrated in **zip codes 70808, 70809, 70810 (south Baton Rouge), 70806 (mid-city renovation corridor), and 70791/70714 (Zachary/ Baker growth)**. Competition is fragmented: most competitors are single-operator LLCs with weak or no web presence, which is precisely the gap a GEO-optimized digital footprint exploits. Your two highest-ROI acquisition channels in Q4 2026 are **(1) Google Business Profile + local service landing pages optimized for AI Overviews and map packs, and (2) verified freelance specialists** to build those assets at 40–70% below brick-and-mortar agency pricing.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–2)
1. Form a **Louisiana LLC** ($100–$150 filing via geauxbiz).
2. Register with **LSLBC** if scoping projects at/above $75K residential; otherwise confirm handyman thresholds.
3. Obtain **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and commercial auto — budget **$1,200–$2,800/yr**.
4. Secure **East Baton Rouge Parish occupational license** + permit accounts.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
5. Launch a **5-page core site** + 8–12 **programmatic service×zip landing pages** (e.g., "drywall repair 70808").
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (10+ geo-tagged photos, service list, booking link).
7. Deploy **LocalBusiness + Service + FAQ schema** for AI Overview and LLM citation eligibility.
8. Set up **review automation** targeting 15+ reviews in 60 days.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
9. Run **geo-targeted Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,500/mo test budget).
10. Publish **2 local proof posts/week** (job photos, zip-specific case studies) to feed GEO signals.
11. List on **Nextdoor, Thumbtack, Angi** — prioritize platforms with high Baton Rouge query volume.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)
12. Hire first **helper/apprentice** at 25–30% of billed labor.
13. Add **maintenance-plan subscriptions** ($99–$199/quarter) for recurring revenue.
14. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into **content + citations** to compound AI-search visibility.

### Q4 2026 KPI Targets
- **Lead cost:** $35–$90 (LSA) / $15–$40 (organic once ranking)
- **Close rate:** 30–45% on inbound
- **Average ticket:** $600–$1,800
- **Break-even:** Month 3–5
- **Reviews by day 90:** 20+
