## 2026 Q4 Lexington Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers (Verified Local Context)

- **Housing stock:** Lexington has a high concentration of rental housing (roughly 40% renter-occupied), and property management firms in the Hamburg, Beaumont, and Chevy Chase corridors are the highest-value B2B channel — they need recurring turnover cleaning.
- **Commercial segment:** Office parks along Harrodsburg Road, the Coldstream Research Campus, and hospitality near Rupp Arena generate steady commercial carpet maintenance contracts ($0.18–$0.30/sq ft, recurring monthly).
- **Competitive pricing floor:** Franchise operators (Chem-Dry, Stanley Steemer) anchor the market at **$99–$149 "3-room specials"** — do not compete on that price. Compete on IICRC certification, truck-mounted hot-water extraction, and same-week scheduling.
- **Seasonality:** Peak = April–June (spring cleaning) and October–December (holidays). Q4 launch means you enter a demand tailwind, not a trough.

### Regulatory & Licensing Reality (Fayette County)

- **Business license:** Lexington-Fayette Urban County Government (LFUCG) occupational license — ~$50–$100.
- **LLC formation:** Kentucky Secretary of State filing, **$40**.
- **Sales tax:** KY 6% — carpet cleaning services are generally **not taxable** in Kentucky, but verify with a CPA.
- **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) runs **$1,200–$2,400/year**; commercial auto adds **$1,800–$3,200/year**.
- **IICRC certification:** ~$500–$800 (highly recommended — it's a trust signal that wins commercial bids).

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026)

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. Form LLC with KY Secretary of State ($40); obtain LFUCG occupational license.
2. Secure general liability + commercial auto insurance.
3. Open business banking; set up QuickBooks or Wave.
4. Purchase equipment: used truck-mount ($8,000–$18,000) **or** start with a high-end portable ($3,500–$6,000) + van wrap later. Budget $1,200–$2,000 for chemicals and hoses.
5. Complete IICRC certification (if budget allows).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
6. Launch 6-page website with booking form + schema (freelancer: $900–$2,200).
7. Create and fully optimize Google Business Profile — categories, services, photos, service area.
8. Build 15–20 local citations (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Thumbtack, Chamber of Commerce).
9. Publish 10 GEO-optimized pages: pricing, neighborhood pages, FAQ, "pet stain removal Lexington," "commercial carpet cleaning Lexington."
10. Set up review automation (SMS request 2 hours post-service).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
11. Launch Google Ads: **$1,200–$2,500/month**, targeting "carpet cleaning Lexington KY" + neighborhood terms. Expect $18–$35 cost per lead, 25–35% close rate.
12. Direct outreach to **20–30 property managers** in Hamburg/Beaumont/Chevy Chase — offer a free demo clean on one unit.
13. Partner with 5–10 local realtors (pre-listing cleans) and 3–5 Airbnb hosts.
14. Post weekly on Nextdoor and local Facebook groups (non-spammy, value-first).

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 8–12)
15. Target **40+ Google reviews** by end of Q4 — this is the single strongest ranking and trust lever.
16. Add recurring commercial contracts (target 3–5 by year-end at $400–$900/month each).
17. Reinvest 15–20% of revenue into content and ads.
18. Track cost-per-booked-job weekly; kill any channel above $60 CPL.
