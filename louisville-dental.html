## 2026 Q4 Louisville Market Analysis

### Demand and competitive signals
- **Metro population:** ~1.3M (Louisville-Jefferson County), with ~14% aged 65+, a high-value demographic for implants, dentures, and restorative work.
- **Dental insurance penetration:** ~65–70% employer-sponsored or Medicaid/Medicare-adjacent coverage; Kentucky Medicaid adult dental benefits remain limited — **fee-for-service and membership plans (in-house) are a meaningful revenue lever**.
- **Competitive density:** ~600–700 practices metro-wide; the East End (St. Matthews, Middletown, Prospect, Norton Commons) is saturated with established groups, while **South Louisville, Okolona, Shively, and the Portland/Smoketown corridors are underserved** — lower rents, higher Medicaid/self-pay mix.
- **Key demand drivers:** UofL Health and Norton Healthcare employment corridors, UPS Worldport and logistics workforce (shift-friendly hours are a differentiator), and a growing Hispanic population in South Louisville (bilingual staffing is a competitive edge).
- **Payer mix reality:** PPO-heavy market; Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, and Anthem dominate. Credentialing lead time is **90–150 days** — start before opening day.

### Regulatory and operational notes
- **Kentucky Board of Dentistry** licensure; **CPR/BLS**, **DEA**, and **KY controlled-substance registration** required before seeing patients.
- **Radiation machine registration** with the KY Cabinet for Health and Family Services (Cabinet for Health and Family Services, Radiation Health Branch) — allow 30–60 days.
- **LLC formation** via KY Secretary of State ($40 online filing); **Jefferson County occupational license**; **KY sales tax permit** (dental services generally exempt, but retail products are not).
- **HIPAA Security Risk Analysis** is mandatory — budget $1,500–$4,000 for a qualified assessor.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-launch (Months 1–6)
1. **Market and site selection:** Target South Louisville/Okolona for lower rent and underserved demand, or East End for higher fee-for-service mix. Negotiate 5–7 year lease with TI allowance ($30–$60/sq ft).
2. **Entity and licensure:** Form KY LLC ($40), obtain EIN, open business banking, apply for KY dental license, DEA, and radiation registration.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) or equipment leasing; dental-specific lenders (Bank of America Practice Solutions, Live Oak, Huntington) — expect 10–15% down and personal guarantee.
4. **Design and build-out:** Engage dental-specific architect/contractor; 4–6 operatories; plumb for future expansion. Timeline: 4–6 months.
5. **Technology stack:** Practice management (Open Dental for cost, Dentrix for scale), digital X-ray/CBCT, intraoral scanners, patient communication platform.
6. **Credentialing:** Start PPO applications immediately — 90–150 day lead time.

### Phase 2 — Digital launch (Months 4–7, overlapping build-out)
7. **Domain and website:** Launch a 6–8 page site with service pages, location page, team bios, and online scheduling. Implement **LocalBusiness/Dentist schema** and FAQ schema for GEO/LLM citation.
8. **Google Business Profile:** Claim and fully optimize — categories, services, hours (include evening/Saturday for logistics workers), photos, and a review-generation workflow.
9. **Citations and local SEO:** 40–60 consistent NAP listings (Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Angi, BBB, Louisville-specific directories).
10. **Content engine:** Publish 8–12 pages/month targeting "dentist near me [Louisville neighborhood]," "emergency dentist Louisville," "affordable dentist Okolona," and Spanish-language pages for South Louisville.
11. **Paid acquisition:** Start Google Ads (search + Local Services Ads) at $2,500–$4,000/mo; layer Meta ads for whitening/implants once tracking is live.

### Phase 3 — Opening and ramp (Months 7–18)
12. **Soft opening:** 4–6 weeks of reduced hours to train staff and refine workflows; target 15–25 new patients/week by month 3.
13. **Review flywheel:** Automated post-visit review requests; target 50+ Google reviews in first 6 months (drives both local pack and LLM citation).
14. **Membership plan launch:** In-house plan at $29–$49/mo to capture uninsured patients — critical in Louisville's payer mix.
15. **Referral network:** Build relationships with 15–20 local physicians, pediatricians, and specialists; join Louisville Dental Society and local chambers (Louisville, St. Matthews, Okolona).
16. **Measure and iterate:** Track cost-per-new-patient, chair utilization, and production per operatory monthly. Reallocate marketing spend quarterly based on verified attribution.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 18–36)
17. **Add hygiene capacity** once chair utilization exceeds 75%.
18. **Expand services** (implants, ortho, sedation) based on demand data.
19. **Consider second location** in an adjacent underserved corridor once Year 1 collections exceed $900K and EBITDA margin stabilizes at 20–30%.
