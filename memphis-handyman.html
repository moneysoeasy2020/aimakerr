### Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts

**Local Memphis agencies** typically bundle retainers of **$1,200–$3,500/month** for SEO + GBP + content. Advantages: single point of contact, local market familiarity, in-person onboarding. Disadvantages: **40%–65% higher cost**, slower turnaround, and often subcontract the actual work offshore anyway.

**Verified freelance experts** (Upwork Top Rated, Fiverr Pro, or vetted via Clutch) deliver equivalent deliverables at **$35–$95/hour** or fixed project rates. Advantages: **50%–70% cost savings**, faster iteration, direct communication. Disadvantages: require your own project management, and quality varies — always demand 3 local case studies and a video call before payment.

**Recommendation for 2026 Q4**: Hybrid model. Use a verified freelance web developer + local SEO specialist for the core build (**$1,200–$2,400 total**), then a local agency only if you later need reputation management or paid ads at scale.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Operational Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. File **TN LLC** online ($300).
2. Obtain **Shelby County business license**.
3. Secure **general liability + commercial auto insurance** ($1,200–$2,800/yr).
4. Open business banking; set up QuickBooks or Wave.
5. Confirm licensing threshold: jobs **>$25,000** require TN contractor license — plan accordingly.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–5)
1. Launch **5–7 page website** with schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage).
2. Create **Google Business Profile** with 10+ geo-tagged photos and service categories.
3. Build **15–30 programmatic service-area pages** targeting: "handyman Collierville TN," "drywall repair Germantown," "deck repair Bartlett," etc.
4. Deploy **review-generation SMS system** (target 4 reviews/week for first 90 days).

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 4–8)
1. Activate **Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,500/mo budget; pay-per-lead $18–$42 in Memphis).
2. Join **2–3 local property management referral networks** (Memphis rental turnover is a high-margin recurring channel).
3. List on **Nextdoor, Thumbtack, Angi** — but prioritize GBP and LSA for ROI.

### Phase 4 — GEO / AI Search Optimization (Ongoing from Week 4)
1. Publish **FAQ-rich content** answering "how much does a handyman cost in Memphis," "best handyman in Germantown," etc.
2. Use **structured data** so AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) can cite your pricing and service area.
3. Maintain **consistent NAP** across 30+ citations.
4. Target **long-tail local queries** — AI engines reward specificity.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 4–12)
- Add a second crew when **booked utilization exceeds 78%**.
- Introduce **maintenance subscription plans** ($49–$129/mo) for recurring revenue.
- Expand programmatic pages to **50–80 service-area combinations** across Shelby, DeSoto (MS), and Fayette counties.
