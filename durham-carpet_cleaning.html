## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register LLC in NC** ($125 filing fee) and obtain Durham business privilege license.
2. **Secure insurance:** general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto; budget $1,200–$2,400/year.
3. **Equipment:** used truck-mount extractor ($6,000–$12,000) or commercial portable ($2,500–$5,000); chemicals and supplies ($800–$1,500).
4. **IICRC certification** for credibility ($500–$900) — a strong trust signal in GEO citations.

### Phase 2: Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
5. **Website:** 6 core pages + 25–35 programmatic service-area pages (Durham neighborhoods: Trinity Park, Watts-Hillandale, Hope Valley, Southpoint, Downtown, RTP corridor).
6. **Google Business Profile:** complete every field, add 15+ geo-tagged photos, set service areas, enable messaging.
7. **Review engine:** automated post-job SMS request; target 20 reviews in first 90 days.

### Phase 3: Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)
8. **Google Local Services Ads** first (pay-per-lead, Google Guaranteed badge) — typically $18–$35/lead in Durham.
9. **Google Ads** on high-intent keywords ("carpet cleaning Durham NC," "move out carpet cleaning near me") with $800–$1,500/month starting budget.
10. **Partnerships:** 10 property management firms + 5 realtors for referral pipeline.

### Phase 4: Scale (Months 4–9)
11. Add second van/technician once you sustain 15+ jobs/week.
12. Launch commercial contracts (quarterly maintenance agreements).
13. Publish 2 GEO-optimized blog posts/month targeting Durham-specific queries ("best carpet cleaning for pet stains Durham," "apartment move-out carpet cleaning Durham NC").
