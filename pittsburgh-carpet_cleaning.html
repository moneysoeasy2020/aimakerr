## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Weeks 1–3, ~$6,500–$12,000)
1. **Entity & compliance:** PA LLC filing ($125), EIN, PA sales tax registration, city business license, liability insurance ($600–$1,200/yr).
2. **Equipment:** Truck-mount (used $12K–$22K) or portable extractor ($2,800–$5,500) + van ($4K–$9K used). Start portable if capital-constrained.
3. **Chemicals & supplies:** $400–$700 initial stock.
4. **Google Business Profile:** Claim, verify, add 10+ photos, set service area (Allegheny + Butler + Washington counties).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6, ~$1,800–$4,200)
5. Launch 6-page website with online booking (Jobber/Housecall Pro integration).
6. Build 8–12 service-area landing pages (Squirrel Hill, Mount Lebanon, Cranberry, etc.).
7. Set up Google Ads ($800–$1,500/mo test budget) + LSA enrollment.
8. Install review-request automation (SMS after job completion).

### Phase 3 — First Revenue (Weeks 6–12)
9. Target **14–22 jobs/month** to break even. At $285 avg ticket = $4,000–$6,300/mo gross.
10. Pursue **rental-turnover contracts** with 3–5 property managers (Lawrenceville, Oakland, North Side) — recurring volume stabilizes cash flow.
11. Run a Q4 promo: "Pre-holiday deep clean — $40 off 4 rooms" (Nov 1–Dec 10).

### Phase 4 — Scale (Month 4+)
12. Reinvest 12–15% of revenue into local SEO and review velocity.
13. Add commercial accounts at $0.18–$0.32/sq ft.
14. Hire first technician once monthly jobs exceed 45.
