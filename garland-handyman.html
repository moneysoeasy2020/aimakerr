## 2026 Q4 Garland Market Analysis

Garland, TX (Dallas County) is a mature, owner-occupied-heavy suburb of roughly **245,000 residents** across **~78,000 housing units**, with a median home age of **~40 years** (much of the housing stock built 1970–1995). That age profile is the single most important market metric for a handyman/contractor: older homes generate **continuous repair, remodel, and maintenance demand** that new-build suburbs do not. The Garland housing market median sale price sits in the **$300K–$340K** band as of Q3 2026, and the city's owner-occupancy rate of roughly **60–62%** means a large, stable base of repeat-customer households rather than transient renters.

Realistic 2026 Q4 pricing for a Garland handyman operation: **$75–$110/hour** for labor (metro Dallas average ~$85/hr), **$125–$175** for a typical 2-hour punch-list job, **$250–$600** for small drywall/trim repairs, and **$1,800–$6,500** for mid-size bathroom or kitchen refresh projects (labor only). Startup capital to launch lean is **$8,000–$22,000** (truck/tools/insurance/licensing/marketing), and a solo operator targeting **$9,000–$14,000/month gross** with **55–65% net margin** after materials and overhead is a realistic Year-1 benchmark. The Garland permit office (City of Garland Building Inspection, 972-205-2000) requires registration and permits for structural, electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work — handymen legally cap out at cosmetic/non-permitted work unless licensed as a contractor.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)**
- Register LLC with Texas SOS (~$300); obtain Garland business registration.
- Secure **general liability insurance** ($1M/$2M, ~$900–$1,800/yr) and commercial auto.
- Confirm scope limits: cosmetic work only unless you hold TX contractor licenses (electrical/plumbing/HVAC require state licensure).
- Open business banking; set up QuickBooks/Wave.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)**
- Build a 6-page site with schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ) — this is the core GEO lever for AI-generated answers.
- Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (services, hours, photos, Q&A, review prompts).
- Deploy **programmatic service pages** for Garland ZIPs (75040–75049) and adjacent cities (Richardson, Rowlett, Sachse, Mesquite).
- Set up SMS review-request automation.

**Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 4–8)**
- Launch Google Local Services Ads (pay-per-lead, ~$25–$60/lead in DFW).
- List on Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor Business (Nextdoor is disproportionately strong in Garland).
- Post 2–3 completed jobs/week to GBP with geo-tagged photos.

**Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Months 2–6)**
- Standardize a 3-tier pricing sheet (diagnostic visit / punch-list / project).
- Target **15–25 reviews in first 90 days**; reply to 100%.
- Reinvest 10–15% of gross into ads + content; add a second tech at ~$12K/mo gross.
