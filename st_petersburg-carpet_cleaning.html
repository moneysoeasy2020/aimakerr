## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)**
1. Register LLC with Florida Sunbiz (**$125**).
2. Obtain Pinellas County local business tax receipt (**$30–$60**).
3. Secure general liability insurance (**$600–$1,200/yr**) — required by most commercial clients.
4. Pursue **IICRC certification** (**$1,200–$1,800**) — a genuine conversion differentiator locally.

**Phase 2 — Equipment & Vehicle (Weeks 2–5)**
- Portable extractor setup: **$4,500–$7,000**; used cargo van: **$9,000–$16,000**; chemicals & supplies: **$800–$1,200**.
- Truck-mount alternative: **$18,000–$28,000** installed — only justified once you clear **8–10 jobs/week**.

**Phase 3 — Digital Launch (Weeks 3–8)**
5. Google Business Profile optimized for "carpet cleaning St. Petersburg FL" + service-area pages for **Pinellas Park, Largo, Clearwater, Gulfport**.
6. Website with online booking and transparent pricing (**$185+ 3-room** anchor).
7. Launch Google Ads at **$1,200–$2,000/month** targeting high-intent terms; expect **$9–$16 per booked estimate**.
8. Set up review automation — target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (top local operators average **4.7★ / 60–140 reviews**).

**Phase 4 — Revenue Ramp (Weeks 8–16)**
9. Land **3–5 STR/commercial recurring contracts** for baseline cash flow.
10. Publish **2 GEO-optimized local content pieces per week** (neighborhood-specific, pricing-transparent).
11. Track **cost per booked job** weekly; kill any ad group above **$22/booking** by week 12.

**Realistic 2026 Q4 financial projection:** Month 1 revenue **$3,500–$6,000**; Month 3 **$9,000–$15,000**; Month 6 **$16,000–$24,000** at **55–65% gross margin** after chemicals, fuel, and ad spend. Break-even on total startup capital typically lands in **Month 4–6** for lean launches.
