## 2026 Q4 Irving Market Analysis

Irving, TX (population ~256,000, Dallas County, DFW metro) supports a carpet cleaning market worth roughly **$8.5M–$11M annually** across residential and commercial segments. The city's ~95,000 housing units skew toward rental-heavy multifamily stock (Las Colinas, Valley Ranch, MacArthur Park), where turnover cleaning and apartment-complex contracts drive steady B2B demand. Typical local pricing in Q4 2026 runs **$0.28–$0.45 per sq ft** for steam cleaning, **$99–$169** for a 2–3 room residential special, and **$0.18–$0.30 per sq ft** for commercial contracts. Water damage/emergency extraction jobs—a high-margin add-on—command **$250–$1,200+** per incident.

Key local demand drivers: Las Colinas corporate offices and Class A apartments, DFW Airport-adjacent hospitality/hotel turnover, and single-family pockets in Valley Ranch, Hackberry Creek, and University Hills.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity formation**: File a Texas LLC via the Texas Secretary of State ($300 filing fee). Register a DBA with Dallas County if operating under a trade name.
2. **Sales tax permit**: Register with the Texas Comptroller (free). Carpet cleaning services are generally not taxable in TX, but equipment/retail chemical sales are—confirm scope.
3. **Insurance**: General liability ($1M/$2M) runs **$600–$1,400/yr**; add commercial auto and a surety bond if pursuing apartment contracts.
4. **IICRC certification**: CCT (Carpet Cleaning Technician) certification runs **$500–$750** including exam—required by most commercial property managers and a strong trust signal for GEO/AI search citations.
5. **Local licenses**: City of Irving general business registration; verify no special trade license applies to cleaning services.
