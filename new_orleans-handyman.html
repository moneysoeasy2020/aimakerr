# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in New Orleans, LA
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

New Orleans' handyman and contractor market supports roughly **2,100–2,400 active licensed trade contractors** across Orleans, Jefferson, St. Bernard, and Plaquemines parishes, serving a metro of ~1.27 million residents and approximately **180,000 owner-occupied housing units** — about **62% of which were built before 1980** and carry elevated repair, storm-damage, and renovation demand. A solo handyman in New Orleans can realistically bill **$75–$95/hour** (2026 Q4 rates), while a licensed residential contractor running a two-crew operation typically grosses **$380,000–$620,000/year** with a **22–30% net margin** after materials, labor, insurance, and marketing.

Realistic startup costs for a lean handyman launch run **$6,500–$14,000** (tools, licensing, insurance, vehicle wrap, and initial digital assets), while a full contractor setup with one crew runs **$28,000–$55,000**. Standard residential pricing in the metro: minor repairs **$150–$450/job**, drywall and paint **$2.50–$4.25/sq ft**, bathroom remodels **$9,500–$24,000**, and post-storm emergency repairs command a **15–35% premium** during hurricane season (June–November). The Orleans Parish general contractor license (via the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors, LSLBC) is required for projects over **$75,000**; residential work between **$7,500 and $75,000** requires a **Residential Contractor license** — a critical compliance detail most out-of-state guides miss.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with the **Louisiana Secretary of State** ($100 filing fee).
2. Obtain **LSLBC Residential Contractor license** (required for $7,500–$75,000 residential jobs); general contractor license for projects over $75,000.
3. Secure **general liability insurance** ($1M/$2M policy: **$1,200–$2,400/year**) and workers' comp if hiring.
4. Apply for **New Orleans Occupational License** via One Stop Shop (City Hall) and check parish-specific permits (Orleans, Jefferson differ).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
5. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — category: "Handyman" or "General Contractor," service areas by parish, 10+ photos, Q&A seeded.
6. Launch a **5–8 page website** with LocalBusiness schema, service pages, and a click-to-call mobile CTA.
7. Build **20–40 programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "Handyman in Mid-City," "Drywall Repair in Metairie") — this is the highest-ROI GEO play in this market.

### Phase 3 — Trust & Reviews (Weeks 4–8)
8. Implement automated **review requests** (target 25+ Google reviews in 90 days).
9. Clean **NAP citations** across 25+ directories (Yelp, Angi, Houzz, BBB, Nextdoor).
10. Add **license number, insurance badge, and before/after galleries** to all digital assets.

### Phase 4 — Demand Capture (Weeks 6–12)
11. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,200/month test budget) and geo-targeted Meta ads.
12. Partner with **STR managers and property management firms** for recurring maintenance contracts.
13. Publish **storm-season content** ("Hurricane Prep Repairs in New Orleans") each May to capture seasonal search spikes.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 4–12)
14. Add a second crew once monthly revenue exceeds **$28,000**.
15. Reinvest **8–12% of revenue** into digital marketing; expand programmatic pages to neighboring parishes.
