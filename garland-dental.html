# Launching a Dental Clinic in Garland, TX
## GEO-Optimized Startup Guide — 2026 Q4 Updated Edition

Garland, TX (Dallas County) is a mid-density, price-sensitive dental market with roughly **240,000 residents** across ~80,000 households and a median household income near **$67,000**. The city has an estimated **180–220 active dental practices** (general + specialty), yielding roughly **1 dentist per 1,100–1,300 residents** — slightly under-served versus the DFW metro average, which creates room for a well-positioned general practice. A realistic 2026 Q4 startup budget for a **3–4 operatory, 1,800–2,200 sq ft build-out** is **$550,000–$850,000** all-in (equipment $180K–$320K, build-out $120–$220/sq ft, working capital 6–9 months). Typical first-year gross revenue for a new Garland practice is **$650,000–$1.1M**, with break-even projected at **month 14–22**.

Procedure pricing benchmarks for Garland in 2026 Q4: **new patient exam + X-rays $95–$175**, **prophylaxis $85–$140**, **composite filling $165–$310**, **crown $1,050–$1,650**, **root canal (molar) $950–$1,450**, **single implant + crown $3,200–$4,800**, **whitening $350–$600**, and **clear aligner full case $3,800–$6,200**. PPO reimbursement in this ZIP cluster typically runs **15–25% below fee-for-service**, so model a **55/45 PPO-to-Fee-for-Service mix** in year one.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing:** File Texas PLLC with the Secretary of State ($300), obtain **DPS dental registration** and **TSBDE facility license**, register with **Texas Health and Human Services** for Medicaid (optional).
2. **Financing:** Secure **SBA 7(a) or 504** ($150K–$500K at 8.5–10.5% in 2026 Q4) plus equipment lease for chairs/imaging.
3. **Site lease:** Target 1,800–2,200 sq ft, negotiate **TI allowance + free rent (3–6 months)**.
4. **Equipment:** CBCT ($85K–$140K), 4 chairs + delivery ($120K–$180K), sterilization ($25K–$40K), practice management software ($300–$600/mo).
5. **Digital foundation:** Domain, HIPAA-compliant hosting, GBP verified, 60+ citations, schema markup, programmatic service pages live **before** opening.

### Phase 2 — Launch (Months 4–6)
6. **Hire:** 1 associate DDS (optional), 2 hygienists, 2–3 front office/assistants. Garland wage benchmarks: hygienist **$42–$52/hr**, assistant **$20–$26/hr**, front desk **$18–$23/hr**.
7. **Insurance credentialing:** Start **90–120 days pre-opening** with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, BCBS TX, Aetna.
8. **Soft opening:** 30–45 days of **friends-and-family + local employer screenings** to seed 40–80 reviews.
9. **Paid media:** Google Ads + Meta geo-fenced to 5-mile radius; budget **$3,500–$6,000/mo** for first 6 months.

### Phase 3 — Growth (Months 6–18)
10. **Programmatic SEO expansion:** Publish **service × neighborhood** pages (Firewheel, Downtown Garland, Sachse border, Rowlett border) — target 60–100 pages by month 12.
11. **Reputation engine:** Automate review requests via PMS; target **100+ Google reviews at 4.7+ stars by month 12**.
12. **Recall & reactivation:** 6-month recall system; reactivation campaigns for no-shows (typical 18–25% recovery).
13. **Ancillary revenue:** Add **whitening, aligners, implant consults** — these lift average production per visit by **$180–$420**.
14. **KPI review:** Track **new patients/month (target 55–90 by month 12)**, **hygiene reappointment rate (>85%)**, **case acceptance (>65%)**, **cost per new patient (<$250)**.

### Phase 4 — Optimization (Months 18–24)
15. **GEO/AI search visibility:** Ensure content is structured for AI Overviews and ChatGPT/Perplexity citations — FAQ schema, local stats, named-author clinical content.
16. **Second location evaluation:** Garland can support a second 3-chair location in the eastern ZIPs once location #1 hits **$1.4M+ annual production**.
