## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Market & site selection.** Target ZIPs with >1,800 residents per active dentist. Prioritize North County, South City, or metro-east IL. Confirm parking, ADA compliance, and visibility from a major arterial.
2. **Entity & licensing.** Missouri dental license via MO Dental Board; LLC formation ($50–$150); NPI registration; DEA if prescribing; malpractice insurance ($2,800–$6,500/yr).
3. **Financing.** SBA 7(a) ($250K–$500K, 10-yr term) + equipment leasing. Expect 10–15% down.
4. **Build-out & equipment.** Budget $145K–$260K build-out; $95K–$180K equipment (3–4 ops). Negotiate 90–120 day rent abatement.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 3–5, overlapping build-out)
5. **Website + booking.** HIPAA-compliant forms, online scheduling, schema markup (Dentist, LocalBusiness), mobile-first. Budget $2,800–$6,500 freelance.
6. **Google Business Profile + citations.** Verify GBP at address; build 20+ local citations; NAP consistency audit.
7. **Review engine.** Automated post-visit review requests; target 40+ Google reviews by month 6.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Demand Capture (Months 5–7)
8. **Paid search.** $2,500–$6,000/mo Google Ads targeting "dentist near me," "emergency dentist St. Louis," and ZIP-level terms. Target CPA $95–$210.
9. **Insurance credentialing.** Begin 90–120 days pre-launch with top 5 PPOs (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian) — credentialing lag is the #1 revenue killer.
10. **Staffing.** Hire hygienist ($38–$46/hr) + front office ($22–$28/hr) + 1–2 assistants before opening.

### Phase 4 — Stabilize & Scale (Months 7–18)
11. **Content program.** 8 dental-specific posts/month targeting St. Louis neighborhood + procedure keywords (e.g., "invisalign Kirkwood MO").
12. **Recall & reactivation systems.** Automated recall at 6 months; reactivation campaigns for no-shows.
13. **KPI review monthly.** Track new patients/mo (target 35–55 by month 12), production/op, collections rate (>95%), and CAC by channel.
