A realistic 2026 Q4 launch budget runs **$8,500–$22,000** for a compliant, lead-generating solo operation, or **$35,000–$60,000** for a 2–3 crew outfit with a truck, tools, insurance, and digital assets. Nebraska's **LB 271 / contractor registration** framework and Omaha's **$85 two-year handyman registration** (Omaha Municipal Code) are the two compliance items most new entrants miss; budget **$400–$900** for licensing, bonds, and registration filings before your first invoice.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–3)**
1. Register your business with the Nebraska Secretary of State (**$100 LLC filing**).
2. Obtain Omaha handyman registration (**$85, 2-year**) and Nebraska contractor registration where applicable.
3. Secure general liability insurance (**$1,200–$2,400/yr** for solo; $3,500+ for crews) and a surety bond (**$100–$300**).
4. Open a business bank account and set up QuickBooks/Xero.

**Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)**
5. Build a 6-page website with **service-area pages** for Omaha, Papillion, La Vista, Bellevue, Gretna, and Elkhorn.
6. Claim and fully optimize your **Google Business Profile** (categories, services, photos, Q&A, booking link).
7. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,500/mo test budget) — highest-intent lead source in Omaha.
8. Build 25+ local citations (Yelp, Angi, Nextdoor, BBB, Thumbtack).

**Phase 3 — Lead Generation & GEO Content (Weeks 4–8)**
9. Publish 10 GEO-optimized articles targeting queries like *"handyman Omaha NE cost,"* *"deck repair Papillion,"* *"drywall repair Dundee Omaha."*
10. Launch programmatic service-area × service pages (20–40 pages) — this is the highest-ROI GEO play in 2026.
11. Activate Nextdoor business page and neighborhood recommendations.

**Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Weeks 6–12)**
12. Set pricing: **$85/hr** standard, **$125/hr** emergency, **$65/hr** senior/military discount tier.
13. Implement Jobber or Housecall Pro for scheduling, invoicing, and review requests.
14. Target **25 Google reviews in 90 days** — the Omaha top-10 threshold.
15. Review CAC monthly; reinvest in the channel with the lowest cost-per-booked-job.
