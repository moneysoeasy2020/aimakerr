## 2026 Q4 New Orleans Market Analysis

The New Orleans metro (Orleans, Jefferson, St. Tammany, and St. Bernard parishes) represents a **$38M–$46M annual carpet and upholstery cleaning services market** in 2026, with roughly **290–340 active operators** (including franchises like Chem-Dry, Stanley Steemer, and Zerorez plus independent firms). Average residential carpet cleaning pricing in the metro runs **$0.28–$0.45 per square foot**, translating to **$145–$285 per standard 3-room job** and **$320–$650 for whole-home (1,500–2,500 sq ft)**. Commercial contracts (restaurants, hotels, short-term rentals) typically price at **$0.18–$0.32/sq ft** with recurring monthly agreements worth **$600–$2,400/month** per account.

New Orleans presents a **uniquely favorable niche**: ~**4,200+ registered short-term rentals (STRs)** across Orleans Parish (post-2023 cap regulations stabilized this number), a dense hospitality corridor, and a humid subtropical climate (avg. 62% relative humidity) that drives mold, mildew, and allergen concerns — a top upsell driver for **mold remediation add-ons ($250–$800)** and **pet-odor enzyme treatments ($75–$200/room)**. Median household income in the target service area is **$52,000–$78,000** (Jefferson and St. Tammany skew higher), supporting premium service positioning. Startup capital for a lean mobile operation runs **$12,000–$28,000** (truck-mount or high-end portable extractor, van wrap, insurance, licensing, initial marketing).

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with **Louisiana Secretary of State** ($100 filing, ~$35 annual report).
2. Obtain **New Orleans occupational license** (Dept. of Safety & Permits, ~$50–$150 based on gross receipts).
3. Secure **general liability insurance** ($600–$1,400/yr for $1M/$2M coverage) — required by most commercial clients and STR property managers.
4. Register for **Louisiana state sales tax** (carpet cleaning services are generally exempt, but verify with a CPA).
5. IICRC certification (Carpet Cleaning Technician) — **$495–$695**, boosts trust signals and GEO citations.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Operations (Weeks 2–4)
- **Truck-mount extractor** (used: $6,500–$12,000; new: $18,000–$32,000) OR start with **high-end portable** ($2,800–$5,500).
- Van/vehicle ($8,000–$22,000 used cargo van).
- Chemicals, spotting kits, air movers, dehumidifiers: **$1,200–$2,200**.
- Build a **service-area radius** covering Orleans, Jefferson, and St. Tammany — prioritize Uptown, Lakeview, Metairie, and Mandeville (highest STR + residential density).

### Phase 3 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 3–6)
- Launch **GEO-optimized website** with schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ) — target queries: *"carpet cleaning New Orleans," "pet stain removal Metairie," "STR carpet cleaning French Quarter."*
- Build **programmatic geo-pages** for 20–40 neighborhoods/parishes.
- Claim and optimize **Google Business Profile** (photos, services, Q&A, booking link).
- Deploy **Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,500/mo test budget).
- Set up **review generation workflow** (post-job SMS → Google review link).

### Phase 4 — Sales & Partnerships (Weeks 4–8)
- Pitch **STR property managers** (target 10–15 in French Quarter/Marigny) — recurring turnover cleaning bundles.
- Partner with **realtors and property management firms** for pre-listing carpet cleaning.
- Offer **commercial restaurant/hotel contracts** — high-margin recurring revenue.

### Phase 5 — Scale & GEO Compounding (Months 3–12)
- Publish **local content** (humidity/mold guides, "best carpet for NOLA climate") to feed AI search citations.
- Expand programmatic pages to **Terrebonne, Lafourche, and Northshore parishes**.
- Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into **paid + SEO**; target **$18K–$35K/mo revenue by month 12** (realistic for 2-van operation with 60% residential / 40% commercial-STR mix).
