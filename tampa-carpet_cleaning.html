## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2)**
1. Register LLC with Florida Sunbiz (**$125**).
2. Obtain Hillsborough County Local Business Tax Receipt (**~$30–$60**).
3. Secure general liability + commercial auto insurance (**$1,800–$3,200/year** for single truck).
4. IICRC certification (CCT) — **$500–$800** — a key trust signal in Tampa's competitive market.

**Phase 2 — Equipment & Vehicle (Weeks 2–4)**
- Used van-mounted unit: **$8,000–$16,000**
- Chemicals + spotting kit: **$600–$1,200**
- Portable backup + upholstery tools: **$1,500–$3,000**

**Phase 3 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–5)**
- Build website + GBP via verified freelancer (**$3,000–$7,000** total per matrix above).
- Implement local schema, service-area pages (Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel), and review-request automation.

**Phase 4 — B2B Outreach (Weeks 4–6)**
- Target 40–60 property management firms (Tampa has 1,200+); offer turnover pricing at **$129–$159 per unit**.
- Pitch Westshore/Brandon commercial accounts at **$0.18–$0.32/sq ft**.

**Phase 5 — Paid + Organic Launch (Weeks 5–8)**
- Google Ads: **$1,500–$3,000/month** starting budget; target $25–$45 cost-per-lead in Tampa.
- Review velocity goal: **15–25 Google reviews in first 90 days**.

**Phase 6 — Scale (Months 4–7)**
- Add second truck when monthly revenue exceeds **$18,000**; hire IICRC-certified tech at **$18–$24/hour**.
- Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into local SEO and review generation.
