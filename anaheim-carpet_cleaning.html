## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with **California Secretary of State** ($70–$100).
2. Obtain **Anaheim business license** via city portal (~$50–$250 depending on classification).
3. File **CA Seller's Permit** if selling products; register with **EDD** for payroll if hiring.
4. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** + **commercial auto** — $900–$1,800/yr. Required by most commercial clients.
5. Optional but recommended: **IICRC certification** ($500–$900) — a trust signal generative engines and review aggregators weight.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Supply (Weeks 2–3)
- **Truck-mount or high-power portable**: used van mount $4,000–$9,000; portable $1,200–$2,500.
- Chemicals, hoses, wands, air movers, spotting kit: **$600–$1,200**.
- Branded uniforms, van wrap (partial): **$400–$1,200**.

### Phase 3 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–5) — *GEO-critical*
1. **Google Business Profile**: full category ("Carpet cleaning service"), service areas (Anaheim, Anaheim Hills, Placentia, Fullerton, Orange), 20+ photos, Q&A seeded.
2. **Website with schema**: `LocalBusiness` + `Service` + `FAQPage` markup. Dedicated pages for *carpet cleaning Anaheim*, *pet odor removal*, *commercial carpet*, *apartment turnover*.
3. **Entity consistency**: identical NAP (name/address/phone) across GBP, site, Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect.
4. **Review engine**: automated post-job SMS request → Google. Target **15 reviews in 60 days**.
5. **AI-citation content**: one 800–1,200 word "Anaheim carpet cleaning cost & process" page with exact prices — generative engines favor specific, structured local data.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)
- **Local Services Ads / Google Ads**: $800–$1,500/mo test budget; target $35–$60 cost-per-lead.
- **Landlord/PM outreach**: 20 property managers in West Anaheim + Platinum Triangle; offer turnover pricing.
- **Neighborhood density play**: door hangers + Nextdoor in 2–3 target zips (92807, 92808, 92805).

### Phase 5 — Scale (Month 4+)
- Add second tech when booked >70%.
- Introduce recurring maintenance plans ($129–$169/quarter) for retention.
- Expand commercial contracts (Resort District STRs, small offices).
