# Launching a Carpet Cleaning Business in Aurora, CO

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register LLC in Colorado** (~$50), get EIN, open business bank account.
2. **Secure licensing/insurance:** Aurora business license, general liability + equipment coverage (~$600–$1,400/yr).
3. **Choose model:** van-mounted (higher ticket, higher capex) vs. portable/lease (fast start).
4. **Buy/lease equipment:** truck-mount $18K–$40K; portable $3K–$8K; chemicals/accessories $800–$1,500.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
5. **Launch GEO-optimized website** with service-area pages (Aurora, Centennial, Parker, Denver SE).
6. **Optimize Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas, photos, Q&A, booking link.
7. **Build 30+ local citations** (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor).
8. **Set up booking + review software** (Jobber/Housecall Pro + review automation).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–10)
9. **Launch Google Local Services Ads + targeted Google Ads** ($1,000–$2,500/mo initial).
10. **Review velocity campaign:** target 15–25 reviews in first 90 days.
11. **Partner channels:** realtors, property managers, apartment complexes (deposit-clean contracts).
12. **Seasonal promos:** "Holiday Ready" (Oct–Nov), "New Year Deep Clean" (Jan).

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Month 4–12)
13. **Track CAC, repeat rate, and ticket size** monthly; kill underperforming channels.
14. **Add commercial contracts** for revenue stability.
15. **Expand service-area pages** and AI-search-optimized FAQ content.
16. **Hire first technician** at ~40–50 jobs/month; document SOPs.
