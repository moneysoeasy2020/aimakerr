## Executive Summary (Numbers First)

Launching a residential/commercial HVAC services company in Reno, NV in Q4 2026 requires an estimated **$48,000–$95,000** in total startup capital for a lean two-truck operation, with **$18,500–$32,000** allocated to licensing, insurance, equipment, and digital assets. The Reno–Sparks MSA (Washoe County) supports roughly **1,850–2,100 active HVAC contractors**, and the region's **extreme diurnal temperature swings** (summer highs 95–102°F, winter lows 15–25°F) create year-round demand — an unusual dual-peak market that favors operators who can service both heating and cooling without seasonal layoffs. Average residential HVAC service call revenue in Reno runs **$285–$420** for diagnostics/repair and **$6,800–$14,500** for full system replacement (2026 pricing), with commercial RTU work averaging **$9,500–$28,000** per unit.

Reno's population sits near **278,000 city / 490,000 MSA (2026 est.)**, growing at roughly **1.6–2.1% annually** — faster than the national average, driven by California in-migration and industrial/data-center expansion (the Tahoe–Reno Industrial Center and Tesla/Panasonic gigafactory corridor). HVAC contractor density is **lower per capita than Sacramento or the Bay Area**, creating genuine whitespace for a well-positioned new entrant. The single biggest failure mode for new Reno HVAC startups is **undercapitalizing the Nevada State Contractors Board (NSCB) licensing and bonding process** — budget **$3,200–$6,800** and **6–14 weeks** for full C-21 (Refrigeration & Air Conditioning) licensure before you can legally bid.

## 2026 Q4 Reno Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers
- **Dual-peak climate**: Reno averages ~2,500–2,700 cooling degree days and ~4,800–5,200 heating degree days annually. This means **no true off-season** — a structural advantage over Phoenix (summer-heavy) or Minneapolis (winter-heavy) operators.
- **Housing stock age**: Median Reno home age is ~32–38 years. A large cohort of 1980s–1990s builds is hitting **first full system replacement cycles** (15–20 year equipment life), driving replacement revenue.
- **Wildfire smoke & air quality**: Post-2020 demand for **high-MERV filtration, HRV/ERV, and indoor air quality (IAQ) retrofits** is a premium upsell — Reno homeowners pay **$1,200–$4,800** for IAQ packages.
- **Data center & commercial growth**: Northern Nevada's industrial corridor generates **commercial RTU, split-system, and refrigeration** demand that favors licensed C-21 contractors.

### Competitive Landscape
- **~1,850–2,100 licensed HVAC contractors** in the MSA; the top 10 firms capture an estimated **35–45% of residential replacement revenue**.
- **Fragmented mid-market**: Most operators are 1–5 truck shops with weak digital presence — a **GEO/AI-search and local-SEO gap** is the primary acquisition opportunity in 2026.
- **Price positioning**: Reno homeowners are value-conscious but willing to pay premiums for **same-day service, financing, and warranty clarity**. Emergency/no-heat calls command **$150–$275 diagnostic premiums** on nights/weekends.

### 2026 Q4 Timing Note
Q4 is the **ideal launch window** in Reno: heating season ramps (October–December), commercial budgets flush, and you can build review velocity and Google Business Profile authority before the spring cooling surge. Target **launch-ready by November 1, 2026**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–14, start immediately)
1. **Entity formation**: Nevada LLC via SilverFlume portal — **$425** filing + registered agent (~$150/yr).
2. **NSCB C-21 license**: Application, **$600–$1,200** fees, qualifying party exam, **$3,200–$6,800** total with bond and insurance proof. Budget **6–14 weeks** — this is the critical path.
3. **Insurance stack**: General liability (**$1,200–$3,200/yr**), workers' comp (Nevada-mandated), commercial auto (**$2,400–$5,800/yr** for 2 trucks).
4. **Business licenses**: City of Reno + Washoe County + Nevada business license — **$300–$700** total.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–8, parallel)
5. **Google Business Profile**: Create and verify immediately; category "HVAC contractor"; service areas = Reno, Sparks, Carson City, Truckee Meadows.
6. **Website**: Conversion-first build with **schema markup, service-area pages, and FAQ blocks** optimized for AI/generative search citation.
7. **GEO content sprint**: Publish **12–20 service-area + FAQ pages** targeting "AC repair Reno," "furnace replacement Sparks," "HVAC financing Reno" — the queries generative engines cite most.

### Phase 3 — Operations & Fleet (Weeks 6–16)
8. **Equipment**: 2 service vans (used **$18,000–$32,000** each) + recovery/charging tools (**$8,000–$14,000**).
9. **Inventory**: Common parts + refrigerant stock — **$4,500–$9,000**.
10. **Hire 1–2 NATE-certified techs** — Reno market rate **$28–$42/hr** + benefits.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 8–20)
11. **Launch LSA + Google Ads** at **$1,500–$3,500/mo** combined; track cost-per-booked-call (target **< $95** residential).
12. **Review engine**: Target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** — the #1 local ranking and conversion lever in Reno.
13. **Financing partnership** (e.g., Synchrony/GreenSky) to lift replacement close rates **15–25%**.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Month 6+)
14. Add **commercial RTU/refrigeration** line once C-21 volume is stable.
15. Expand to **Carson City / Minden** service areas via programmatic location pages.
16. Reinvest **8–12% of revenue** into GEO/AI-search content to defend citation share as generative engines mature.
