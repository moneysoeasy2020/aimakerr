## 2026 Q4 Memphis Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers
- **Climate:** Memphis averages **94°F summer highs** and 3+ months above 85°F. HVAC failure calls spike June–August and again during December cold snaps (lows near 30°F).
- **Housing stock:** ~**62% of Shelby County homes are older than 1980**, driving replacement demand; average home age in Memphis is **~48 years**.
- **Population churn:** Memphis sees steady relocation traffic (FedEx, St. Jude, AutoZone HQ workforce), producing move-in inspection and system-replacement demand.
- **Competitive density:** Approximately **180–220 licensed HVAC contractors** operate in Shelby County. The market is fragmented — no single player holds >8% residential share — leaving room for a well-positioned newcomer.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. Register LLC with **Tennessee Secretary of State** (~$300).
2. Obtain **TN HVAC contractor license** (Class CMC-A or CMC-C depending on scope) via TN Board for Licensing Contractors — exam + insurance + experience requirements.
3. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and commercial auto — **$2,400–$4,800/year**.
4. Obtain **EPA Section 608 certification** for refrigerant handling (all techs).
5. Set up business banking, EIN, and TN sales/use tax registration.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 3–8)
6. Launch GEO-optimized website with **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**, service-area pages for **Memphis, Germantown, Collierville, Bartlett, Cordova, Southaven**.
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, Q&A, photos weekly).
8. Build **25+ local citations** (BBB, Angi, Yelp, Nextdoor, Thumbtack, Chamber).
9. Deploy **review-generation SMS** post-service; target **25 reviews in first 90 days**.
10. Publish **8–12 FAQ/blog posts** answering AI-search queries ("How much does AC replacement cost in Memphis?").

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)
11. Activate **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$28–$65/lead in Memphis HVAC).
12. Launch **Google Ads** on high-intent keywords — budget **$1,500–$3,500/mo**.
13. Run **Nextdoor + neighborhood direct mail** to 2,000 homes in Cordova/Germantown.
14. Partner with **3–5 Memphis realtors and property managers** for recurring referrals.

### Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Months 4–12)
15. Implement **Jobber or Housecall Pro** for dispatch, invoicing, and review automation (**$99–$350/mo**).
16. Hire first **licensed tech** ($28–$42/hr) + apprentice.
17. Launch **maintenance plan** ($180–$420/year) to stabilize recurring revenue.
18. Add second truck once monthly revenue exceeds **$35K**.
19. Reinvest **8–12% of revenue** into SEO/GEO and paid media.

### 90-Day Milestone Targets
- **Website live + schema + 20 service-area pages** by Day 30
- **25 Google reviews** by Day 90
- **40–70 inbound leads/month** by Day 90
- **$18K–$32K monthly revenue** by Day 90 (2-tech operation)
