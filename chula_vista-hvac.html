### Demand Drivers
- **Population:** ~285,000 (San Diego County's second-largest city), growing ~1% annually.
- **Housing units:** ~80,000; roughly 55% owner-occupied.
- **Climate:** Coastal-inland transition zone. Cooling degree days (CDD) ~1,400–1,700/yr; heating degree days (HDD) ~900–1,200/yr. Year-round demand — no true off-season.
- **Equipment age:** The 2005–2012 construction wave (Otay Ranch, Eastlake) means thousands of systems are now 14–21 years old — prime replacement window through 2028.

### Competitive Landscape
- **Licensed HVAC contractors in Chula Vista:** ~120–160 active (CSLB C-20 / C-38 classifications), plus ~40–60 San Diego metro firms that market into the city.

- **Typical competitor weakness:** Slow callback times (24–72 hrs), opaque pricing, weak Google Business Profile management. A new entrant with <2-hour response and published pricing can win share within 6–9 months.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–6)
1. **Licensing:** Obtain CSLB C-20 (Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating & Air-Conditioning) license. Budget **$400–$800** in fees + exam prep; expect 4–8 weeks processing. Secure EPA Section 608 certification ($150–$400).
2. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto + workers' comp. Budget **$3,500–$7,500/yr**.
3. **Entity & bonding:** LLC formation ($800–$1,500 in CA), contractor bond ($25,000 bond, ~$300–$600/yr premium).
4. **Equipment & vehicles:** Used service van ($18,000–$32,000), recovery machines, gauges, vacuum pumps, brazing kit, refrigerant stock. Budget **$12,000–$22,000**.

### Phase 2 — Digital Launch (Weeks 4–10)
5. **Google Business Profile:** Create, verify, and optimize with service-area pages for Otay Ranch, Eastlake, Bonita, Chula Vista proper. This is the #1 lead source for local HVAC — prioritize it.
6. **Website:** Launch with programmatic neighborhood landing pages, transparent pricing tables, and online booking. Target Core Web Vitals (LCP <2.5s).
7. **Review engine:** Automate post-job review requests. Goal: **50 reviews at 4.8+ stars within 9 months**.
8. **Paid search:** Start Google Local Services Ads + a $1,500–$3,000/mo Search campaign targeting "AC repair Chula Vista," "HVAC near me," "heat pump install South Bay."

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 8–24)
9. **Maintenance plan launch:** Sell annual plans at $280–$420 to build recurring revenue and replacement pipeline.
10. **Bilingual dispatch:** Staff or contract Spanish-speaking CSR — measurable conversion lift in Chula Vista.
11. **Partnerships:** Realtors, property managers (South Bay has ~15,000+ rental units), and home warranty networks.
12. **Programmatic SEO:** Publish 40–80 localized pages ("AC repair Otay Ranch," "furnace tune-up Eastlake") over 12 months.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 6–12)
13. **Second technician** once monthly service revenue exceeds $28,000.
14. **Fleet branding** and local sponsorships (youth sports, community events) for brand recall.
15. **Track CAC by channel** monthly; kill any channel above $180 CAC on service calls.
