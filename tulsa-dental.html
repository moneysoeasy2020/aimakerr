## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Tulsa, 2026 Q4)

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing:** File Oklahoma LLC ($100), obtain **Oklahoma Board of Dentistry license** (verify via ok.gov), register DEA and Oklahoma CDS for controlled substances, and secure **Tulsa County health department** occupancy permits.
2. **Site selection:** Target 2,200–3,200 sq ft in **Broken Arrow, Owasso, or South Tulsa**; negotiate **6–9 months free rent** on a 7–10 year NNN lease (standard Tulsa concession in 2026 Q4).
3. **Financing:** Secure **SBA 7(a)** ($50K–$5M, 2026 rates ~9.5–11.5%) or **bank dental practice loan** (5–7 yr, 8.5–10%); budget 15% contingency.
4. **Equipment:** Get 3 quotes from **Patterson, Henry Schein, and Benco**; lease vs. buy analysis at 2026 rates.

### Phase 2 — Build-Out & Digital Foundation (Months 4–7)
5. **TI construction:** Dental-specific plumbing, vacuum, and electrical; expect **90–120 days** in Tulsa permitting.
6. **Digital assets:** Launch website, Google Business Profile, and 20+ citations **before opening** to build indexing age; publish 8–12 service-area pages targeting **"dentist in Broken Arrow," "emergency dentist Tulsa," "dental implants Owasso."**
7. **Insurance credentialing:** Begin PPO credentialing **90 days pre-opening** (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Guardian are top Tulsa carriers); credentialing takes 60–120 days.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 7–12)
8. **Soft launch:** 4–6 weeks of friends/family + referral pricing to generate **40–60 Google reviews** before paid ads scale.
9. **Paid acquisition:** Launch Google Ads at **$2,500–$4,000/mo** targeting high-intent queries; expect **$180–$320 cost per new patient** in Tulsa.
10. **Membership plan:** Launch in-house plan at **$29–$39/mo** to capture the ~15% uninsured segment.
11. **KPI targets by Month 12:** **180–260 new patients/month**, **$95K–$140K monthly collections**, **$1.1M–$1.6M annualized run-rate**, and **4.7+ star rating with 150+ reviews**.
