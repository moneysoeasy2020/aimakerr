## Executive Snapshot: The Numbers First

Launching a dental clinic in New Orleans, LA in Q4 2026 requires a realistic total startup budget of **$485,000–$780,000** for a brick-and-mortar practice (3–5 operatories), or **$95,000–$180,000** for a lean startup model (1–2 operatories in a shared suite). The single largest cost driver is dental equipment and build-out: a fully equipped 4-operatory clinic runs **$280,000–$420,000** in equipment (chairs, delivery units, digital X-ray/CBCT, autoclave, cabinetry) plus **$85,000–$160,000** in tenant improvement (TI) construction at New Orleans commercial rates of **$95–$180 per square foot**. Dental-specific commercial lease space in Orleans Parish averaged **$24–$38/sq ft NNN** in Q3 2026, with prime Uptown and CBD locations at the higher end.

New Orleans' dental market is genuinely underserved: Orleans Parish has approximately **1 dentist per 2,100 residents**, below the national ratio of roughly 1 per 1,600, and roughly **38% of Louisiana adults report no dental visit in the past 12 months**. The metro area (population ~1.27 million) supports roughly **560 active dental practices**, with heavy concentration in Metairie and the Northshore, leaving gaps in Gentilly, New Orleans East, and the Lower Ninth Ward. Medicaid adult dental coverage in Louisiana remains limited, but the **Louisiana Department of Health's 2026 Medicaid dental expansion pilot** creates new patient volume for clinics willing to credential. Expect **$18,000–$45,000** in Year-1 digital marketing spend to reach a realistic **$850,000–$1,400,000** in Year-1 collections for a 4-operatory practice at 65–75% chair utilization.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensure:** Form LA LLC/PC ($125), obtain LSBD license ($250), secure NPI and Medicaid/Medicare credentialing (start early — 90–150 days).
2. **Site selection:** Target 1,800–2,800 sq ft; negotiate TI allowance ($30–$75/sq ft typical) and free-rent period (3–6 months).
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) or equipment leasing; expect 10–20% down, 7–10% APR in Q4 2026.
4. **Equipment procurement:** Get 3 quotes; digital workflow (intraoral scanner, CBCT, 3D printer) is table stakes for 2026.

### Phase 2 — Build-Out & Digital Foundation (Months 3–6)
5. **Construction:** 90–150 days typical; permit delays in Orleans Parish can add 30–45 days — budget for it.
6. **Website + GBP:** Launch 60 days before opening; claim/verify Google Business Profile immediately; add Dentist + LocalBusiness schema.
7. **Programmatic SEO:** Build service×location pages (e.g., "emergency dentist Gentilly," "Invisalign Uptown") — 25–60 pages at launch.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 6–12)
8. **Soft launch:** 2–4 weeks of friends/family + discounted hygiene to build reviews (target 40+ Google reviews in 90 days).
9. **Paid ads:** Google Search (emergency + high-intent) + Meta retargeting; $3,000–$6,000/mo starting budget.
10. **Insurance credentialing live:** This unlocks the bulk of patient volume — chase it relentlessly.

### Phase 4 — Optimization (Months 12–18)
11. **GEO/AI-search optimization:** Structured data, FAQ schema, authoritative citations, and consistent NAP across 40+ directories so generative engines cite your clinic.
12. **Review velocity:** Automate post-visit review requests; target 8–15 new reviews/month.
13. **Recall & reactivation:** Automated recall system to lift hygiene reappointment rate above 70%.
