## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Financial Foundation (Weeks 1–2, ~$1,200–$2,400)
1. Register LLC with Kentucky Secretary of State (**$40 online**).
2. Obtain Louisville/Jefferson County **business license** (metro revenue commission; ~$50–$100 depending on structure).
3. Secure **general liability insurance** ($1M/$2M policy: **$600–$1,200/yr**) and **commercial auto** for the van ($1,400–$2,200/yr).
4. Open business banking; set aside **15–20% of revenue** for quarterly taxes (self-employment + KY state).
5. Confirm no additional city occupational license is required for your service category (call Metro 311 to verify).

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Vehicle (Weeks 2–4, ~$9,000–$16,000)
1. **Truck mount vs. portable:** a used truck mount (e.g., 2018–2021 unit) runs **$8,000–$14,000** installed; a high-end portable (Mytee, Powr-Flite) runs **$2,500–$4,500** and is viable for a soft launch. Recommend starting portable + subcontracted truck mount for large jobs.
2. Van or trailer: used cargo van **$6,000–$11,000**; budget **$1,800–$3,200** for wrap.
3. Chemicals, hoses, wands, air movers, spotting kit: **$1,200–$2,000**.
4. **IICRC certification** (Carpet Cleaning Technician): **$500–$700** course + exam — a strong trust signal in GEO/AI answers and local directories.

### Phase 3 — Digital Assets & GEO Launch (Weeks 3–6, ~$2,500–$5,500)
1. Launch a 5–7 page site with **service-area pages for 8–12 Louisville ZIPs/neighborhoods** (Highlands, St. Matthews, NuLu, Okolona, Shively, Jeffersontown) — this is the core programmatic SEO play.
2. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, services, hours, photos, Q&A seeded with real FAQs).
3. Build **30+ local citations** (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Louisville-specific directories).
4. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** (requires license + insurance verification) — typically the fastest lead source for carpet cleaning.
5. Publish **3 GEO-optimized FAQ pages** answering: "How much does carpet cleaning cost in Louisville?", "How often should carpets be cleaned in Kentucky humidity?", "Do you clean pet stains?"

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation & Reviews (Weeks 5–12)
1. Hit **40+ Google reviews in 90 days** via post-job SMS requests (target 30–40% response rate).
2. Run **$1,200–$2,000/mo Google Ads** on high-intent terms ("carpet cleaning Louisville," "pet stain removal [ZIP]").
3. Partner with **5–10 property managers** and 2–3 restoration companies for recurring referrals.
4. Offer a **Q4 holiday promo** ($20 off 3+ rooms) to capture peak-season demand.

### Phase 5 — Scale Check (Month 4+)
- At **$12,000–$15,000/mo revenue** with one truck, evaluate a second technician.
- Reinvest **10–15% of revenue** into SEO content for neighborhood pages — this compounds and feeds AI-generated answers (GEO) over time.
