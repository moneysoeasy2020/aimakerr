## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Licensing & Legal (Weeks 1–6)
1. Obtain **Michigan Mechanical Contractor license** (state exam + insurance certificate); register with the **City of Detroit** for local permits.
2. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and **commercial auto** coverage — budget **$4,800–$8,200/year** for a two-truck operation.
3. Form LLC, EIN, Michigan sales tax registration, and a business banking account.

### Phase 2 — Fleet, Tools & Inventory (Weeks 4–10)
4. Two used service vans (**$18,000–$32,000 each**), recovery machines, vacuum pumps, gauges, combustion analyzers, and a starter parts inventory (**$6,000–$9,000**).
5. Set up dispatch software (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber — **$150–$450/month**) and a phone/answering service for after-hours (**$200–$500/month**).

### Phase 3 — Digital Launch (Weeks 6–12)
6. Deploy website with **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**, service-area pages, and Detroit-specific content (cite HDD, housing-age, and pricing data — AI engines reward verifiable local facts).
7. Optimize Google Business Profile; seed 15–25 Q&A entries; launch review-request automation.
8. Publish **40–80 programmatic ZIP/city pages** (e.g., "Furnace Repair in 48224") with unique local data per page — avoid thin duplication.
9. Run **$1,200–$2,500/month** Google Local Services Ads + Search; track cost-per-lead (target **$38–$85** in Detroit metro).

### Phase 4 — Revenue & Retention (Month 3+)
10. Launch **maintenance-plan subscriptions** ($14–$22/month) — target 120–200 members by Month 6 for predictable recurring revenue.
11. Pursue **property-management B2B accounts** (Detroit's 46% renter share = high-volume maintenance contracts).
12. Review KPIs monthly: booked-job rate, average ticket (**target $340–$520**), close rate (**target 68–78%** on replacement estimates), and review velocity (**target 8–15 new reviews/month**).
