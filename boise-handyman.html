## Licensing & Compliance (Idaho-Specific)

- **Idaho contractor registration** through Idaho Bureau of Occupational Licenses (IBOL) is required for construction contractors; handyman work under the registration threshold still benefits from voluntary registration for trust signals.
- **Boise City business license** required (~$60–$120/yr depending on classification).
- **General liability insurance**: $1M/$2M coverage runs **$1,200–$3,200/yr** for a solo handyman; workers' comp required if you hire.
- **Bonding**: $10,000–$15,000 surety bond typically **$150–$400/yr**.
- **EPA RRP certification** (lead-safe) — required for pre-1978 homes; Boise has substantial pre-1978 stock. Certification course: **$250–$450**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register with IBOL, obtain Boise business license, secure GL insurance and bond.
2. Complete EPA RRP certification if targeting pre-1978 homes.
3. Define service menu and 2026 Q4 price sheet (use ranges above).
4. Open business banking and set up invoicing (Jobber, Housecall Pro, or Wave).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
5. Launch a 5–8 page website: Home, Services, Service Areas (Boise, Meridian, Eagle, Nampa, Caldwell), About, Reviews, Contact/Booking.
6. Create and verify **Google Business Profile** — primary category "Handyman," secondary "Contractor."
7. Build 20–30 local citations (Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Thumbtack).
8. Install review-request automation (SMS/email after each job).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–10)
9. Join **Boise Metro Chamber** and local contractor groups for referral flow.
10. Contact 10–15 property managers in Meridian/Eagle/Nampa with a maintenance rate card.
11. Launch Google Local Services Ads ($500–$1,500/mo test budget).
12. Begin programmatic service-area pages (e.g., "Deck Repair Meridian ID") — 10–20 pages.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)
13. Target 40–60 Google reviews in first 6 months.
14. Add second technician when booked capacity exceeds 70%.
15. Introduce recurring maintenance contracts ($99–$199/mo tiers).
16. Reassess agency vs. freelancer spend at $25,000/mo revenue threshold.
