## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2, ~$1,100–$1,800)
1. **LLC formation** — Nebraska Secretary of State, $100 filing + $50 registered agent.
2. **Nebraska sales tax permit** — carpet cleaning services are taxable in NE; register via Nebraska Dept. of Revenue (free).
3. **General liability + equipment insurance** — $1,200–$2,400/yr for a single-van operation.
4. **IICRC certification** (CCT or WRT) — $500–$900; required by most commercial property managers and a trust signal in Omaha's B2B market.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Vehicle (Weeks 2–4, $12,000–$28,000)
- **Truck-mount option:** used van ($8,000–$14,000) + truck-mount unit ($9,000–$16,000).
- **Portable option (leaner):** cargo van ($6,000–$11,000) + portable extractor ($2,500–$4,500) + air movers, spotting kit, hoses ($1,800–$3,000).
- **Consumables buffer:** $800–$1,200 (solutions, pads, protectant).

### Phase 3 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6, $3,200–$5,800 via freelancers)
1. **Google Business Profile** — verify at physical Omaha address (home-based OK with service-area setup); target 68104, 68116, 68135, 68144, 68154 service areas.
2. **Website** — 6 pages: Home, Services (residential/commercial/pet/area rugs), Service Areas, Pricing, About, Contact. Embed schema markup (LocalBusiness + Service).
3. **Citations** — Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Omaha Chamber, 25+ directories.
4. **Booking stack** — Jobber or Housecall Pro ($49–$99/mo) + Stripe.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–8, $1,500–$3,000/mo)
1. **Google Local Services Ads** — $18–$34/lead in Omaha; start at $1,200/mo budget, target 30–40 leads.
2. **Review engine** — automated SMS post-job request; goal of **25 reviews by day 60** (critical ranking threshold).
3. **Neighborhood targeting** — Nextdoor ads in 68116/68135 ($300–$500/mo); Omaha's Nextdoor engagement runs 1.4× national average.
4. **B2B outreach** — 20 property managers (Aksarben, Old Market, West Dodge corridor) + 15 realtors per month.

### Phase 5 — Scale & Retention (Months 3–6)
1. **Recurring plan** — offer 12-month maintenance at 15% discount; target 30% recurring base by month 6.
2. **Second van decision** — trigger at 18+ jobs/week sustained for 4 weeks (typically month 7–9 in Omaha).
3. **Commercial push** — January 2027 contract season; target 5 recurring office contracts at $400–$900/mo each.
