Disadvantages: 12-month contracts are common, junior staff often execute the work, and you pay for overhead (office, account managers) that doesn't touch your rankings.

**Verified freelance experts (vetted via portfolio + client references + platform verification):** Comparable scope runs **$1,100–$2,100/month**, or **$3,500–$6,000 one-time** for a launch package (site + GBP + 10 city/service pages + review funnel). Disadvantages: you must verify proof of work — demand **3 live GBP case studies in service trades** and **2 client references in the same metro size**.

**Recommendation for a Q4 2026 Boise launch:** Hire a verified freelance web/SEO specialist for the build ($2,500–$4,000 one-time) and a freelance ads manager at **$500–$700/month** for the first two quarters. Reassess at month 6 — if monthly booked revenue exceeds **$18,000**, an agency retainer becomes defensible; below that, freelancers preserve margin.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Financial Setup (Weeks 1–2)**
1. Register an LLC with the **Idaho Secretary of State** ($100 filing fee); obtain an **Idaho seller's permit** and Boise business license.
2. Secure general liability insurance (**$600–$1,200/year** for solo carpet cleaning) plus commercial auto coverage on the van.
3. Open a business checking account; set aside **$6,000–$9,000** working capital for the first 90 days.

**Phase 2 — Equipment & Supply (Weeks 2–4)**
4. Purchase a used truck-mount (e.g., 2018–2021 unit, **$9,000–$16,000**) or a commercial portable (**$3,500–$6,000**) if starting in an apartment/condo market. Budget **$1,200–$1,800** for hoses, wands, and chemicals; **$800–$1,500** for van shelving and branding.

**Phase 3 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6)**
5. Build the website with **service pages + 8–12 Boise neighborhood pages** (North End, Bench, Meridian, Eagle, Garden City) — programmatic city-page structure drives long-tail GEO visibility.
6. Claim and fully optimize the **Google Business Profile**: categories, service areas, 20+ photos, Q&A seeding, and booking link.
7. Deploy a **review-request SMS sequence** triggered at job completion — target **25 reviews by day 90, 60 by day 180**.

**Phase 4 — B2B Channel Development (Weeks 4–10)**
8. Contact **15–25 Boise property management firms** (there are 60+ active in Ada County) with a move-out rate sheet; 3–5 accounts can fill 30–40% of capacity.
9. Partner with **2–3 real estate agents per office** in the Bench/Meridian corridor for pre-listing cleans.

**Phase 5 — Paid Acquisition & Scale (Months 3–9)**
10. Launch Google Local Services Ads (**$8–$18/lead** in Boise carpet cleaning) alongside a modest Search campaign (**$600–$1,200/month** budget).
11. Add a second technician at **~$22–$26/hour** once monthly revenue holds above **$14,000** for two consecutive months.

**Key Q4 2026 milestones:** 60+ reviews and local-pack entry by month 6; **$12,000–$16,000/month** revenue by month 9; second van decision by month 12–14 based on route density in Meridian/Eagle.
