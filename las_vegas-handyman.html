# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Las Vegas, NV
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

The Las Vegas–Henderson–Paradise metro (Clark County) represents one of the most active handyman markets in the U.S., with roughly **2.3 million residents** across ~**780,000 occupied housing units** and a median home age of **~24 years** — a prime repair-and-remodel demographic. The Nevada State Contractors Board (NSCB) classifies most handyman work as **"minor repairs"** and enforces a **$1,000 combined labor-and-materials threshold per job**; anything above requires a licensed contractor. Expect startup costs of **$3,500–$9,000** for a compliant, mobile-first handyman operation, with typical Q4 2026 billing rates of **$65–$95/hour** for handyman labor, **$95–$150/hour** for specialty trades, and minimum service call fees of **$125–$185**.

The 2026 Q4 Las Vegas market is defined by three forces: post-2024 HOA repair mandates across Summerlin and Henderson, a wave of **short-term rental (STR) turnover maintenance** demand near the Strip and Downtown, and a seasonal Q4 "pre-holiday repair" surge that typically lifts average job tickets **18–25%** versus Q3. Digital customer acquisition in this metro is competitive but tractable — a well-optimized Google Business Profile plus a programmatic service-area site can generate **15–40 qualified leads/month** within 90 days at a blended cost per lead of **$22–$58**, provided you avoid the overpriced local agency trap outlined below.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Entity Setup (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Register LLC** with Nevada Secretary of State ($75 online, ~$150 with expedite).
2. **Obtain Nevada Business License** via SilverFlume (state fee $200; Clark County business license $100–$400 depending on classification).
3. **Confirm NSCB classification** — stay under the $1,000 minor-repair cap OR obtain a **Class B (general) or specialty license** if targeting remodel work. Budget $500–$1,200 for licensing/insurance bonding if pursuing full contractor status.
4. **Secure general liability insurance** — $1M/$2M policy runs $1,200–$2,400/year for a handyman operation in Clark County.
5. **Open business banking** and set up a separate tax account (Nevada has no state income tax — a real margin advantage).

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–5)
1. **Launch a 5–8 page website** with service pages (drywall, plumbing, electrical fixtures, assembly, STR turnover) and a booking form.
2. **Build 40–80 programmatic service-area pages** targeting Las Vegas neighborhoods (Summerlin, Henderson, Green Valley, Spring Valley, Centennial Hills, Anthem, Downtown, North Las Vegas).
3. **Optimize Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas, 20+ photos, Q&A, and a booking link.
4. **Set up Google Local Services Ads** (handyman category) — budget $800–$2,500/month in ad spend.
5. **Install a review-generation system** — automated SMS/email review requests post-job.

### Phase 3 — B2B Channel Development (Weeks 4–8)
1. **Pitch 10–20 STR property managers** near the Strip and Downtown — offer same-day turnover maintenance at a contracted rate.
2. **Contact 5–10 HOA management companies** in Summerlin and Henderson for pre-winter repair contracts.
3. **Partner with 3–5 real estate agents** for pre-listing repair packages.

### Phase 4 — Q4 Demand Capture (Weeks 6–12)
1. **Run a "Pre-Holiday Repair Special"** — flat-rate packages ($149–$299) for gutter cleaning, caulking, and fixture checks.
2. **Deploy branded vehicle wraps** on 1–2 trucks for local visibility.
3. **Publish 4–6 local content pieces** (e.g., "Las Vegas Winter Home Prep Checklist") to feed GEO/AI-search visibility.
4. **Track cost per lead weekly** — target $22–$58 blended; cut any channel above $75 CPL.

### Phase 5 — Scale & Optimize (Month 4+)
1. **Review 90-day data** — double down on top 2 lead channels, cut underperformers.
2. **Hire first technician** at $22–$30/hour + performance bonus once you exceed 60 jobs/month.
3. **Expand programmatic pages** to 120+ as revenue supports it.
4. **Reassess agency vs. freelance** engagement at the $40K/month revenue threshold.
