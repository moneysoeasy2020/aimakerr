## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–3)
1. **Market & site selection:** Target North Lincoln or Yankee Hill; budget **$16–$24/sq ft NNN** for 2,400 sq ft.
2. **Entity & licensure:** Form Nebraska LLC/PC, obtain DHHS facility license, register with Nebraska Dental Board.
3. **Financing:** Secure **$750K–$1.1M** via SBA 7(a) (typical 10-yr term, 7.5–9.5% in Q4 2026) or equipment leasing.
4. **Equipment:** 4 chairs + digital X-ray + sterilization — **$180K–$320K** (mid-tier packages).

### Phase 2 — Buildout & Digital Foundation (Months 3–6)
5. **TI buildout:** 10–14 weeks; budget **$140–$210/sq ft**.
6. **Website launch:** HIPAA-aware, mobile-first, schema markup for `Dentist` and `LocalBusiness`.
7. **Google Business Profile:** Verify, complete all fields, upload 15+ photos, enable messaging.
8. **Citation buildout:** 40+ directories (Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Yelp, NPI registry).

### Phase 3 — Payer & Patient Pipeline (Months 4–7)
9. **Credentialing:** Submit to Delta Dental NE, Principal, BCBSNE, Medicaid — **start at month 4** (90–150 day cycle).
10. **PPC launch:** $2,500–$4,000/month Google Ads, geo-fenced to 8-mile radius.
11. **Review engine:** Automated post-visit SMS review requests; target **25+ Google reviews by month 9**.

### Phase 4 — Launch & Scale (Months 7–12)
12. **Soft opening:** 60–90 days of reduced hours to calibrate scheduling.
13. **Grand opening marketing:** Local mailers ($0.55–$0.75/piece), community sponsorships ($500–$2,500).
14. **Monthly KPI review:** New patients, CAC, chair utilization (target **65%+ by month 12**), recall rate (target **78%+**).
