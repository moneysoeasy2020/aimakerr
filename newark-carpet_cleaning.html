### Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts — Explicit Breakdown

**Local brick-and-mortar agencies (Newark/Ironbound/Jersey City corridor):**
- Retainers of **$1,200–$3,500/month** for local SEO
- 3–6 month minimum contracts common
- Advantages: in-person meetings, bundled reputation management, accountability via local presence
- Risks: overkill for a single-van startup; slow iteration; you pay for account management layers

**Verified freelance experts (Upwork, Contra, Clutch-verified, or local referrals):**
- Project rates **40–75% lower** than agencies for identical deliverables
- Advantages: pay-per-deliverable, faster turnaround, direct communication
- Risks: vet rigorously — demand 3 local-service case studies, verify Google Business Profile results, and use milestone-based payments

**Verdict for a 2026 Q4 Newark launch:** Use freelancers for website + GBP + citations (**$1,500–$3,400 total**), and reserve agency retainers until you exceed **$15,000/month revenue**. At that point, a **$1,200–$1,800/month** local agency retainer becomes cost-justified.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Equipment (Weeks 1–3, $4,500–$9,000)

1. **Register the LLC** with NJ Division of Revenue — **$125** filing fee; obtain EIN free.
2. **Secure NJ sales tax certificate** (carpet cleaning services are generally exempt, but equipment purchases are not — confirm with a CPA).
3. **Insurance:** general liability **$500K–$1M** coverage at **$600–$1,200/year**; add commercial auto for the van.
4. **Equipment:** hot-water extraction unit (**$2,800–$5,500** new; **$1,800–$3,200** refurbished), wand set, hoses, spotting kit, air movers. Budget **$3,500–$6,500** total.
5. **Van/vehicle:** used cargo van **$4,000–$9,000** (separate from startup capital if financed).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5, $1,500–$3,400 via freelancers)

1. **Google Business Profile** — category: "Carpet cleaning service"; service area: Newark + adjacent (Bloomfield, Belleville, East Orange, Harrison). Add service list with Newark-specific pricing ranges.
2. **Website** — 6 core pages: Home, Residential, Commercial, Move-Out Cleaning, Service Areas (Ironbound, North Ward, Downtown, West Ward, South Ward, East Ward), Contact/Booking.
3. **GEO/AI-search content** — write neighborhood-specific service pages answering: *"How much does carpet cleaning cost in the Ironbound?"* and *"Who does apartment move-out carpet cleaning near NJIT?"* AI engines cite pages with specific local numbers.
4. **Citations** — Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB, Bing Places (25 total).

### Phase 3 — B2B Channel Development (Weeks 4–10)

1. Build a list of **60–100 Newark property-management firms** and multifamily owners (5+ unit buildings).
2. Offer a **turnover package at $180–$220/unit** with 48-hour scheduling — the core B2B hook.
3. Target **8–12 accounts** in the first 90 days; each generates **$800–$2,500/month** recurring.
4. Attend Newark Regional Business Partnership events and Ironbound business association meetings.

### Phase 4 — Reviews & AI-Search Visibility (Ongoing)

1. Deploy SMS/QR review requests after every job — target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**.
2. Respond to every review within 24 hours (AI engines weight responsiveness).
3. Publish a quarterly "Newark Carpet Cleaning Price Guide" page — priced content gets cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.
