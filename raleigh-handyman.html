# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Raleigh, NC
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Raleigh's handyman and home-services market is projected to generate **$410M–$460M in annual contractor revenue in 2026**, fueled by a metro population exceeding **1.55 million** and roughly **38,000–42,000 new residential permits** issued across Wake County annually. The average Raleigh handyman charges **$65–$95/hour** (up from $55–$75 in 2024), with a minimum service call of **$125–$175**. A licensed general contractor in Wake County typically bills **$85–$135/hour**, while specialty trade work (electrical, plumbing) runs **$95–$150/hour**. Startup capital for a solo handyman operation ranges from **$8,500–$22,000**; a small crew-based contractor business requires **$35,000–$75,000**.

Demand is concentrated in ZIP codes **27601–27617** (Raleigh proper), plus high-growth suburbs **27502 (Apex), 27511 (Cary), 27587 (Wake Forest), and 27540 (Holly Springs)**. Homeowner median income in Wake County sits at **$92,000+**, and the median home age is **22–28 years** — a sweet spot for repair, refresh, and remodel work. Customer acquisition costs (CAC) in this market average **$85–$210 per booked job** through paid channels, making local SEO and GEO the highest-ROI investment for a new operator.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register the LLC** in NC ($125 filing fee) and obtain EIN.
2. **Secure licensing:** NCGC license if targeting $30K+ projects; otherwise register as a handyman entity.
3. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto — budget **$1,800–$3,200/year**.
4. **Open a business bank account** and set up QuickBooks or Wave.
5. **Domain + hosting:** Purchase a Raleigh-relevant domain (e.g., `raleighhandymanpro.com`) — **$15–$40/year** + hosting **$120–$360/year**.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
6. **Build a 6–8 page website** with service pages for top Raleigh demand: drywall repair, deck building, bathroom refresh, fence installation, TV mounting, furniture assembly.
7. **Create and verify Google Business Profile** — target the "handyman near me" and "contractor Raleigh NC" queries.
8. **Set up local citations** on Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB, Houzz (budget **$0–$300**).
9. **Install review capture** (SMS + email automation) — target **25 reviews in 90 days**.

### Phase 3 — GEO & Programmatic SEO (Weeks 6–10)
10. **Publish neighborhood landing pages** for Apex, Cary, Wake Forest, Holly Springs, Garner, Knightdale — 8–12 pages with local pricing, permit info, and project examples.
11. **Optimize for AI search engines** (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews): structured FAQs, schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage), and factual pricing tables.
12. **Create a "Raleigh Handyman Pricing Guide 2026"** — a high-citation asset for AI engines.

### Phase 4 — Paid + Local Outreach (Weeks 8–12)
13. **Launch Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,500/mo starting budget) — highest-intent channel in Raleigh.
14. **Add Nextdoor + Facebook local ads** ($300–$800/mo).
15. **Partner with 3–5 Raleigh realtors and property managers** — recurring job pipeline.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 4–12)
16. **Hire 1–2 crew members** once monthly revenue exceeds **$18,000–$25,000**.
17. **Expand service radius** to full Wake County + Johnston County.
18. **Reinvest 8–12% of revenue** into SEO/GEO and review generation.
