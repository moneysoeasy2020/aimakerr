## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2, $1,200–$2,400)
1. Register LLC with **Louisiana Secretary of State** ($100 online).
2. Obtain **East Baton Rouge Parish occupational license** (~$50–$150 based on revenue tier).
3. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** + **commercial auto** — expect **$1,400–$2,600/year** combined for a single van.
4. Apply for **Louisiana sales tax permit** (cleaning services generally exempt, but verify with CPA).
5. IICRC certification (CCT) — **$500–$800**, strongly recommended for commercial bids.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Van Setup (Weeks 2–4, $8,000–$14,000)
- Used truck-mount (HydraMaster, Prochem): **$6,000–$11,000**
- Portable backup (Mytee, Sapphire): **$1,800–$3,200**
- Chemicals, hoses, wands, air movers: **$900–$1,600**
- Branded van wrap (partial): **$1,200–$2,800**

### Phase 3 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6, $2,400–$5,800 via freelancers)
1. **Google Business Profile** — primary category "Carpet Cleaning Service"; service area: Baton Rouge, Central, Zachary, Baker, Denham Springs, Prairieville.
2. **Website** with programmatic pages for each service area + service type (pet stain, water damage, commercial).
3. **Citations**: Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Houzz.
4. **Review engine**: automated SMS post-job request (target 40+ reviews by month 6).

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–12, $1,500–$2,500/mo)
- **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$18–$35/lead in Baton Rouge).
- **Google Search Ads** on "carpet cleaning baton rouge," "pet stain removal near me" (~$3.50–$6.00 CPC).
- **Nextdoor + Facebook local groups** — organic posts 3x/week.
- **Partnerships**: property managers (LSU-area rentals), real estate agents, hotels on I-10 corridor.

### Phase 5 — Scale Triggers (Month 6+)
- Add second van at **$7,500/mo gross** sustained.
- Hire first technician at **$18–$22/hr** + commission.
- Layer commercial contracts (offices, clinics) for weekday utilization.
