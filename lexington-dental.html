## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months -6 to -3)
1. **Entity formation:** KY LLC or PLLC (dental PLLC required for practicing dentists); register with KY Secretary of State (~$40).
2. **Licensing:** KY Board of Dentistry license, DEA, CLIA (if in-office labs), radiation machine registration (Cabinet for Health).
3. **Site selection:** Target **1,800–2,600 sq ft** in Hamburg, Beaumont, or Brannon Crossing; negotiate **TI allowance of $30–$60/sq ft**.
4. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) via Live Oak, Huntington, or local KY banks; dental-specific lenders (Bank of America Practice Solutions, Provide) quote **7.5–9.5% APR** in 2026 Q4.
5. **Hire digital freelancers** (web, SEO, ads) — lock in before build-out completes.

### Phase 2 — Build-Out (Months -3 to 0)
6. **Construction:** 10–14 weeks typical; permit through LFUCG Division of Building Inspection.
7. **Equipment:** Order 4–5 ops (A-dec, Planmeca, or refurbished Pelton & Crane to control cost).
8. **Software stack:** Dentrix Ascend or Carestack (~$400–$700/mo); imaging (DEXIS, Carestream).
9. **Website launch** with **local schema, GBP verification, 40+ citations** — 6 weeks before opening.

### Phase 3 — Launch (Month 0 to 3)
10. **Google Business Profile** fully optimized; post weekly; collect **first 25 reviews in 60 days**.
11. **Paid ads:** $2,500–$5,000/mo Google Ads; $1,000–$2,000/mo Meta — target **$200 CPA** ceiling.
12. **Insurance credentialing:** Start 90 days pre-launch with Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, and KY Medicaid (DentaQuest/Passport).
13. **Referral network:** 15–20 primary care, pediatric, and specialist referral relationships within 5 miles.

### Phase 4 — Growth (Months 3 to 12)
14. **Programmatic SEO:** Launch 20–40 geo service pages (Hamburg dentist, Beaumont emergency dentist, etc.).
15. **GEO optimization:** Structured data, entity consistency, FAQ schema — target **AI Overview citation share of 15%+** for "Lexington dentist" queries by month 9.
16. **Review velocity:** **8–12 new Google reviews/month** minimum.
17. **KPI targets by month 12:** **450–650 active patients**, **$850K–$1.2M collections**, **$180–$250 blended CAC**, **4.7+ star rating**.
