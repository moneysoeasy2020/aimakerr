## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 0 — Pre-Launch (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity formation**: NM LLC ($50 online filing), EIN, business bank account.
2. **Licensing & insurance**: NM requires no state carpet-cleaning license, but Albuquerque business registration (CRS) + **$1M/$2M general liability** and commercial auto are non-negotiable for property-manager contracts.
3. **Equipment decision**: Truck-mount ($14k–$22k used) vs. high-end portable ($4k–$7k). For Q4 launch, **portable + van** preserves capital; upgrade in Q2 2027.
4. **IICRC certification** (Carpet Cleaning Technician) — **$500–$800**, signals legitimacy and unlocks commercial bids.

### Phase 1 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
5. **Website** with LocalBusiness + Service schema, service-area pages, and click-to-call above the fold.
6. **Google Business Profile**: verify, add 10+ photos, set service area to Albuquerque + Rio Rancho + Corrales, enable messaging.
7. **Citation build**: 30+ directories (Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, chamber of commerce).
8. **Review engine**: automated post-job SMS requests; target **25 reviews by day 60** (critical GEO threshold).

### Phase 2 — Programmatic SEO / GEO Assets (Weeks 4–8)
9. Build **30–60 location-intent pages** ("carpet cleaning in [NE Heights / Rio Rancho / Nob Hill / War Zone / Foothills]") with unique local proof (job photos, neighborhood references, pricing).
10. Publish **10–15 FAQ/answer-engine pages** targeting conversational queries ("how much does carpet cleaning cost in Albuquerque," "best pet odor removal Albuquerque") — these feed AI Overviews and ChatGPT/Perplexity citations.
11. Add **Q4 seasonal content** ("holiday carpet cleaning Albuquerque," "monsoon water damage carpet").

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–12)
12. **Property-manager outreach**: target 20–30 multifamily complexes in the UNM/KAFB corridor; offer turn-clean volume pricing ($175–$225/unit).
13. **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$18–$35/lead in ABQ) — faster than Ads for trust signals.
14. **Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook groups** — highest-ROI organic channel for local services in ABQ.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Defend (Month 4+)
15. Hire first technician at **$18–$22/hr** once monthly gross exceeds **$12k**.
16. Add **commercial contracts** (offices, churches, dental practices) for recurring revenue.
17. Refresh programmatic pages quarterly with new photos and pricing to maintain GEO freshness signals.
