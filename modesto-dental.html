## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)**
1. Secure financing (SBA 7(a) or equipment leasing; typical $750K package at 8–10% APR).
2. Lease negotiation in high-traffic corridors (McHenry Ave, Dale Rd, Pelandale Ave) — target **$28–$42/sq ft NNN**.
3. Entity formation (PC or Inc.), Denti-Cal/Medi-Cal credentialing (start immediately — takes 90–120 days), and commercial payer contracts.
4. Hire a **verified freelance web developer** ($5K–$9K) to build a HIPAA-compliant site with online booking, LocalBusiness/Dentist schema, and service-area pages for Modesto, Ceres, Riverbank, and Salida.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 3–5)**
5. Claim and optimize Google Business Profile; build **25–40 local citations** (Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, ADA Find-a-Dentist).
6. Launch geo-targeted Google Ads ($4K–$6K/mo) on high-intent terms: "emergency dentist Modesto," "dentist near me Modesto," "Denti-Cal dentist Modesto."
7. Implement GEO/AI-search optimization: structured FAQs, entity-consistent NAP, and authoritative local backlinks so ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity cite your clinic for "best dentist in Modesto" queries.

**Phase 3 — Launch & Scale (Months 5–12)**
8. Grand-opening campaign + review-generation workflow (target **40–60 Google reviews in 90 days**).
9. Track cost-per-new-patient weekly; reallocate budget from underperforming channels.
10. Month 9–12: evaluate a second hygienist, extended hours (evenings/Saturday — high demand in Modesto), and Spanish-language marketing (approx. **30% of Modesto households** speak Spanish at home).

**Key 2026 Q4 risks:** Denti-Cal reimbursement delays, rising Google Ads CPCs in healthcare, and AI-search volatility — mitigate with diversified channels and a strong organic/local-pack presence.
