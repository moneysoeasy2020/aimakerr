# Launching an HVAC Services Business in Las Vegas, NV
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

---

The Las Vegas–Henderson metro contains roughly **2.35 million residents** across **~900,000 occupied housing units**, and with summer highs routinely exceeding **110°F**, HVAC is a year-round necessity rather than a seasonal luxury. The metro's HVAC services market is estimated at **$480M–$620M in annual revenue**, supported by an installed base of approximately **750,000–820,000 residential HVAC systems** averaging **12–15 years old** — well into replacement territory. A new entrant should budget **$85,000–$180,000** in total startup capital, with **$18,000–$35,000** allocated to digital assets, licensing, and lead generation in the first 90 days. Realistic Year-1 revenue for a lean two-truck operation is **$420,000–$780,000**, with net margins of **8–14%** after technician payroll, fleet, and marketing.

Pricing benchmarks for 2026 Q4 Las Vegas: **diagnostic/service calls $89–$149**, **AC tune-ups $99–$179**, **refrigerant recharge (R-410A) $250–$550**, **capacitor/contactor repairs $180–$420**, **full system replacement $6,800–$14,500** (3-ton split system, installed), **duct cleaning $350–$700**, and **emergency after-hours premiums of 1.5x–2x standard rates**. Nevada requires a **State Contractor's License (C-21, Refrigeration & Air Conditioning)** through the Nevada State Contractors Board, a **$1,000–$2,500 bond**, and EPA **Section 608 certification** for all refrigerant-handling technicians. Clark County business licensing adds **$200–$450** annually.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Form LLC (Nevada, **$425** state fee + $150/yr list), obtain **Nevada C-21 license** (application + exam, **$600–$1,200** total), Clark County business license (**$200–$450**), EPA 608 certification for techs (**$150–$350** each).
2. **Insurance:** General liability (**$1,200–$2,800/yr**), commercial auto (**$3,500–$6,500/yr** for 2 trucks), workers' comp (**$2,000–$5,000/yr**).
3. **Banking & CRM:** Business account, ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro (**$300–$800/mo**).

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–6)
4. Hire **verified freelancers**: web developer ($1,800–$4,500), GEO content writer ($1,500–$3,500), GBP/citations specialist ($900–$2,200).
5. Build **service-area pages** for Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Spring Valley, Enterprise — each with local schema, pricing tables, and FAQ blocks optimized for AI answer engines.
6. Launch **Google Business Profile**, LSA, and review-generation SMS (**target: 50 reviews / 90 days**).

### Phase 3 — Fleet & Team (Weeks 4–8)
7. Acquire **2 used service vans** ($18,000–$32,000 each) + tools/inventory ($12,000–$20,000).
8. Hire **2 EPA-certified techs** (Las Vegas market wage: **$24–$38/hr** + benefits) and 1 dispatcher/CSR ($18–$24/hr).

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)
9. Activate **Google Ads** ($2,500–$6,000/mo) + LSA ($18–$45/lead) targeting "AC repair Las Vegas," "HVAC replacement Henderson," "emergency AC repair near me."
10. Publish **10–15 GEO-optimized pages** with exact pricing, local metrics, and structured data — the format AI engines cite most.
11. Run **Q4 pre-season tune-up promotions** ($99–$129) to build customer base before summer 2027 surge.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Month 4+)
12. Track **cost-per-acquired-customer (target < $180)** and **close rate (target 55–70% on LSA leads)**.
13. Add commercial contracts (property managers, HOAs) — **$8,000–$35,000** annual maintenance agreements per building.
14. Reinvest **12–18% of revenue** into GEO content and review velocity to defend Map Pack and AI-search rankings.
