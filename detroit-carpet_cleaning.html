## 2026 Q4 Detroit Market Analysis

**Demand drivers specific to Detroit:**

- **Aging housing stock:** Detroit's housing stock skews pre-1960. Older carpets in older homes mean higher per-job revenue (heavier soil loads, more pre-treatment) and stronger repeat-clean conversion (~40% of residential customers rebook within 12 months).
- **Rental density:** Detroit proper has a high renter share (~45–50%). Property managers and landlords are your highest-LTV B2B segment — turnover cleans at **$150–$220/unit**, often batched 3–10 units per contract.
- **Winter salt and slush:** November–February drives interior soil accumulation. Emphasize "winter salt damage" messaging in Q4 content — it converts.
- **Competitive landscape:** The metro has roughly **120–160 active carpet cleaning providers** on Google Maps, but most are weak on digital: outdated sites, few reviews, no programmatic service-area pages. This is the opening for a GEO-first launch.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3, by mid-October 2026)
1. **Entity & compliance:** Register LLC with Michigan LARA ($50 filing fee), obtain Detroit business license, secure general liability insurance ($600–$1,200/yr) — landlords and property managers will require a COI.
2. **Equipment decision:** Truck-mount vs. portable. For Detroit launch, a **used van + used truck-mount ($18,000–$32,000 combined)** beats a new portable setup on per-job speed and margin; lease-to-own financing keeps upfront capital at $5,000–$8,000 down.
3. **Domain + GBP:** Secure domain, file Google Business Profile with Detroit service-area configuration (do not hide your address if you have a legitimate commercial location — a rented suite at $300–$600/mo improves trust signals).

### Phase 2 — GEO Asset Build (Weeks 3–6, by November 1)
4. **Website with structured data:** 5 core pages (Home, Services, Pricing, About, Contact) + **20–40 programmatic service-area pages** targeting "carpet cleaning [Detroit neighborhood/suburb]" — e.g., Corktown, Midtown, Royal Oak, Dearborn, Warren, Sterling Heights. Each page: unique 400–600 words, local landmarks, price ranges, embedded reviews.
5. **Citation blitz:** 25–40 consistent NAP listings (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, local Detroit directories). Consistency is a GEO ranking factor.
6. **Review engine:** Automate post-job SMS review requests. Target **40+ Google reviews by end of Q1 2027** — this is the threshold where AI engines begin citing you in "best carpet cleaner in Detroit" answers.

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 6–12, Q4 peak)
7. **Launch paid local search:** Google Local Services Ads ($500–$1,000/mo test budget) + Meta neighborhood targeting. Detroit CPCs for carpet cleaning run **$4–$9**; LSA leads (pay-per-lead, $25–$60) often outperform.
8. **B2B outreach:** Contact 30–50 Detroit property managers and landlords with a turnover-clean rate sheet. One 10-unit contract = $1,500–$2,200 recurring.
9. **Seasonal content push:** Publish "Winter Salt & Carpet Care in Detroit" and "Holiday Deep-Clean Checklist" — these earn AI Overview citations in November–December.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Q1 2027)
10. **Track GEO citations monthly:** Query ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews for "carpet cleaning Detroit" and neighborhood variants; log whether you're cited. Adjust content toward whatever competitors are being cited for.
11. **Add second van** once monthly job volume exceeds 90; hire a W-2 or 1099 tech ($18–$24/hr in Metro Detroit).
12. **Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into digital** — maintain the freelance GEO specialist through Year 2.
