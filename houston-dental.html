## 2026 Q4 Houston Market Analysis

Houston's dental services market is projected at **$1.42 billion in annual consumer spending** for 2026, spread across roughly **1,850 active dental practices** in the Greater Houston MSA (Harris, Fort Bend, Montgomery, Brazoria counties). The market supports approximately **1 licensed dentist per 1,980 residents** — below the national ratio of 1:1,650 — meaning demand density in high-growth suburbs (Katy, Sugar Land, Cypress, Pearland, The Woodlands) still outpaces supply. A de novo general dentistry clinic in an average-traffic Houston location can realistically target **$85,000–$140,000/month in gross production** by month 18, with hygiene revenue comprising 22–28% of collections. Typical startup capital ranges from **$480,000 (leasehold + 4 chairs + used equipment)** to **$1.1M (build-out + 8 chairs + CBCT + full digital workflow)**; SBA 7(a) lending at 2026 Q4 rates (prime + 2.25–2.75%) is the dominant financing path.

Realistic fee benchmarks for Houston in 2026 Q4: new-patient exam + X-rays **$95–$185**, adult prophylaxis **$110–$165**, composite filling **$185–$340**, crown **$1,150–$1,750**, single implant (surgical + restoration) **$3,800–$5,600**, and clear aligner case **$3,200–$5,800**. PPO-adjusted collections typically run **62–78% of gross fees**. Commercial lease rates for dental-suitable retail (1,800–2,800 sq ft, medical-zoned) run **$28–$46/sq ft NNN** in inner-loop locations and **$22–$34/sq ft NNN** in Katy/Sugar Land/Pearland corridors. Build-out costs run **$140–$260/sq ft** depending on plumbing runs and imaging room shielding.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Months 0–18)

**Months 0–3: Foundation**
1. Entity + licensing: Texas DPS radiation registration, TSBDE facility permit, DEA (if prescribing), Harris County assumed-name filing.
2. Financing: SBA 7(a) application with a dental-specific lender; expect 60–90 day close.
3. Site selection: prioritize 3,000+ households within 3 miles, 25,000+ vehicles/day, and co-tenancy with grocery/pharmacy anchors.
4. Engage freelance web/SEO team **before** signing lease — domain, GBP, and schema should be live 60 days pre-opening.

**Months 3–9: Build & Pre-Launch**
5. Build-out (12–16 weeks typical in Houston; permit review 4–8 weeks via City of Houston HAS).
6. Equipment: new-patient chairs (A-dec/Pelton & Crane class) $8,500–$14,000 each; CBCT $65,000–$140,000; intraoral scanners $22,000–$45,000.
7. Hire: 1 associate or lead GP ($165,000–$240,000 base + production bonus), 2 hygienists ($42–$52/hr), 2–3 front office.
8. Pre-launch marketing: GBP live, 25–40 review seed strategy (compliant), soft-open event, insurance credentialing with top 6 PPOs (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, Guardian, United Concordia — 90–120 day cycle).

**Months 9–18: Growth**
9. Month 1–3 target: 45–70 new patients/month; Month 6: 90–120; Month 12: 130–180.
10. Reinvest 6–9% of collections into marketing; track cost-per-new-patient (Houston benchmark: $180–$420 blended).
11. Add specialty revenue (ortho consult days, implant placement) once GP schedule hits 78% utilization.
