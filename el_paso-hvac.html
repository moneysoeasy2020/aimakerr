## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Step 1: Licensing & Compliance (Weeks 1–4)
- **Texas HVAC License:** Obtain the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation (TDLR) A/C Contractor License. Requires 4 years of experience and passing the exam. **Cost: $350–$500.**
- **EPA Section 608 Certification:** Mandatory for refrigerant handling. Ensure your techs have the new A2L certification. **Cost: $150–$300 per tech.**
- **El Paso Business Permits:** Register with the Texas Secretary of State ($300) and obtain an El Paso Business License ($50–$150).
- **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) and commercial auto. **Cost: $3,500–$6,000/year.**

### Step 2: Fleet & Equipment (Weeks 3–6)
