Realistic startup capital for a solo-operator carpet cleaning business in Plano ranges from **$8,500 to $18,000** (van-mounted entry: $22,000–$35,000). Commercial jobs (Plano's Legacy West and Granite Park office corridors) typically price at **$0.18–$0.32 per sq ft**, with recurring contracts at 15–25% below one-off rates.

Labor is the largest variable: a certified technician in the Plano–Frisco–Allen corridor commands **$18–$26/hour**, and a two-person crew is standard for jobs above 1,200 sq ft. Customer acquisition cost (CAC) in this market benchmarks at **$65–$140 per booked residential job** through paid search, and **$25–$60** through organic/local SEO once rankings stabilize (typically month 4–7). Target gross margin: **55–65%** on residential, **40–50%** on commercial contracts. Break-even for a lean solo operation is typically **22–30 jobs/month** at a $240 average ticket.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register the business** — Texas SOS LLC filing (~$300), Plano assumed name certificate, EIN, and general liability insurance ($600–$1,200/yr).
2. **Secure equipment** — hot water extraction unit, hoses, wand, spotting kit, air movers. Budget $6,000–$12,000 for portable entry or $22,000+ for van-mounted.
3. **Set up payments and scheduling** — Square/Stripe + Jobber or Housecall Pro ($50–$150/mo).
4. **Build the core website** — 5–8 pages: Home, Services, Service Areas (Plano, Frisco, Allen, Richardson, McKinney), Pricing, About, Contact. Include schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ).

### Phase 2: Local Visibility (Weeks 2–6)
5. **Google Business Profile** — claim, verify, add 20+ photos, set service areas, enable messaging and booking.
6. **Citations** — Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB, Chamber of Commerce (Plano Chamber), and 20+ aggregators.
7. **Review engine** — automated post-job review requests; target **25+ Google reviews by day 90** (critical for Map Pack ranking in Plano).
8. **Launch LSA + Google Ads** — start at $1,500–$2,500/mo; geo-fence Plano + adjacent ZIPs (75023, 75024, 75025, 75074, 75075, 75093).

### Phase 3: GEO & Programmatic SEO (Weeks 4–10)
9. **Publish service-area pages** — one per neighborhood/ZIP (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in Willow Bend," "Carpet Cleaning near Legacy West") with unique local details (housing age, common carpet types, pricing).
10. **GEO-optimize content** — answer-first formatting, explicit pricing tables, local citations of Plano landmarks and ZIP codes, FAQ schema. Generative engines reward specificity.
11. **Publish 10 cornerstone articles** — "How much does carpet cleaning cost in Plano?", "Best carpet cleaning for Plano's older homes," "Pet stain removal in Collin County," etc.

### Phase 4: Commercial & Retention (Weeks 6–12)
12. **Pursue commercial contracts** — target Legacy West, Granite Park, and Shops at Legacy offices; pitch quarterly deep-clean contracts at $0.22–$0.28/sq ft.
13. **Build a referral program** — $25–$50 credit per referral; partner with Plano realtors and property managers (highest-ROI channel in this market).
14. **Track unit economics weekly** — CAC, average ticket, jobs/month, review velocity. Adjust ad spend to keep CAC below $120.
