## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Licensing & Legal (Weeks 1–6)
1. **Obtain CSLB C-20 license** — requires 4 years journeyman experience, $25,000 bond, and exam. Budget **$1,200–$2,500** (fees + exam prep).
2. Form LLC in California (**$70–$100** filing fee) and register with Riverside County.
3. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and **commercial auto** insurance — **$3,500–$8,000/yr**.
4. Register for CA sales tax and obtain EPA Section 608 certification for refrigerant handling.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 4–10)
5. Build a **GEO-optimized website** with LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema, service-area pages for Riverside, Corona, Moreno Valley, Eastvale, and Jurupa Valley.
6. Claim and optimize **Google Business Profile** — category "HVAC contractor," service areas, photos, Q&A seeding.
7. Deploy **review generation system** targeting 25+ reviews in first 90 days (critical for AI-search citation).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 8–16)
8. Launch **Google LSA + Local Services** campaigns — budget **$1,500–$3,000/mo** for first 90 days.
9. Set up **call tracking + CRM** (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber) — **$150–$500/mo**.
10. Publish **12 GEO-optimized FAQ/service pages** answering "how much does AC replacement cost in Riverside," "best heat pump installer Riverside," etc.

### Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Weeks 12–20)
11. Hire 1–2 EPA-certified technicians (**$28–$42/hr** in Riverside market).
12. Stock R-454B/R-32 compatible equipment; partner with distributors (Ferguson, Johnstone, Baker).
13. Launch **maintenance agreement program** ($180–$320/yr) for recurring revenue.
14. Pursue **TECH Clean California rebate** partnerships to capture heat pump install demand.
