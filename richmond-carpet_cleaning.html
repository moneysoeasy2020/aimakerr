4. Set service radius: prioritize **Henrico + Chesterfield** for route density; city core for margin.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)**
5. Launch GBP with 10+ geo-tagged photos, service categories, and booking link. Target "carpet cleaning Richmond VA," "pet stain removal Henrico," "commercial carpet cleaning Short Pump."

7. Claim and optimize **LSA** profile; set weekly budget **$400–$700** to start.
8. Seed **15–25 reviews** in first 60 days via post-job SMS automation.

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)**
9. Launch **Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook groups** (Fan, Museum District, Short Pump) with a Q4 holiday special.
10. Direct-mail / door-hanger campaign to **3–5 apartment complexes** near VCU (turnover season).
11. Pitch **5–10 property managers** in Innsbrook/West Creek for Q1 2027 contracts — sign by mid-December.
12. Publish **4 GEO-optimized service pages** (pet odor, wool rugs, commercial, move-out) with Richmond-specific pricing and neighborhoods.

**Phase 4 — Optimize & Scale (Weeks 10–16)**

15. Build a **referral program** ($25 credit) — referral is the #1 channel for Richmond owner-operators at scale.
