A realistic 2026 Q4 launch budget for a 4-operatory clinic — buildout, equipment, working capital, and 6-month marketing reserve — lands at **$850,000 median**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Form PLLC, obtain NYS dental license, DEA registration (if prescribing), malpractice insurance ($3,500–$9,000/yr), and confirm Article 28 applicability.
2. **Site selection:** Target 1,800–2,500 sq ft with existing dental infrastructure where possible (saves $60K–$120K). Verify zoning and DOB filing path.
3. **Buildout & equipment:** Engage dental-specific contractor; order chairs/CBCT 12–16 weeks ahead (lead times remain elevated in 2026).
4. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) up to $5M; dental-specific lenders (Bank of America Practice Solutions, Live Oak) at 7.5–9.5% APR.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 3–5, parallel)
5. **Domain, website, booking:** HIPAA-compliant booking (NexHealth, LocalMed). Target launch 6 weeks pre-opening.
6. **Google Business Profile + local citations:** 40+ directory submissions, NAP consistency, schema markup.
7. **GEO setup:** Structured data (Dentist/LocalBusiness schema), FAQ blocks, llms.txt, service-area content targeting ZIP-level queries.
8. **Insurance credentialing:** Begin 90–120 days pre-opening — PPO contracts take 60–90 days.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 5–9)
9. **Soft launch:** 4–6 weeks of reduced hours; capture reviews (target 25+ Google reviews by day 60).
10. **Paid acquisition:** Google Local Services Ads + Meta geo-targeted; cap at $3K/month Manhattan / $1,500 outer boroughs until CAC stabilizes.
11. **Recall system:** Automated hygiene recall (target 70%+ rebooking rate) — the single highest-ROI retention lever.
12. **GEO content cadence:** 4 service pages + 2 blog posts/month, optimized for AI answer engines.

### Phase 4 — Optimization (Months 9–12)
13. **Review CAC by channel** quarterly; cut underperforming spend.
14. **Add specialty services** (implants, Invisalign) once 150+ active patients.
15. **Evaluate second location** at 400+ active patients or 85%+ chair utilization.
