## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Financial Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with Virginia SCC (**$100**), obtain Chesapeake business license (**$50–$75**).
2. Secure general liability insurance (**$650–$1,100/yr**) — landlords and commercial clients require it.
3. Open business banking; set aside **18%** for quarterly estimated taxes.
4. Apply for EIN (free) and set up Stripe/Square for card-on-file deposits.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Vehicle (Weeks 2–3)
- **Truckmount**: used Prochem/HydraMaster (**$4,500–$7,500**) or portable extractor for condo work (**$1,800–$2,800**).
- **Van**: used cargo van, 2016–2020 (**$9,000–$15,000**).
- **Supplies**: hoses, wand, spotting kit, air movers (**$1,200–$1,800**).
- **Chemicals**: pre-spray, rinse, pet enzyme, protector (**$400–$600/mo**).

### Phase 3 — Digital Asset Launch (Weeks 3–5)
1. **Google Business Profile**: complete 100%, add 12+ photos, enable messaging, set service radius to Chesapeake + 15 mi.
2. **Website**: 7 pages minimum — Home, Services, Pricing, Service Areas (Great Bridge, Greenbrier, Western Branch, Deep Creek, Hickory), About, Contact. Embed **LocalBusiness + Service schema**.
3. **Citations**: Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Chamber of Commerce — NAP identical everywhere.
4. **Booking**: integrate Housecall Pro or Jobber (**$49–$129/mo**) for scheduling + invoicing.

### Phase 4 — GEO Content Engine (Weeks 5–8)
Publish 8–12 pages targeting generative-engine queries:
- "How much does carpet cleaning cost in Chesapeake, VA?"
- "Best pet stain removal in Great Bridge"
- "Same-day carpet cleaning near Greenbrier"
- "Landlord carpet cleaning Chesapeake — move-out specials"

Each page: **600–900 words**, local landmarks, price ranges, FAQ schema, and a clear CTA.

### Phase 5 — Review Velocity & Paid (Weeks 6–12)
- Automate SMS review requests post-job; target **25 reviews in 90 days** (top-quartile threshold).
- Launch Google Local Services Ads (**$600–$1,200/mo**) — pay-per-lead, high intent.
- Add Nextdoor neighborhood posts weekly; Chesapeake homeowners respond strongly to local recommendations.

### Phase 6 — Scale (Months 4–12)
- Add second technician at **$18–$22/hr** once booked >70%.
- Pursue commercial contracts (property managers along Battlefield Blvd).
- Reinvest 12–15% of revenue into content and review systems.
