## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing**: Form PLLC/PC, obtain CA dental license, DEA (if prescribing), and Fremont business license. Register NPI and enroll with Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, Guardian, MetLife.
2. **Site selection**: Target 94536/94555 ZIPs; 1,800–2,600 sq ft; negotiate TI allowance ($40–$90/sq ft typical in East Bay medical).
3. **Build-out & equipment**: Budget $280,000–$420,000 (3 chairs, CBCT, digital X-ray, sterilization, IT). Lead time on chairs/CBCT: 10–16 weeks.
4. **Digital foundation**: Domain, GBP verification, HIPAA-compliant hosting, NAP consistency across 40+ citations.

### Phase 2 — Launch (Months 4–6)
5. **Website + GEO layer**: Service pages (general, cosmetic, implants, Invisalign, emergency), FAQ schema, `Dentist` + `LocalBusiness` structured data, location pages for Fremont/Newark/Union City.
6. **Review engine**: Automate post-visit review requests; target **25–40 Google reviews in first 90 days** (critical GEO signal).
7. **Paid search**: Launch $4,000–$6,000/month Google Ads on high-intent terms ("dentist Fremont", "emergency dentist Fremont", "Invisalign Fremont").

### Phase 3 — Growth (Months 6–12)
8. **Programmatic SEO**: 15–25 service-area + procedure pages targeting Fremont neighborhoods and adjacent cities.
9. **Recall & reactivation**: 6-month recall automation; target **70%+ recall rate** by month 12.
10. **KPI review**: PAC <$320, production/chair >$28,000/month, review count >100, GBP calls >120/month.
