## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Obtain **TDLR HVAC Contractor License** (Class A or B based on scope) — application + exam + insurance proof.
2. Register with the **City of Plano** (contractor registration, $300K/$600K liability).
3. Secure **$1M general liability + commercial auto + workers' comp**.
4. Form LLC, obtain EIN, open business banking, set up **QuickBooks or Jobber** for invoicing.

**Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–8)**
5. Build a **5–8 page website** with service pages (AC repair, furnace, heat pump, maintenance plans) + online booking. Include **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**.
6. Claim and optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas: Plano, Frisco, Allen, Richardson).
7. Build **40–60 local citations** (Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, DFW directories).
8. Deploy **call tracking** (CallRail) and a **review request automation** (e.g., Podium or Birdeye).

**Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 6–12)**
9. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, $38–$85/lead) — highest-intent channel for HVAC.
10. Run **Google Search Ads** on 15–25 high-intent keywords (geo-targeted to Plano + 10-mile radius), budget **$1,500–$3,000/mo**.
11. Publish **10 GEO-optimized service/location pages** with FAQ schema and pricing transparency.
12. Activate **review velocity campaign** — target 40+ Google reviews in 90 days.

**Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Weeks 10–16)**
13. Implement **flat-rate pricing book** and same-day response guarantee.
14. Launch **maintenance plan** ($14–$22/mo recurring) for retention and predictable revenue.
15. Track **cost-per-booked-job** (target: <$120) and **close rate** (target: 55–70% on LSA leads).
16. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into marketing; expand service radius once Plano route density is profitable.

**Q4 2026 targets for a 2-tech launch:** 25–40 service calls/month by month 3, **$18,000–$32,000 monthly revenue**, and a **Google review count of 40+** to unlock generative-engine citation eligibility.
