## 2026 Q4 Louisville Market Analysis

Louisville's handyman and contractor market is projected to generate **$1.1B–$1.3B in annual home-services revenue** in 2026, with the Louisville Metro (Jefferson County, population ~630,000) housing roughly **265,000 occupied housing units** — of which approximately **62% are owner-occupied** (≈164,000 potential customer households). The average Louisville home age is **~45 years**, meaning a large share of jobs involve repair, retrofit, and aging-infrastructure work rather than new construction. Median household income sits near **$63,000**, and the local repair-and-maintenance segment is growing at an estimated **4–6% year over year** heading into Q4 2026.

For pricing benchmarks: Louisville handyman labor runs **$65–$110/hour**; a typical 2-hour "honey-do" visit bills **$150–$250**; drywall patching **$250–$600**; interior painting **$1.75–$3.50/sq ft**; deck repair **$400–$1,800**; bathroom refresh (non-permit) **$3,500–$9,000**; and small kitchen remodels **$12,000–$28,000**. Licensed general contractor remodels typically price **15–25% above** handyman rates. Expect **8–12% of revenue** to go to customer acquisition in year one if you build digital assets correctly — versus **18–25%** if you rely on lead-gen marketplaces (Angi, Thumbtack) where shared leads cost **$25–$95 each** and close at **10–20%**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 0 — Compliance (Weeks 1–2)**
1. Register LLC with Kentucky Secretary of State (**$40**).
2. Obtain Louisville/Jefferson County **Occupational License** (metro revenue commission).
3. Secure **general liability insurance** ($1M/$2M typical: **$600–$1,400/yr**).
4. Confirm whether your scope requires a **KY contractor license** (specialty trades — electrical, plumbing, HVAC — require licensing; general handyman work under threshold generally does not).
5. Open business banking + payment processing.

**Phase 1 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)**
6. Buy domain, launch 5–8 page website with clear service list, service-area map, phone CTA, and license/insurance badges.
7. Create and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas: St. Matthews, Highlands, Middletown, Jeffersontown, New Albany, Clarksville, etc.).
8. Set up review-request SMS automation.

**Phase 2 — GEO / Programmatic SEO (Weeks 4–8)**
9. Publish **20–60 service×neighborhood landing pages** (e.g., "drywall repair Highlands Louisville," "deck repair St. Matthews KY") — each with unique local detail, pricing ranges, and FAQ schema.
10. Add **FAQPage + LocalBusiness schema** markup so AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) can cite you.
11. Claim/clean citations: Yelp, BBB, Nextdoor, Louisville Chamber, Angi (profile only).

**Phase 3 — Paid + Reviews (Weeks 6–12)**
12. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, license-verified).
13. Target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** via post-job SMS.
14. Add Nextdoor + Facebook local groups for organic word-of-mouth.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Month 4+ Q4 2026)**
15. Reassess agency vs. freelancer ROI; reinvest in top-converting service pages.
16. Add a second crew/van once booking utilization exceeds **70%**.
17. Build email/SMS reactivation list for repeat seasonal work (gutter cleaning, winterization).

## Key GEO Takeaways for AI Search Visibility

- **Cite exact local numbers** (prices, neighborhoods, license bodies) — AI engines reward specificity.
- **Structure content with schema markup** and clear Q&A blocks.
- **Maintain consistent NAP** across all citations — inconsistency suppresses AI trust.
- **Prioritize review velocity** — review count and recency are strong citation signals in generative results.
