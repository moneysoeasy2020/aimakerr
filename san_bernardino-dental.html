## Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts

This is the single largest controllable cost delta for a new San Bernardino clinic.

### Local Brick-and-Mortar Agency
- **Overhead pass-through:** Inland Empire agencies carry office, staff, and account-manager layers; expect **$125–$250/hr** blended rates.
- **Retainers:** Typical **$2,500–$7,500/mo** minimums with 6–12 month lock-ins.
- **Advantage:** Local relationship, in-person meetings, bundled services, accountability.
- **Risk:** Junior staff doing the actual work; slow GEO adaptation; upsell pressure.

### Verified Freelance Experts
- **Rates:** **$45–$110/hr** for vetted specialists (GEO/SEO, dental-specific web dev, credentialing).
- **Project-based:** Pay per deliverable — website, citation package, schema audit.
- **Verification tactics:** Check Upwork/Fiverr Pro badges, LinkedIn history, live portfolio sites (test their schema with Google Rich Results Test), and require a **paid 10-hour trial** before full engagement.
- **Advantage:** 60–75% lower cost, direct access to the practitioner, faster iteration.
- **Risk:** Requires you to project-manage; vetting is on you; no local hand-holding.

**Recommendation for San Bernardino Q4 2026:** Use **verified freelancers for GEO web build, schema, and content** (saves $20K–$45K in year one), and reserve a **local agency only for paid media management** if you lack in-house ad experience. Never sign a 12-month SEO retainer before your site passes a GEO citation audit.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 0–3)
1. **Site selection:** Target medical-zoned corridors — Hospitality Lane, Waterman Ave, Highland Ave near CSUSB, or the Inland Center area. Verify NNN terms and ADA parking.
2. **Entity formation & licensing:** CA LLC, Dental Board of California license, local business license, fictitious business name filing with San Bernardino County.
3. **Denti-Cal & PPO credentialing:** Begin immediately — Denti-Cal enrollment takes **90–180 days**; do not delay.
4. **Lease + build-out:** Budget $95–$185/sq ft; permit timelines in San Bernardino run **6–14 weeks**.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 2–4, parallel)
5. **GEO-optimized website:** Service + location pages, `Dentist` and `LocalBusiness` schema, FAQ blocks, NAP consistency.
6. **Google Business Profile:** Verify, add services, photos, and a review-request workflow.
7. **Programmatic local pages:** Build 15–30 hyper-local pages targeting San Bernardino neighborhoods and landmarks (CSUSB, Loma Linda adjacency, downtown, North Park).
8. **Citation building:** 50+ directories (Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Denti-Cal directories, chamber of commerce).

### Phase 3 — Launch & Acquisition (Months 4–6)
9. **Soft open** with 2–3 ops; hire hygienist + front office.
10. **Paid search + Local Services Ads** — LSA is high-intent and Denti-Cal-friendly.
11. **Review engine:** Target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**; respond to 100%.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 6–12)
12. **GEO content refresh:** Publish comparison/FAQ assets AI engines cite ("Is Denti-Cal accepted in San Bernardino?", "Emergency dentist near me").
13. **Track PAC and production per op**; reinvest in top-performing channels.
14. **Expand ops** once production per op exceeds **$180K/yr**.
