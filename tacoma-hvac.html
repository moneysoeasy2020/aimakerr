### Demand Drivers
- **Housing stock age:** ~42-year median home age in Pierce County drives replacement demand; ~38% of Tacoma housing was built pre-1980.
- **Climate shift:** Increasing summer heat events (2021 heat dome reached 109°F in Tacoma) are accelerating heat pump adoption; Washington's **Climate Commitment Act** and utility incentives favor electrification.
- **Regulatory tailwind:** WA's **HEEHRA** (Home Electrification and Appliance Rebates) and PSE/Tacoma Power heat pump rebates create a **$1,000–$4,000** customer incentive stack — a strong sales lever.
- **Competition density:** ~180–220 licensed contractors; top 10 firms capture an estimated **55–65%** of residential install revenue. Local SEO and review velocity are the primary differentiators for new entrants.
