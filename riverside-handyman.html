## 2026 Q4 Riverside Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers
- **Housing stock age:** ~62% of Riverside single-family homes were built before 2000, producing recurring demand for drywall repair, plumbing fixture replacement, deck maintenance, and electrical outlet upgrades.
- **Renter-to-owner ratio:** Roughly 55% owner-occupied — a healthy mix of repair-ready homeowners and landlord/property-manager accounts (a high-value B2B channel in Riverside).
- **Seasonality:** Q4 (Oct–Dec) is peak "pre-holiday repair" season — gutter cleaning, weatherproofing, interior paint touch-ups, and heater servicing. Expect **15–25% higher lead volume** in October–November vs. summer.
- **Competition density:** Moderate. Riverside has fewer CSLB-licensed handyman specialists per capita than Orange County or LA, creating a GEO content gap for "handyman Riverside [ZIP]" queries.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with CA Secretary of State (**$70–$100**).
2. Obtain CSLB license — apply, fingerprint, bond (**$450–$600** total).
3. Secure general liability + commercial auto insurance (**$1,800–$3,200/yr**).
4. Get Riverside business license + seller's permit (if selling materials).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–6)
1. Build website with 5 core pages + 20–30 programmatic ZIP landing pages.
2. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (photos, services, Q&A, posts).
3. Set up review automation (SMS/email after each job).
4. Launch Google Local Services Ads (**$500–$1,000/mo starting budget**).

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 4–12)
1. Publish 8–12 GEO-optimized blog posts ("handyman cost Riverside 2026," "CSLB license check Riverside").
2. Build 30 local citations.
3. Partner with 3–5 Riverside property managers for recurring retainers.
4. Run Nextdoor + Facebook local ads (**$300–$600/mo**).

### Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Month 3+)
1. Hire first helper/apprentice at **$22–$28/hr**.
2. Add service-area expansion to Corona, Norco, Eastvale.
3. Track cost-per-booked-job weekly; cut channels above **$120/lead**.
