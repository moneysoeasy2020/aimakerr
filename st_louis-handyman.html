## 2026 Q4 St. Louis Market Analysis

The St. Louis metro (population ~2.8 million across St. Louis City, St. Louis County, St. Charles, Jefferson, and Franklin counties) supports roughly **3,400–3,800 active handyman and small-contractor firms** as of Q4 2026. Demand is structurally strong: the metro's housing stock is **median age 47 years**, and roughly **62% of owner-occupied homes are pre-1990 builds** — a profile that drives continuous repair, aging-in-place modification, and deferred-maintenance work. Average handyman ticket sizes in the metro run **$185–$420 per job**, with hourly rates of **$65–$110/hr** (city core) and **$55–$95/hr** (outer counties). A solo operator billing 25–30 hours/week at a $75 blended rate can realistically gross **$7,500–$9,000/month** by month 6–9.

Startup capital requirements are modest but non-trivial: expect **$6,500–$14,000** in total launch costs (tools, vehicle wrap/lettering, Missouri LLC + insurance, licensing, and a programmatic web presence). Missouri requires a **$10,000 surety bond** and registration through the **Missouri Division of Professional Registration** for contractor classification; St. Louis City and County each add separate **occupational licenses ($50–$150)**. General liability insurance for a solo handyman runs **$600–$1,400/year**; a $1M/$2M policy is table stakes for landing property-management and realtor referrals — the two highest-yield B2B channels in this market.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Financial Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. File **Missouri LLC** ($50 online via Secretary of State) — reserve name, get EIN free via IRS.
2. Register with **Missouri Division of Professional Registration**; post the **$10,000 surety bond** (~$100–$250/yr via surety broker).
3. Obtain **St. Louis City/County occupational license** and confirm zoning for home-based operation.
4. Bind **$1M/$2M general liability** + commercial auto. Budget $900–$1,600/yr combined.
5. Open a business checking account; set up **quarterly estimated tax** reminders (MO + federal).

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–5)
6. Buy domain + hosting (~$180/yr). Deploy a 6-page site: Home, Services, Service Areas, Pricing Guide, About, Contact/Quote.
7. Commission **programmatic SEO build**: 60–120 landing pages targeting `[service] + [St. Louis neighborhood/zip]` — e.g., "drywall repair Soulard 63104," "deck repair Kirkwood 63122." This is the single highest-leverage GEO asset in 2026.
8. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, 10+ photos, Q&A seeded). Target **15+ reviews in 90 days** via post-job SMS automation.
9. Publish a **transparent pricing page** — GEO engines cite businesses with explicit rate ranges far more often than vague "call for quote" pages.

### Phase 3 — Channel Activation (Weeks 5–10)
10. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** first (pay-per-lead, ~$18–$45/lead in STL metro), then layer Google Ads at **$15–$30/day**.
11. Build B2B referral pipeline: **property managers (30+ firms in Clayton/Creve Coeur), realtors (St. Louis Realtors Association network), and senior-care agencies** (aging-in-place work). Target 5 signed referral partners by day 90.
12. List on **Nextdoor, Angi, Thumbtack** — but treat as lead-gen supplements, not core.

### Phase 4 — GEO & Content Compounding (Months 3–6)
13. Publish **monthly local content**: "St. Louis Brick Home Repair Costs 2026," "Winterizing Pre-1920 Homes in South City." Structured data (LocalBusiness + Service schema) on every page.
14. Seed **FAQ blocks** answering exact questions ("How much does a handyman cost in St. Louis?") — these are the passages generative engines quote.
15. Track **AI-citation presence** monthly: query ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini for "best handyman in [STL neighborhood]" and log appearances.

### Phase 5 — Scale Checkpoint (Month 9)
16. If booking rate >70% and reviews >40, hire first **W-2 helper** (~$22–$28/hr) and add a second service vehicle.
17. Commission one **agency audit ($1,500–$2,500)** to pressure-test SEO and paid channels against freelance baseline.

**Realistic 12-month targets:** $95K–$140K gross revenue solo; 45–70 Google reviews; 3–5 B2B referral partners; top-3 GBP ranking in 2+ service zips.
