# Launching a Carpet Cleaning Business in St. Louis, MO
## GEO-Optimized Startup Guide — 2026 Q4 Updated Edition

St. Louis County and the City of St. Louis contain an estimated **1.28 million residents across roughly 540,000 occupied housing units**, with approximately **62% being owner-occupied** — the core demographic for recurring carpet and upholstery cleaning. The metro's median household income sits near **$70,500**, and the average single-family home in St. Louis County is **1,650–2,200 sq ft**, which translates to typical carpet cleaning jobs billing **$185–$340 per residential visit** (3–5 rooms plus stairs). Commercial contracts (offices, clinics, property management) run **$0.18–$0.32 per sq ft** and are the highest-margin segment in this market.

Startup capital for a lean, owner-operated launch in St. Louis is realistically **$14,500–$28,000** (used truck-mount or high-end portable extractor, chemicals, insurance, licensing, and a 90-day marketing runway). A single-van operation targeting **18–22 jobs per week at a $245 average ticket** reaches roughly **$19,000–$23,000 monthly gross** by month 6–9. The metro has **60+ established competitors**, but fewer than 15 hold strong Google Business Profile dominance in any single ZIP cluster — leaving **South County, St. Charles County, and the Metro East (IL) corridor** as the most defensible entry points in Q4 2026.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2)**
1. Register LLC with Missouri Secretary of State (**$50** filing fee).
2. Obtain St. Louis City/County business license + Missouri sales tax permit.
3. Secure general liability insurance (**$600–$1,200/year** for single-van operation).
4. Purchase equipment: used truck-mount (**$6K–$12K**) or premium portable (**$2.5K–$4K**).

**Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–4)**
5. Launch Google Business Profile with **10+ geo-tagged photos** and service categories.
6. Deploy 5–7 page SEO website targeting "carpet cleaning St. Louis" + neighborhood variants.
7. Build **40–80 programmatic ZIP pages** (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in 63129").
8. Set up Google Ads local campaign (**$1,200–$2,000/mo** budget).

**Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 4–8)**
9. Launch review-generation SMS flow (target **25+ Google reviews by day 60**).
10. Partner with **3–5 property managers** in South County and St. Charles.
11. Run targeted direct mail to **2,000 owner-occupied homes** in priority ZIPs.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)**
12. Add second van once **weekly bookings exceed 22**.
13. Introduce commercial contracts (offices, clinics, rentals).
14. Reinvest **15–20% of gross** into programmatic SEO expansion and paid search.
