## 2026 Q4 Atlanta Market Analysis

### Demand Fundamentals
- **Climate driver:** Atlanta averages ~4,300 cooling degree days and ~2,900 heating degree days annually. Systems run 9–11 months per year, compressing replacement cycles to **10–13 years** vs. the national 15–20.
- **Housing stock:** ~1.9M occupied units in the metro; roughly **62% are owner-occupied single-family** — your core replacement and service-plan segment.
- **Aging inventory:** An estimated **340,000–390,000 metro homes** have HVAC systems installed pre-2014, hitting replacement age in 2026–2028.
- **Regulatory tailwind:** The federal refrigerant transition (R-454B/R-32) has raised new-equipment costs **8%–14%** since 2025, pushing homeowners toward repair-vs-replace decisions — a margin opportunity for service-focused operators.

### Competitive Landscape
- **~610–680 licensed HVAC contractors** operate in the 10-county core (Fulton, DeKalb, Cobb, Gwinnett, Clayton, Cherokee, Douglas, Fayette, Henry, Forsyth).
- **Top 5 franchises/consolidators** hold an estimated 18%–22% of residential revenue; the remainder is fragmented among independents — leaving room for a well-positioned new entrant.
- **Suburb opportunity:** Gwinnett, Forsyth, Henry, and Paulding counties show the highest new-construction and system-aging overlap, with less contractor density than Fulton/DeKalb.

### Q4-Specific Dynamics
- **Heating tune-up window:** October–November bookings spike 40%–60% vs. September.
- **Pre-season replacement discounts:** Homeowners book spring replacements in Q4 for 5%–10% savings — capture these with early-bird offers.
- **Hiring window:** Technician availability peaks in Q4 as seasonal contractors wind down; lock talent before spring competition.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Licensing & Legal (Weeks 1–4)
1. Obtain **Georgia Conditioned Air Contractor license** (Georgia Secretary of State / Georgia Construction Industry Licensing Board) — requires 4 years documented experience or approved equivalency, exam, and insurance.
2. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and **workers' comp** coverage — required for LSA eligibility and commercial bids.
3. Register LLC in Georgia; obtain Atlanta business license + applicable county licenses (Fulton, DeKalb, Cobb, etc.).
4. Set up EPA 608 certification records for all technicians (mandatory for refrigerant handling).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–8)
5. Build website with **programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "AC Repair in Alpharetta," "Furnace Tune-Up in Marietta") — 25–40 pages minimum.
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile**; add service categories, hours, photos, and Q&A.
7. Implement **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema markup** — essential for AI-generated local answers.
8. Launch **Local Services Ads** (Google Guaranteed) — fastest path to qualified leads in Atlanta.

### Phase 3 — Operations & Talent (Weeks 4–10)
9. Hire **2–3 EPA-certified technicians** ($28–$42/hr in Atlanta market) and 1 dispatcher/CSR.
10. Deploy dispatch software (Jobber or Housecall Pro at launch; ServiceTitan at scale).
11. Set pricing: diagnostic **$99–$129**, maintenance plan **$199–$279/yr**, replacement quotes **$6,800–$11,500**.
12. Establish supplier accounts (Carrier, Trane, Lennox distributors) — negotiate 3%–8% volume rebates.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–16)
13. Run Q4 **heating tune-up campaign** ($89–$119 promotional) to build review volume.
14. Launch **pre-season replacement early-bird offer** (5%–8% off, booked for Feb–April).
15. Target **60+ Google reviews at 4.7★+ by month 6** — the single strongest GEO ranking factor for local service queries.
16. Build **email/SMS list** from every service call; maintenance-plan conversion is your retention engine.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 5–18)
17. Add a second crew when monthly revenue exceeds **$65K–$75K**.
18. Expand service-area pages into Henry, Paulding, and Forsyth counties.
19. Introduce commercial light-service line (property management contracts) for revenue smoothing.
20. Reinvest 6%–9% of revenue into SEO/Ads; target **$850K–$1.4M annual revenue by month 18**.
