## 2026 Q4 Baton Rouge Market Analysis

Baton Rouge's HVAC market is a high-demand, high-margin opportunity driven by a brutal Gulf Coast climate: summer heat indices routinely exceed 105°F, and the cooling season runs roughly April through October—about 210+ days of near-continuous AC demand. The metro area (East Baton Rouge, Ascension, Livingston, and West Baton Rouge parishes) holds an estimated **850,000+ residents across ~330,000 households**, with roughly **62–68% of housing stock built before 2000**, meaning a large installed base of aging systems due for replacement. A realistic startup can target **$1.2M–$2.4M in Year 1 gross revenue** with 2–4 trucks, based on an average residential service ticket of **$280–$450**, average replacement jobs of **$6,500–$11,500**, and a blended **$95–$145/hour labor rate**. Startup capital realistically falls between **$85,000 and $180,000** depending on whether you lease trucks and buy used equipment or finance new.

Pricing benchmarks for 2026 Q4 in Baton Rouge: diagnostic/service call **$89–$149**, refrigerant recharge (R-410A) **$350–$650**, full AC replacement (3-ton, standard efficiency) **$6,800–$9,500**, high-efficiency variable-speed systems **$11,000–$16,500**, and commercial RTU service contracts **$1,800–$6,500/year**. Louisiana's contractor licensing (State Licensing Board for Contractors, Mechanical Work classification) and EPA Section 608 certification are non-negotiable entry requirements—budget **$1,200–$3,500** for licensing, bonding, and insurance setup. The market is competitive (200+ licensed HVAC contractors in the metro), but demand outstrips supply during peak summer, and **response time is the #1 differentiator**—firms promising same-day service win disproportionate share.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–6)**
1. Register LLC with Louisiana Secretary of State ($100–$125).
2. Obtain State Licensing Board for Contractors mechanical license; secure EPA 608 certification.
3. Bind general liability ($1M/$2M) and commercial auto insurance ($2,400–$5,500/yr).
4. Open business banking; set up accounting (QuickBooks or similar).

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 4–10)**
5. Launch conversion-focused website with online booking and service-area pages.
6. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (categories, service areas, photos, Q&A).
7. Deploy review-generation automation; target 25+ reviews in first 90 days.
8. Build programmatic parish/neighborhood pages with LocalBusiness + FAQ schema for GEO/AI citation capture.

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 8–16)**
9. Launch Google Local Services Ads (highest-intent HVAC channel in Baton Rouge).
10. Run tightly geo-fenced Google Ads on emergency/repair keywords.
11. Publish 12–20 AI-answer-optimized content pieces (cost guides, "AC not cooling in Baton Rouge humidity" style queries).

**Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Weeks 12–24)**
12. Hire 1–2 EPA-certified techs; equip 2 trucks.
13. Implement dispatch software (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber).
14. Introduce maintenance agreements ($180–$320/yr) for recurring revenue.
15. Track cost-per-lead by channel monthly; cut underperformers, double down on winners.
