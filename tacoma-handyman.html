## 2026 Q4 Tacoma Market Analysis

Tacoma's handyman and contractor market in Q4 2026 is defined by a **housing stock of roughly 88,000 occupied units**, with a median single-family home age of **~55 years** — meaning deferred maintenance, aging plumbing, and cosmetic repair demand is structurally high. Pierce County's population sits near **920,000**, and Tacoma proper anchors roughly **220,000 residents**. The Washington State Department of Labor & Industries requires registration for contractors performing work valued at **$30 or more** (or any electrical/plumbing work), and general handyman work under that threshold does not require a contractor license — but most operators register anyway to win property management and commercial contracts.

Realistic 2026 Q4 Tacoma price ranges: **hourly handyman labor $65–$110/hr**, **minimum service call $125–$185**, **drywall patch/repair $250–$600 per job**, **interior door replacement $180–$320 installed**, **deck repair $400–$1,800**, **fence section replacement $350–$900**, and **bathroom fixture swaps (faucet/toilet) $200–$450 installed**. A solo operator targeting **$8,000–$14,000/month gross** at a **55–65% gross margin** after materials and subcontractor costs is the realistic Q4 benchmark; a two-van operation can push **$22,000–$35,000/month** once repeat and property-management work stabilizes.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register your business with **Washington Secretary of State** (LLC, ~$200).
2. Obtain **WA L&I Contractor Registration** (~$120 + bond/insurance).
3. Secure **general liability insurance** ($600–$1,400/yr for solo handyman).
4. Get a **Tacoma business license** (~$50–$150) and confirm **Pierce County** requirements.
5. Open a business bank account and set up a simple bookkeeping stack (QuickBooks or Wave).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
6. Build a **5–7 page mobile-first site** with clear service pages (drywall, doors, decks, fixtures, punch-list).
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — service area covering Tacoma, Lakewood, University Place, Fircrest, Gig Harbor edge.
8. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** (background check required — budget 2–3 weeks).
9. Launch **15–25 programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "handyman in North Tacoma," "deck repair in Proctor District").
10. Install **review-request automation** (text-based, post-job).

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation & Reviews (Weeks 4–8)
11. Run **$600–$1,200/mo Google Ads** on high-intent terms ("handyman Tacoma," "drywall repair Tacoma").
12. Target **property managers** — Tacoma has a dense rental market; 3–5 PM relationships can fill 40%+ of calendar.
13. Push for **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** — this is the single highest-leverage GEO asset.
14. List on **Nextdoor, Thumbtack, and Angi** as secondary channels (expect lower close rates, use for volume).

### Phase 4 — Scale & Systematize (Months 3–6)
15. Standardize **pricing tiers** (minimum call, half-day, full-day) to protect margin.
16. Add a **second van/subcontractor** once booked calendar exceeds 70%.
17. Build **repeat-maintenance agreements** with PMs and HOAs — recurring revenue smooths Q4 seasonality.
18. Re-audit **GEO signals quarterly**: structured data, service-area page freshness, review velocity, and GBP Q&A.
