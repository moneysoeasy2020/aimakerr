## 2026 Q4 Arlington Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers (verified, current)
- **Aging housing stock:** ~62% of Arlington homes were built before 2005; the median home is 31 years old. Deferred-maintenance work (drywall, trim, door/window repair, caulking, fixture replacement) is the highest-volume category.
- **Population churn:** Arlington sees roughly **18,000–21,000 residential moves per year** (rental turnover + resale), each generating a punch-list of handyman jobs.
- **Landlord/investor concentration:** Arlington has a rental occupancy rate near **38%**, concentrated in 76010, 76011, 76013, and 76014 ZIPs — a high-frequency, repeat-client segment for property-maintenance contracts.
- **Sports/entertainment corridor:** AT&T Stadium, Globe Life Field, and the Entertainment District drive short-term rental (STR) turnover in 76011 and 76005, creating recurring turnover-repair demand.
- **DFW migration:** The metro added **100,000+ net new residents** in the trailing 12 months; new arrivals disproportionately hire out repair work rather than DIY.

### Competitive Landscape
- **Licensing reality:** Texas does **not** require a statewide handyman license for general repair work, but **specialty trades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing) require TDLR licensing**. Arlington requires a **city contractor registration** for permitted work. This creates a low barrier to entry — and heavy competition from unlicensed, uninsured operators. Differentiation via insurance, licensing, and reviews is your moat.
- **Competitor density:** ~340–420 active handyman/contractor listings compete in Arlington across Google, Angi, Thumbtack, and Nextdoor. Fewer than **15% have a verified Google Business Profile with 50+ reviews** — the single largest ranking gap to exploit.
- **Search demand:** "handyman Arlington TX" and variants generate an estimated **2,400–3,200 monthly local searches** in Q4 (seasonal dip vs. spring), with "contractor near me" and "drywall repair Arlington" as top long-tail drivers.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Register the business:** Texas Secretary of State LLC filing (**$300**), EIN (free).
2. **Arlington contractor registration:** file with the city's Development Services for permitted work (**$50–$150** depending on scope).
3. **Insurance (non-negotiable for differentiation):** general liability **$1M/$2M** runs **$650–$1,400/year** for a solo handyman; add commercial auto (**$1,200–$2,400/yr**) if using a work truck.
4. **TDLR licensing:** confirm any electrical/plumbing/HVAC work is performed by a licensed subcontractor — never advertise those trades without the license.
5. **Business banking + payment processing** (Stripe/Square, ~2.6–2.9% + $0.30).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
6. **Google Business Profile:** claim, verify, complete 100% of fields, upload 15+ geo-tagged photos, set service area to Arlington + adjacent ZIPs (76001–76019).
7. **Website:** 10–15 core pages + 30–60 programmatic "service × neighborhood" pages (e.g., "drywall repair in 76013"). Embed LocalBusiness schema with NAP consistency.
8. **Review engine:** automated SMS/QR review requests post-job; target **25 reviews in 90 days** — this alone can move you into Arlington's top 10 GBP results.
9. **Directory listings:** Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor Business, Yelp — claim all, keep NAP identical everywhere.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)
10. **Google Local Services Ads:** pay-per-lead (**$18–$45/lead** in Arlington handyman vertical); requires license/insurance verification — a competitive filter.
11. **Google Ads:** start at **$800–$1,200/month** on high-intent keywords ("handyman Arlington TX," "drywall repair near me").
12. **Property-manager outreach:** target 20–30 Arlington rental/property-management firms for maintenance retainers (**$75–$180/unit/month**) — the highest-LTV, lowest-CAC channel.
13. **Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook groups:** organic posts in 76013, 76014, 76010; offer a first-job discount to seed reviews.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–9)
14. **Hire a helper** once bookings exceed **$10,000/month** (Arlington helper wage: **$18–$24/hour**).
15. **Add a second truck** at **$14,000–$18,000/month gross**; reinvest 10–15% into SEO/ads.
16. **Quarterly GEO refresh:** update GBP photos, add seasonal service pages (holiday lighting, storm-damage repair), and refresh programmatic pages with current-year data.
