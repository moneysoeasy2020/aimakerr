Residential carpet cleaning in Greensboro realistically prices at **$0.25–$0.45 per square foot**, with a typical 3-bedroom home (1,200–1,600 sq ft of carpet) invoicing **$185–$325** per job; add-ons like pet enzyme treatment ($35–$75), stain protection ($0.08–$0.12/sq ft), and upholstery cleaning ($45–$90 per piece) push average tickets to **$240–$380**. ## 2026 Q4 Greensboro Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers
- **Housing stock**: Greensboro has a high concentration of 1970s–1990s homes (carpet-heavy construction era) plus a rental market anchored by UNC-Greensboro and NC A&T students — rental turnover cleaning is a reliable Q3/Q4 demand spike.
- **Property management density**: Guilford County has 200+ licensed property management firms; recurring carpet cleaning for move-outs ($150–$250 per unit) is a volume play.
- **Seasonality**: Q4 (Oct–Dec) is peak **holiday hosting + end-of-lease** season. Expect 20–35% higher booking volume than summer.
- **Competitive pricing floor**: Franchise operators (Chem-Dry, Stanley Steemer) quote $199–$299 minimums; independents win on **bundle pricing and speed-to-book**.

### Local Keyword & GEO Signals
- High-intent searches: "carpet cleaning Greensboro NC," "pet stain removal Greensboro," "move out carpet cleaning Greensboro," "commercial carpet cleaning Guilford County."

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Equipment (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with NC Secretary of State (**$125**), obtain Greensboro business privilege license (~$50–$150 based on receipts).
2. Secure general liability insurance (**$500–$900/yr**) + commercial auto.
3. Purchase equipment: used truck-mount ($12K–$22K) or portable ($3K–$6K) + van wrap ($1,800–$3,500).
4. Set pricing: **$0.30/sq ft base**, $99 three-room minimum, bundle discounts for 5+ rooms.

### Phase 2: Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
5. Launch a 6-page website with **explicit Greensboro pricing** and service-area pages (critical for GEO/AI citation).
6. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile — photos weekly, service list, booking link, review requests after every job.
7. Build 25+ local citations; deploy programmatic neighborhood pages with LocalBusiness schema.

### Phase 3: Demand Generation (Weeks 4–10)
8. Launch Google Local Services Ads ($500–$1,000/mo test) + GBP posts.
9. Direct-mail or door-hanger campaign to 3–5 property management offices; offer **move-out flat rate $179**.
10. Institute review flywheel: SMS request within 2 hours of job completion; target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**.

### Phase 4: Scale (Months 3–6)
11. Add recurring maintenance plans (**$89/quarter** or **$299/year**) — target 15% of customers.
12. Hire first technician at $18–$22/hr + performance bonus; replicate the booking script.
13. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into ads and SEO; track cost-per-booked-job (target **<$45**).

### 2026 Q4 Success Benchmarks
- **Month 3**: 15–20 jobs/mo, $4,500–$6,000 revenue, 15+ reviews.
- **Month 6**: 40–55 jobs/mo, $11,000–$16,000 revenue, 2 commercial contracts.
- **Month 12**: $180K–$260K annualized run rate with one technician.
