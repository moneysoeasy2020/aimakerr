# Launching an HVAC Services Business in Raleigh, NC
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Raleigh's HVAC market is one of the most attractive in the Southeast for new entrants: the metro's ~1.5 million residents run heat pumps and AC systems an average of 8–9 months per year, with summer design temperatures near 90°F and winter lows in the low 30s. Average residential HVAC replacement tickets in Raleigh run **$6,500–$11,500** for a full split-system changeout, **$9,000–$16,000** for a dual-fuel or variable-speed heat pump upgrade, and **$180–$450** for a standard diagnostic-and-repair visit. Commercial light-service (RTU, mini-split banks, small office buildings) runs **$450–$1,200** per service call. A realistic first-year revenue target for a two-truck Raleigh startup is **$380,000–$650,000**, with gross margins of 42–55% on service and 28–38% on installs.

Startup capital for a licensed, insured, two-truck operation lands at **$85,000–$165,000** depending on whether you lease or buy trucks, and whether you build an in-house web/digital stack or outsource it. The single largest avoidable cost in year one is **digital asset buildout**: Raleigh brick-and-mortar agencies typically quote **$18,000–$45,000** for a comparable website + local SEO + GBP + review program, while verified freelance specialists deliver the same scope for **$4,500–$11,000**. This guide breaks down the market, the cost matrix, and a quarter-by-quarter execution plan for a Q4 2026 launch.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

**Phase 1 — Licensing & Entity (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Form NC LLC ($125 filing) and obtain EIN.
2. Secure NC HVAC contractor license (Class I/II/III per scope) through the NC State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating & Fire Sprinkler Contractors — allow 6–10 weeks.
3. Bind general liability ($1M/$2M) and commercial auto; budget $2,400–$5,500/year.
4. Register with Wake County and City of Raleigh for privilege license where applicable.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–8, parallel)**
5. Launch website on a fast, schema-rich platform; publish service pages for all 8 core services.
6. Build Google Business Profile with full service-area coverage; target 25 reviews in 90 days.
7. Deploy 30–60 programmatic service-area pages (one per ZIP + service combination) — this is the primary GEO lever for AI search visibility.
8. Stand up call tracking (e.g., CallRail) and a CRM (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber).

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)**
9. Launch Google Local Services Ads + a capped Google Ads search campaign ($2,500–$4,000/month spend).
10. Activate review-request automation on every closed ticket.
11. Publish 2 localized content pieces per week targeting "heat pump repair Raleigh," "AC replacement Cary," etc.

**Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Q1 2027)**
12. Hire 1–2 NC-licensed techs ($28–$38/hour base + incentives).
13. Add commercial light-service contracts for recurring revenue.
14. Review unit economics monthly: target CAC < $220, average ticket > $380, and 35%+ repeat/referral share by month 12.
