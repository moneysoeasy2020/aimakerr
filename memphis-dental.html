## Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts

**Local Memphis brick-and-mortar agencies** (full-service, 10–40 staff):
- **Cost premium:** 2.5–4x freelance rates; retainers typically **$2,500–$6,000/month** with 6–12 month minimums.
- **Advantages:** single point of accountability, in-person meetings, bundled reporting, established local media relationships (e.g., Memphis Business Journal, Commercial Appeal PR).
- **Risks:** junior staff doing the work, slow turnaround, lock-in contracts, and **generic national playbooks** that ignore Memphis ZIP-level demand.

**Verified freelance experts** (vetted via portfolio, case studies, references):
- **Cost:** **$900–$2,200/month** SEO, **$2,500–$6,000** one-time website — **40–65% cheaper** than agencies.
- **Advantages:** senior-level execution, flexible scope, direct communication, faster iteration on GEO/AI-search optimization.
- **Risks:** vetting burden on you, single-point-of-failure risk, less PR/media muscle.

**Recommendation for a 2026 Q4 Memphis launch:** Hybrid model — hire a **verified freelance web + local SEO specialist** for the core digital asset build ($4K–$8K one-time), a **freelance PPC manager** for the first 90 days of paid acquisition ($800–$1,500/mo), and reserve a **local agency only for PR/community launch** if budget allows. This typically saves **$20K–$40K in year one** versus a full agency retainer while preserving senior execution.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 0 — Pre-Launch (Months -4 to 0)
1. **Entity + licensing:** Register PLLC in Tennessee, obtain TN dental license, DEA (if prescribing), and Shelby County health permits.
2. **Location selection:** Target ZIPs 38120, 38125, 38017 (Collierville), or 38018 — verify **dentist density <1:1,800** and **household income >$75K**.
3. **Lease negotiation:** Aim for **$24–$32/sq ft NNN**, 7–10 year term with tenant improvement allowance ($30–$60/sq ft).
4. **Equipment financing:** Compare **equipment leases (5–7 yr, 6–9% APR)** vs. cash purchase; refurbished packages save 40–50%.

### Phase 1 — Digital Asset Build (Months -2 to 0)
5. **Website:** 10–12 pages, mobile-first, online booking, schema markup (Dentist, LocalBusiness), **sub-2.5s LCP**.
6. **Google Business Profile:** Verify, complete all fields, add 20+ photos, enable messaging, set service areas.
7. **Citation build:** NAP consistency across 40+ directories (Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Yelp, Bing Places).
8. **Review engine:** Automate post-visit review requests; target **25+ Google reviews in first 90 days**.

### Phase 2 — Soft Launch (Months 0–3)
9. **Paid search:** $2,500–$5,000/mo on "emergency dentist Memphis," "dentist near me [ZIP]," and procedure-specific keywords.
10. **Local SEO content:** 8–12 service pages + 4 neighborhood pages (Germantown, Collierville, Bartlett, Cordova).
11. **Community:** Partner with Memphis Chamber, sponsor 2–3 local events; target **15–25 new patients/month** by month 3.

### Phase 3 — Scale (Months 3–12)
12. **GEO/AI-search optimization:** Structured FAQs, entity-rich content, and citations that surface in ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews.
13. **Recall + reactivation:** Automated recall campaigns; reactivation targets lapsed patients at **$18–$35 per recovered patient**.
14. **Referral program:** $50–$100 patient referral credits; specialist referral network (ortho, oral surgery, endo).
15. **KPI review quarterly:** CPA, chair utilization (target 70%+), collections/visit, and **Google review velocity**.

**Year-1 targets (realistic):** 180–320 active new patients, **$850K–$1.3M collections**, **14–22 month breakeven**, and **top-3 Google Map Pack** for 3+ high-intent keywords in your primary ZIP by month 9.
