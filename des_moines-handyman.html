## 2026 Q4 Des Moines Market Analysis

Des Moines metro (Polk, Dallas, Warren counties) entered Q4 2026 with a population of roughly **735,000** and a housing stock of approximately **310,000 units**, of which about **62% were built before 1990** — a repair-heavy, remodel-ready base. Median household income sits near **$78,500**, and the metro's homeownership rate of **~67%** supports steady demand for punch-list, drywall, deck, and small-remodel work. Realistic 2026 Q4 pricing for a solo handyman in Des Moines runs **$65–$95/hour** (average $80), with minimum service calls at **$125–$175**; contractors doing kitchen/bath remodels typically quote **$150–$350/sq ft** for mid-grade finishes. Expect first-year gross revenue of **$95,000–$140,000** for a solo operator working 40–45 billable hours/week at 70% utilization.

Startup capitalization for a licensed, insured Des Moines handyman operation is **$12,000–$28,000** all-in: Iowa contractor licensing and registration, general liability ($1M/$2M, roughly **$1,200–$2,400/year**), tools and van outfitting ($6,000–$15,000), and a lean digital presence ($1,500–$4,500). The metro's contractor market is fragmented — roughly **1,400–1,800** active handyman/remodeling firms — but demand outpaces supply in Ankeny, Waukee, and West Des Moines suburbs, where new-home warranty and aging-in-place work is growing fastest.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with **Iowa Secretary of State** ($50 online filing).
2. Obtain **Des Moines contractor registration** and Polk County permits as required; Iowa requires **$25,000 surety bond** for residential contractors.
3. Secure **GL insurance** ($1M/$2M) and commercial auto; budget $1,800–$3,000/year combined.
4. Open business banking; set up QuickBooks or Wave.

### Phase 2 — Digital GEO Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
5. Build a **mobile-first website** with service pages (drywall, deck repair, tile, punch-list, small remodel) and **suburb-specific landing pages** (Ankeny, Waukee, Urbandale, West Des Moines, Johnston).
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas, 10+ photos, Q&A seeded with real customer questions.
7. Deploy **programmatic service-area pages** (service × suburb matrix = 25–40 pages) with unique local proof (completed jobs, neighborhoods, permit notes).
8. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** ($300–$600/mo test budget) and request 15+ reviews in first 60 days.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–10)
9. Join **Des Moines Home Builders Association** and **Iowa Remodelers Council** for referral flow.
10. Partner with 3–5 local realtors and property managers for punch-list and pre-sale repair work.
11. Run **Nextdoor + Facebook local** ads ($400–$800/mo) targeting 5-mile radius.
12. Post weekly project photos to GBP; respond to every review within 24 hours.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 10–16)
13. Track cost-per-booked-job by channel; kill channels above **$85/lead**.
14. Add a second crew or 1099 helper once utilization exceeds **80%**.
15. Publish quarterly "Des Moines Home Repair Cost" content to capture GEO/AI-search citations.
