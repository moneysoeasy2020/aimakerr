# Launching an HVAC Services Business in Spokane, WA
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Spokane's HVAC market is entering Q4 2026 with roughly **1,400–1,600 licensed HVAC contractors** serving a metro population of approximately **600,000**, of which an estimated **62% of homes were built before 1990** — meaning aging furnaces, heat pumps, and ductwork are the dominant service demand. A realistic startup budget for a licensed, insured Spokane HVAC business is **$85,000–$180,000** in year one (licensing, tools, vehicle, insurance, marketing), with residential service call pricing averaging **$95–$150/hour** and typical furnace replacement jobs running **$4,500–$9,000** installed.

Spokane's climate — cold winters averaging **20–32°F** and hot summers reaching **85–95°F** — drives a genuine two-season demand curve, with heating emergencies peaking November–February and AC installs peaking June–August. A well-positioned new entrant can realistically target **$350,000–$600,000** in first-year revenue with 2–3 technicians, provided licensing, insurance, and digital lead generation are executed correctly before the heating season rush.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. Obtain **WA L&I HVAC specialty license** (required for all HVAC work in Washington).
2. Register LLC with WA Secretary of State (~$200).
3. Secure general liability + commercial auto insurance.
4. Open business banking and set up merchant processing.
5. Purchase core tools and a wrapped service van.

### Phase 2: Digital Asset Build (Weeks 3–6)
6. Launch a conversion-focused website with **service-area pages for Spokane, Spokane Valley, Cheney, and Coeur d'Alene**.
7. Create and verify **Google Business Profile** with service categories, hours, and photos.
8. Implement **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema markup** for GEO/AI search visibility.
9. Build 30–50 local citations (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, chamber of commerce).
10. Set up field service CRM (Jobber or ServiceTitan) with online booking.

### Phase 3: Lead Generation (Weeks 5–8, before Nov 1)
11. Activate **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, high intent).
12. Launch targeted PPC for "furnace repair Spokane" and "heat pump installation Spokane."
13. Publish 4–6 service pages optimized for AI search (clear Q&A format, pricing ranges, local references).
14. Set up review-generation workflow (target 25+ Google reviews by end of Q4).

### Phase 4: Operations & Scale (Weeks 8–12)
15. Hire 1–2 licensed technicians (Spokane market rate: $28–$42/hr).
16. Implement dispatch and emergency call protocols for heating season.
17. Track cost-per-lead and close rate weekly; cut underperforming channels.
18. Plan Q1 2027 cooling-season prep (AC tune-up campaigns, heat pump rebate marketing).
