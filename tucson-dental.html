## 2026 Q4 Tucson Market Analysis

Tucson's dental market is **underserved relative to population growth**. As of Q4 2026, the Tucson metro (Pima County) supports roughly **1.06 million residents** with approximately **620 active dental practices** — a ratio of about **1 dentist per 1,700 residents**, versus the national average of 1 per 1,600. The gap is widest on the **southeast side (Vail, Rita Ranch) and northwest (Marana, Oro Valley)**, where new housing tracts have outpaced provider supply. Median household income sits near **$62,400**, and **18.2% of adults report no dental insurance** — meaning **fee-for-service and membership plans** are a major revenue lever here.

Realistic startup economics for a **3-chair general practice, 1,800 sq ft**: **$480,000–$720,000** total build-out (tenant improvements $95–$140/sq ft, equipment $180K–$260K, working capital reserve $80K–$120K). Expect **$14,000–$22,000/month** in rent for a Class B medical-retail location, **5.5–7.5%** commercial loan rates, and **12–18 months** to break-even at 65% chair utilization. Average new-patient acquisition cost in Tucson runs **$180–$320**, and a single restorative case lifetime value averages **$3,400–$5,800**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Arizona Corporation Commission LLC filing ($50–$85), AZDHS dental facility license, DEA registration if prescribing, Pima County zoning clearance.
2. **Location lock:** Target **Marana/Oro Valley or Vail** — highest patient-per-dentist gaps. Negotiate **6–12 months free rent** on a 7-year term.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) at **6.5–8.0%**; bring 15–20% equity. Equipment leasing preserves cash.
4. **Build-out:** Budget **$95–$140/sq ft**; permit timelines in Pima County run **6–10 weeks**.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 3–5)
5. **Website + booking** (freelance, $3,200–$6,500) with schema markup, service-area pages for **Tucson, Marana, Oro Valley, Vail, Sahuarita**.
6. **Google Business Profile** optimized with 10+ photos, Q&A seeded, and **weekly posts**.
7. **Citations:** Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Arizona Dental Association directory.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Months 4–8)
8. **Google Ads:** start **$2,500–$4,000/mo** on high-intent terms ("dentist near me Tucson," "emergency dentist Tucson"). Target **$180–$320 CPA**.
9. **Review engine:** automated post-visit requests; target **40+ reviews in 90 days**.
10. **Membership plan** ($29–$49/mo) to convert the **18.2% uninsured** segment.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 8–18)
11. Add **hygiene capacity** first — it drives recall revenue and referral loops.
12. Layer **programmatic SEO** service-area pages (one per ZIP in Pima County).
13. Reinvest **8–12% of revenue** into marketing until 65% chair utilization, then optimize to **6–8%**.
