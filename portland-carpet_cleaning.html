## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register LLC in Oregon** ($100 filing) and obtain Portland business license.
2. **Secure equipment** — used truck-mount ($6,500–$11,000) or portable system ($2,800–$4,500).
3. **Insurance** — general liability + commercial auto: $1,800–$3,200/year.
4. **IICRC certification** ($500–$900) — required trust signal for property managers.

### Phase 2 — Digital Launch (Weeks 3–6)
5. **Build website** with programmatic service-area pages for 25–40 Portland neighborhoods (Pearl District, Sellwood, Alberta Arts, Beaverton, Lake Oswego).
6. **Optimize Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas, Q&A, weekly posts.
7. **Deploy review engine** — automated post-job SMS requests targeting 40+ reviews in 90 days.

### Phase 3 — Revenue Engine (Weeks 6–12)

9. **Google Local Services Ads** — $800–$1,500/month budget; Portland LSA cost-per-lead runs $18–$42.
10. **Restoration partnerships** — 3–5 referral agreements for water damage overflow.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–12)
11. **Route density optimization** — cluster jobs by ZIP to cut drive time 20–30%.
12. **Add commercial lane** — office parks in Tualatin/Tigard at $0.22–$0.30/sq ft.
13. **Reinvest 15% of revenue** into programmatic SEO expansion (Vancouver WA, Gresham, Hillsboro).
