## Executive Snapshot: The Numbers First

Launching a **de novo dental clinic in Phoenix, AZ** in Q4 2026 requires a realistic all-in capital range of **$480,000–$1,150,000** for a 1,800–2,500 sq ft, 4-operatory build-out — with leasehold improvements alone running **$180–$420 per sq ft** depending on whether you target a Class A medical retail strip in Scottsdale/Arcadia versus a value-oriented corridor in west Phoenix (Maryvale, Laveen) or the East Valley (Mesa, Gilbert). Phoenix's **median household income sits near $78,000** (Maricopa County), but dental spend is highly zip-code dependent: Arcadia/Paradise Valley patients frequently support **$1,400–$2,200 restorative case averages**, while west-side corridors run **$650–$1,100** — this delta drives your entire fee schedule and marketing budget.

The Phoenix metro dental market is **saturated but fragmented**: roughly **2,900+ active dental practices** across Maricopa County serving ~5.1 million residents (≈1 dentist per 1,750 people), yet **Medicaid-accepting capacity is critically undersupplied** — AHCCCS (Arizona Medicaid) covers ~2.1 million Arizonans, and fewer than **18% of Phoenix private practices** accept adult AHCCCS. A new clinic can hit **$55,000–$95,000 monthly gross** by month 12 if it commits to a **$9,000–$18,000/month** blended patient acquisition budget (Google Ads + Local Services Ads + programmatic location pages + recall automation). Break-even on a $650K build-out typically lands at **month 14–20**.

## 2026 Q4 Phoenix Market Analysis

### Demographic & Demand Signals
- **Population:** Phoenix metro ~5.1M (Maricopa County), growing ~1.4%/yr — among the fastest-growing large metros in the U.S.
- **Dental insurance penetration:** ~68% commercial/employer, ~19% AHCCCS, ~13% uninsured/cash.
- **High-growth submarkets (2026 Q4 priority):** Buckeye, Goodyear, Queen Creek, San Tan Valley, north Phoenix (Anthem/Norterra), downtown Phoenix (Roosevelt Row) — all showing 3–6% annual household growth with under-supplied general dentistry.
- **Competitive density:** Highest in Scottsdale, Tempe, and central Phoenix; **lowest** in Laveen, Buckeye, and Apache Junction.
- **Seasonality:** Q4 (Oct–Dec) is **peak PPO benefit-utilization season** — patients rush to use remaining annual maximums. Launch timing matters: opening **October–November 2026** captures this wave; opening in January forfeits it.

### Regulatory & Licensing (Arizona-Specific)
- **Arizona State Board of Dental Examiners (AzSBoDE)** — licensure, anesthesia permits, and facility inspection.
- **Dental corporation registration** with the **Arizona Corporation Commission** (DDS must hold majority ownership in an AZ dental PC).
- **DEA registration** (Schedule II–V) + **ADHS** controlled-substance compliance.
- **X-ray equipment registration** with **Arizona Department of Health Services (ADHS)** Bureau of Radiation Control.
- **OSHA + HIPAA** compliance program required before first patient; **Arizona is a one-party consent state** for recording (relevant to call-tracking compliance).

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months -6 to -3: Apr–Jul 2026)
1. **Site selection:** Target 1,800–2,500 sq ft, 4 ops, with plumbing rough-in; negotiate **TI allowance of $40–$90/sq ft** and 7–10 year lease with 2–3% annual escalators. Phoenix medical retail NNN runs **$28–$48/sq ft** depending on corridor.
2. **Entity & licensure:** AZ dental PC formation, AzSBoDE facility application, DEA, ADHS X-ray registration.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) up to $5M (10-yr term, ~prime + 2.75%); equipment leasing for chairs/imaging (CBCT $85K–$180K; intraoral scanners $25K–$45K).
4. **PMS selection:** Open Dental (cost-efficient) vs. Dentrix (enterprise) — decide before build-out so IT conduit planning matches.

### Phase 2 — Build & Digital Foundation (Months -3 to 0: Jul–Sep 2026)
5. **Build-out:** GC contract with dental-specific contractor; budget **$180–$420/sq ft**; permit timeline in City of Phoenix runs **6–12 weeks** (factor into Q4 opening).
6. **Website + GBP:** Launch site 8 weeks pre-opening; create and verify Google Business Profile; publish 20–40 programmatic service/location pages targeting "dentist in [Laveen/Buckeye/Arcadia]" queries.
7. **Hire team:** 1 associate DDS (optional), 2 hygienists, 2–3 front office/RDA; Phoenix RDA wages **$22–$30/hr**, hygienists **$42–$52/hr**.
8. **Insurance credentialing:** Begin PPO contracting (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna) **90–120 days pre-opening** — credentialing is the #1 launch delay.

### Phase 3 — Soft Launch & Ramp (Month 0–3: Oct–Dec 2026)
9. **Soft open** with 20–30 pre-booked patients from a "founding patient" offer (exam + X-rays + cleaning at $79–$129).
10. **Activate paid media:** Google Ads + LSA + Meta geo-targeted; target **$55–$95 cost-per-new-patient** blended in Phoenix.
11. **Review engine:** Automated post-visit SMS/email requests; goal **40+ Google reviews by day 90** (Phoenix competitive benchmark).
12. **Capture Q4 PPO wave:** Run "use it or lose it" insurance-maximization campaign Oct–Dec.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–12: Jan–Sep 2027)
13. **Recall automation:** 6-month hygiene recall + reactivation sequences; target **65–75% recall compliance**.
14. **Expand service mix:** Add ortho aligners, implants, or sedation once general dentistry hits **$70K+/mo**.
15. **Programmatic SEO expansion:** Add neighborhood + procedure pages monthly; target **top-3 local pack** for 15+ "dentist near me" variants.
16. **Evaluate second location** at month 18–24 if monthly gross exceeds **$110K** and EBITDA margin clears **20–25%**.
