## Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts

### Local Brick-and-Mortar Agency
**Pros**: Single point of accountability, in-person onboarding, local market familiarity, bundled reporting.
**Cons**: **2.5–4x cost**, slower turnaround on small edits, retainer lock-in (6–12 months typical), junior staff turnover on accounts under $2,500/mo.
**Realistic Sacramento pricing**: $1,800–$3,500/mo for local SEO; $4,500–$9,500 for a booking-capable site.

### Verified Freelance Experts
**Pros**: **55–70% lower cost**, direct access to the specialist, faster iteration, pay-per-deliverable flexibility.
**Cons**: Requires vetting (portfolio, references, contract), no single accountable party across disciplines, quality variance.
**Vetting protocol**: Demand 3 live local-service case studies, verify GBP ownership transfer, confirm 2 client references in home services, and use milestone-based payment (50/50 or 40/40/20).

**Recommendation for a $25K-budget startup**: Freelance stack for website + programmatic pages + GBP, with a **$600–$900/mo** freelance SEO retainer. Reserve agency spend until revenue exceeds **$25K/month**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register **Sacramento City Business License** + CA LLC ($70–$120 filing).
2. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and commercial auto — **$1,400–$2,600/yr**.
3. Obtain **IICRC certification** ($1,200–$1,800) — a trust signal that converts in Sacramento's review-driven market.
4. Purchase equipment: used truck-mount ($14K–$32K) or portable ($3K–$7K) + chemicals ($600–$1,200 initial).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
5. Launch **Google Business Profile** with service-area radius covering Sacramento, Elk Grove, Roseville, Folsom.
6. Build **5-page website** with online booking (Calendly or Housecall Pro embed).
7. Deploy **25–40 programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in [ZIP/Neighborhood]") targeting long-tail GEO queries.
8. Publish **10 GEO-optimized articles** answering: pricing, pet stains, move-out cleaning, allergen removal.

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 6–12)
9. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** ($800–$1,500/mo test budget) — highest-intent Sacramento channel.
10. Implement **automated review request** (SMS post-job); target **25 reviews by day 90**.
11. Sign **3–5 property management contracts** (Sacramento has 200+ PM firms; pitch turnover pricing).
12. Run **Q4 holiday promotion** (pre-Thanksgiving deep clean bundle).

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–12)
13. Add **second van/technician** once monthly revenue exceeds **$18K**.
14. Layer **commercial contracts** (offices, clinics) for recurring revenue.
15. Reinvest **8–12% of revenue** into programmatic SEO expansion (new ZIP pages, commercial verticals).
