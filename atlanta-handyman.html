## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Financial Foundation (Weeks 1–2)

1. **Register LLC with Georgia Secretary of State** — $100 filing fee, 1–3 business days online.
2. **Obtain Atlanta/Fulton County business license** — $75–$400 depending on projected gross receipts; apply via Atlanta's online portal.
3. **Secure EIN** (free, IRS online) and open a business checking account.
4. **Purchase liability insurance** — $1,100–$1,800/yr for solo GL + tools; add commercial auto if using a personal truck ($600–$1,200/yr).
5. **Confirm county-specific contractor thresholds** — projects over $2,500 generally require a GC license in Fulton, Cobb, DeKalb, and Gwinnett; handyman work under threshold is exempt.

### Phase 2: Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–4)

6. **Register domain + launch 5–8 page website** — Home, Services (drywall, fixtures, decks, painting, assembly), Service Areas, About, Contact, Reviews. Budget $800–$2,400 via verified freelancer.
7. **Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile** — Primary category "Handyman," 10+ service categories, 20+ geo-tagged photos, weekly posts. This is your #1 lead source.
8. **Build programmatic SEO structure** — 25–50 landing pages targeting `[service] + [Atlanta neighborhood]` (e.g., "drywall repair Buckhead," "deck repair Sandy Springs"). Target the 47 highest-value submarkets first.
9. **Set up Jobber or Housecall Pro CRM** — $49–$129/mo; enables online booking, invoicing, and review requests.

### Phase 3: Lead Generation Engine (Weeks 4–8)

10. **Launch Google Local Services Ads** — $500–$1,500/mo budget; ~$28–$55/lead in Atlanta; requires background check + insurance verification (2–3 week approval).
11. **Deploy review generation workflow** — automated SMS/email review requests post-job; target 25+ Google reviews by day 90 (critical for map-pack ranking).
12. **Build citation profile** — Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB (all free tiers); consistent NAP across 15+ directories.
13. **Activate Nextdoor Business** — Atlanta's ITP neighborhoods (Decatur, Virginia-Highland, Grant Park) over-index on Nextdoor recommendations.

### Phase 4: Operations & Scale (Weeks 8–12)

14. **Set flat-rate pricing tiers** — Diagnostic $95, Small job $185–$260, Half-day $420–$520, Full-day $780–$950. Flat-rate converts 22–31% better than hourly in Atlanta market tests.
15. **Establish supplier accounts** — Home Depot Pro Xtra, Lowe's Pro, and local lumber (Randall Brothers, Peachtree Lumber) for 5–12% material discounts.
16. **Hire first helper at month 4–6** — Atlanta helper labor $18–$24/hr; enables 2x job throughput.
17. **Reinvest 15–20% of revenue into LSA + programmatic SEO expansion** — scale to 100+ landing pages by month 12.
