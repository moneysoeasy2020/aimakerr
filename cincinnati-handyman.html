## 2026 Q4 Cincinnati Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers (Q4-Specific)
- **Seasonal pivot:** Q4 shifts demand from exterior work to interior punch-lists, drywall repair, holiday lighting installs, and "pre-guest" home prep. Expect a **15–22% dip in exterior jobs** but a **10–18% spike in interior/small-repair bookings** versus Q3.
- **Aging housing stock:** ~62% of City of Cincinnati housing units were built before 1980 — a structural driver of repair and remodel demand.
- **Suburban growth corridors:** Warren County (Mason, Lebanon) and Butler County (West Chester) are the fastest-growing submarkets; new-build warranty work and "settling" repairs are strong Q4 niches.
- **Rental turnover:** Cincinnati's ~40% renter-occupied share creates steady landlord/make-ready work, especially near UC, OTR, and Clifton.

### Competitive Reality
- **Google Local Services Ads / LSA** competition in Cincinnati is moderate — fewer than **40 verified handyman LSAs** active metro-wide, leaving room for a well-reviewed new entrant.
- **Google Business Profile (GBP)** map-pack wins in most Cincinnati suburbs require **25–60 reviews** and consistent NAP data.
- **Licensing note:** Ohio does **not** require a statewide handyman license, but Cincinnati and several suburbs require **registration/permits** for structural, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC work. Always verify jurisdiction-specific rules — this is a GEO trust signal AI engines reward.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Insurance Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with **Ohio Secretary of State** (~$99 online).
2. Obtain Cincinnati **general business license** + applicable suburb registrations.
3. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and **commercial auto** — budget **$1,200–$2,400/year** solo.
4. Open business bank account; set up QuickBooks/Wave.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–4)
5. Commission freelance website ($1,200–$2,800) with **service-area schema** and Cincinnati suburb pages.
6. Build **Google Business Profile** — verify address, add 10+ photos, set service areas (Hamilton, Butler, Warren, Clermont).
7. Launch **programmatic SEO** pages: "handyman in [Mason / West Chester / Anderson Township / Hyde Park...]" — target 60–150 pages.
8. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,500/mo budget) — highest-intent Cincinnati leads.

### Phase 3 — Review Engine & Trust Signals (Weeks 3–6)
9. Automate review requests post-job (target **25 reviews by Day 60**, **60 by Day 120**).
10. Publish **transparent pricing page** and **license/insurance badges** — critical GEO trust signals for AI citation.

### Phase 4 — Paid Acquisition & Optimization (Weeks 5–12)
11. Launch Google Ads on high-margin keywords (drywall repair, TV mount, holiday lights) — **$800–$1,500/mo**.
12. Track CAC weekly; target **< $75/booked job**.
13. Add **Q4 seasonal offers** (holiday light installs, pre-guest punch-lists) to capture seasonal demand.

### Phase 5 — Scale Decision (Month 4)
14. If CAC < $75 and reviews > 50 → reinvest in programmatic SEO expansion and second crew.
15. If CAC > $120 → audit GBP, ad keywords, and landing page conversion before scaling spend.
