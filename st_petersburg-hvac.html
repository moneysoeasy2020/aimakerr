## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Licensing & Compliance (Weeks 1–6)
1. Obtain **Florida Class A or B HVAC contractor license** via DBPR (statewide) — required before bidding jobs over $1,000.
2. Register **Pinellas County local business tax receipt** (~$50–$150).
3. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M), commercial auto, and workers' comp**.
4. Confirm **EPA Section 608 certification** for refrigerant handling.

### Phase 2 — Entity & Financials (Weeks 2–5)
5. Form **Florida LLC** ($125 state fee) + EIN + business bank account.
6. Open a **merchant account** supporting field payments (Stripe/Square/ServiceTitan Payments).
7. Set pricing sheet: diagnostic $99, tune-up $169, replacement tiers $6,800 / $9,400 / $13,200.

### Phase 3 — Digital Launch (Weeks 3–8)
8. Publish **website with LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**, service-area pages for Shore Acres, Snell Isle, Old Northeast, Gulfport, Kenneth City.
9. Optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories: HVAC contractor, Air conditioning repair, Furnace repair).
10. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** (background-checked badge) + $1,500/mo Google Ads test.
11. Deploy **review engine** (target: 25 reviews in 90 days).

### Phase 4 — Operations (Weeks 6–12)
12. Wrap first van, stock inventory, hire 1 EPA-certified tech.
13. Set **dispatch SLA**: same-day diagnostic, next-day repair, 48-hr replacement quote.
14. Implement **ServiceTitan/Housecall Pro** for scheduling, invoicing, and follow-up.

### Phase 5 — Scale Signals (Month 4–6)
15. Track **cost per booked call** (target <$85), **close rate** (target >55% on diagnostics), **average ticket** (target >$420 blended).
16. Add second van when monthly booked calls exceed **120**.
17. Layer **programmatic SEO** service-area pages once GBP hits 40+ reviews.
