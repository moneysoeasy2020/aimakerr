## 2026 Q4 Modesto Market Analysis

Modesto (Stanislaus County) has a population of roughly **220,000** with a metro area of **~550,000**. The median household income sits near **$72,000**, and roughly **54% of housing units are owner-occupied** — most built between 1960 and 2005, meaning a steady pipeline of deferred maintenance, aging fixtures, and remodel demand. The California Contractors State License Board (CSLB) reports **fewer than 400 actively licensed general/specialty contractors operating primarily in Stanislaus County**, against a serviceable addressable market of an estimated **$180M–$240M annually** in residential repair and small-remodel spend. That ratio — high demand, thin licensed supply — is the core opportunity.

Realistic 2026 Q4 pricing in Modesto: **$75–$110/hour** for handyman labor (billing model), **$95–$150/hour** for licensed specialty trades. Typical job tickets: **$150–$350** for small repairs, **$400–$1,200** for multi-task punch lists, **$2,500–$8,000** for bathroom refreshes, and **$8,000–$25,000** for kitchen/room remodels. California requires a **CSLB license for any job over $500 in labor + materials combined** — so plan your tiering accordingly (see Step 2). Startup capital for a lean, compliant launch runs **$8,000–$18,000**; a fully equipped van + tools + insurance + licensing + first-quarter marketing runs **$22,000–$38,000**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Step 1 — Licensing & Legal (Weeks 1–4, $1,200–$2,800)
- **CSLB license**: Decide tier — *Handyman* (jobs ≤$500 labor+materials, no license required but **strongly recommended** to register as a sole proprietor) vs. *General B (or specialty C-classes)* for jobs >$500. Apply via CSLB (6–10 week processing in 2026).
- **LLC formation** ($800–$1,500 in CA), **EIN**, **city of Modesto business license** (~$150–$400), **Stanislaus County** registration.
- **Insurance**: General liability **$1M/$2M** ($1,200–$2,400/yr), workers' comp if hiring ($2,000+/yr).

### Step 2 — Service Tiering & Pricing (Week 3)
- **Tier A (no license needed):** TV mounting, furniture assembly, drywall patch, caulking, door adjustments — $95–$150 flat.
- **Tier B (license required):** Electrical, plumbing, structural — subcontract to licensed partners or obtain license.
- Set **$89 diagnostic/trip fee** (credited to job) — standard in Modesto market.

### Step 3 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6, $2,000–$4,500 freelance)
- Website with **LocalBusiness + Service schema**, booking widget (Jobber/Housecall Pro), service-area pages.
- **Google Business Profile** optimized: categories (Handyman, Contractor), 10+ photos, service areas (Modesto, Ceres, Turlock, Riverbank, Oakdale, Salida).
- **Programmatic SEO**: one page per `service + city` combo — e.g., "drywall repair Modesto," "TV mounting Ceres." 40–80 pages, each with unique local proof (neighborhoods, permit notes, pricing).

### Step 4 — Lead Generation Launch (Weeks 5–8, $1,500–$3,000/mo)
- **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$25–$60/lead in Modesto handyman vertical) — requires license + insurance verification.
- **Google Ads** on high-intent terms ($8–$18 CPC locally).
- **Review engine**: automated SMS/QR post-job → target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (top-3 GBP ranking threshold).

### Step 5 — Operations & Retention (Ongoing)
- Jobber or Housecall Pro ($49–$129/mo), QuickBooks Simple Start ($30/mo).
- **Referral program**: $50 credit per referred job — Modesto's tight-knit market rewards this heavily.

### Step 6 — 90-Day Milestone Targets
| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Google reviews | 25+ |
| LSA leads/week | 8–15 |
| Close rate | 35–50% |
| Avg. ticket | $280–$450 |
| Monthly revenue (month 3) | $9,000–$16,000 |
