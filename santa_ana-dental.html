A realistic **de novo general dental clinic** here requires **$650,000–$1,100,000** in total capitalization: **$350,000–$550,000** for build-out and equipment (4–6 operatories at **$55,000–$85,000 per operatory** fully equipped), plus **$300,000–$450,000** in working capital to survive the typical **9–15 month** ramp to break-even. ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–6)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Form CA professional corporation (dental corp), obtain **DDS/DMD license**, DEA registration, and **Santa Ana business license** (~$100–$400). File fictitious business name with Orange County.
2. **Site selection:** Target **1,800–2,500 sq ft** near anchor retail or medical clusters; negotiate **TI allowance** ($25–$60/sq ft) and **free rent** (3–6 months).
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) or equipment leasing; budget **$650K–$1.1M** total capitalization.
4. **Equipment orders:** Place by **August 2026** for Q4 delivery (6–12 week lead times).

### Phase 2 — Build-Out & Digital Foundation (Months 4–7)
5. **Build-out:** Dental-specific plumbing/electrical; permit timelines in Santa Ana run **8–16 weeks** — start early.
6. **Website + GBP:** Launch site with online booking, Spanish toggle, insurance list, and **LocalBusiness/Dentist schema**. Claim and optimize Google Business Profile.
7. **Citations & reviews:** Seed **40+ directories**; pre-load review request workflow (target **25+ reviews in first 90 days**).

### Phase 3 — Patient Acquisition (Months 6–9, Q4 2026)
8. **Paid search:** Launch Google Ads + **Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$35–$90/lead in OC dental). Cap CPC bids at **$12–$18** initially.
9. **Open-enrollment push:** October–November campaigns targeting plan-switchers; email/SMS to neighborhood lists.
10. **Community outreach:** Partner with Santa Ana schools, churches, and **bilingual community health fairs**; offer $49–$99 new-patient specials.

### Phase 4 — Optimization (Months 9–12)
11. **Track KPIs:** Cost per new patient (**target <$150**), show rate (**>80%**), production per operatory (**$18K–$28K/mo** by month 12).
12. **Review & referral engine:** Automate post-visit review requests; launch **$25–$50 referral credits**.
13. **Reinvest:** At **$1.2M+ run-rate**, evaluate agency upgrade or in-house marketing hire.
