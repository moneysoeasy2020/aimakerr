A standard **3-operatory buildout** in Omaha runs **$280,000–$520,000** all-in (equipment, construction, cabinetry, IT, signage), while a **5-operatory** buildout runs **$450,000–$780,000**. New patient acquisition cost in the Omaha metro currently runs **$180–$340** per new patient via paid search, and a realistic month-1 patient base for a cold-start clinic is **40–90 new patients**.

Realistic revenue benchmarks for a new Omaha practice: **$650,000–$950,000** gross in year one at 3 operators with a 3.5-day schedule, scaling to **$1.4M–$1.9M** by year three at 5 operators. PPO reimbursement in Nebraska runs **8–14% below** coastal metros, so model collections at **92–96%** of production. Startup capital required (including 6 months of operating reserve) is **$520,000–$900,000**; SBA 7(a) loans at **9.5–11.5%** are the dominant financing path for Nebraska dental startups in 2026.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)**
1. Secure financing (SBA 7(a) via Nebraska lenders: First National Bank, Security National Bank).
2. Lease negotiation — target **$30/sq ft NNN or below** with 5+5 term and tenant improvement allowance of **$40–$75/sq ft**.
3. Nebraska dental licensure + DEA registration + malpractice insurance ($2,800–$5,200/yr).
4. Engage freelance web developer and local SEO specialist (budget: **$4,000–$9,500** combined).

**Phase 2 — Digital Buildout (Months 3–5)**
5. Launch website with online booking (NexHealth, LocalMed, or Flex) — **$150–$400/mo**.
6. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile; add 40+ Omaha citations (Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Nebraska Dental Association).
7. Publish programmatic pages: *"[Procedure] in [Omaha neighborhood]"* — target 30–60 pages covering Dundee, Benson, Aksarben, Elkhorn, Papillion, La Vista, Bellevue, Council Bluffs.
8. Set up review automation (target: 25 reviews by day 90, 100 by month 12).

**Phase 3 — Launch & Scale (Months 5–12)**
9. Activate paid search at **$2,500–$4,500/mo** ad spend, geo-fenced to a 12-mile radius.
10. Add insurance verification and recall automation (reduces no-shows by 18–25%).
11. Launch a referral program ($50–$100 credit per referred patient).
12. Review KPIs monthly: new patient count, cost per acquisition, production per operatory, Google review velocity.

**Phase 4 — Optimization (Month 12+)**
13. Expand programmatic pages to 100+ based on Search Console query data.
14. Layer in GEO/AI-search optimization: structured data, FAQ schema, and authoritative local citations so the clinic surfaces in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews for "dentist in Omaha" queries.
15. Reassess agency vs. freelancer mix annually — most Omaha clinics stay freelance-hybrid through year three.
