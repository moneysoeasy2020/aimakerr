## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Licensing Foundation (Weeks 1–3)

1. Register LLC with NYS Department of State — **$200 filing fee**.
2. Obtain **NYS Home Improvement Contractor license** (required for jobs $500+): $150 application + $50 exam fee.
3. Secure **EPA Section 608 certification** for refrigerant handling — $120–$250 exam.
4. Bind general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto: **$2,400–$4,200/year** for a single-van operation.
5. Obtain Erie County DBA and set up NYS sales tax registration (HVAC installs are taxable in NY; repairs are not — track separately).

### Phase 2: Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–5, parallel)

6. Launch website with **15 service-area pages** targeting: Buffalo, Cheektowaga, Tonawanda, Amherst, West Seneca, Lackawanna, Depew, Lancaster, Hamburg, Orchard Park, Kenmore, Williamsville, Grand Island, Niagara Falls, North Tonawanda.
7. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile — **20+ geotagged photos, weekly posts, Q&A seeding**.
8. Install CallRail + Jobber; set up lead-source tracking before spending a dollar on ads.

### Phase 3: Pre-Freeze Marketing Push (Weeks 4–7, ending before Nov 2)

9. Launch Google Local Services Ads (pay-per-lead, **$38–$65/lead** in Buffalo HVAC vertical).
10. Deploy "winterization tune-up" offer at **$89–$129** as a loss-leader to build review volume — target **25 Google reviews before Nov 1**.
11. Publish rebate-navigation content ("NYS Clean Heat rebate guide for Buffalo homeowners") — high-intent, low-competition GEO target for AI search engines.

### Phase 4: Capture the Freeze Surge (Weeks 8–12)

12. Activate after-hours dispatch at **$175–$260** premium — staff on-call rotation from day one.
13. Run retargeting ads to tune-up customers for full system replacement quotes.
14. Track **cost-per-booked-job** weekly; kill any channel exceeding **$180/lead** by Week 10.

### Phase 5: Q1 2027 Retention Engine (Week 12+)

15. Convert tune-up customers into **annual maintenance agreements at $14–$22/month** — this stabilizes the 32% summer revenue trough.
16. Expand service-area pages based on which ZIPs generated the highest ticket values in Q4.
