## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–6)
1. **Entity & compliance:** Form PLLC in WA ($200 filing), obtain UBI number, register with WA Dept. of Revenue; confirm dentist licensure via WA DOH.
2. **Site selection:** Target 2,500–3,200 sq ft in North End/Proctor or University Place; negotiate 7–10 yr lease with 3-month TI allowance; budget $180–$320/sq ft build-out.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) or equipment leasing; expect $480,000–$850,000 total package with 10–15% owner equity.
4. **Payer strategy:** Decide PPO panel (2–4 networks), Apple Health acceptance, and self-pay membership plan ($25–$35/mo).
5. **Digital foundation:** Register domain, GBP, and 40+ citations; commission website with **LocalBusiness + Dentist schema, FAQ schema, and AI-answer-ready content clusters**.

### Phase 2 — Build & Pre-Open Marketing (Months 4–7)
6. **Build-out & equipment:** 4–5 operatories, CBCT/pan, digital impressions; equipment $220,000–$380,000.
7. **Hire core team:** 1 office manager, 2 hygienists, 2 assistants — Tacoma wage range $28–$52/hr depending on role.
8. **Pre-open GEO push:** Publish 8–12 location/service pages ("dentist North End Tacoma," "emergency dentist Stadium District"), 6 FAQ clusters, and 3 comparison pages; submit to AI-crawlable directories.
9. **Review engine:** Set up automated post-visit review requests; target **40+ Google reviews by month 6**.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 7–14)
10. **Soft open:** 20–30 patients/week via friends-and-family and local employer outreach.
11. **Paid acquisition:** Google Ads $2,500–$4,500/mo on high-intent terms; track cost per booked appointment ($145–$310 benchmark).
12. **Community presence:** Sponsor 2–3 local events (Proctor Farmers Market, youth sports); partner with 5–8 primary care clinics for referrals.
13. **Monthly GEO audit:** Check AI-answer citations for "dentist Tacoma" and "emergency dentist Tacoma" queries; adjust content clusters quarterly.

### Phase 4 — Stabilize & Scale (Months 14–24)
14. **Optimize payer mix:** Shift toward 60%+ PPO/FFS; review fee schedule annually against Tacoma market rates.
15. **Add service lines:** Invisalign, implants, or sedation once 200+ active patients — each adds $8,000–$25,000/mo production potential.
16. **Consider second location** only after month 24 and $1.2M+ annual collections.
