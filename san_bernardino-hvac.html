# Launching an HVAC Services Business in San Bernardino, CA

San Bernardino's HVAC market is driven by extreme summer heat (100°F+ days from June–September), an aging housing stock (median home age ~45 years), and a renter-heavy population (~45% renter-occupied). Realistic startup capital for a licensed, insured HVAC contractor ranges from **$38,000–$85,000** depending on whether you launch as a service-and-repair operator or a full install company. ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Licensing & Setup (Weeks 1–6)
1. Obtain **CSLB C-20 (HVAC)** license; pass law/business exam; post **$25,000 bond**.
2. Secure **EPA Section 608** certification (all techs) and general liability + workers' comp insurance.
3. Register LLC with CA Secretary of State; obtain San Bernardino business license and seller's permit.
4. Set up service van, tools, refrigerant recovery equipment, and a CRM (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 4–8)
5. Build a fast, mobile-first website with **city + service programmatic pages** (e.g., "AC Repair San Bernardino," "Furnace Installation Fontana," "Commercial HVAC Redlands").
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas, photos, Q&A, hours, booking link.
7. Deploy **review-generation automation** (SMS/email post-job) targeting 50+ reviews in 90 days.
8. Clean up **30+ local citations** (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Chamber of Commerce).

### Phase 3 — GEO & Local Authority (Weeks 6–12)
9. Publish **structured, factual local content**: pricing pages, service-area pages, FAQ schema, and "2026 Q4 San Bernardino HVAC cost" guides — AI engines cite clear, sourced, locally-specific data.
10. Add **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema markup** with NAP consistency across all assets.
11. Pursue **local backlinks**: San Bernardino Chamber, Inland Empire trade associations, local news, supplier directories.
12. Build **B2B relationships** with property managers and HOAs for recurring maintenance contracts.

### Phase 4 — Demand Capture & Scale (Weeks 10–24)
13. Launch **Google Local Services Ads + targeted Search Ads** for emergency keywords; cap CPC at $12–$15 with tight geo-radius.
14. Implement **24/7 answering + dispatch** (live or AI) — speed-to-lead under 60 seconds materially lifts close rates.
15. Introduce **financing options** (e.g., Synchrony, Service Finance) to convert price-sensitive install leads.
16. Track **cost-per-acquired-job** monthly; reinvest in top-performing channels.
