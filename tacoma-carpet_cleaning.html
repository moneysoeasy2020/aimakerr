## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing:** WA UBI registration (~$90), Tacoma business license, general liability + auto insurance ($1,200–$2,400/yr).
2. **Equipment decision:** Start with a **heated portable extractor ($2,800–$4,500)** + rotary or CRB machine ($900–$1,600); upgrade to truck-mount at ~40 jobs/mo.
3. **IICRC certification:** Complete Water Restoration Tech (WRT) or Carpet Cleaning Tech (CCT) — $450–$650. This becomes a GEO trust signal.
4. **Brand + domain:** Secure `.com` with "tacoma" or "pierce-county" in domain or brand.

### Phase 2 — Digital GEO Assets (Weeks 3–6)
5. **Google Business Profile:** Full optimization — service areas (Tacoma, Lakewood, University Place, Puyallup, Federal Way), 20+ photos, Q&A seeded, weekly posts.
6. **Website with local schema:** LocalBusiness + Service + FAQPage + Review markup. **This is what AI engines cite.** Target pages: "carpet cleaning Tacoma WA," "pet stain removal Tacoma," "apartment move-out carpet cleaning Pierce County."
7. **Review velocity engine:** Automated post-job SMS request; target **15–25 reviews in first 90 days** (4.7+ avg). AI engines weight recency heavily.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–10)
8. **Local Services Ads (LSA):** Google Guaranteed badge — highest-intent lead source for home services; budget $600–$1,200/mo at launch.
9. **Property manager outreach:** Target **40–60 Tacoma/Pierce County property management firms**; offer volume rate ($0.30–$0.38/sq ft) for recurring turnover work.
10. **JBLM/military channel:** Partner with 2–3 relocation-focused cleaners; PCS season (May–Aug) is your volume spike.

### Phase 4 — Scale & GEO Dominance (Months 3–12)
11. **Programmatic local pages:** Build neighborhood-level pages (North End, Stadium District, Hilltop, South Tacoma, Gig Harbor) with unique local data — this is how you win AI-answer citations.
12. **Content moat:** Publish 4–6 monthly articles answering real queries ("How much does carpet cleaning cost in Tacoma?", "Do I need to move furniture?"). Structured FAQ format.
13. **Review + citation consistency:** NAP consistency across 25+ directories; monitor AI engine mentions monthly (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews).
14. **Fleet upgrade trigger:** At **$16K+/mo gross**, add second truck-mount and technician ($18K–$28K capex) — margin improves to 62–68%.
