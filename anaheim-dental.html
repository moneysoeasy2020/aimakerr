## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–6)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Form CA professional corporation (dental), obtain DDS/DMD license, DEA, CDPH X-ray registration, Anaheim business license
2. **Site selection:** Target 1,800–2,500 sq ft, 4–6 operatories, parking ratio ≥4/1,000 sq ft; negotiate 7–10 year term with TI allowance of $40–$90/sq ft
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) or commercial dental lending at 7.5–9.5% (Q4 2026); expect 10–20% down
4. **Build-out & equipment:** Award dental-specific contractor; order equipment 12–16 weeks lead time
5. **Digital foundation:** Domain, HIPAA-compliant hosting, GBP verification, schema markup, service-area pages (Anaheim, Anaheim Hills, West Anaheim, Platinum Triangle, Buena Park, Fullerton)

### Phase 2 — Launch (Months 6–9)
6. **GBP + review engine:** 25+ reviews in first 90 days; respond to 100%
7. **Local SEO:** 40–60 programmatic service pages (e.g., "emergency dentist Anaheim," "pediatric dentist near Disneyland," "Spanish-speaking dentist Anaheim")
8. **Paid search:** $2,500–$5,000/mo Google Ads on high-intent terms; track cost-per-new-patient ($120–$280 target)
9. **Insurance credentialing:** PPO contracts (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Guardian) — 90–120 day cycle; start pre-launch

### Phase 3 — Scale (Months 9–18)
10. **Recall + reactivation systems:** Automated recall, unscheduled treatment follow-up
11. **Reputation moat:** Target 150+ Google reviews by month 18
12. **Expansion decision:** Add hygiene capacity or second location if operatory utilization >75%
