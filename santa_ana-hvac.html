## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Licensing & Compliance (Weeks 1–6)
1. Obtain **C-20 (HVAC)** or **C-38 (Refrigeration)** license via CSLB — exam + application fees run **$450–$900**; processing 4–8 weeks.
2. Secure **EPA Section 608 Universal certification** ($400–$700) — legally required to handle refrigerants.
3. Register LLC with CA Secretary of State (**$70–$100**), get Santa Ana business license (**~$150–$400/yr** based on gross receipts), and pull sales tax permit.
4. Bind **general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto + workers' comp** — budget **$3,200–$6,500/yr**.

### Phase 2 — Operational Build (Weeks 4–10)
5. Purchase one used service van (**$18,000–$32,000**) equipped with recovery machine, vacuum pump, gauges, and stock (**$4,500–$7,000**).
6. Set pricing: diagnostic **$89–$129** (waived with repair), maintenance plan **$189–$329/yr**, replacement installs **$9,500–$18,500**.
7. Open trade accounts with **2–3 supply houses** (Johnstone, Ferguson, Baker) within a 10-mile radius.

### Phase 3 — Digital Launch (Weeks 6–12)
8. Publish website with **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**, service pages, and online booking.
9. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas (92701–92707 ZIPs), 25+ photos, Q&A.
10. Deploy **30–60 programmatic service-area pages** ("AC repair 92703," "furnace tune-up Santa Ana") with unique local content.
11. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** ($1,500–$3,000/mo) + **Google Ads** ($2,000–$3,500/mo) targeting "AC repair near me" and emergency intent.
12. Install review-request automation; target **40+ Google reviews in 90 days** (review velocity is the #1 GBP ranking factor).

### Phase 4 — Channel Expansion (Months 4–9)
13. Build **property-manager referral pipeline** — Santa Ana's ~71% renter share makes 15–25 recurring B2B accounts worth **$8K–$20K/month** in steady work.
14. Add **Yelp, Nextdoor, and Angi** profiles; negotiate flat-rate lead pricing.
15. Launch **maintenance-plan memberships** — target 150 members by month 9 (**$28K–$49K** in recurring annual revenue).

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 9–12)
16. Hire second technician when monthly call volume exceeds **140**.
17. Reinvest **8–12% of revenue** into SEO; expand programmatic pages to neighboring cities (Tustin, Costa Mesa, Garden Grove).
18. Target month-12 KPIs: **120–160 calls/month, 4.7+ star rating, 90+ reviews, $95K–$135K monthly revenue.**
