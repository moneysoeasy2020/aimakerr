## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with Florida Sunbiz — **$125** filing fee.
2. Obtain Orlando/Orange County Local Business Tax Receipt — **$35–$60**; add Osceola/Seminole if you cross county lines.
3. Florida sales tax registration (carpet cleaning services are generally exempt, but cleaning *products* retail is taxable — confirm with a CPA).
4. General liability insurance — **$600–$1,200/yr**; add commercial auto.
5. IICRC certification (CCT or equivalent) — **$500–$900**; it is a trust signal in every estimate conversation.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
6. Secure domain + 5-page site with online booking (Housecall Pro or Jobber embedded) — target **$1,200** via freelancer.
7. Build Google Business Profile with 10+ geo-tagged job photos; add service-area pages for **Orlando, Kissimmee, Winter Park, Lake Nona, Dr. Phillips, Altamonte Springs**.
8. Deploy schema markup (LocalBusiness + Service) — this is the core GEO lever for AI-generated answer engines.
9. Set up review automation (SMS request at job completion); target **25 reviews in 90 days**.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 3–8)
10. Launch Google Local Services Ads + a **$900–$1,400/mo** Search campaign on "carpet cleaning orlando" and 12 long-tail variants.
11. Direct-outreach sprint: 40 property managers and 15 STR co-hosts per week; offer a **$149 first-turnover trial clean**.
12. Publish 2 GEO-structured articles monthly ("cost to clean carpet in [neighborhood]") with exact price tables — AI engines cite specific numbers.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–12)
13. Add second van at **$12k+/month** gross; hire IICRC-trained tech at **$18–$24/hr**.
14. Convert 30% of revenue to recurring commercial contracts.
15. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into ads + content; audit freelancer ROI quarterly.
