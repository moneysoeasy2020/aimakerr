# Launching a Carpet Cleaning Business in El Paso, TX
### 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

El Paso's carpet cleaning market supports roughly **180–220 active operators** (sole proprietors through multi-truck franchises), competing for an estimated **$38–46M annual residential + commercial cleaning spend** across a metro of ~870,000 residents. A realistic startup budget for a lean, licensed, insured operation is **$18,000–$32,000** (van-mounted entry setup) or **$6,500–$11,000** (portable-extraction starter). Expect residential ticket averages of **$185–$320** for 3–5 rooms and **$0.22–$0.38/sq ft** for commercial contracts; drive-time economics matter here because El Paso's geography (mission valley to northeast, 25–40 min cross-town) compresses route density if you don't cluster jobs.

Pricing benchmarks for Q4 2026: standard 3-room residential clean **$165–$240**, 5-room + hallway **$265–$380**, pet-odor/traffic-lane treatment add-ons **$45–$95**, tile & grout **$0.65–$1.10/sq ft**, and water-damage extraction emergency calls **$350–$900** (highest-margin, fastest-growth segment). Commercial recurring (offices, apartments, property management) runs **$0.18–$0.30/sq ft** monthly with 12–24 month contracts. Your break-even at 60 jobs/month with a $14,500 monthly overhead load lands around **month 4–6** if you secure two property-management accounts early.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Financial Foundation (Weeks 1–3)**
1. Register LLC with **Texas Secretary of State** ($300 filing).
2. Obtain **El Paso business license** + Texas Sales & Use Permit (no state income tax; budget for franchise tax at scale).
3. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** + commercial auto — required by property managers; ~$1,200–$2,400/yr.
4. Open business banking; set aside 25–30% for taxes.

**Phase 2 — Equipment & Operations (Weeks 2–5)**
5. Choose model: **van-mounted** ($18k–$32k all-in incl. used van) vs **portable** ($6.5k–$11k). Van-mounted wins on speed and commercial credibility.
6. Source truck-mount unit (used units $8k–$15k), hoses, wand, chemicals, air movers.
7. Set service radius: cluster **Westside → Northeast → Horizon City** to protect route density.

**Phase 3 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–8)**
8. Launch 5–7 page site with online booking + **Spanish version** (freelance: $800–$2,200).
9. Claim & optimize **Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas (El Paso, Socorro, Horizon City, San Elizario), photos, Q&A.
10. Build review engine: target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (ask every job; SMS automation).
11. Set up Google Ads *only after* GBP + site are live; cap at $1,500/mo test budget.

**Phase 4 — Sales & Partnerships (Weeks 6–12)**
12. Door-knock **10–15 property-management firms** (apartments, PM companies on Westside/Eastside) — recurring contracts are your revenue floor.
13. Register with **Fort Bliss relocation/off-base housing networks** for PCS turnover cleaning.
14. Launch **seasonal promos** timed to monsoon (water damage) and spring turnover.

**Phase 5 — Scale (Months 4–12)**
15. Reinvest 15–20% of revenue into local SEO + reviews.
16. Add **tile & grout** and **water-damage extraction** (highest-margin add-ons).
17. Hire first technician at ~$18–$22/hr + performance bonus; target 2nd truck by month 10–14.
