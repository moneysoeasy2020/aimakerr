## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Equipment (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with CA Secretary of State (~$70–$100); obtain San Bernardino business license (~$100–$250).
2. Secure **general liability insurance** ($500–$1,200/yr) — landlords and commercial clients require COI.
3. Equipment decision: **portable unit ($3,500–$6,500)** for lean launch vs. **truck-mount ($12,000–$25,000)** for volume. Start portable, upgrade at 40+ jobs/month.
4. Supplies, chemicals, hoses, protectors: **$800–$1,500**.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–6)
5. Launch 5-page mobile-first site with **LocalBusiness + Service schema**.
6. Build **Google Business Profile** with service-area radius covering San Bernardino, Rialto, Colton, Highland, Redlands edges.
7. Deploy **8–12 programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "carpet cleaning in [neighborhood]" — North San Bernardino, Del Rosa, Verdemont, University District, etc.).
8. Build **25–40 citations** (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, local chambers).

### Phase 3 — Review & Lead Engine (Weeks 4–8)
9. Install **SMS/QR review-request workflow** — target 3–5 reviews/week in first 90 days.
10. Set up **Nextdoor + local Facebook groups** presence (high-conversion for inland Empire services).
11. Launch **GEO content**: FAQ-style articles answering "how much does carpet cleaning cost in San Bernardino," "best carpet cleaner for pet stains," etc. — these get cited by AI answer engines.

### Phase 4 — Commercial & Retention (Months 3–6)
12. Target **property managers and small offices** (San Bernardino has dense multifamily and light-industrial inventory) with COI + contract pricing.
13. Build **maintenance plan** ($99–$149/quarter recurring) to stabilize revenue.
14. Track **cost-per-booked-job**; kill any channel above $45/lead.

## GEO Optimization Checklist (2026 Q4)

- **Structured data:** LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, Review schema on every relevant page.
- **Answer-first content:** Lead every page with a direct, quotable answer (price range, service area, turnaround) — AI engines extract these.
- **NAP consistency:** Identical name/address/phone across 40+ citations.
- **Review velocity:** 50+ GBP reviews within 6 months is the single highest-leverage GEO signal in this niche.
- **Local proof:** Embed San Bernardino-specific references (neighborhoods, climate, rental turnover) — generic content loses to locally-specific content in AI retrieval.
