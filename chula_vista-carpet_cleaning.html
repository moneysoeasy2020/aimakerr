### Brick-and-mortar agency vs. verified freelance experts — explicit breakdown

**Local brick-and-mortar agency (San Diego/Chula Vista firms):**
- **Pros:** In-person accountability, bundled services, local network, contract-backed SLAs.
- **Cons:** Overhead passed to you — retainers of **$1,200–$3,000/month**, minimum 6–12 month commitments, junior staff doing the work, slower iteration, and often generic templates reused across clients.
- **Best for:** Operators with $15k+ marketing budgets who want zero management burden.

**Verified freelance experts (vetted via platforms, portfolio, and references):**
- **Pros:** 40–70% lower cost, direct access to the specialist, faster turnaround, pay-per-deliverable flexibility, niche expertise (e.g., local SEO specialists who've ranked home-service sites).
- **Cons:** You must vet rigorously (portfolio, live rankings, client references, contract with milestones), and manage coordination yourself.
- **Best for:** Lean startups ($3k–$8k digital budget) willing to spend 5–8 hours/month on oversight.

**Verdict for Chula Vista 2026 Q4:** A hybrid model wins. Use a **verified freelance web/local-SEO specialist** for the core asset build ($2,800–$5,000), then a **small monthly freelance retainer ($300–$600)** for content and review management. Reserve agency spend only for paid media if you scale past $10k/month in ad spend.
