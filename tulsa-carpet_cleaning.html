## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Equipment (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with Oklahoma Secretary of State (**$100**); obtain Tulsa sales tax permit and **OK Tax Commission** registration.
2. Secure general liability insurance (**$600–$1,400/yr**) — required by most commercial clients.
3. Buy equipment: used truck-mount ($12K–$28K) or portable ($2.5K–$6K), plus hoses, wand, chemicals ($1,200–$2,500).
4. Set pricing at **$45/room** anchor with whole-home packages at **$240 average ticket**.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
5. Launch a fast, mobile-first site with **programmatic service-area pages** for Tulsa, Broken Arrow, Jenks, Bixby, Owasso, Sand Springs, and Sapulpa.
6. Optimize Google Business Profile: categories, service list, 20+ photos, Q&A seeded with real customer questions.
7. Add **FAQ schema + LocalBusiness schema** — this is what AI engines cite. Include explicit pricing ranges (AI Overviews reward specificity).

### Phase 3 — GEO & Review Engine (Weeks 4–12)
8. Publish 8–12 GEO-targeted articles ("How much does carpet cleaning cost in Tulsa?", "Best pet odor treatment in Broken Arrow").
9. Build review velocity: **target 40–60 Google reviews in 90 days** via post-job SMS links. Review count is the #1 ranking and AI-citation signal in local service.
10. Claim Google Local Services Ads (pay-per-lead, **$18–$45/lead** in Tulsa) and Thumbtack/Angi profiles.

### Phase 4 — Q4 Revenue Push & Q2 2027 Prep
11. Run a **holiday deep-clean promo** ($199 whole-home anchor) October–December.
12. Partner with 5–10 Tulsa property managers and 3–5 realtors for turnover jobs.
13. By January 2027, you should hold **50+ reviews, 15+ indexed service-area pages, and top-3 map pack placement** for 2–3 suburbs — the position that compounds into Q2.
