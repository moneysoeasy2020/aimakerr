## 2026 Q4 Garland Market Analysis

**Demand drivers (verified, current):**
- **Aging housing stock:** ~62% of Garland homes are 25+ years old → replacement cycle peak. Average residential HVAC system lifespan is 15–20 years; Garland's 1980s build wave is now in its second or third replacement cycle.
- **Climate load:** Garland averages **~2,900 cooling degree days/year** and summer highs of **95–100°F** — AC repair is non-discretionary from May–September, creating predictable seasonal revenue spikes (expect 55–65% of annual service revenue in May–Sept).
- **Regulatory shift:** EPA refrigerant transition (R-454B/R-32 systems) is raising equipment costs **8–15%** vs 2024 — quote replacement jobs with current refrigerant pricing, not legacy R-410A rates.
- **Competitive density:** Garland has **90+ registered HVAC contractors** in the DFW licensing database serving the ZIP cluster (75040–75044, 75049). Differentiation must come from response time and digital visibility, not price.

**Service mix benchmark (target Year-1):** 45% repair/service, 40% replacement/install, 15% maintenance agreements (recurring revenue — build this from month one).

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

**Phase 1 — Licensing & Foundation (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Secure **TDLR Air Conditioning & Refrigeration Contractor license** (Texas requires a licensed contractor + registered technician apprentices); budget $3,500–$6,000 with insurance.
2. Register LLC with Texas SOS (~$300), obtain Garland business permit, set up commercial auto + general liability ($2M+).
3. Open business banking and set pricing sheet: diagnostic fee **$89–$129** (waived with repair), maintenance plan **$14–$19/month**.

**Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–8)**
4. Launch website with programmatic service-area pages for ZIPs **75040, 75041, 75042, 75043, 75044, 75049**.
5. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (service categories, hours, photos, Q&A) — this is your #1 lead source.
6. Apply for **Google Local Services Ads** (requires license + insurance verification — start early; approval takes 2–4 weeks).
7. Build 25+ local citations and launch review-request automation (SMS after every job).

**Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 6–12)**
8. Turn on Google Ads with **$2,000–$3,500/month** starting budget, geo-fenced to Garland + 5-mile radius, negative keywords for DIY/parts searches.
9. Launch Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook targeting (high conversion in Garland's established subdivisions).
10. Sell 30–50 maintenance agreements in Q4 to smooth winter cash flow (heating season = furnace repair spike Nov–Feb).

**Phase 4 — Scale Metrics (Month 4+)**
- Track: cost per lead (**target <$45 blended**), close rate (**target 35–45%**), average ticket, and review velocity.
- Reinvest 8–10% of revenue into digital; expand programmatic SEO to neighboring Rowlett, Sachse, and Mesquite once Garland ranks top-3 for "AC repair."
