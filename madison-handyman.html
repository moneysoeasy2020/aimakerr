# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Madison, WI
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Madison, WI represents a **$180M–$220M annual addressable market** for handyman and light contractor services, driven by a metro population of ~690,000, roughly **38,000 owner-occupied single-family homes** in Dane County aged 20+ years, and a rental vacancy rate under **3.5%** that keeps property managers actively sourcing reliable trades. ### Q4 2026 Seasonal Dynamics

Q4 in Madison splits into two distinct operating windows:

- **October–mid-November:** Peak "winterization rush" — gutter cleaning ($150–$350), exterior caulking ($200–$600), storm window installs, deck winterization. Book rates run **85–95% capacity** for established operators.
- **Late November–December:** Shift to **interior work** — drywall repair, trim carpentry, holiday lighting installs ($300–$800), and punch-list projects for landlords closing year-end books. This is your customer acquisition window for Q1 2027 bookings.

**Critical GEO insight:** Searches for "handyman Madison WI" spike **40–60% in the first two weeks of October** and again in **early January**. Your digital assets must be live and indexed by **September 30, 2026** to capture the October wave.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Credentialing (Weeks 1–2, Sept 2026)
1. Form WI LLC ($130 online) and obtain EIN.
2. Apply for **WI Dwelling Contractor credential** (DSPS) — required for projects over $2,500; takes 2–4 weeks.
3. Secure general liability insurance ($1,200–$2,800/yr) — required before most property managers will contract.
4. Register for Dane County sales tax where applicable.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5, Sept–Oct 2026)
5. Hire a **verified freelancer** for website + GBP + schema ($1,500–$3,000 total). Brief must include: service-area pages for Madison, Middleton, Sun Prairie, Fitchburg, Verona; LocalBusiness + Service schema; click-to-call above fold.
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — categories: Handyman, Contractor, Deck Builder. Upload 15+ geotagged job photos.
7. Set up review automation (SMS/email request within 2 hours of job completion).

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 4–8, Oct–Nov 2026)
8. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,500/mo test budget) — highest-intent lead source for Madison handyman searches.
9. Build 10–15 **programmatic service-area pages** targeting "[service] + [Madison suburb]" long-tail queries.
10. Distribute door hangers + flyers in 3 target ZIPs (53711, 53704, 53719) — cost ~$400 for 2,000 units.

### Phase 4 — B2B Pipeline (Weeks 6–12, Nov–Dec 2026)
11. Contact **15–20 Madison property management firms** (e.g., those managing UW-adjacent rentals) with a winterization + punch-list package.
12. Offer a **Q1 2027 booking incentive** (5% off January–February interior work) to smooth the seasonal trough.

### Phase 5 — Measure & Iterate (Ongoing)
13. Track cost-per-lead by channel weekly. Target: **LSA CPL under $45**, organic CPL under $15 by month 4.
14. Reinvest 15–20% of revenue into review generation and one new service-area page per month.
