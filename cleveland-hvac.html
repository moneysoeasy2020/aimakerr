## 2026 Q4 Cleveland Market Analysis

**Demand drivers:**
- **Climate:** Cleveland winters average 25–32°F with regular single-digit cold snaps; summer cooling demand is moderate but rising. Heating-related service calls outnumber cooling calls roughly 3:1.
- **Housing stock:** ~60% of metro housing units were built before 1980, meaning legacy forced-air systems, older ductwork, and frequent replacement cycles.
- **Regulatory tailwind:** Ohio's adoption of refrigerant transition rules (A2L refrigerants, R-454B/R-32 systems) is pushing 2026–2027 replacement decisions — homeowners with R-410A systems facing repairs are increasingly opting for full replacement, which favors new entrants who can quote modern equipment confidently.
- **Competition:** The metro has 200+ licensed HVAC contractors, but the market is fragmented — the top 10 firms hold a minority share. Suburban corridors (Parma, Strongsville, Westlake, Mentor, Brunswick) are underserved relative to contractor density in the city core.

**Positioning opportunity:** Same-day emergency response + transparent flat-rate pricing + heat pump expertise. Heat pump adoption in Northeast Ohio is accelerating due to federal incentives, and few small contractors market it credibly.

**Licensing & compliance baseline (verify current requirements):**
- Ohio HVAC contractor license via the **Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board (OCILB)** — required for HVAC work.
- City of Cleveland contractor registration; separate registrations for many suburbs.
- EPA Section 608 certification (required for refrigerant handling).
- Ohio Bureau of Workers' Compensation coverage and general liability insurance (**$1M/$2M** typical minimum; expect **$2,400–$5,500/year** for a two-tech startup).

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Secure licensing:** File OCILB application, city/suburb registrations, EPA 608 certification, and BWC coverage. Budget 3–6 weeks for processing.
2. **Insurance & bonding:** Bind general liability ($1M/$2M) and commercial auto for two service vans.
3. **Entity & banking:** Form LLC in Ohio, obtain EIN, open business checking, set up merchant processing (expect 2.6–2.9% + $0.10 per swipe).
4. **Equipment:** Purchase or lease two used service vans ($12,000–$22,000 each used; $38,000–$55,000 new), recovery machines, gauges, vacuum pumps, and a starter parts inventory ($4,000–$8,000).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–8, overlapping)
5. **Google Business Profile:** Create and verify listing immediately — verification can take 1–3 weeks. Add service categories, hours, service area (Cuyahoga + adjacent counties), and photos.
6. **Website:** Launch 5–8 core pages plus programmatic suburb pages (Parma, Strongsville, Westlake, Mentor, Brunswick, Lakewood). Include click-to-call, online booking, and financing partner integration (e.g., Synchrony, GreenSky).
7. **Citations & reviews:** Build 30+ consistent directory listings; implement automated review requests post-service. Target 40+ Google reviews by end of Q1 2027.
8. **Google Ads:** Launch search campaigns targeting "furnace repair Cleveland," "AC repair near me," "heat pump installation Cleveland," and suburb variants. Start at $2,500/month ad spend, scaling to $6,000 in December–February.

### Phase 3 — Operations & Sales (Weeks 5–12)
9. **Dispatch software:** Implement Housecall Pro or Jobber ($100–$250/mo) with flat-rate pricing book.
10. **Pricing book:** Build flat-rate pricing for the top 25 jobs (diagnostic, capacitor replacement, blower motor, thermostat, tune-up, full replacement tiers).
11. **Financing:** Partner with a consumer financing provider — replacement jobs above $6,000 convert 20–35% better with monthly-payment options.
12. **Commercial outreach:** Target 20–30 small commercial accounts (restaurants, property managers, churches) with $1,800–$4,500/year PM contracts for recurring revenue.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Q1 2027+)
13. **Hire:** Add an EPA-certified tech at $28–$38/hour plus benefits once call volume exceeds ~6 jobs/day.
14. **Expand service area:** Add programmatic pages for Lorain, Medina, and Geauga counties.
15. **Review performance:** Reassess agency vs. freelancer mix at $75K/month revenue; consider in-house marketing coordinator.
