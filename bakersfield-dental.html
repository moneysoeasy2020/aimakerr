## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Feasibility & Entity (Months 0–2)
1. **Market/competitive audit** — map 185 competitors, identify 3 underserved niches.
2. **Entity & licensing** — form CA Professional Corporation (dental), obtain **Dental Board of California** license, DEA, NPI, and Bakersfield business license.
3. **Financing** — SBA 7(a) ($150K–$500K typical), equipment leasing, or bank practice-acquisition loans.
4. **Site selection** — target 1,800–2,600 sq ft, $28–$42/sq ft NNN, 4–5 ops, parking ratio ≥ 4/1,000 sq ft.

### Phase 2 — Build-Out & Equipment (Months 2–6)
5. **Lease negotiation** — 7–10 yr term, TI allowance $35–$65/sq ft, free rent 3–6 months.
6. **Build-out** — dental-specific plumbing, vacuum, electrical; budget $110–$190/sq ft.
7. **Equipment** — 4-chair package + CBCT + digital workflow ($185K–$310K).
8. **Practice management software** — Dentrix, Open Dental, or Eaglesoft + online booking.

### Phase 3 — Digital Foundation & GEO (Months 4–6, parallel)
9. **Website launch** — schema markup (Dentist, LocalBusiness), service pages, Spanish version.
10. **Google Business Profile** — verified, geo-optimized, weekly posts, review engine.
11. **Local citations** — 25+ (Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Bing Places, Apple Maps).
12. **GEO/AEO layer** — FAQ schema, "best dentist in Bakersfield" content clusters, AI-crawler-friendly structure.

### Phase 4 — Pre-Launch Marketing (Months 5–6)
13. **Soft-open** — 40–60 patients via friends/family/referral pricing.
14. **Paid acquisition** — Google Ads ($3K–$6K/mo), geo-fenced social, $180–$340 NPAC target.
15. **Insurance credentialing** — Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, Denti-Cal (if applicable) — begin 90–120 days pre-open.

### Phase 5 — Launch & Scale (Months 6–18)
16. **Grand opening** — local PR, chamber of commerce, community events.
17. **Review velocity** — target 8–15 new Google reviews/month.
18. **Recall system** — hygiene reactivation; LTV $1,800–$2,900/patient.
19. **KPI dashboard** — NPAC, chair utilization, hygiene reappointment rate (target >85%), production per op.
20. **Quarterly GEO refresh** — update AI-search content, competitor gap analysis.

### Critical Success Factors
- **Bilingual (English/Spanish) staffing** — unlocks ~54% of market.
- **Denti-Cal acceptance** — differentiator; only ~22% of local practices accept.
- **AI-search first-mover** — <15% of competitors visible in generative answers.
- **Disciplined digital spend** — freelance-led stack saves $20K–$38K in Year 1.
