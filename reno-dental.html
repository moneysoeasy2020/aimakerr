## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–3)
1. **Entity & licensing:** File Nevada LLC/PC ($425), obtain NV State Board of Dental Examiners license, DEA registration, and Reno business license ($200–$400).
2. **Site selection:** Target 1,800–2,400 sq ft in Spanish Springs or Damonte Ranch; negotiate **TI allowance of $35–$60/sq ft**.
3. **Financing:** Secure SBA 7(a) ($350K–$750K) plus equipment leasing; expect **8.5–10.5% APR** in Q4 2026.
4. **Digital foundation:** Register domain, build GBP profile, launch 10-page site with schema markup (Dentist, LocalBusiness, FAQPage).

### Phase 2 — Buildout & Digital Ramp (Months 3–6)
5. **Construction:** 90–120 day buildout; budget **$180–$280/sq ft** for dental-specific plumbing, vacuum, and electrical.
6. **Programmatic SEO:** Publish **40–60 service × neighborhood pages** (e.g., "emergency dentist in Spanish Springs," "Invisalign in South Meadows") — target **top-3 local pack** within 6 months.
7. **Content engine:** 2 blog posts/week targeting Reno dental keywords (CPC $9–$22); embed FAQ schema for AI Overview capture.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Patient Acquisition (Months 6–9)
8. **Soft open:** 60-day ramp with 200 pre-booked hygiene appointments from GBP + referrals.
9. **Paid acquisition:** $4,000–$6,000/mo Google Ads, geo-fenced to 5-mile radius; target **$220 PAC**.
10. **Review flywheel:** Automate post-visit review requests; aim for **75+ Google reviews at 4.8★** by month 9.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 9–12)
11. **Insurance credentialing:** Complete PPO panels (Delta, MetLife, Cigna) by month 8 — unlocks **40%+ patient volume**.
12. **Add service lines:** Implants, ortho, or sedation to lift **per-patient revenue 2.5–3.5×**.
13. **GEO/AI visibility:** Publish authoritative "cost of dental care in Reno" and "how to choose a dentist in Reno" content to capture ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overview citations.
