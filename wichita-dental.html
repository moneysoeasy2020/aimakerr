## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–6)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Register LLC in Kansas (~$160), obtain Kansas Dental Board license, DEA registration if prescribing, and NPI number.
2. **Location selection:** Target ZIPs 67206/67212/67230; negotiate **$28–$42/sq ft/yr** NNN for 1,800–2,500 sq ft medical-grade space.
3. **Build-out & equipment:** Budget **$350,000–$600,000** (4 operatories, CBCT, chairs, sterilization, IT).
4. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) loans at **~9–11%** (2026 Q4 est.); dental-specific lenders (Bank of America Practice Solutions, Live Oak) offer **80–100% financing**.
5. **Insurance credentialing:** Begin 90–120 days pre-opening with Delta Dental, BCBS KS, MetLife, Cigna.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 4–7)
6. **Website launch:** Conversion-first design; online booking (NexHealth, LocalMed); schema markup for LocalBusiness + Dentist.
7. **Google Business Profile:** Verify, complete 100% of fields, add 10+ photos, enable messaging and booking.
8. **Local citations:** Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Angi, BBB, Wichita Chamber — NAP consistency is non-negotiable.
9. **Review engine:** Automated post-visit SMS/email requests; target **25+ reviews in first 90 days**.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Months 6–12)
10. **Google Ads:** Start with **$2,500–$4,000/month** on high-intent terms ("emergency dentist Wichita," "dentist 67206"); expect **$180–$320 NPAC**.
11. **Local SEO:** GBP posts 2x/week; service-area landing pages; programmatic pages for "dentist in [Wichita neighborhood]" (target 40–80 pages).
12. **Content:** 4–6 blog posts/month targeting local long-tail ("cost of dental implants Wichita," "sedation dentist Wichita").
13. **Community:** Sponsor Wichita events, partner with local pediatricians and employers for referral flow.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 12–18)
14. **Track KPIs:** NPAC, cost per booked appointment, chair utilization, recall rate (target **>75%**), and review velocity.
15. **Expand services:** Add hygiene-driven recall program; introduce membership plan for uninsured (~22% of market).
16. **Reinvest:** Shift 20–30% of ad spend to SEO/content as organic rankings mature (organic NPAC drops to **$45–$110**).
