# Launching an HVAC Services Business in Oklahoma City, OK
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Oklahoma City's HVAC market is projected to generate **$310M–$340M in residential and light-commercial service revenue in 2026**, driven by a metro population of **1.48M** across 1,400+ square miles and a climate that swings from **summer highs of 95–100°F** to **winter lows of 25–30°F** — meaning your customers need both cooling and heating service within the same fiscal year. A typical residential service call in OKC prices at **$95–$150** (diagnostic), with common repairs at **$180–$650**, full system replacements at **$5,500–$12,000**, and commercial RTU replacements at **$12,000–$28,000**. Realistic startup capital for a licensed, insured HVAC operator in OKC ranges **$85,000–$180,000** (vehicle, tools, EPA 608 certification, state licensure, insurance, and 90 days of working capital).

The competitive landscape is favorable for a new entrant: OKC has roughly **280–320 licensed HVAC contractors** serving a metro of 1.48M people — a ratio of about **1 contractor per 4,600 residents**, versus a national average closer to 1 per 3,200. Your realistic Year 1 revenue target is **$280,000–$450,000** with a two-truck operation, and Year 1 marketing spend should land at **$18,000–$36,000 (6–9% of revenue)**. ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing:** File Oklahoma LLC ($100–$150), obtain EIN, apply for OKC business license, submit CIB mechanical contractor application, secure EPA 608 certification.
2. **Insurance:** Bind general liability ($1M/$2M) and workers' comp — budget $3,500–$7,000/year for a 2-person operation.
3. **Equipment:** Purchase/lease 2 service vans ($18,000–$35,000 used; $4,500–$7,000/mo lease new), recovery machines, gauges, vacuum pumps, brazing kits — budget $22,000–$40,000 total.
4. **Banking & payments:** Business checking + field payment processing (Square/ServiceTitan-class tools).

### Phase 2: Digital Build-Out (Weeks 3–7)
5. **Google Business Profile:** Create and fully optimize — categories, service areas (OKC, Edmond, Yukon, Moore, Norman, Midwest City), photos, Q&A. Target **25+ reviews in first 90 days**.
6. **Website:** 6–8 pages minimum — Home, Services (AC repair, heating repair, installation, maintenance plans, commercial), Service Areas, About, Contact, Financing. Include LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema markup.
7. **Programmatic local pages:** Build service-area pages for the 8–10 highest-value OKC suburbs — these capture "AC repair [city]" searches at near-zero marginal cost.
8. **Call tracking + CRM:** Attribute every lead to source from day one.

### Phase 3: Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)
9. **Google Ads:** Launch with $2,500–$4,000/mo on emergency-intent keywords ("AC repair OKC," "furnace repair near me"). Expect **$18–$45 cost per lead** in OKC HVAC; target 3:1 revenue-to-ad-spend by month 3.
10. **Review engine:** Automated post-service review requests; respond to 100% of reviews within 24 hours.
11. **Service agreements:** Sell $149–$249/year maintenance plans — these create recurring revenue and priority-call positioning.

### Phase 4: Scale (Months 4–12)
12. **Add truck 3** when utilization exceeds 75% for 60 consecutive days.
13. **Commercial push:** Target property managers and small retail — OKC commercial RTU work prices at **$12,000–$28,000** per replacement.
14. **Seasonal ad reallocation:** Shift 60% of budget to cooling ads in April; 60% to heating ads in October.
