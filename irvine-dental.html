## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Entity & Compliance Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Form the entity** — CA Professional Dental Corporation (PC) filing, ~$800–$1,500 with counsel; confirm DSO/CPOM compliance.
2. **Secure the space** — target 1,400–2,200 sq ft medical suite; negotiate 5-year term with TI allowance ($40–$80/sq ft typical in Irvine medical parks).
3. **Licensure & permits** — Dental Board of CA license, city business license, radiation machine registration, OSHA/HIPAA program docs.
4. **Build the digital identity layer** — domain, HIPAA-compliant hosting, NAP consistency across 40+ citations, Google Business Profile verification (start early — verification can take 2–4 weeks).

### Phase 2 — GEO & Website Build (Weeks 4–8)
5. **Website with generative-engine architecture** — FAQ schema, LocalBusiness/Dentist schema, service-page clusters, `llms.txt`, clean entity markup.
6. **Programmatic local pages** — 20–40 pages targeting Irvine neighborhoods (Woodbridge, Turtle Rock, Great Park, Northwood, University Park) × services (cleanings, implants, Invisalign, emergency).
7. **Review engine** — automated post-visit SMS/email requests; target **40+ Google reviews at 4.8★** by month 6.

### Phase 3 — Paid & Local Capture (Weeks 8–16)
8. **Google Ads** — launch on high-intent terms ("dentist Irvine," "emergency dentist Irvine," "Invisalign Irvine") with **$3,000–$6,000/mo** budget; expect $180–$420 CPA.
9. **Local Services Ads** — apply for Google Guaranteed badge; budget **$1,500–$3,500/mo**.
10. **Meta/Instagram retargeting** — $800–$1,500/mo for awareness in Irvine zip codes.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 5–12)
11. **Track GEO performance** — monitor AI Overview citations, ChatGPT referrals, and branded search lift monthly.
12. **Expand service lines** — add high-margin services (implants, ortho) once base is stable; each new service = new programmatic page cluster.
13. **Review quarterly** — re-benchmark CPA, LTV, and map-pack rank against Irvine competitors; reallocate budget to lowest-CPA channels.

**Month-12 targets:** 25–40 new patients/month, 40+ reviews at 4.8★+, top-3 map-pack rank for 3–5 core terms, and **15–25% of new-patient inquiries originating from generative/AI search**.
