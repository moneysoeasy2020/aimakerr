### Local Regulatory & Licensing Reality (verify before launch)

- **Maryland Home Improvement Commission (MHIC)** license required for home-improvement work above threshold — carpet cleaning is generally exempt, but **confirm scope** with MHIC; many operators still register for trust signal.
- **Baltimore City Minor Privilege / business license** and **Maryland SDAT** trade-name registration.
- **IICRC certification** (Carpet Cleaning Technician — CCT) strongly recommended: **$500–$900** course + exam; improves conversion and insurance rates.
- **General liability + commercial auto**: **$1,800–$3,400/year** for a one-van operator in Baltimore.
- **Wastewater discharge**: comply with Baltimore City DPW requirements for extraction water disposal.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register **Maryland SDAT trade name** + **Baltimore City business license** (~$100–$300).
2. Secure **MHIC scope confirmation**, **IICRC CCT certification**, and **GL + auto insurance** ($1,800–$3,400/yr).
3. Purchase equipment: **truck-mount or high-end portable** ($8,000–$18,000), chemicals ($600–$1,200), van wrap or magnetic signage ($400–$1,500).
4. Open **business bank account** and set up **Stripe/Square** for card-on-file deposits.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
5. Build **5–8 page website** with schema markup, service pages (pet odor, upholstery, tile/grout, commercial), and **neighborhood landing pages** for Canton, Fells Point, Federal Hill, Hampden, Towson, Dundalk.
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, 10+ photos, Q&A seeded).
7. Launch **review generation flow** (SMS post-job ask) targeting **40 reviews by day 90**.
8. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** + a capped **Google Ads** campaign ($1,200–$2,000/mo spend).

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 4–8)
9. List on **Thumbtack, Angi, Yelp** (budget $300–$600/mo) — track cost-per-booked-job weekly.
10. Build **B2B pipeline**: property managers, apartment complexes (Baltimore City renter density = high-value), offices, and Airbnb hosts.
11. Implement **CRM + booking** (Jobber or Housecall Pro, $50–$150/mo) with automated reminders and review requests.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 8–16)
12. Track **cost-per-booked-job monthly** — hold at **$95–$140**; cut channels above $180.
13. Add a **second van** once monthly revenue exceeds **$25K** and utilization >70%.
14. Publish **4 SEO/GEO posts/month** targeting Baltimore neighborhood + service queries to earn AI-search citations.
15. Reinvest in **review velocity** (target +15/month) — the single highest-ROI GEO lever in 2026 Q4.
