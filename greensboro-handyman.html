The average Greensboro homeowner now spends **$2,850–$4,200/year** on home maintenance and repairs (up ~9% year-over-year), and demand is concentrated in the **27403, 27408, 27410, and 27455 zip codes**, where aging 1960s–1990s housing stock drives recurring repair work. ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register LLC** with NC Secretary of State — **$125** filing fee.
2. **Obtain NC general contractor license** if projects exceed $30,000 (NC Licensing Board for General Contractors) — application **$100–$250**; exam prep **$300–$800**.
3. **Register for handyman work** under $30,000 — no state GC license required, but verify Guilford County and City of Greensboro permit thresholds per job type.
4. **Secure EIN** (free, IRS) and open a business bank account.
5. **Insurance**: General liability **$1,000–$2,500/year** ($1M/$2M coverage); workers' comp if hiring — **$800–$2,000/year** per employee.
6. **Business privilege license** — City of Greensboro, **$50–$150**.

### Phase 2: Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
1. **Google Business Profile**: Claim, verify, set service areas to Greensboro + 15-mile radius (Summerfield, Oak Ridge, Jamestown, McLeansville). Add 8–12 service categories. Seed 10 Q&A entries.
2. **Website**: 5–7 pages — Home, Services (Handyman, Drywall, Painting, Deck Repair, Gutter, Weatherization), Service Areas, About, Contact, Reviews. Target zip-code + service keyword pages.
3. **Review system**: Automate post-job SMS review requests; aim for **15–25 verified Google reviews in 60 days** (critical GEO ranking threshold in this metro).
4. **Citations**: Build 40+ directory listings (Yelp, Angi, Nextdoor, BBB, Thumbtack, Porch, local Greensboro directories).

### Phase 3: Lead Generation (Weeks 4–8)
1. **Google Local Services Ads** — pay-per-lead, **$18–$45/lead** in Greensboro; budget **$400–$800/month** initially.
2. **Nextdoor + neighborhood targeting** — free, high-conversion in Greensboro's established neighborhoods.
3. **Referral program** — **$50–$100 credit** per referred job; target real estate agents and property managers (Greensboro has ~40+ active property management firms).
4. **Partnerships**: Local hardware stores (Lowe's, Home Depot, Ace on Battleground/Lawndale) — leave cards, offer bundle discounts.

### Phase 4: Operations & Scaling (Weeks 6–12)
1. **Job scheduling software**: Jobber or Housecall Pro — **$49–$129/month**.
2. **Pricing**: Standardize a rate card — minimum call **$95–$125**, hourly **$75–$95**, project quotes with 15–20% material markup.
3. **Target metrics by Day 90**: 12–18 jobs completed, **$8,000–$14,000 gross revenue**, 15+ Google reviews, 4.7+ star average.
4. **Year 2 trigger**: At **$18,000+/month recurring revenue**, hire first W-2 helper and consider agency retainer for multi-service-area expansion.
