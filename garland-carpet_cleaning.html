# Launching a Carpet Cleaning Business in Garland, TX
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Garland, TX (Dallas County, ~240,000 residents, median household income ~$68,000) supports roughly **85–110 active carpet cleaning operators**, with typical residential jobs priced at **$180–$350 for 3 rooms** and **$0.28–$0.45 per sq ft** for whole-home steam cleaning. Startup capital ranges from **$8,500 (van + portable extractor)** to **$42,000 (truck-mount + full branding)**, with a realistic sweet spot of **$18,000–$24,000** for a solo operator targeting 8–12 jobs/week by month six.

Recurring demand is driven by Garland's aging housing stock (median home age ~40 years), high rental density (~38% renter-occupied), and a large multifamily/apartment corridor along I-635 and the George Bush Turnpike. A well-optimized operator can realistically hit **$9,000–$14,000/month gross by Q2 2027** at a **55–65% gross margin** after chemicals, fuel, and subcontract labor.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Weeks 1–4, ~$4,500)

1. **Entity & licensing:** Register Texas LLC ($300), obtain Garland business license ($50–$150), secure general liability insurance ($600–$1,200/yr).
2. **Equipment:** Truck-mount extractor (used, $6,000–$9,000) OR portable ($1,800–$3,200) + van ($4,000–$8,000 used). Chemicals + supplies: $600.
3. **Google Business Profile:** Claim and verify in week 1 — this is the #1 GEO asset. Add service area (Garland + 10-mile radius: Richardson, Rowlett, Sachse, Mesquite).
4. **Website:** 5 pages (Home, Services, Pricing, Service Area, Contact) with schema markup, local citations, and a booking form. Budget $900–$2,200 via freelancer.

### Phase 2 — Launch (Weeks 5–8, ~$2,000)

5. **Citations & directories:** Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB — 30 total. ($350–$700 freelancer)
6. **Review engine:** Automated SMS/email request post-job. Target **15 reviews by day 60** — this outranks 80% of Garland competitors.
7. **Google Ads:** $1,200–$2,000/mo on "carpet cleaning garland," "upholstery cleaning near me," "pet stain removal garland." Target $18–$35 cost-per-lead.

### Phase 3 — Scale (Months 3–6, ~$1,500/mo)

8. **B2B outreach:** Contact 40+ apartment complexes and property managers along I-635. Offer turnover pricing at $0.18–$0.22/sq ft.
9. **Programmatic SEO:** Build service-area landing pages for each ZIP (75040, 75041, 75042, 75043, 75044, 75045) + service-type pages (pet odor, tile & grout, upholstery).
10. **Retention:** 12-month reminder program — carpet cleaning repeats every 9–14 months. A 20% repeat rate stabilizes cash flow by Q2 2027.
