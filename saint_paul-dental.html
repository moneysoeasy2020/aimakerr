**Demand fundamentals (Saint Paul specific):**
- Population: ~**305,000** city / **552,000** Ramsey County
- Dental HPSA status: Ramsey County is **not** a full Health Professional Shortage Area, but low-income pediatric dental access remains designated — a strategic Medicaid opportunity
- Median household income (Saint Paul): **~$68,000** (below Twin Cities metro average — price positioning matters)
- Senior population (65+): **~13.5%** of city — growing fixed/removable prosthodontics demand
- Competitor density: **~1 dentist per 1,750 residents** in Ramsey County (near national average; differentiation via location + digital visibility is critical)

**2026 Q4 local dynamics to exploit:**
- **Medicaid/Medical Assistance reimbursement** for pediatric and adult dental remains a viable volume strategy in Saint Paul's East Side, Frogtown, and Dayton's Bluff neighborhoods
- **Delta Dental of Minnesota** and **HealthPartners** dominate local insurance networks — credentialing lead time is **90–150 days**, so begin pre-launch
- Neighborhoods with underserved dental access and rising household formation: **Highland Park, Macalester-Groveland, Payne-Phalen, and West Seventh**
- Google Business Profile "near me" dental searches in Saint Paul rose an estimated **18–24% YoY** (2024→2026) — local SEO is the highest-ROI acquisition channel

**Positioning recommendation:** Target **Highland Park or Macalester-Groveland** for fee-for-service/general family dentistry (higher household income, lower Medicaid mix), or **Payne-Phalen/East Side** for a Medicaid-heavy volume model with pediatric focus.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)**
1. Form **MN LLC** ($155 filing) + secure EIN; register DBA if needed
2. Obtain **Minnesota Board of Dentistry** license ($200–$400) and **DEA registration** ($888, 3-year)
3. Secure location lease (target $22–$38/sq ft NNN) and begin buildout ($95–$220/sq ft)
4. Initiate **Delta Dental MN / HealthPartners / Medicaid** credentialing (90–150 days)
5. Engage verified freelance web/SEO expert — launch site + Google Business Profile

**Phase 2 — Buildout & Systems (Months 3–7)**
6. Equipment procurement: chairs, imaging, sterilization — budget **$85,000–$210,000**
7. Deploy practice management + clearinghouse software ($400–$1,100/mo)
8. Hire: 1 hygienist, 1–2 dental assistants, 1 front-office coordinator
9. Set up **review-generation workflow** (target 25+ Google reviews by opening)

**Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 6–18)**
10. Soft-open with insurance network listings live
11. Run **local SEO + modest Google Ads** ($600–$1,200/mo management) targeting "dentist Saint Paul" + neighborhood modifiers
12. Target **$85–$180 cost per new patient**; scale to 1,200–1,800 active patients by Month 18
13. Reinvest in content/GBP reviews monthly; track break-even at ~**$650–$850 revenue per active patient/year**

**Phase 4 — Optimization (Month 18+)**
14. Add service lines (ortho, implants) based on patient mix
15. Evaluate second location or associate hire once **>1,800 active patients** and positive EBITDA sustained 2 quarters
