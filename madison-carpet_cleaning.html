## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Register LLC with WI DFI ($130), obtain seller's permit, and confirm City of Madison Public Health / DATCP requirements for cleaning services.
2. **Insurance:** General liability ($500K–$1M) + commercial auto + bonding — budget **$1,800–$3,200/year**.
3. **Equipment decision:** Used truck-mount ($8K–$16K) vs. portable ($2.5K–$5K). Truck-mount wins on ticket size and speed; portable lowers entry risk.
4. **Brand & domain:** Secure `.com` with "carpet" + "madison" keyword adjacency; set up Google Business Profile immediately.

### Phase 2 — Digital GEO Build (Weeks 3–6)
5. **Website with schema:** LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ schema; dedicated pages for *carpet cleaning Madison*, *pet stain removal*, *apartment move-out cleaning*, *commercial carpet cleaning*.
6. **Programmatic service-area pages:** 15–25 pages targeting Madison zip codes (53703, 53711, 53715, 53719) and suburbs (Sun Prairie, Fitchburg, Verona, Middleton, Waunakee) — each with unique local proof points.
7. **AEO content blocks:** Q&A-formatted sections answering "How much does carpet cleaning cost in Madison?" and "How long does carpet take to dry?" — these feed AI Overviews and ChatGPT/Perplexity citations.

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 6–10)
8. **Google Local Services Ads + GBP:** LSA is highest-intent for home services; pair with 3–5 GBP posts/week and photo uploads.
9. **Review engine:** Automate SMS review requests at job completion; target **40+ reviews in 90 days** to enter Madison's top-5 map pack.
10. **Partnerships:** Sign 3–5 property management firms and 2–3 apartment complexes for recurring turnover work — this stabilizes Q4–Q1 cash flow.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 10–16)
11. **Track cost-per-booked-job** by channel (target: LSA $28–$55, organic $12–$25).
12. **Add upsells:** Scotchgard ($45–$85), pet enzyme treatment ($35–$70), upholstery ($120–$280/sofa set).
13. **Reinvest:** At $18K+/mo revenue, hire first technician and add second van; revisit agency vs. freelancer decision at the $1M revenue mark.
