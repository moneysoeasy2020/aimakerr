# Launching an HVAC Services Business in Long Beach, CA

The city's **2026 Title 24 / CALGreen-aligned retrofit incentives** and SCE heat-pump rebates (typically **$1,000–$3,500 per qualifying install**) are pushing replacement demand toward high-efficiency systems.
