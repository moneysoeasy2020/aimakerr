### Licensing Reality Check (Critical)
Florida requires a **state Certified or Registered Contractor license** for work over **$2,500** (labor + materials) or structural/electrical/plumbing/gas work. A **handyman exemption** exists for jobs under $2,500 that don't touch permitted trades. Plan your service menu around this threshold or secure the license — this is the #1 compliance trap in Tampa.

### Agency vs. Freelance: The Honest Breakdown

**Brick-and-mortar agency ($1,800–$3,500/mo retainer):**
- ✅ Single point of contact, coordinated deliverables
- ✅ Team redundancy if one person leaves
- ❌ 40–60% of your spend covers overhead, account managers, and sales commissions
- ❌ 12-month lock-in contracts common; slow iteration
- ❌ Generic templates reused across clients

**Verified freelance experts ($35–$85/hr, project or $400–$1,200/mo):**
- ✅ Direct access to the person doing the work
- ✅ 2–4× faster turnaround on changes
- ✅ 75–82% lower cost for identical output quality
- ❌ You must vet and manage them (use platform verification badges + portfolio review)
- ❌ No team redundancy — document everything

**Verdict for a sub-$15K startup:** Freelance-first is the correct 2026 Q4 play. Reserve agency spend until you're clearing **$25K/month** and need strategic account management.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2) | Budget: $1,100–$1,800
1. **Form LLC** with Florida Sunbiz — **$125** filing fee.
2. **Secure licensing**: Register with DBPR (Registered Contractor) or confirm handyman exemption scope.
3. **General liability insurance**: **$85–$180/month** for $1M/$2M coverage (essential for Tampa storm-related work).
4. **Business bank account + payment processor** (Stripe/Square): **$0–$25/mo**.
5. **Domain + hosting**: **$15–$40/mo** (Cloudflare Pages or SiteGround).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–4) | Budget: $1,900–$3,430
6. **Launch 5–7 page website** via verified freelancer — lead with a **"Free Estimate in 24 Hours"** CTA.
7. **Google Business Profile**: verify, add 15+ real project photos, set service radius to **25 miles** (covers Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel).
8. **Programmatic service-area pages**: one page per ZIP/neighborhood (e.g., "Handyman in Hyde Park," "Contractor in South Tampa") — target **20 pages** at launch.
9. **Review engine**: automated SMS request post-job (target: **25+ reviews in 90 days**).

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 3–6) | Budget: $400–$900/mo
10. **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, **$28–$65/lead** in Tampa): highest-ROI channel for handymen.
11. **Nextdoor + Facebook local groups**: free, high-conversion in Tampa neighborhoods.
12. **Referral program**: **$50 credit** per referred job — drives 30–40% of Month 3+ volume.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 2–4)
13. **Add a second crew/helper** at **$22–$28/hr** once bookings exceed **25 jobs/month**.
14. **Upsell maintenance plans**: **$149–$299/quarter** recurring contracts (huge for snowbird absentee owners).
15. **Track CAC vs. LTV**: target CAC **<$85**, LTV **>$1,200** per customer.
