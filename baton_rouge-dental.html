## 2026 Q4 Baton Rouge Market Analysis

Baton Rouge's dental market supports roughly **310–340 active dental practices** (general + specialty) serving an estimated metro population of **~875,000** across East Baton Rouge, Ascension, Livingston, and Iberville parishes. The metro's dental spend is projected at **$310M–$340M annually** in 2026, with per-patient average annual outlay of **$480–$620** for insured adults and **$210–$290** for uninsured. A new general practice entering the market should model **1,800–2,600 active patients by month 24** to reach break-even, with a target **$1.1M–$1.6M gross production** by year three at a **$165–$240 average production per visit** and **2.1–2.6 visits per patient per year**.

Realistic startup capital for a **3–4 operatory, 1,600–2,200 sq ft** clinic in Baton Rouge runs **$680,000–$1,150,000** all-in (build-out at **$175–$260/sq ft**, equipment **$220,000–$380,000**, working capital reserve **$90,000–$140,000**). Lease rates in high-traffic corridors (Perkins Rowe, Bluebonnet/Coursey, Siegen Lane, Airline Hwy) run **$28–$42/sq ft NNN**; secondary corridors (Florida Blvd, Jones Creek, O'Neal) run **$18–$26/sq ft NNN**. Dental insurance credentialing with Delta Dental, Blue Cross LA, and United Concordia typically takes **60–120 days**, and Medicaid dental (Louisiana Dental Association–tracked) reimburses at **$38–$72 per procedure** — viable only as a volume supplement, not a primary payer mix.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing:** File Louisiana LLC ($100), obtain LA State Board of Dentistry license, DEA registration, and CLIA waiver if applicable.
2. **Location:** Secure letter of intent in a corridor with **≥28,000 vehicles/day** (LA DOTD traffic counts) and **≥1.4 dentists per 1,000 residents** in the 3-mile radius.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) at **9.5–11.5%** (2026 Q4 estimate) or equipment leasing at **7–9%**.
4. **Digital foundation:** Domain, HIPAA-compliant hosting, Google Business Profile, and 40+ directory citations.

### Phase 2 — Build-Out (Months 4–8)
5. **Construction:** Permit through EBR Parish (4–8 weeks), ADA-compliant build-out, dental vacuum/compressor infrastructure.
6. **Equipment:** Chairs, CBCT, intraoral scanners, sterilization — budget **$220K–$380K**.
7. **Insurance credentialing:** Start **90 days before opening** — this is the #1 cash-flow killer for new clinics.

### Phase 3 — Launch (Months 8–10)
8. **Soft open** with 60–90 pre-booked patients from a **$3,500–$6,000** pre-launch ad spend.
9. **Google Ads:** Target "dentist near me," "emergency dentist Baton Rouge," "teeth cleaning [zip]" — expect **$9–$18 CPC** and **8–14% conversion**.
10. **Review engine:** Automate post-visit review requests; target **40+ Google reviews in 90 days**.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 10–24)
11. **SEO compounding:** Publish **2 posts/month** targeting Baton Rouge + parish long-tail ("dentist in Prairieville," "emergency dentist near LSU").
12. **Recall system:** 6-month hygiene recall automation — this drives **55–65% of lifetime patient value**.
13. **Payer mix optimization:** Cap Medicaid at **≤15%** of appointments; grow PPO and fee-for-service.
