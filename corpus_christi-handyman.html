## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with **Texas Secretary of State** ($300).
2. Obtain **EIN** (free) and open business banking.
3. Confirm **TDLR** requirements for scope of work (handyman vs. regulated trades — electrical/plumbing/HVAC require licensed subcontractors).
4. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** + commercial auto — budget **$1,800–$3,600/year**.
5. Register with **City of Corpus Christi** for applicable permits; check **Nueces County** and HOA rules for submarkets like Padre Island.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
6. Launch 5–8 page website with service + neighborhood pages (Calallen, Flour Bluff, Padre Island, Portland, etc.).
7. Create and verify **Google Business Profile** — complete categories, service areas, hours, photos (10+), and Q&A.
8. Build **25–40 local citations** (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Chamber of Commerce).
9. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** (background-checked badge) — budget **$500–$1,200/mo** initially.

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation & Reviews (Weeks 4–8)
10. Launch a **review-generation SOP**: request review within 24 hrs of job completion; target **25 reviews in 90 days**.
11. Deploy **call tracking** + a simple CRM (e.g., Jobber/Housecall Pro) for scheduling and invoicing.
12. Run **$300–$600/mo** geo-targeted Google Search ads on high-intent terms ("handyman Corpus Christi," "drywall repair near me").

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 8–12)
13. Add **programmatic SEO pages** for service × neighborhood combinations (e.g., "fence repair Flour Bluff").
14. Introduce **maintenance plans** ($99–$199/quarter) for recurring revenue.
15. Track **cost per booked job** — target **$35–$75** via LSA/GBP vs. **$120–$250** via paid search.
16. Reinvest 15–20% of gross revenue into reviews, content, and ads through Q1 2027.
