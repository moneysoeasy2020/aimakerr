## 2026 Q4 Oakland Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers
- **Housing stock age:** Oakland's median single-family home was built in the 1920s–1950s. Deferred maintenance, seismic retrofit demand (soft-story ordinances), and ADU conversions are the three highest-margin service lines.
- **Rental density:** ~55% renter-occupied units means a large recurring base for property-management contracts (turnover repairs, punch lists).
- **Seismic retrofit mandate:** Oakland's Soft-Story Retrofit Program continues to generate compliance work through the late 2020s — a high-ticket niche ($8,000–$25,000 per project).
- **ADU pipeline:** Alameda County ADU permits remain elevated; handyman-adjacent finish work (trim, fixtures, punch lists) is steady downstream demand.

### Competitive Landscape
- **Fragmentation:** Hundreds of sole proprietors; few operators have professional digital presence. A clean Google Business Profile with 25+ reviews reliably ranks in the local map pack.
- **Licensing split:** Handyman work under **$500 per job (labor + materials)** does not require a CSLB license in California; anything above requires a licensed contractor (B-General Building or specialty). Price and scope your jobs accordingly.
- **Seasonality:** Q4 is punch-list, weatherization, and pre-holiday repair season — strong for indoor work; exterior painting drops off.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (90 Days)

### Phase 1: Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity formation:** Register LLC with California Secretary of State (~$70 filing).
2. **CSLB license:** If scoping jobs over $500, obtain B-General Building license (application ~$450, exam, fingerprinting). Timeline: 6–12 weeks — start immediately.
3. **Business license:** City of Oakland business tax certificate (~$100–$300 based on gross receipts).
4. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) ~$800–$1,800/year; commercial auto if using a work vehicle.
5. **Contractor bond:** $25,000 CSLB bond (~$200–$400/year premium).

### Phase 2: Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
6. **Google Business Profile:** Claim and fully optimize — categories, service areas (Oakland, Piedmont, Alameda, Berkeley border), photos, hours. This is your #1 lead source.
7. **Website:** 5 pages minimum — Home, Services, Service Areas, About, Contact/Quote. Include schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service) for GEO/AI-search visibility.
8. **Review engine:** Set up automated post-job review requests. Target 25 reviews in 90 days.
9. **Local citations:** Yelp, Nextdoor, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB — consistent NAP (name, address, phone).

### Phase 3: Lead Generation (Weeks 4–10)
10. **Google Local Services Ads:** Apply (requires license + insurance verification). Budget $20–$50/day initially.
11. **Property manager outreach:** Target 10–15 small Oakland property managers with a one-page rate sheet and turnaround guarantee.
12. **Nextdoor + neighborhood targeting:** Post in 5–10 Oakland neighborhood groups; offer a launch discount for first 20 jobs.

### Phase 4: Operations & Scaling (Weeks 8–12)
13. **Job management:** Use Jobber or Housecall Pro (~$50–$150/mo) for scheduling, invoicing, and review automation.
14. **Pricing discipline:** Enforce the $150–$250 minimum call. Track blended hourly rate weekly; target $95+.
15. **Reinvest:** At $10K/month gross, allocate 8–12% to marketing — prioritize GBP optimization and review velocity over paid ads.
