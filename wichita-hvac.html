## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Pre-launch (Months 0–2)**
1. Secure **Kansas HVAC contractor licensing** (KS requires a license through the State of Kansas) and confirm Wichita/Sedgwick County permitting and EPA 608 certification for refrigerant handling.
2. Register LLC, obtain **$1M general liability + commercial auto** (Wichita premiums ~$1,800–$3,600/yr for a small HVAC shop).
3. Secure a service vehicle, recovery equipment, and a starter parts inventory (**$12,000–$22,000**).
4. Build the digital base: website + Google Business Profile + call tracking (budget per matrix above).

**Launch (Months 2–4)**
5. Launch with a **maintenance-plan offer** ($14–$19/mo) to lock recurring revenue and generate review velocity.
6. Run **geo-targeted Google Ads** on emergency-intent keywords ("AC repair Wichita," "furnace not heating") — cap at **$1,500–$2,500/mo** initially and track cost-per-booked-call.
7. Publish **programmatic service-area pages** for Wichita + 6–8 surrounding cities with unique local content (permit notes, response times, local rebates).

**Scale (Months 4–12)**
8. Hire your first licensed tech once booked-call volume sustains **$18k–$25k/mo revenue**; add a second van at **$40k+/mo**.
9. Layer **commercial RTU service contracts** in Q1–Q2 (off-peak) to smooth the seasonal revenue curve.
10. Reinvest **8–12% of revenue into marketing**, prioritizing GBP reviews, local SEO, and retargeting.
