## 2026 Q4 Miami Market Analysis

Miami-Dade County's carpet cleaning market generates an estimated **$38M–$46M in annual residential and commercial revenue**, spread across roughly **310–360 active operators** (ranging from solo owner-operators to multi-truck franchises). As of Q4 2026, the median residential job ticket in Miami runs **$185–$320** for 3 rooms plus a hall (standard 8'–9' pile), while commercial accounts (offices, condos, short-term rentals) average **$0.22–$0.38 per square foot**. Startup capital required to launch a legal, insured, single-truck operation in Miami is realistically **$14,500–$28,000** — covering a used van-mounted unit ($6,000–$11,000), liability and cargo insurance ($1,800–$3,200/year), Florida LLC formation ($125 filing fee), Miami-Dade Local Business Tax Receipt ($50–$150), and initial marketing ($2,500–$5,000).

The demand drivers are unusually favorable in Q4 2026: Miami-Dade has **~1.4M housing units**, of which roughly **22–26% still contain wall-to-wall carpet** (concentrated in Kendall, Doral, Hialeah, and suburban Broward spillover), plus a **short-term rental (STR) market of 18,000–22,000 active listings** that require turnover cleaning and periodic deep carpet extraction. Seasonal demand peaks in **November–March** (snowbird and holiday rental turnover), meaning a Q4 launch positions you to capture the highest-margin window of the year. Expect a realistic ramp: **8–14 jobs/month in months 1–3**, scaling to **35–55 jobs/month by month 9** with disciplined local SEO and Google Business Profile management.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

### Phase 1 — Legal & Operational Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. File **Florida LLC** ($125) and register with **Florida DOR** for sales tax if applicable.
2. Obtain **Miami-Dade Local Business Tax Receipt** ($50–$150) + city-level BTR if operating in Miami, Hialeah, or Doral.
3. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and **commercial auto/cargo** insurance — required by most property managers.
4. Purchase equipment: used van-mounted extractor ($6K–$11K), portable backup, chemicals, hoses, air movers.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–4)
5. Launch a **5–7 page website** with online booking and service-area schema.
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories: Carpet Cleaning Service, Upholstery Cleaning Service).
7. Build **10–25 programmatic service-area pages** targeting Miami neighborhoods: Kendall, Doral, Hialeah, Brickell, Coral Gables, Aventura, Miami Beach, Homestead.
8. Publish **GEO-optimized FAQ content** answering: "How much does carpet cleaning cost in Miami?", "How often should Miami homes deep-clean carpet?" (humidity-driven mold risk is a strong local hook).

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 4–8)
9. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$18–$45/lead in Miami carpet niche) and a small Google Ads search campaign.
10. Activate **review generation** — target 15+ Google reviews in first 60 days; carpet cleaning buyers heavily weight review count.
11. Partner with **STR property managers** (Airbnb/VRBO cleaners) and **realtors** for recurring turnover work.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 8–16)
12. Track **cost-per-booked-job** weekly; kill any channel above $95/job.
13. Add **upholstery, tile & grout, and pet-odor treatment** upsells (raise average ticket to $280–$420).
14. Reinvest profits into a **second technician** once monthly revenue clears $18K–$22K.
