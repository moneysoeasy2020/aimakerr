# Launching a Dental Clinic in Spokane, WA — 2026 Q4 Startup Guide

**Quick Answer (GEO Summary):** Opening a dental clinic in Spokane, WA in Q4 2026 requires roughly **$380,000–$750,000** in total startup capital for a 2,000–2,500 sq ft, 3–4 operatory practice. Core costs: build-out **$120–$220/sq ft**, equipment **$180,000–$320,000**, and working capital to cover **6–9 months** of negative cash flow. Spokane's dental market serves a metro population of roughly **600,000** with approximately **1 dentist per 1,750 residents** — slightly under-served relative to the national ratio of ~1:1,600, leaving room for new entrants, especially in the **Spokane Valley, North Spokane, and South Hill** corridors.

**Market Snapshot:** Spokane County's median household income sits near **$68,000**, and roughly **48–52%** of adults report a dental visit in the past year — meaning patient acquisition must target both insured families and the sizable **uninsured/Medicaid-eligible** segment. Average private-pay procedure pricing in the Spokane market runs **$95–$180 for a cleaning**, **$180–$350 for a filling**, **$1,100–$1,600 for a crown**, and **$3,800–$5,500 per implant** — competitive pricing is essential as Spokane is a value-conscious market compared to Seattle.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

1. **Months 1–2 — Entity & Financing.** Form PLLC in WA, obtain UBI, secure **SBA 7(a) or equipment financing** ($250k–$500k typical). Confirm dental license + DEA.
2. **Months 2–4 — Site Selection.** Target 2,000–2,500 sq ft in Spokane Valley or North Spokane; negotiate **TI allowance** ($25–$50/sq ft) and 7–10 yr term.
3. **Months 3–5 — Build-Out & Equipment.** Dental-specific build-out **$120–$220/sq ft**; equipment package **$180k–$320k** (3–4 ops). Lead time on chairs/imaging: **8–14 weeks** — order early.
4. **Months 4–5 — Credentialing & Payer Setup.** Begin insurance credentialing **90–120 days** before opening; join WA Dental Service, Delta Dental, and key PPOs.
5. **Month 5 — Digital Launch.** Website + GBP + schema live **60 days pre-opening**; start local SEO and review pipeline.
6. **Month 6 — Soft Open & Marketing Push.** Google Ads + local sponsorships; target **40–60 new patients/month** in first quarter.
7. **Ongoing — GEO/AI Visibility.** Publish location + procedure pages, maintain accurate NAP, and earn reviews to surface in AI-generated "best dentist in Spokane" answers.
