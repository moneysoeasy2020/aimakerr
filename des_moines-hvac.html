## 2026 Q4 Des Moines Market Analysis

Des Moines is a mid-sized Midwest metro of roughly **220,000 residents** (about **710,000** in the greater metro), with a housing stock skewed toward homes built between **1950 and 1990** — a profile that drives steady furnace replacement, AC retrofits, and ductwork repair demand. The metro's utility landscape is dominated by **MidAmerican Energy**, and Iowa's four-season climate produces a hard split in demand: heating calls peak **November–February** and cooling calls peak **June–August**, with shoulder-season revenue dropping 30–40% unless you sell maintenance agreements.

A realistic 2026 Q4 revenue model for a new HVAC operator in Des Moines: **2–3 trucks** generating **$18,000–$35,000/month each** in gross service revenue. Typical ticket prices in the metro as of Q4 2026: **diagnostic/service call $99–$149**, **residential furnace replacement $4,200–$9,500 installed**, **central AC replacement $3,800–$8,200 installed**, and **maintenance agreements $14–$24/month**. Startup capital for a lean launch (used van, core tools, EPA 608 certification, insurance, bonding, and licensing) runs **$28,000–$65,000**; a two-truck launch with a wrapped vehicle and paid lead gen runs **$85,000–$140,000**. Des Moines' lower cost of living versus national metros means labor is **15–20% cheaper** than the U.S. average, but customer price sensitivity is real — expect to compete on trust and speed, not on premium branding.

### Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts — Explicit Breakdown

**Local Des Moines agencies** offer accountability, in-person meetings, and bundled teams (SEO + ads + design under one roof). The trade-off: **30–60% overhead markup**, slower turnaround on small requests, and long minimum contracts (often 6–12 months). Best for operators who want zero management overhead and have $30K+ to deploy.

**Verified freelance experts** (vetted via portfolio, references, and platform verification) deliver the same deliverables at **40–60% lower cost**, with faster iteration and direct communication. The trade-off: you must manage multiple contractors and verify credentials yourself. Best for lean launches where every dollar of ad spend matters.

**Recommendation for a 2026 Q4 Des Moines launch:** Hybrid model — hire a verified freelancer for website + GBP + local SEO ($3,500–$6,000 setup, $900–$1,400/mo), and manage Google Ads in-house or via a freelancer with HVAC-specific proof. Reserve agency spend until you exceed $75K/month revenue.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with **Iowa Secretary of State** (~$50).
2. Obtain **Iowa HVAC contractor licensing** through the state plumbing/mechanical board and confirm **Polk County** local permits.
3. Secure **EPA 608 certification** for refrigerant handling (required).
4. Purchase **general liability + commercial auto insurance** ($2,400–$4,800/yr for a 2-truck operation).
5. Open a business bank account and set up a **Merchant services** account for card-on-site payments.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 3–6)
6. Launch a GEO-optimized website with **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**, service-area pages for Des Moines, West Des Moines, Ankeny, Urbandale, and Johnston.
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, photos, Q&A).
8. Set up **call tracking** and a CRM (e.g., ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber).
9. Deploy **Google Ads** Local Services + Search campaigns targeting $6–$14 CPC keywords.
10. Build review-generation workflow (SMS post-service) — target **50 reviews in 90 days**.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Capture Heating Season (Weeks 6–10)
11. Launch with a **"$99 furnace tune-up"** loss-leader to fill the calendar in November.
12. Run **direct mail + neighborhood door hangers** in 3 target ZIP codes (50131, 50265, 50311).
13. Publish 2 GEO-optimized blog posts/week ("furnace replacement cost Des Moines," "why is my furnace short-cycling in Iowa winters").
14. Track cost-per-lead weekly; kill ad groups above **$120 CPL**.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Stabilize (Months 3–6)
15. Add a second truck once monthly revenue exceeds **$30,000**.
16. Launch **maintenance agreements** to smooth shoulder-season revenue.
17. Expand service-area pages to 10+ Des Moines suburbs (programmatic SEO).
18. Review agency vs. freelancer performance against the cost matrix above and reallocate budget quarterly.
