## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Operations (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with **Maryland SDAT** ($100 filing fee).
2. Obtain **Baltimore City Trader's License** ($55–$125) and **Maryland Home Improvement Commission** registration if offering installation.
3. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and **commercial auto** insurance: **$2,400–$4,200/year**.
4. Set up **IICRC certification** (CCT or WRT): **$500–$1,200**.
5. Choose equipment: used truck-mount ($9K–$22K) or portable ($2.5K–$5.5K).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
6. Launch a **5–8 page website** with service-area pages for **Canton, Fells Point, Federal Hill, Hampden, Mount Vernon, Towson, Dundalk, Glen Burnie**.
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (10+ photos, service list, Q&A, booking link).
8. Deploy **booking software** (Housecall Pro, Jobber, or Launch27): **$40–$100/month**.
9. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** ($300–$800/month budget) — highest-intent lead source in Baltimore.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 3–8)
10. Run **Google Ads** at $1,200–$2,500/month targeting "carpet cleaning [neighborhood]" long-tail.
11. Launch **review generation** workflow (target 25+ Google reviews in 90 days).
12. Build **programmatic neighborhood landing pages** — 1 per zip code (21224, 21230, 21231, 21211, 21218, 21207, 21222, 21225).
13. Outreach to **property managers** (Baltimore has ~140 active PM firms) and **Airbnb hosts** for recurring contracts.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–9)
14. Add **commercial B2B outreach** in October–November for Q4 contract renewals.
15. Layer **retargeting + email** to past customers (recurring 6-month reminder).
16. Hire first technician at **$18–$22/hour** once monthly job volume exceeds 45.
