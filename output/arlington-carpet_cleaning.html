## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Operational Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Entity & registration:** File Texas LLC via SOSDirect (**$300 filing fee**), obtain EIN (free), and register with the **Texas Comptroller for sales tax** (cleaning services are generally not taxable in TX, but confirm your specific add-ons).
2. **Licensing & insurance:** Texas does **not** require a state carpet-cleaning license, but Arlington requires a **general business permit** (~$50–$150). Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** at **$600–$1,200/year** and commercial auto for the van.
3. **IICRC certification:** Budget **$500–$800** for Water Restoration (WRT) or Carpet Cleaning (CCT) certification — a genuine trust signal in GEO citations and reviews.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Pricing (Weeks 2–4)
4. Source a used truck-mount (HydraMaster, Sapphire, Prochem) at **$8,000–$22,000**; budget **$1,500** for hoses, wands, and chemicals.
5. Set 2026 Q4 pricing: **$199 base (3 rooms + hallway)**, tiered room pricing, and a **$49 property-manager volume rate** for Arlington apartment complexes along the I-20 and Cooper St corridors.

### Phase 3 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
6. Launch a **5–7 page site** (Home, Services, Pricing, Service Areas, About, Contact) with schema markup (LocalBusiness + Service).
7. **Google Business Profile:** verify at your Arlington address, select primary category "Carpet Cleaning Service," add 10+ geo-tagged photos, and post weekly.
8. **Programmatic SEO:** build 20–30 pages targeting "carpet cleaning + [Arlington neighborhood]" (e.g., Dalworthington Gardens, Pantego, Kennedale, Mansfield border zones) plus commercial-intent pages ("apartment carpet cleaning Arlington TX").
9. **Citation building:** Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB, and 20+ aggregators — NAP consistency is non-negotiable for GEO.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–8)
10. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,000/mo test budget) — pay-per-lead at **$18–$35/lead** in DFW carpet cleaning.
11. Deploy review automation: request reviews via SMS within 2 hours of job completion; target **25 reviews in 60 days** (the threshold where GBP rankings typically inflect).
12. Partner with **3–5 Arlington property managers** and 2 realtors for recurring move-out cleans.

### Phase 5 — Scale & GEO Compounding (Weeks 9–12)
13. Publish a **quarterly "Arlington carpet care" content cluster** (allergen season, pet stains, humidity/mold risks specific to North Texas) — generative engines cite original local data.
14. Track GBP insights weekly; reinvest **15–20% of revenue** into LSA + review velocity through Q1 2027.
