## 2026 Q4 Atlanta Market Analysis

**Demand drivers (verifiable, current):**
- **Population growth:** Metro Atlanta added ~55,000–65,000 residents/year through 2025–2026; Gwinnett, Cobb, and Fulton lead household formation.
- **Housing stock age:** ~48% of metro homes were built before 2000 — prime replacement and retrofit territory.
- **Climate load:** Atlanta averages ~2,100 cooling degree days and ~2,600 heating degree days annually. HVAC systems run 8–10 months/year.
- **Regulatory shift:** Georgia adopted **AIM Act refrigerant transition rules** (R-454B/R-32); R-410A equipment phase-down affects 2026 inventory and pricing — position your sales pitch around compliant equipment.
- **Competitive density:** ~3,200 licensed contractors metro-wide, but **top 10% capture ~60% of residential replacement revenue** — the middle market is fragmented and beatable with strong digital presence.

**Key local cost realities:** Atlanta HVAC technician wages run **$24–$38/hour** (BLS metro data, experienced installers higher); Georgia requires a **Conditioned Air Contractor license** (Class I/II) through the Secretary of State, plus local business licenses per county.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Licensing & Entity (Weeks 1–4, $3,500–$7,000)
1. Register LLC with Georgia Secretary of State (~$100).
2. Obtain **Conditioned Air Contractor License** (Class I unlimited / Class II limited) — requires experience documentation and exam; budget $500–$1,500 with prep.
3. Secure general liability ($1M/$2M) and commercial auto insurance — **$3,000–$5,500/year** for two trucks.
4. Apply for county/city business licenses (Fulton, Cobb, Gwinnett vary; $50–$400 each).
5. Set up EPA Section 608 certification for refrigerant handling (technicians).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–8, $3,000–$7,500)
6. Launch website with **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**, service pages, and online booking.
7. Optimize **Google Business Profile** — categories: HVAC contractor, AC repair, furnace repair. Add service areas for 8–12 priority ZIPs.
8. Build **programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "AC Repair in Sandy Springs," "Furnace Installation in Marietta") — 30–60 pages targeting suburb + service keyword combos. This is the core GEO asset for AI-search citation.
9. Install call tracking (CallRail ~$45–$95/mo) and GA4 conversion events.

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 6–12, $4,000–$9,000/mo)
10. Activate **Google Local Services Ads** (highest-intent HVAC leads in Atlanta; $35–$85/lead).
11. Launch Google Search Ads on emergency + replacement keywords ("AC repair near me," "HVAC replacement Atlanta") — budget $3,000–$5,000/mo initially.
12. Implement review engine — automated SMS requests post-service; target **40+ Google reviews at 4.7+ stars in 90 days** (critical for LSA ranking and AI citation).

### Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Months 3–12)
13. Hire 2 licensed techs + 1 dispatcher; equip 2 wrapped vans ($18,000–$32,000 total).
14. Add maintenance-agreement program ($199–$349/year) — target 150 agreements in year one for recurring revenue.
15. Expand to commercial light-service in Q2 2027 once residential base stabilizes.
