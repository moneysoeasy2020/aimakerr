## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

**Phase 0 — Pre-Launch (Weeks 1–2)**
1. Register LLC with Texas SOS (~$300), obtain Lubbock business license, and secure **general liability + commercial auto insurance** ($1,200–$2,400/yr).
2. Confirm equipment: truck-mount vs. portable. Lean launch = **portable/extraction unit ($2,500–$6,000)** + chemicals + van wrap ($1,500–$3,500).
3. Open business bank account and set pricing tiers ($180 / $240 / $320 for 3/4/5 rooms).

**Phase 1 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)**
4. Build a **5–8 page GEO-structured website** with schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ).
5. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, 10+ photos, Q&A seeded).
6. Build **25–40 local citations** (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Lubbock Chamber).
7. Launch **programmatic location pages** for Lubbock neighborhoods (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in Southwest Lubbock," "Texas Tech Move-Out Cleaning") — 15–40 pages.

**Phase 2 — Demand Capture (Weeks 4–8)**
8. Launch **Google Ads** ($800–$2,500/mo) targeting "carpet cleaning Lubbock" and move-out keywords.
9. Deploy **review-generation system** (SMS/email post-job) — target 25+ reviews in 90 days.
10. Outreach to **10–15 property managers** and Texas Tech housing offices for B2B contracts.

**Phase 3 — GEO Content & Authority (Weeks 6–12)**
11. Publish **10–20 GEO-optimized articles** (cost guides, neighborhood pages, "how much does carpet cleaning cost in Lubbock").
12. Pursue **local backlinks** (Lubbock Chamber, local news, community sponsorships).
13. Track rankings weekly via GBP Insights + rank tracker.

**Phase 4 — Optimize & Scale (Month 4+)**
14. Pre-sell **maintenance plans** ($99–$149/quarter) in Q4 to smooth Jan–Feb trough.
15. Reinvest 15–20% of revenue into paid + content; target **$15K–$25K/mo by Month 9–12**.
