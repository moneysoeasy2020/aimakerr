## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3, ~$1,800–$3,400)

1. **Decide your license tier.** California requires a CSLB license for any job where labor + materials exceed **$500**. Handyman-only work (small repairs, no structural/electrical/plumbing permits) can operate under the **"minor work" exemption** but caps your ticket size. For Chula Vista's $400–$700 average ticket, **pursuing a B-General Building license is strongly recommended** — it unlocks remodel revenue and LSA eligibility.
2. **File CSLB application** ($450–$600 exam + license fees). Timeline: 6–10 weeks. Start now to be licensed by January 2027 surge.
3. **Form LLC** ($70–$100 CA filing) and obtain **Chula Vista Business Tax Certificate** (~$50–$150).
4. **Secure insurance:** general liability ($1M/$2M) at **$650–$1,100/yr**, commercial auto at **$200–$300/yr** add-on.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5, ~$2,450–$5,800)

5. **Claim and optimize Google Business Profile** — the single highest-ROI action. Category: "Handyman" + secondary "Contractor." Service area: 91910, 91911, 91913, 91914, 91915. Upload 10+ geo-tagged job photos in week 1.
6. **Launch website** with 5 ZIP-specific service-area pages (Eastlake, Otay Ranch, Rancho del Rey, Sunbow, Downtown Chula Vista) — this is the programmatic SEO foundation.
7. **Install review automation** (SMS request 2 hours post-job). Target: **15 reviews in 60 days, 40 by month 6**.
8. **Set up Google Local Services Ads** — budget **$800–$1,500/mo** for the first 90 days. LSA leads in Chula Vista average **$28–$55** per qualified call.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12, ~$1,200–$2,400/mo)

9. **Nextdoor + local Facebook groups** (Eastlake, Otay Ranch community groups) — post 2x/week with before/after photos. Free, high-conversion channel in Chula Vista.
10. **Direct mail to HOA boards** — Chula Vista has 40+ HOAs; a single HOA referral relationship can generate 8–15 jobs/month.
11. **Partner with 3 local real estate agents** in 91913/91914 — pre-listing repair work is recurring and high-margin.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 4–9)

12. **Track cost-per-lead by channel** weekly. Kill any channel above **$85/lead** by month 3.
13. **Add a second crew** when monthly revenue exceeds **$18,000** and booked-out ratio exceeds 85%.
14. **Refresh service-area pages quarterly** with real job photos and updated pricing — this is what keeps GEO/AI-search visibility (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) citing your business for "handyman Chula Vista" queries.
