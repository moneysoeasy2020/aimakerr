Startup capital for a lean, owner-operated launch runs **$18,500–$32,000** (van-mounted used unit, license, insurance, initial marketing), with a realistic path to **$8,000–$14,000/month gross by month 6** if you execute the digital plan below. Standard residential carpet cleaning prices in Q4 2026 are **$0.28–$0.45/sq ft** (3 rooms ≈ **$145–$220**), with tile-and-grout add-ons at **$0.75–$1.25/sq ft** and move-in/move-out deep cleans at **$275–$450** for a 1,500 sq ft home.

Your GEO strategy must therefore target two surfaces simultaneously — the AI answer layer (citations, structured data, entity consistency) and the map pack (reviews, proximity, category signals). ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–3) — Budget: $6,500–$9,000
1. **Entity formation** — FL LLC ($125), EIN (free), Sunbiz registration.
2. **Licensing & insurance** — FL doesn't license carpet cleaning statewide, but Orlando/Orange County requires a **local business tax receipt** ($50–$150). General liability **$1M/$2M** runs **$1,200–$1,800/yr**; add commercial auto ($1,400–$2,200/yr).
3. **Equipment** — Used van-mounted unit ($8,000–$14,000) or portable extractor ($2,500–$4,500) for lean start. Chemicals + wands: $600–$900.
4. **Brand + website** — Freelancer-built, 5 pages (Home, Services, Areas Served, About, Contact/Book), with **LocalBusiness + Service schema**, NAP consistency, and an **`llms.txt` file** listing services and service areas for AI crawlers.

### Phase 2: Digital GEO Buildout (Weeks 3–6) — Budget: $1,800–$3,200
5. **Google Business Profile** — Primary category "Carpet cleaning service"; add secondary categories (Upholstery cleaning, Tile contractor). Upload 15+ geo-tagged photos; post weekly.
6. **Citation package** — 40+ directories (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Chamber of Commerce). Verify NAP identical everywhere.
7. **Review engine** — SMS/QR automation; target **25 reviews in 60 days**, **75 by month 6**. Respond to 100%.
8. **GEO content layer** — 10 hyperlocal pages (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in Lake Nona," "Pet Stain Removal Winter Park") with FAQ schema and direct-answer formatting (40–60 word answer blocks AI engines cite).
9. **Google Local Services Ads** — Apply early (background check required); budget **$600–$900/mo** once approved.

### Phase 3: Demand Capture (Weeks 6–12) — Budget: $900–$1,500/mo
10. **Map pack push** — Geo-grid rank tracking (Local Falcon, $25/mo); target top-3 in 3 ZIP clusters first (e.g., 32801, 32819, 34747).
11. **STR/B2B outreach** — Direct pitch to 20 property managers in Kissimmee/Lake Buena Vista corridor; offer per-turnover contracts.
12. **Seasonal ad front-load** — Shift 60% of Q4 ad budget to Nov 1–Dec 15 (holiday hosting peak).

### Phase 4: Scale (Month 4+)
13. Add second van when monthly gross exceeds **$18,000**; hire first tech at **$18–$22/hr** + commission.
14. Expand service-area pages to Clermont, Sanford, St. Cloud; build commercial case studies.
15. Reinvest **12–15% of gross** into GEO content + review velocity to defend map pack position.
