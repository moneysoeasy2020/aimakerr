**Agency vs. freelancer tradeoffs:**
- **Agencies** offer project management, bundled reporting, and accountability — but carry 40–60% overhead markup, slower turnaround, and often assign junior staff to local-service accounts.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register your LLC with the Wisconsin DFI** (~$130 online) and obtain an EIN.
2. **Secure Milwaukee business licenses** — city permit if operating from a commercial address; check Wisconsin DNR requirements for wastewater disposal (critical for truck-mount operators).
3. **Bind insurance:** general liability ($1M/$2M), commercial auto, and cargo. Budget $1,400–$2,400/yr for lean launch.
4. **Source equipment:** used truck-mount ($6,500–$14,000) or high-end portable; buy chemicals from a Milwaukee-area distributor to reduce freight.
5. **Set pricing** using the table above; publish a transparent price page (AI engines reward explicit pricing).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5, parallel)
6. **Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile** — primary category "Carpet Cleaning Service," service areas for Milwaukee + 8–12 suburbs, 10+ service listings, 5 seeded Q&As.
7. **Launch a 5–8 page website** with LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ schema; include a pricing page and a booking form.
8. **Build 15–25 programmatic suburb landing pages** targeting "[service] + [suburb]" queries.
9. **Install review automation** — SMS request 2 hours post-job; target 40+ reviews by end of Q1 2027.
10. **Publish GEO-optimized FAQ content** answering "how much does carpet cleaning cost in Milwaukee," "best pet stain removal Milwaukee," and similar — formatted with direct answers in the first sentence.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
11. **Launch Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,200/mo budget) — highest-intent lead source for carpet cleaning.
12. **Run a Q4 holiday promotion** (pre-Thanksgiving / pre-Christmas deep clean) across GBP posts and email.
13. **Partner with 3–5 Milwaukee property managers** for move-out cleaning contracts — recurring, low-CAC revenue.
14. **Distribute door hangers** in 2–3 target neighborhoods (Wauwatosa, Shorewood) with a QR code to your booking page.

### Phase 4 — Optimize and Scale (Months 3–6)
15. **Track cost-per-booked-job** weekly; target under $45 by month 4.
16. **Add a second technician or van** once you sustain 14+ jobs/week for 4 consecutive weeks.
17. **Refresh programmatic pages quarterly** with seasonal pricing and local proof (job counts, neighborhood mentions) to maintain AI citation visibility.
18. **Audit competitor GBP reviews monthly** and outpace review velocity in your top 3 suburbs.
