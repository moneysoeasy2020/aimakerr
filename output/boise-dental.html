## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing**: Register Idaho PLLC, obtain Idaho Board of Dentistry license, DEA registration, and NPI. Budget **$3,500–$6,000** in legal/formation costs.
2. **Site selection**: Target Meridian W, Kuna, or North End. Negotiate **TI allowance of $35–$60/sq ft**; avoid spaces requiring >$280k build-out.
3. **Equipment financing**: Dental-specific lenders (e.g., specialized healthcare equipment financiers) at **7.5–9.5% APR**; expect **$180k–$320k** financed over 5–7 years.
4. **Insurance credentialing**: Submit to Delta Dental of Idaho, Regence, Blue Cross of Idaho, and Cigna **90–120 days pre-open**. This is the #1 launch bottleneck.

### Phase 2 — Digital Build (Months 3–5)
5. **Website**: HIPAA-aware, mobile-first, with **service-area pages** for Boise, Meridian, Eagle, Kuna, and Star. Embed **Dentist schema + LocalBusiness schema**.
6. **Google Business Profile**: Claim, verify, and optimize with **10+ geo-tagged photos** and service categories. GBP drives **35–50%** of new-patient discovery in Boise.
7. **Citations & reviews**: Build **40+ NAP-consistent citations** (Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Bing Places). Seed **15–25 reviews** in the first 90 days via post-visit SMS.

### Phase 3 — Paid & Content (Months 4–9)
8. **Google Ads**: Start at **$2,500–$4,000/mo** ad spend targeting "dentist near me," "emergency dentist Boise," "teeth cleaning Meridian." Expect **$18–$45 cost-per-lead** and **22–30% lead-to-patient conversion**.
9. **Programmatic SEO**: Publish **30–50 location/service pages** ("emergency dentist Kuna," "Invisalign Meridian") over 6 months. Target **top-3 local pack** for 8–12 core keywords by month 9.
10. **Content**: 2 blog posts/month on local-intent topics (e.g., "Boise water fluoride and dental health").

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 9–12)
11. **Recall & reactivation**: Automate recall reminders; a **5% recall lift** = ~$40k–$60k annual revenue.
12. **Review velocity**: Sustain **8–12 new Google reviews/month**; local pack ranking correlates strongly with review count and recency in Boise.
13. **Measure & reallocate**: Kill ad groups above **$55 CPL**; reinvest into SEO pages ranking positions 4–10.
