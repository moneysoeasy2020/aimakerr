## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 0 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–3)
1. **Entity formation & licensing:** NC PLLC filing (~$125), NC dental license verification, Guilford County privilege license, and **DEA registration** if prescribing.
2. **Site selection:** target 27410/27408/27403; negotiate 5–7 year NNN lease with **TI allowance of $45–$75/sq ft**.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) or equipment leasing; budget **$60,000–$90,000** working capital for 6-month runway.
4. **Credentialing:** begin PPO credentialing (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, BCBS NC) **90–120 days** before opening — the single most common launch delay.

### Phase 1 — Digital Foundation (Months 2–4)
5. **Website + schema:** service pages, provider bio, online booking (NexHealth/LocalMed), LocalBusiness + Dentist schema markup.
6. **Google Business Profile:** verify at physical address; upload 20+ photos; set service areas to Greensboro + Summerfield/Oak Ridge.
7. **Programmatic SEO:** build **40–80 pages** (procedure × neighborhood), each with unique local proof and pricing transparency.

### Phase 2 — Demand Capture (Months 3–6)
8. **Google Ads:** start with **$2,500–$4,000/mo** on high-intent terms (emergency, implants, Invisalign); target **$180–$260 CAC**.
9. **Review engine:** automate post-visit requests; target **25–40 reviews in first 90 days** and **4.8+ star average**.
10. **Referral network:** 15–20 primary-care and specialist referral relationships within the first two quarters.

### Phase 3 — Scale (Months 6–12)
11. **Add service lines** (implants, ortho) once hygiene is at **70%+ chair utilization**.
12. **Reinvest 6–9% of collections** into marketing; audit CAC quarterly against the **$180–$340** benchmark.
