## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-launch (Months 0–6)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Form PLLC in Texas ($300), obtain TSBDE license (~$600–$900), register with DEA and Texas controlled-substance registration.
2. **Site selection:** Target 1,800–2,600 sq ft in a high-income ZIP with <1.2 dentists per 1,000 residents. Negotiate 5-year lease with 90-day build-out allowance.
3. **Financing:** Secure $450K–$750K via SBA 7(a) (typical 10-year term, ~8–9% in Q4 2026) or equipment leasing for chairs/CBCT.
4. **Build-out & equipment:** Budget $320K–$520K for 4 operatories including CBCT, digital workflow, and IT.
5. **Digital foundation:** Commission website + GBP + local citations (freelancer: $3.5K–$7.5K; agency: $8.5K–$17.5K).

### Phase 2 — Launch (Months 6–9)
6. **Credentialing:** Enroll with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna, and BCBS TX (4–8 week cycles). Self-pay pricing should be published transparently.
7. **Google Business Profile optimization:** Category, services, photos (20+), Q&A, review generation workflow. This is the single highest-ROI asset in Dallas dental.
8. **Paid search launch:** $3,000–$6,000/month starting spend; target $180–$280 CPA.
9. **Review engine:** Automate post-visit review requests; target 40+ reviews by month 12, 4.8+ star average.

### Phase 3 — Growth (Months 9–24)
10. **Local SEO content:** 2–4 service-area pages/month targeting "dentist in [ZIP]," "emergency dentist Dallas," "Invisalign Uptown."
11. **Recall & reactivation:** Automate 6-month recall; reactivation campaigns recover 8–14% of lapsed patients.
12. **Referral network:** Build relationships with 15–25 local PCPs, pediatricians, and specialists.
13. **KPI review (quarterly):** New patients/month, cost per acquisition, chair utilization %, production per chair, recall rate.
