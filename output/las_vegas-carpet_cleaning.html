# Launching a Carpet Cleaning Business in Las Vegas, NV
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Las Vegas' carpet cleaning market generates an estimated **$48M–$62M in annual residential and commercial revenue** across roughly **310–360 active operators** (Clark County business licenses), with the metro's 2.3M population and ~1.9M annual visitors creating dual demand streams. A realistic 2026 Q4 startup budget is **$14,500–$32,000** for a solo-operator residential model (truck-mount or high-end portable), or **$38,000–$75,000** for a two-van commercial-capable operation — with break-even typically at **55–90 residential jobs/month** depending on ticket size.

Residential carpet cleaning in Las Vegas prices at **$0.28–$0.45/sq ft** for standard steam cleaning, with a typical 3-bedroom home (1,200–1,600 sq ft) running **$185–$340**; tile-and-grout add-ons command **$0.75–$1.40/sq ft**, and commercial contracts (casinos, hotels, offices) average **$0.18–$0.32/sq ft** at volume. Las Vegas' dry desert climate (8–12% summer humidity) reduces dry times to 4–6 hours versus 12–24 hours in humid markets — a genuine marketing differentiator, but also a seasonal risk: **Q4 (October–December) is peak move-out/lease-turn season** thanks to the metro's ~35% renter population and heavy relocation churn, making your launch timing favorable.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register** Nevada LLC + Clark County business license ($425–$650 total); obtain sales tax permit.
2. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto — budget **$1,800–$3,200/yr**.
3. **Equipment:** Truck-mount ($18K–$32K used/new) or high-end portable ($4,500–$9,000) + van ($12K–$28K used).
4. **IICRC certification** (CCT) — $650–$1,200; required for commercial credibility.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
5. Launch 6-page website with online booking (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber integration — $99–$349/mo).
6. Build **Google Business Profile** with 15+ geo-tagged photos; target "carpet cleaning [neighborhood]" for Summerlin, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Spring Valley, Enterprise.
7. Deploy **12–20 programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in Green Valley Ranch, NV — $0.32/sq ft, 4-hour dry time").

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–10)
8. **Google Local Services Ads** — priority for carpet cleaning; budget $800–$1,500/mo in Q4.
9. **Review engine:** automated SMS post-job requests; target 25+ Google reviews by day 60.
10. **B2B outreach:** property managers (Clark County has 400+ PM firms), Airbnb hosts, and casino/hotel facilities teams.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Month 3+)
11. Add second van at ~$28K/month revenue.
12. Launch commercial division targeting the **$850–$4,200/job** segment.
13. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into SEO + LSA expansion.
