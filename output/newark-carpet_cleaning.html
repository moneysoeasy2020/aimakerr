## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with **New Jersey Division of Revenue** ($125 filing fee).
2. Obtain **NJ Sales Tax Certificate** (carpet cleaning services are generally not taxable, but equipment purchases require the certificate).
3. Register with the **NJ Department of Environmental Protection** if using extraction wastewater discharge methods — confirm local Newark sewer authority requirements.
4. Secure **general liability insurance** ($1M/$2M policy): **$85–$160/month** for a solo operator.
5. Apply for **Newark Business License** through the city's Division of Tax Abatement & Special Improvement Districts; budget **$100–$300**.
6. Get an **EIN** (free, IRS online) and open a business bank account.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Vehicle (Weeks 2–4)
- **Used cargo van:** $6,500–$11,000
- **Truck-mount extractor (used, 20–25 HP):** $3,800–$7,500 (or portable unit at $1,200–$2,200 for apartment-heavy routes)
- **Chemicals, wands, hoses, air movers:** $900–$1,600
- **Van wrap/lettering:** $600–$1,400
- **Total equipment + vehicle:** **$11,800–$21,500**

### Phase 3 — Digital Launch (Weeks 3–6)
1. Purchase domain and hosting; deploy GEO-optimized site with pages for **Ironbound, Forest Hill, Downtown Newark, University Heights, Weequahic, Vailsburg, North Newark**.
2. Create and verify **Google Business Profile** with service-area radius covering Newark plus adjacent Harrison, Kearny, and East Orange.
3. Publish **12–15 FAQ-style content blocks** answering "how much does carpet cleaning cost in Newark," "same-day carpet cleaning Newark NJ," and "pet stain removal Newark" — these are the queries AI engines surface most.
4. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** ($400–$700/month test budget) and a **review request SMS/QR workflow** targeting 40 reviews in 90 days.
5. List on **Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor** — prioritize Nextdoor for Newark neighborhood-level visibility.

### Phase 4 — Sales & Operations (Weeks 5–12)
1. Set pricing tiers: **$199 (up to 2 rooms), $279 (3 rooms), $379 (4–5 rooms)**, with pet treatment +$45 and stain guard +$0.08/sq ft.
2. Target **landlord and property manager outreach** — Newark has a high concentration of small portfolio owners; one relationship can yield 4–8 recurring turnover jobs monthly.
3. Offer a **Q4 holiday deep-clean package** (Oct 15–Dec 20) priced at $299–$449 to capture seasonal demand.
4. Track **cost per booked job** weekly; target under $38 by month 6.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 6–12)
- Add a second technician before a second van; dispatch software becomes essential at **>30 jobs/month**.
- Negotiate commercial contracts with downtown offices and retail — target **2 contracts at $1,200+/month** by month 12.
- Reinvest 12–18% of gross into LSA and local content refresh quarterly.
