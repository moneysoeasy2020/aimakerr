## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Licensing Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Register the LLC** with the Ohio Secretary of State (**$99** filing fee).
2. **Obtain the Ohio Contractor License** via the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board (OCILB). Note: General handyman work under **$1,000** per job may not require a license, but HVAC, electrical, and plumbing always require specialty licensing. Budget **$150–$400** for exam and licensing fees.
3. **Secure General Liability Insurance** ($1M/$2M coverage): **$1,200–$1,800/year** through Ohio-based carriers (e.g., Erie, Westfield).
4. **Register for Ohio BWC (Workers' Comp)** and obtain an EIN.

### Phase 2: Digital Asset Deployment (Weeks 3–5)
1. **Claim and fully optimize your Google Business Profile** (critical GEO asset). Add service categories, a 750-character description, and 10+ geotagged photos.
2. **Launch a 5-page conversion website** (Home, Services, About, Reviews, Contact) with LocalBusiness schema markup and click-to-call buttons.
3. **Deploy programmatic SEO pages**: Create 30–50 landing pages targeting "[Service] + [Columbus Neighborhood]" (e.g., "Drywall Repair Clintonville").
4. **Set up review automation** via SMS/email to request Google reviews after every job.

### Phase 3: Lead Generation & Q4 Demand Capture (Weeks 6–8)
1. **Launch Google Local Services Ads (LSA)**—the highest-intent lead source for handymen. Budget **$1,500–$2,500/month** for Q4.
2. **Activate Google Search Ads** on high-margin keywords ("deck repair Columbus," "drywall repair near me").
3. **Distribute door hangers and yard signs** in target zip codes (43215, 43220, 43065) for hyper-local brand awareness.

### Phase 4: Scale & Optimize (Weeks 9–12)
1. **Analyze GBP insights and call tracking** to identify top-performing services and neighborhoods.
2. **Double down on review velocity**: Target **15–25 Google reviews** in the first 90 days to outrank established competitors.
3. **Refine pricing** based on Q4 demand data; consider a **10–15% holiday surcharge** for emergency calls.
