## 2026 Q4 Lincoln Market Analysis

Lincoln, Nebraska is a **~295,000-person metro** (Lancaster County ~340,000) with a housing stock dominated by pre-1990 single-family homes — roughly **58% of owner-occupied units were built before 1990**, which means aging furnaces, AC condensers, and ductwork are the primary demand drivers. Average residential HVAC replacement runs **$6,800–$13,500** (furnace + AC combo), while a single-system swap lands at **$4,200–$7,900** for a standard 2,000 sq ft home. Emergency/after-hours service calls in Lincoln typically bill **$185–$320** for the diagnostic + first hour, and seasonal maintenance plans (2 visits/year) sell at **$189–$349 annually** — a high-margin recurring revenue anchor. The local market supports roughly **70–90 active HVAC contractors**, with the top 5 firms capturing an estimated 35–40% of residential replacement volume, leaving meaningful share for a well-positioned new entrant.

Demand is highly seasonal and predictable: **June–August** drives AC service (peak call volume up 3–4x baseline), **October–December** drives furnace tune-ups and replacements, and **January–February** produces the highest-margin emergency no-heat calls. Lincoln's climate — design temps near **0°F winter / 95°F summer** — means dual-system households are the norm, doubling lifetime customer value versus single-system markets. A realistic Year-1 revenue target for a lean 2-technician operation is **$380,000–$620,000 gross**, with **22–30% net margins** achievable once recurring maintenance contracts reach ~250 accounts.

**Phase 3 — Scale (Months 4–12)**
