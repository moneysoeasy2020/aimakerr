# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Irving, TX

Irving, TX sits at the center of the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex, with a population of roughly **256,000** and a median household income near **$72,000** (2026 estimate). The city's **~48,000 renter-occupied units** and aging housing stock (much of it built 1970–2000) drive steady demand for repair, maintenance, and small-remodel work. A realistic startup budget for a licensed, insured handyman operation is **$8,500–$22,000** in Year 1, with the largest line items being insurance ($1,800–$3,600/yr), tools ($2,500–$6,000), and Texas state licensing/registration fees.

Pricing in Irving tracks DFW metro norms in 2026 Q4: **$65–$95/hour** for general handyman labor, **$45–$75/hour** for helper/apprentice rates, and **$150–$350** for common flat-rate jobs (ceiling fan install, drywall patch, faucet replacement). Target a **15–25% net margin** after materials, insurance, and marketing — achievable within 6–9 months if you secure 3–5 recurring property-management accounts.

## 2026 Q4 Irving Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers
- **Housing age:** ~62% of Irving's housing units were built before 2000 — prime repair-and-maintenance territory.
- **Rental density:** Irving has one of DFW's highest renter shares (~45%), concentrated in the Las Colinas and Valley Ranch corridors. Property managers are your highest-LTV B2B segment.
- **Commercial corridor:** Las Colinas hosts 4,000+ businesses; light commercial handyman work (tenant improvements, fixture installs) adds a premium tier at **$95–$140/hour**.
- **Competition:** ~180–220 active handyman/contractor listings on Google Business Profile within a 10-mile radius. Differentiation via response time (<2 hrs) and transparent flat-rate pricing is your edge.

### Regulatory Snapshot (Texas + Irving, 2026 Q4)
- **Texas does NOT require a statewide general contractor license** for most residential handyman work — but **electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and roofing above threshold require licensed subs**.
- **Irving requires a Home Occupation Permit** (~$50–$150) if operating from a residence; a **General Contractor registration** applies to permitted structural work.
- **Texas HB 2025-series updates** tightened disclosure requirements for jobs over $5,000 — carry a written contract.
- **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) runs **$1,800–$3,600/yr**; workers' comp (if hiring) adds **$1,200–$2,800/yr per employee**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 0: Pre-Launch (Weeks 1–3) — Budget: $2,500–$4,500
1. **Entity + licensing:** File Texas LLC ($300), obtain EIN (free), secure Irving Home Occupation Permit ($50–$150).
2. **Insurance:** Bind GL policy ($1,800–$3,600/yr); add commercial auto if using a work vehicle ($1,200–$2,400/yr).
3. **Tools + vehicle:** Core handyman kit ($2,500–$4,000); used truck/van ($8,000–$18,000 or lease at $450–$700/mo).
4. **Bank + accounting:** Business checking (free–$25/mo); QuickBooks or Wave ($0–$30/mo).

### Phase 1: Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–5) — Budget: $1,200–$2,800
5. **Domain + hosting:** $80–$180/yr.
6. **Website:** 5-page freelance build with service pages for *drywall repair, ceiling fan install, faucet replacement, deck repair, TV mounting, furniture assembly* ($600–$1,800).
7. **Google Business Profile:** Claim, verify, upload 15+ photos, set service area to Irving + 10-mile radius ($150–$400 freelance optimization).
8. **Programmatic service-area pages:** 15–25 pages targeting "handyman in [Irving neighborhood]" — critical for AI-search visibility in 2026 ($400–$1,200).

### Phase 2: Lead Generation (Weeks 5–8) — Budget: $500–$1,200/mo
9. **Google Local Services Ads:** Apply for Google Guaranteed badge; set budget at $1,500–$3,000/mo, expect $8–$35 per lead.
10. **Review engine:** SMS/QR review requests after every job (target 25+ Google reviews in 90 days — this is the #1 local ranking factor).
11. **Property-manager outreach:** Target 40–60 Irving rental managers (Las Colinas, Valley Ranch); offer net-30 terms and priority scheduling.

### Phase 3: Scale (Months 3–9)
12. **Hire first helper** at $18–$24/hr when monthly revenue hits $12K.
13. **Add commercial tier:** Light commercial handyman at $95–$140/hr — Las Colinas office parks are underserved.
14. **Reinvest in programmatic SEO:** Expand to 50+ pages targeting adjacent cities (Coppell, Grand Prairie, Euless) by month 6.
15. **Consider agency retainer** only after $25K/mo revenue — until then, freelance + DIY wins on ROI.
