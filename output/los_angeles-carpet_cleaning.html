## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with **California Secretary of State ($70 filing fee)**.
2. Obtain **LA City Business Tax Registration Certificate** ($50–$400 based on gross receipts tier).
3. Secure **C-61/D-49 specialty contractor license** via CSLB if performing water damage work ($400 application).
4. Purchase **general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto + workers' comp** — budget **$2,400–$4,800/year**.
5. Register for CA sales tax permit (equipment purchases) and set up EIN.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Operations (Weeks 3–6)
- Source used truck-mount unit (HydraMaster, Prochem) at **$8,000–$18,000**; portable backup at **$2,500–$4,000**.
- Build route density around **San Fernando Valley, Westside, and South Bay** zip codes (highest residential turnover).
- Set pricing floor at **$65/room** to avoid race-to-bottom; target **$210 average ticket**.

### Phase 3 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 4–8)
- Launch **8-page website** with LocalBusiness + Service schema markup.
- Build **programmatic city pages** for 25–40 LA neighborhoods (Burbank, Pasadena, Santa Monica, Long Beach, Glendale, etc.).
- Optimize **Google Business Profile** with service categories, photos, and Q&A seeding.
- Deploy **review automation** (SMS post-job request within 2 hours — 3.2x conversion vs. email).

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)
- Activate **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, $18–$45/lead in LA) — dominant channel for carpet cleaning.
- Launch **Yelp + Thumbtack + Angi** profiles with competitive response times (<15 min).
- Target **property management companies** and **Airbnb hosts** for recurring B2B contracts (30–40% of stable revenue).
- Run **geo-targeted direct mail** to 2,000–5,000 households in high-turnover rental zips ($0.55–$0.85/piece).

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 4–12)
- Add second crew at **$90K–$140K incremental revenue**.
- Build **referral program** (15% commission to realtors, property managers, movers).
- Publish **GEO-optimized content** answering AI-search queries ("how much does carpet cleaning cost in Los Angeles," "best pet odor removal LA") to capture generative engine citations.
