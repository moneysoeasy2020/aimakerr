# Launching a Dental Clinic in Henderson, NV
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Henderson's dental market supports roughly **1 dental practice per 2,900 residents** across a population of approximately **340,000** (2026 estimate), with the highest demand concentrated in **Green Valley, Seven Hills, Anthem, and Inspirada**. A de novo general dentistry clinic (3–4 operatories, ~1,800–2,200 sq ft) requires **$420,000–$680,000** in total startup capital in 2026 Q4 dollars: **$95,000–$140,000** build-out/tenant improvements, **$180,000–$260,000** equipment (chairs, imaging, sterilization, CBCT if offering implants), **$35,000–$60,000** working capital, and **$60,000–$120,000** for licensing, insurance, and initial staffing. Typical Henderson commercial dental space runs **$28–$42/sq ft NNN**; expect **$8–$14/sq ft** in additional NNN pass-throughs.

Revenue math for planning: a single-operatory general dentist in Henderson bills **$550–$850/hour** of chair time at 2026 Q4 fee schedules, and a 4-operatory clinic at 65% utilization grosses **$1.1M–$1.6M annually** by month 18–24. PPO-adjusted collections typically land at **72–82% of gross production**, and Henderson's payer mix skews **55–65% PPO, 15–20% fee-for-service, 10–15% Medicaid (Nevada Medicaid covers limited adult dental), and 5–10% uninsured**. The digital acquisition budget to reach a stable new-patient flow of **60–90 new patients/month** is **$9,000–$18,000/month** in blended paid + local SEO spend — the rest of this guide shows exactly how to deploy it and where to buy expertise.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (2026 Q4 → 2027 Q4)

### Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–3)
1. **Entity + licensing:** Form Nevada LLC/PC ($500–$1,200), obtain Nevada dental license ($600–$900), Henderson business license ($100–$300), DEA + malpractice ($4,000–$8,000/yr).
2. **Site selection:** Target 89011/89002/89074; negotiate TI allowance ($25–$50/sq ft); confirm NNN terms.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) at 2026 Q4 rates (prime + 2.5–3.5%); expect 10-year term, 10–15% down.
4. **Practice management + imaging stack:** $25,000–$45,000 (software, CBCT, sensors, sterilization).

### Phase 2: Build + Digital Launch (Months 3–5)
5. **Build-out:** 10–14 weeks; budget 15% contingency.
6. **Website + GBP:** launch 6 weeks before opening; submit sitemap; build 72 programmatic service×ZIP pages; add LocalBusiness/Dentist schema.
7. **Citations:** Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, NPI registry, Bing Places — 25+ consistent NAP listings.
8. **Review engine:** automated post-visit requests; target 10 reviews/month from day one.

### Phase 3: Demand Generation (Months 5–9)
9. **Google Ads:** launch "dentist Henderson," "emergency dentist Henderson," "Invisalign Henderson" — $3,000–$6,000/mo, target $150–$220 CPA.
10. **Meta ads:** membership/whitening offers to 89002/89011/89074 radius — $1,500–$3,000/mo.
11. **Local SEO:** 6-month retainer; GBP posts 2×/week; 4–6 new geo-content pages/month.
12. **Recall/reactivation:** automate 6-month recalls; reactivate lapsed patients at 90/180 days.

### Phase 4: Scale (Months 9–18)
13. **Add service lines:** implants, Invisalign, sedation — each gets its own programmatic page cluster.
14. **Hire associate + hygienist** when utilization exceeds 70%.
15. **Audit:** quarterly CPA, LTV, review velocity, and AI-search visibility (test ChatGPT/Perplexity queries monthly).
