## Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts

**Local brick-and-mortar agencies (Newark/Ironbound/Downtown firms):** Typical retainers run **$1,200–$2,800/month** with 3–6 month minimums. Strengths: in-person accountability, bundled photography, and local relationship networks. Weaknesses for a startup: high fixed burn during your cash-tightest months, slower iteration, and often junior staff executing handyman-niche SEO they've never done before.

**Verified freelance experts (Upwork, Contra, vetted local freelancers):** Project-based pricing of **$150–$900 per deliverable**, no retainer lock-in, and specialists who live in the local-SEO niche. Weaknesses: you must manage the workflow and verify credentials (check reviews, portfolio, and a test task). For a Q4 2026 launch, freelancers win on **cost-per-outcome by roughly 60–75%** versus agencies — reserve agency spend for year two when you have cash flow to justify a retainer.

**Verdict:** Launch with **$1,100–$2,550 in verified freelance deliverables** + DIY Google Business Profile management. Re-evaluate agency retainers only after hitting $10K/month recurring revenue.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Insurance (Weeks 1–2, $1,800–$3,200)**
1. Register LLC with NJ Division of Revenue (~$125).
2. Obtain Newark mercantile/license endorsements as required; confirm NJ Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) registration if jobs exceed $500 (~$110–$500 depending on structure).
3. Secure general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto: **$1,400–$2,600/year** for a solo operator.
4. Open business banking + set aside 28–30% for taxes.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4, $1,100–$2,550)**
5. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (categories, service areas: Downtown, Ironbound, North Ward, East Ward, South Ward, University Heights).
6. Launch 5-page site: Home, Services, Service Areas, About, Contact — with schema markup and Newark-specific copy.
7. Build 25 local citations (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB).
8. Deploy review-request QR workflow (target: 15 reviews by day 60).

**Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 4–8, $400–$900)**
9. Publish 4 seasonal service pages: *Winterization, Gutter Repair, Rental Turnover Punch-Lists, Small Commercial Touch-Ups*.
10. Set up Google Local Services Ads ($300–$600 test budget) + Nextdoor recommendations.
11. Partner with 3–5 Newark property managers for recurring turnover work.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Weeks 8–12)**
12. Add flat-rate quote sheet for the 12 highest-demand jobs.
13. Target 40+ reviews and a 4.8+ rating by day 90.
14. Reinvest 15% of revenue into content and LSA budget.
