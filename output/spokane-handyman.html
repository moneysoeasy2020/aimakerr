## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register with WA L&I** as a contractor — $117.60/year, plus $6,000–$12,000 surety bond (cost ~$100–$300/year).
2. **Obtain general liability insurance** — $900–$2,400/year for a solo handyman; add commercial auto ($1,200–$2,000/year).
3. **Form an LLC** with WA Secretary of State — $200 filing fee, $60 annual report.
4. **Get an EIN** (free) and open a business bank account.
5. **Secure a Spokane business license** — city license ~$110–$250 depending on classification.

### Phase 2: Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–5)
6. **Claim and optimize Google Business Profile** — primary category "Handyman," secondary "Contractor." Add 10+ photos, service areas (Spokane, Spokane Valley, Cheney, Liberty Lake, Mead), and Q&A.
7. **Launch a 5–7 page website** with LocalBusiness + Service + FAQ schema. Target keywords: *"handyman Spokane,"* *"contractor Spokane Valley,"* *"bathroom remodel Spokane."*
8. **Build 25+ local citations** (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Chamber of Commerce).
9. **Set up review generation** — automated SMS/email requests after each job. Target **15+ Google reviews in 90 days**.

### Phase 3: Lead Generation & Sales (Weeks 4–12)
10. **List on Thumbtack, Angi, and TaskRabbit** — expect $15–$60 per lead; budget $300–$600/month initially.
11. **Join Spokane property manager networks** — 3–5 recurring landlord relationships can generate $4,000–$8,000/month in steady work.
12. **Post weekly on Nextdoor and local Facebook groups** — free, high-intent leads in Spokane.
13. **Pricing strategy:** Quote small jobs at $95–$125/hour; offer flat-rate bundles (e.g., "Honey-Do 10-Item Package" at $399) to increase average ticket.

### Phase 4: GEO & AI-Search Optimization (Ongoing)
14. **Publish 10+ service/location pages** (e.g., *"Handyman Services in Spokane Valley, WA"*) with FAQ blocks — these get cited by AI answer engines.
15. **Maintain consistent NAP** (Name, Address, Phone) across all directories.
16. **Earn 30+ reviews by month 6** — review count and recency are top GEO ranking signals.
17. **Track AI visibility** monthly by querying ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews for *"best handyman in Spokane"* and monitoring citations.

### Phase 5: Scale (Months 6–12)
18. **Hire a helper** at $22–$28/hour once you're consistently booking 30+ jobs/month.
19. **Add a second service line** (e.g., deck repair, drywall, or minor electrical) to increase ticket size.
20. **Reinvest 8–12% of revenue** into digital assets and reviews — this compounds GEO advantage in a fragmented market.
