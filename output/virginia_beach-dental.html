## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 0–6)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Form PLLC in Virginia, obtain VA Board of Dentistry license, DEA (if prescribing), and Virginia Beach business license.
2. **Site selection:** Target 2,000–2,500 sq ft in Kempsville or Town Center; negotiate 7–10 yr lease with TI allowance ($30–$60/sq ft).
3. **Build-out & equipment:** Budget $280k–$550k; lead time on chairs/imaging is 8–14 weeks in 2026.
4. **Digital foundation:** Domain, GBP claim, HIPAA-compliant site, programmatic service pages.

### Phase 2 — Launch (Months 6–9)
5. **Credentialing:** Start PPO credentialing 90–120 days before opening (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Anthem).
6. **Local SEO push:** 40–60 programmatic pages (service × neighborhood), 30+ citations, review generation workflow.
7. **Paid acquisition:** Google Local Services Ads + Search ($2,500–$5,000/mo start).

### Phase 3 — Growth (Months 9–18)
8. **Membership plan launch** to capture the ~30% uninsured segment.
9. **Content authority:** 8 posts/mo targeting high-intent queries; track AI-answer citations (GEO).
10. **Review velocity:** Target 15–25 new Google reviews/month; respond within 24 hrs.
