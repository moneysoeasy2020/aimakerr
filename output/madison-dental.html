## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–3)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Form WI LLC ($130 filing), obtain **DSPS dental license** (if owner-dentist) and **DHS clinical lab waiver**. Budget $2,400–$4,800 for legal/accounting setup.
2. **Location & lease:** Target **2,500–3,200 sq ft** in Verona/Fitchburg/Middleton. Negotiate **TI allowance of $45–$75/sq ft** and a **7–10 year term with 5-year option**.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) at **10.5–11.5% (Q4 2026)** for equipment + working capital; dental-specific lenders (Bank of America Practice Solutions, Live Oak) offer **equipment loans at 7.9–9.5%**.
4. **Insurance credentialing:** File Delta Dental, WEA Trust, MetLife, Cigna applications **immediately** — 90–120 day clock.

### Phase 2 — Build-Out & Digital Foundation (Months 3–6)
5. **Build-out:** $185–$320/sq ft; permit timeline in Dane County is **6–10 weeks**. Budget 15% contingency.
6. **Website + GBP:** Launch site with **LocalBusiness + Dentist schema**, online booking (NexHealth or LocalMed, $299–$499/mo), and verified GBP with 10+ service categories.
7. **Programmatic SEO:** Build **60–120 neighborhood × service pages** (e.g., "emergency dentist Fitchburg WI," "Invisalign Verona"). Target **3–5 pages ranking in map pack by month 9**.

### Phase 3 — Soft Launch & Acquisition (Months 6–9)
8. **Soft open** with 40–60 pre-booked patients from waitlist + referral campaign ($50 credit per referral).
9. **Paid search:** $2,500–$4,000/mo on high-intent terms ("dentist near me," "emergency dentist Madison"). Target **CPL of $45–$85**.
10. **Review engine:** Automated post-visit SMS (HIPAA-compliant), target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** — the threshold for map-pack competitiveness in Dane County.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 9–18)
11. **Add specialty services** (implants, ortho) once hygiene schedule hits **75% utilization**.
12. **Reinvest 4–6% of revenue** into SEO/content; expect **organic to drive 45–55% of new patients by month 18**.
13. **Evaluate second location** at month 18–24 if EBITDA exceeds **$280K** and chair utilization is above **80%**.
