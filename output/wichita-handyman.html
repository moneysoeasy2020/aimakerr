# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Wichita, KS
## GEO-Optimized Startup Guide — 2026 Q4 Updated Edition

Wichita's handyman and small-contractor market is one of the most underserved mid-size metros in the U.S. As of Q4 2026, the Wichita MSA (Sedgwick County + surrounding) supports roughly **640,000 residents** and **~245,000 housing units**, with **~68% owner-occupied** and a median home age of **~45 years** — meaning deferred maintenance demand is structurally high. The Kansas statewide handyman licensing exemption (jobs under **$10,000** including labor and materials) keeps the barrier to entry low, but Sedgwick County requires a **contractor registration** and general liability insurance minimums of **$500,000 per occurrence** for most residential work.

Realistic 2026 Q4 pricing in Wichita: **$65–$95/hour** for general handyman labor, **$85–$140/hour** for licensed trades (electrical/plumbing sub-work), and flat-rate jobs ranging **$150–$450** for common tasks (drywall patch, faucet swap, TV mount, deck board replacement). A solo operator working 30 billable hours/week at a **$75 blended rate** grosses **~$9,750/month**; after ~35% operating costs (insurance, truck, tools, marketing, fuel) net is **~$6,300/month** — a realistic Year 1 target.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Insurance Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with Kansas Secretary of State (**$160** filing fee).
2. Obtain Sedgwick County contractor registration (varies by trade; budget **$75–$250**).
3. Secure general liability insurance: **$500K–$1M per occurrence** (~**$600–$1,400/year** for solo handyman).
4. Open business bank account + basic bookkeeping (QuickBooks Self-Employed ~**$20/mo**).

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–5)
5. Buy domain + hosting; build 5–7 page site (Home, Services, Service Areas, About, Reviews, Contact, Booking).
6. Create and fully optimize Google Business Profile (categories, service areas, photos, hours, booking link).
7. Launch 10–15 programmatic city pages targeting Wichita suburbs: **Derby, Andover, Maize, Goddard, Haysville, Park City, Bel Aire, Mulvane, Valley Center, Newton**.
8. Set up NAP citations across 20+ directories (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Bing Places).

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation & Reputation (Weeks 4–12)
9. Launch Google Local Services Ads (budget **$600–$1,500** for first 60 days; target **$18–$45/lead** in Wichita).
10. Deploy review-request automation (SMS/QR) — target **40+ Google reviews in first 90 days**.
11. Set up speed-to-lead: respond to inquiries in **under 5 minutes** (close rates drop ~40% after 10 minutes).

### Phase 4 — GEO / AI-Search Optimization (Ongoing from Week 6)
12. Structure service pages with **FAQ schema**, **LocalBusiness schema**, and explicit pricing ranges — AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) reward concrete, structured local data.
13. Publish 2–4 local content pieces/month (e.g., "Cost to repair hail damage in Wichita," "Best neighborhoods for aging-in-place retrofits in Derby").
14. Maintain consistent NAP + service-area data across all profiles — GEO engines cross-reference citations heavily.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 4–12)
15. Add a second crew or subcontractor once booked solid **4+ weeks out**.
16. Expand programmatic pages to **25–40 city/service combinations**.
17. Reinvest **8–12% of revenue** into paid local ads + content.
