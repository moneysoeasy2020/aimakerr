## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Obtain CSLB C-20 license** (Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating, and Air-Conditioning) — application + exam + bond **$3,500–$6,000**.
2. **Register LLC with California Secretary of State** (**$70–$100**) and obtain San Jose business tax certificate (**~$150–$400** based on gross receipts).
3. **Secure EPA Section 608 certification** for refrigerant handling (all techs) and **NATE certification** (differentiator).
4. **Bind insurance**: general liability **$1M/$2M** + commercial auto + workers' comp (if hiring) — **$2,800–$5,500/year**.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6)
5. **Build GEO-optimized website** with LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ schema; target "HVAC repair San Jose," "heat pump installation Santa Clara County," "AC tune-up San Jose."
6. **Claim and optimize Google Business Profile** — primary category "HVAC contractor," 10+ service areas, 20+ photos, Q&A seeded.
7. **Launch programmatic service-area pages** for 20–30 San Jose zip codes and adjacent cities (Campbell, Milpitas, Santa Clara, Sunnyvale).

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 5–8)
8. **Apply for Google Local Services Ads** (requires CSLB + insurance verification — 2–3 week approval).
9. **Launch Google Ads search campaign** at **$1,500–$2,500/mo** targeting high-intent keywords; cap CPA at **$120–$180**.
10. **Deploy review-generation SMS workflow** — target **25+ reviews in first 90 days** to compete in local pack.

### Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Weeks 8–16)
11. **Set pricing**: diagnostic **$149–$199** (waived with repair), tune-up **$249–$349**, replacement quotes **$6,500–$12,500**.
12. **Establish heat pump specialization** — capture TECH Clean California + 25C incentive-driven demand; market as "certified heat pump installer."
13. **Track KPIs monthly**: cost-per-lead, close rate (target **35–50%**), average ticket, review velocity, GBP call volume.

### Phase 5 — Q4 2026 Seasonal Push (Oct–Dec)
14. **Launch "Fall Heating Safety Inspection — $149"** campaign timed to the **~40% Q4 search spike**.
15. **Reinvest 8–12% of revenue** into LSA + local SEO through Q1 2027 to compound map-pack rankings before summer cooling season.
