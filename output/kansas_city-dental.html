## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Regulatory & Location (Months 1–3)
1. **Entity Formation:** File for an LLC in Missouri ($50) or Kansas ($160). Obtain an EIN.
2. **Licensure:** Apply for Missouri Dental License via the Missouri Division of Professional Registration. Allow 6–8 weeks.
3. **Location Scouting:** Target ZIP codes **64118 (North KC)** or **66210 (Overland Park)**. These areas have high household incomes ($85k+) and lower dental saturation.
4. **Lease Negotiation:** Secure a 5-year lease with a 3-month build-out allowance. Ensure the space is zoned for medical use.

### Phase 2: Build-Out & Equipment (Months 3–6)
1. **Architectural Plans:** Hire a KC-based architect familiar with dental plumbing and ADA compliance ($5,000–$10,000).
2. **Equipment Procurement:** Source from Patterson Dental or Henry Schein. Negotiate a 0% financing package for 24 months.
3. **HIPAA Compliance:** Install encrypted IT infrastructure. Budget $2,000 for initial setup.

### Phase 3: GEO Digital Launch (Month 5)
1. **Website Development:** Build a site with **LocalBusiness Schema** and **Dentist Schema**. Include "Kansas City" in H1 tags and meta descriptions.
2. **Google Business Profile (GBP):** Create and verify. Add 10+ high-resolution photos of the clinic exterior and interior. Enable messaging.
3. **Citation Building:** List your clinic on Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, and the Kansas City Dental Society directory.
4. **Content Strategy:** Publish 5 "GEO-optimized" blog posts targeting queries like "Emergency Dentist Kansas City 64118" and "Best Invisalign Provider Overland Park."

### Phase 4: Patient Acquisition (Month 6+)
1. **Google Ads:** Launch a "Search" campaign targeting "Dentist Near Me" and "KC Dental Emergency." Set a daily budget of $100–$150.
2. **Review Generation:** Automate review requests via text after appointments. Aim for 50+ Google reviews in the first 90 days.
3. **Community Outreach:** Sponsor local KC events (e.g., Plaza Art Fair) to build backlinks and brand authority.
