## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (90 Days)

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–21)
1. **Entity & compliance:** Form Illinois LLC ($150–$500), obtain EIN, open business bank account.
2. **Licensing:** City of Chicago General Contractor License if scope requires; register with IL Dept. of Financial & Professional Regulation where applicable.
3. **Insurance:** $1M/$2M general liability ($1,200–$2,400/yr), commercial auto, workers' comp if hiring.
4. **Tools & vehicle:** Budget $3,500–$6,000 for pro-grade kit; wrap or magnet signage ($1,900–$4,200).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Days 15–45)
5. **Website:** 5–8 pages, LocalBusiness + Service schema, mobile-first. Freelance budget $900–$2,200.
6. **Google Business Profile:** Verify, add 10+ photos, service areas (Logan Square, Wicker Park, Lincoln Park, Hyde Park, etc.), Q&A seeding.
7. **Programmatic SEO:** Build 40–80 "service × neighborhood" pages (e.g., *"Faucet replacement in Pilsen — 2026 pricing"*). Target **$600–$1,500** freelance build.
8. **GEO content layer:** Entity-rich FAQs, pricing tables, "how much does X cost in Chicago" answer blocks — these win AI Overview and ChatGPT/Perplexity citations.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Days 30–90)
9. **Reviews engine:** Automated SMS ask post-job; target **8–12 reviews/month** to enter top-decile.
10. **Local Services Ads:** Start at **$1,500–$2,500/mo**; measure cost-per-booked-job (target <$85).
11. **Neighborhood partnerships:** Realtors, property managers, condo boards — Q4 punch-list season is your wedge.
12. **Tracking:** Call tracking + CRM; track cost-per-lead by channel weekly.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Month 4+)
13. Reinvest **15–20% of revenue** into paid + content; add a second tech at **$45k–$58k** loaded cost when booked utilization exceeds 75%.
