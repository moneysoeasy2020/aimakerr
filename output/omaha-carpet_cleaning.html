## 2026 Q4 Omaha Market Analysis

Omaha's carpet cleaning market is a **$38M–$46M annual revenue pool** serving roughly **245,000 occupied housing units** across Douglas, Sarpy, and Washington counties. The metro's median household income sits near **$78,500**, and its large rental stock (approximately **34% renter-occupied**) plus a strong insurance-restoration pipeline (hail/water damage claims) creates steady recurring demand. Typical residential jobs bill **$180–$320 for 3 rooms** and **$0.28–$0.45 per sq ft** for whole-home work; commercial contracts run **$0.18–$0.30 per sq ft**. Truck-mount operators in Omaha charge a **$65–$95 minimum service call**, and add-ons (pet treatment, protectant, tile/grout) carry **35–55% gross margins**.

Startup capital for a lean owner-operator van setup in Omaha is **$18,000–$42,000** (used truck-mount $9K–$22K, van $6K–$15K, chemicals/supplies $1.2K–$2.5K, licensing/insurance $1.5K–$3K). Realistic year-one targets: **8–14 jobs/week at an average ticket of $240**, yielding **$100K–$175K gross revenue** and **$55K–$95K owner profit** after fuel, chemicals, and marketing. Customer acquisition cost in the Omaha metro averages **$28–$55 per booked job** via Google Local Services and **$12–$24** via organic/local SEO once established.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with **Nebraska Secretary of State** ($100 filing).
2. Obtain **Omaha Business License** via the city clerk (~$50–$150).
3. Secure **general liability + commercial auto insurance** ($1,400–$2,800/yr).
4. Confirm **IICRC certification** track (CCT course ~$500–$900).

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Vehicle (Weeks 2–4)
- Source used truck-mount (HydraMaster, Prochem) from regional dealers.
- Budget **$12K–$25K** all-in for van + machine + hoses + wand.
- Stock chemicals: pre-spray, rinse, protectant, pet enzyme, spotter kit.

### Phase 3 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6)
1. Launch **GEO-optimized website** with suburb programmatic pages.
2. Fully build **Google Business Profile** (categories, services, 20+ photos, review schema).
3. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** + call tracking.
4. Deploy **review automation** (target 25 reviews in 60 days).

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–12)
- Run **$600–$1,000/mo Google Ads** on high-intent terms.
- Publish **4 local content pieces/mo** (e.g., "How much does carpet cleaning cost in Papillion?").
- Partner with **12–20 Omaha property managers** and 3–5 realtors for referral flow.
- List on **Thumbtack, Angi, Yelp** (track CAC per channel).

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 4–12)
- Add **second van** once weekly bookings exceed **18**.
- Introduce **commercial contracts** (offices, clinics, apartments).
- Layer **email/SMS reactivation** campaigns (target 22% repeat rate).
- Reinvest **8–12% of revenue** into SEO + LSA to compound organic leads.
