## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity formation:** LLC with Louisiana Secretary of State ($100 filing); EBR Parish occupational license (~$50–$150); state sales tax registration if selling products.
2. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) at **$550–$1,100/year**; add commercial auto if using a van.
3. **Equipment decision:** Portable extractor + low-moisture encapsulation starter kit ($8,500–$14,000) vs. used truck-mount ($22,000–$35,000). For Q4 launch, **portable-first** preserves cash.
4. **GBP creation:** Service-area business (hide address), categories: *Carpet Cleaning* (primary), *Upholstery Cleaning Service*, *Water Damage Restoration Service*. Service radius: 25 miles covering EBR, Livingston, Ascension, West Baton Rouge.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 3–6)
5. **Website:** 6 core pages + 12 programmatic location pages (e.g., *Carpet Cleaning in Zachary LA*, *Central*, *Denham Springs*, *Prairieville*, *Baker*, *Port Allen*). Each page needs unique local data (median home age, neighborhood names, storm-history context).
6. **Schema markup:** LocalBusiness + Service + FAQPage + Review on every relevant page.
7. **Citation build:** Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Baton Rouge Chamber, Houzz — consistent NAP across all.
8. **Review engine:** Automate post-job SMS review requests. Target **25+ Google reviews within 90 days** — this is the #1 ranking lever in this vertical.

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 6–10)
9. **Google Local Services Ads:** Launch at **$500–$800/month budget**; LSA ranks above paid search and converts at 2–3x standard PPC in home services.
10. **Property manager outreach:** Target **15–20 apartment complexes** near LSU and in Central/Zachary. Offer turn pricing at $95–$120/unit.
11. **LSU move-out campaign:** Pre-book August and December slots starting week 8; partner with 2–3 student housing Facebook groups.

### Phase 4 — GEO Content Engine (Weeks 10–16)
12. Publish **8–12 GEO-optimized articles** answering high-intent queries: *"How much does carpet cleaning cost in Baton Rouge?"*, *"Best carpet cleaning for pet stains in Louisiana humidity"*, *"Apartment carpet cleaning requirements Baton Rouge"*. Include exact prices, local references, and FAQ schema.
13. **Quarterly refresh:** Update pricing pages and location pages each quarter — generative engines favor freshness signals.

### Phase 5 — Scale Triggers (Month 6–12)
14. Hire first technician when **booked-job utilization exceeds 70%** for 6 consecutive weeks.
15. Add truck-mount when monthly gross exceeds **$14,000**.
16. Evaluate agency retainer only after **$25K/month gross** — before that, freelance stack wins on ROI.
