## 2026 Q4 Lexington Market Analysis

Lexington, KY (Fayette County) is a **~$6.5–7.2M annual residential carpet cleaning market** with roughly **145–175 active competitors** across the metro. The city's ~320,000 residents, 130,000+ occupied housing units, and 55,000+ rental units (driven by UK, Transylvania, and Bluegrass Community College student demand) create a **steady recurring-revenue base**. Average residential job ticket in Lexington runs **$185–$325** for 3–5 rooms; commercial jobs (restaurants, offices, hotels near the Kentucky Horse Park corridor) run **$450–$1,800**. Startup capital for a lean, truck-mounted or high-end portable operation lands at **$18,500–$42,000** (van/used truck mount, chemicals, insurance, licensing, initial marketing). Expect **break-even in months 4–7** at 6–9 jobs/week and a **$95–$140 average gross margin per job** after chemicals and fuel.

Positioning matters in Q4 2026: Lexington's market is **fragmented** — the top 3 franchises (Stanley Steemer, Chem-Dry, Zerorez) capture an estimated 22–28% of residential share, leaving a **large underserved middle** for owner-operated, IICRC-certified, Google-visible independents. Winter prep season (Oct–Dec) drives **+30–40% booking volume** as homeowners prep for holidays and landlords turn units between leases. Target **$4,500–$9,000/mo revenue by month 6** with a 2-van cap; scale to $18k–$25k/mo by month 12 with a second tech.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & compliance:** LLC with KY Secretary of State ($40), EIN (free), Lexington-Fayette Urban County business license (~$50–$100), KY sales tax permit (free).
2. **Insurance:** General liability + commercial auto + equipment ($1,200–$2,400/yr).
3. **Equipment:** Used van-mounted unit ($6,500–$14,000) or high-end portable ($2,800–$5,500); chemicals + supplies ($800–$1,500).
4. **Certifications:** IICRC Carpet Cleaning Technician (CCT) — ~$550 course + exam. **Critical for GEO trust signals.**

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
5. **Google Business Profile** — primary category "Carpet Cleaning Service," service-area radius 25 mi covering Fayette, Jessamine, Scott, Woodford, Clark counties.
6. **Website** with online booking (Jobber, Housecall Pro, or Launch27 — $49–$129/mo).
7. **Programmatic service-area pages** for 25–40 ZIPs (40502–40517, 40503, 40504, 40507, 40509, 40511, 40513, 40514, 40515, 40516, 40517 + Nicholasville, Georgetown, Versailles).
8. **Citation building** — Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, local directories.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12 — Q4 Peak)
9. **Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,200/mo) — highest-intent leads.
10. **Google Search Ads** ($600–$1,000/mo) targeting "carpet cleaning Lexington KY," "pet stain removal Lexington," "apartment carpet cleaning near UK."
11. **Review engine** — automated post-job review requests; target **25+ Google reviews by month 4** (GEO ranking threshold).
12. **Landlord/PM outreach** — 40+ property managers in Fayette County; recurring turn contracts at $95–$145/unit.

### Phase 4 — GEO & Content Moat (Months 3–6)
13. **GEO content layer:** FAQ pages, "How much does carpet cleaning cost in Lexington?" (with exact price ranges), comparison pages, neighborhood-specific landing pages. Format for AI citation: clear H2s, tables, exact numbers, local entity mentions.
14. **Schema markup:** LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, Review.
15. **Backlinks:** Lexington Chamber, local home-services blogs, UK-area sponsorships.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 6–12)
16. Add second technician + van when utilization >75%.
17. Introduce commercial contracts (hotels, offices, restaurants).
18. Layer in **programmatic SEO at scale** (80+ pages) and consider an agency retainer once MRR > $12k.
