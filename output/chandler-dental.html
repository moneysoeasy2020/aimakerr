## 2026 Q4 Chandler Market Analysis

Chandler, AZ is a high-opportunity dental market entering Q4 2026. The city's population sits at approximately **280,000 residents** (Census-based estimate), with a median household income near **$100,000** and roughly **62% of adults holding some form of dental insurance** — both above national averages. The Chandler–Gilbert–Mesa corridor has a dentist-to-population ratio of roughly **1 dentist per 1,850 residents**, which is slightly underserved versus the national benchmark of ~1 per 1,600, creating room for a new general practice.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–3)
1. **Entity formation & licensing:** AZ Corporation Commission filing, DEMA registration, AZ State Dental Board facility inspection scheduling.
2. **Site selection:** Target Chandler medical-zoned corridors (Dobson Rd, Ray Rd, Chandler Blvd). Negotiate TI allowance of $40–$75/sq ft.
3. **Secure domain + GBP:** Register exact-match or brand domain; create Google Business Profile immediately (verification can take 2–3 weeks).
4. **Hire core team:** 1 lead dentist (if not owner-operator), 1 hygienist, 2 dental assistants, 1 front-office coordinator. Chandler market wages: hygienist $42–$52/hr, assistant $22–$28/hr.

### Phase 2 — Build (Months 3–6)
5. **Build-out & equipment install** (parallel with digital asset build).
6. **Launch website** with LocalBusiness + Dentist schema, service pages, and 5 ZIP-level location pages.
7. **Citation blitz:** 30+ NAP-consistent listings (ADA, Healthgrades, Yelp, Zocdoc, Chandler Chamber).
8. **Content sprint:** 12–20 E-E-A-T articles authored/co-signed by the practicing dentist (critical for AI citation trust).

### Phase 3 — Soft Launch (Months 6–8)
9. **Grand-opening offer:** New-patient exam + X-rays at $59–$99 (Chandler competitive range).
10. **Review engine:** Automated post-visit SMS requests; target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**.
11. **Paid search pilot:** $1,500–$3,000/mo geo-fenced to Chandler + Gilbert; measure PAC weekly.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 9–12)
12. **Programmatic expansion:** Publish service × ZIP × insurance pages (e.g., "Delta Dental dentist in 85248").
13. **Insurance credentialing** with Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, BCBSAZ (start in Month 4 — takes 90–120 days).
14. **GEO monitoring:** Track AI-engine citations (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) monthly via brand-mention audits.
15. **Break-even target:** 35–60 new patients/month by Month 9; $1.2M–$1.8M annualized revenue run-rate by Month 12.
