## 2026 Q4 Austin Market Analysis

Austin's carpet cleaning market is **moderately saturated but geographically fragmented**. Key dynamics for Q4 2026:

- **Population:** ~2.42M metro, ~980,000 households; ~62% are renters or recent movers — high turnover drives recurring cleaning demand.
- **Housing stock:** 45% of metro housing was built before 2000; older carpet in Central Austin (Hyde Park, Allandale, Crestview) and East Austin drives restoration/deep-clean demand.
- **New construction corridor:** Georgetown, Leander, Pflugerville, Kyle, and Buda — builder-grade carpet in 2019–2025 homes is now entering its first deep-clean cycle.
- **Commercial demand:** ~14,000+ office/retail properties in Travis County; property managers are the highest-LTV B2B segment ($1,200–$6,000/contract annually).
- **Seasonality:** Q4 (Oct–Dec) is **peak season** — holiday hosting, end-of-lease turnover, and year-end commercial deep cleans. Launching now is strategically timed.
- **Competitive pricing:** Local average is **$0.28–$0.42/sq ft** for residential; Austin's higher labor costs (technician wage $18–$24/hr) support premium pricing vs. Dallas/San Antonio.

**Demand signals to exploit:** "carpet cleaning near me," "pet stain removal Austin," "move-out carpet cleaning Austin TX," and "commercial carpet cleaning Austin" are the four highest-intent query clusters. ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Financial Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with Texas Secretary of State — **$300 filing fee**.
2. Obtain Austin/Travis County general business license and sales tax permit (Texas Comptroller) — **$0–$50**.
3. Secure general liability + commercial auto insurance — **$1,800–$3,200/year** for a single truck.
4. Open business banking (Mercury, Novo, or local credit union) — **$0**.
5. Set up payment processing (Square/Stripe) — **2.6–2.9% + $0.10/swipe**.

### Phase 2: Equipment & Fleet (Weeks 2–4)
- **Truck-mount extractor** (used, 2019–2022): **$12,000–$22,000**; new: $28,000–$45,000.
- **Van/truck** (used cargo van): **$18,000–$32,000**.
- **Chemicals, hoses, wands, spotter kit:** **$1,500–$2,800** initial stock.
- **Portable backup unit** (for high-rises/commercial): **$2,500–$4,500**.
- **Lean alternative:** Start with a portable unit ($3,500–$6,000) + used van ($12,000–$18,000) = **~$16,000–$24,000** total to launch.

### Phase 3: Digital Buildout (Weeks 3–5)
1. Purchase domain + hosting ($150–$400/yr).
2. Build 6-page site: Home, Services, Service Areas (programmatic), Pricing, About, Contact — via freelancer.
3. Implement **LocalBusiness + Service schema** markup for GEO/AI-search visibility.
4. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (categories, services, photos, Q&A).
5. Launch Google Local Services Ads (pay-per-lead: **$18–$45/lead** in Austin).
6. Build 25+ local citations (Yelp, BBB, Nextdoor, Angi, Thumbtack, Apple Maps).

### Phase 4: Launch & First 90 Days (Weeks 5–14)
- Target **15–30 jobs in month 1** via LSA + Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook groups.
- Price at **$0.35/sq ft** introductory (below Austin avg) to build reviews.
- Aim for **25+ Google reviews in 60 days** — request via automated post-job SMS.
- Land **2–3 commercial contracts** (property managers, small offices) by month 3.
- Reinvest **30% of revenue** into LSA and programmatic SEO expansion.

### Phase 5: Scale (Months 4–12)
- Add second truck when monthly revenue exceeds **$35,000**.
- Expand service-area pages to 40+ Austin neighborhoods (Round Rock, Cedar Park, Pflugerville, Kyle, Buda).
- Introduce recurring maintenance plans (**$89–$129/quarter**) for retention.
- Target **$500K–$750K annual revenue** by month 12 with 2 trucks.
