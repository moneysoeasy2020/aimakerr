## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing:** File Oklahoma LLC ($100), obtain OK State Board of Dentistry license, DEA registration, and OKC business license.
2. **Site selection:** Target 2,000–2,500 sq ft in a medical-retail corridor; negotiate TI allowance ($25–$50/sq ft typical in OKC).
3. **Capital stack:** Secure SBA 7(a) or bank financing; budget $680K–$1.15M total.
4. **Equipment:** Dental chairs, CBCT, intraoral scanners, sterilization — $95K–$180K.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 3–5)
5. **Website + booking** (HIPAA-aware) — $3,500–$9,000.
6. **Google Business Profile** — verify, optimize, seed 15–25 reviews pre-launch.
7. **Programmatic SEO** — build 40–80 service × suburb pages (e.g., "emergency dentist Edmond OK").
8. **Citations & NAP consistency** across 40+ directories.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Acquisition (Months 5–9)
9. **Paid search** — $1,500–$4,000/mo targeting high-intent keywords; CPC $4.50–$9.00.
10. **Review engine** — automate post-visit requests; target 100+ reviews by month 9.
11. **Insurance credentialing** — Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, SoonerCare (pediatric).

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 9–18)
12. **Add operatories** as utilization exceeds 65%.
13. **Expand programmatic SEO** to adjacent suburbs (Yukon, Mustang, Moore).
14. **Introduce specialty services** (sedation, ortho, implants) to lift LTV toward $2,600+.
