## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–3)

1. **Register LLC** with Arizona Corporation Commission (~$50).
2. **Obtain Arizona ROC HVAC contractor license** (Class C-39R or B-2 as applicable) — application, exam, and fees typically **$500–$1,200** all-in.
3. **EPA Section 608 certification** for refrigerant handling (required; ~$300–$600 per tech).
4. **General liability + commercial auto insurance** — expect **$3,000–$7,000/year** for a two-truck operation.
5. **City of Glendale business license** and contractor registration.
6. **Bonding** as required by ROC (~$500–$1,500).

### Phase 2 — Operational Setup (Weeks 3–6)

7. **Equipment:** One equipped service van (used: $22,000–$38,000; lease alternative ~$550–$800/mo), tools ($4,000–$8,000), recovery equipment, gauges.
8. **Software stack:** Field service management (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber — $150–$500/mo), QuickBooks, phone/CRM.
9. **Pricing model:** Flat-rate repair menu + good/better/best replacement tiers. Target **$180–$650 repair tickets** and **$6,500–$14,000 replacements**.

### Phase 3 — Digital Asset Build & GEO Foundation (Weeks 4–8)

10. **Google Business Profile** — full optimization: service categories, service areas (Glendale + Peoria, Sun City, Phoenix 850xx), photos, Q&A seeding.
11. **Website with GEO architecture:**
- Location pages: "HVAC Repair Glendale AZ," "AC Replacement Glendale," "Emergency AC Repair Glendale 85301–85310"
- Cost-transparency pages: "How Much Does AC Replacement Cost in Glendale in 2026?"
- FAQ schema + LocalBusiness schema with verified NAP

12. **Review engine:** Automated post-job review requests; target **25+ Google reviews in first 90 days** (industry data: each review correlates with ~1–2% conversion lift locally).
13. **Citation build:** Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Arizona ROC directory, chamber of commerce.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12, Q4 2026)

14. **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$35–$90/lead in Glendale HVAC) — fastest path to booked calls.

17. **GEO content cadence** — 8–12 locally-specific articles/month (costs, rebates, neighborhood-specific pages, seasonal guides) to earn AI-search citations.

### Phase 5 — Scale & Optimize (Month 4+)
