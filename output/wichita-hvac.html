## Local Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts — Explicit Breakdown

**Brick-and-mortar Wichita agencies** charge **$95–$175/hour** blended rates, carry **$2,000–$5,000 monthly retainers**, and bundle services you may not need in year one. Advantages: local accountability, in-person meetings, established Wichita media relationships. Disadvantages: 3–5x cost, slower iteration, junior staff doing the work.

**Verified freelance experts** (check Upwork/Contra for HVAC-specific portfolios, demand 2+ local service clients, verify Google Partner or GA4 certification) charge **$35–$85/hour** or fixed project fees. You get senior execution at **40–70% lower cost** but must manage coordination yourself. **Recommendation for a Q4 2026 launch:** hire one verified freelancer for website + GBP (fixed $2,000–$3,500), one for programmatic SEO pages ($700–$1,500), and keep paid search in-house initially to control ad spend.
