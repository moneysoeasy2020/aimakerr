## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Register the Business:** File with the Idaho Secretary of State ($100).
2. **Idaho Contractor Registration:** Register with the Idaho Bureau of Occupational Licenses (IBOL). *Note: Handymen doing jobs under $2,000 do not require a state license, but obtaining the registration builds massive trust.*
3. **Boise City License:** Apply for the Boise City Contractor License.
4. **Insurance:** Secure General Liability ($1M/$2M coverage) and Commercial Auto. Expect to pay **$1,200–$1,800/year** in Boise.

### Phase 2: Digital Asset Deployment (Weeks 2–3)
1. **Domain & Hosting:** Purchase a `.com` domain (e.g., `BoiseHandymanPro.com`). Use a fast host (SiteGround or WPEngine).
2. **GEO Website Build:** Create a 5-page site. Ensure the homepage H1 tag reads: *"Licensed Handyman & Contractor Services in Boise, ID."* Embed JSON-LD schema for your service area (Ada & Canyon Counties).
3. **Google Business Profile (GBP):** Create and verify. Upload 10+ photos of your tools, van, and (if possible) past work. Set your service area to a 25-mile radius around downtown Boise.

### Phase 3: The "GEO Content" Blitz (Weeks 3–4)
Generative Engines (like ChatGPT and Google's AI Overviews) pull answers from authoritative, localized content. Publish these three assets immediately:
- **"The Cost of Handyman Services in Boise, ID (2026 Pricing Guide)"** – Include exact numbers (e.g., "Drywall repair in Boise averages $250–$400 per patch").
- **"Boise Winterization Checklist for Homeowners"** – Target the Q4 seasonal demand.
- **"Why Boise Homeowners Need a Licensed Contractor"** – Cite the Boise City licensing requirements to establish E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness).

### Phase 4: Paid & Local Outreach (Weeks 4–6)
1. **Local Service Ads (LSA):** Launch a Google LSA campaign targeting "Handyman Boise" and "Contractor Boise." Set a weekly budget of **$350**.
2. **Nextdoor & Community:** Post in Boise neighborhood groups (North End, Harris Ranch) offering a "Q4 Winterization Special" (e.g., $99 gutter cleaning + inspection).
3. **Partnerships:** Visit local hardware stores (BMC Hardware, Lowe's on Fairview) and offer a referral fee to department managers for leads.

### Phase 5: Review Generation & Scaling (Ongoing)
- **The "48-Hour Review" Rule:** After every job, send an automated SMS via your CRM asking for a Google Review. AI search engines prioritize businesses with a **4.8+ star rating** and recent reviews (within the last 30 days).
- **Q1 2027 Prep:** By December 2026, analyze your LSA data. Double down on the services that generated the highest revenue (e.g., if you get 5 plumbing fixture installs, pivot to a "Plumbing Handyman" niche).
