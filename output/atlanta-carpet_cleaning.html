## 2026 Q4 Atlanta Market Analysis

Atlanta's carpet cleaning market is projected to generate **$118–$142 million in annual residential and commercial revenue in 2026**, driven by a metro population exceeding **6.3 million** across 29 counties and roughly **2.4 million occupied housing units** — a high share of which are rental properties requiring recurring turnover cleaning. Standard residential carpet cleaning in metro Atlanta prices at **$0.28–$0.45 per square foot**, translating to **$135–$225 for an average 500 sq ft job**, with three-room specials commonly advertised at **$99–$149** and commercial contracts at **$0.18–$0.30/sq ft**. Startup capital for a lean owner-operator van setup runs **$18,000–$34,000** (truck-mount or portable unit, chemicals, van wrap, insurance, licensing), while a two-van operation with a leased warehouse lands at **$65,000–$95,000**.

Demand is structurally strong: Atlanta's **~38% renter-occupied housing rate**, high move-in/move-out velocity, and a dense concentration of property management firms (Buckhead, Midtown, Sandy Springs, Alpharetta corridors) create predictable B2B referral volume. Seasonality peaks **March–June** (spring turnover) and **September–November** (holiday prep), with Q4 2026 presenting a favorable launch window if you position against established competitors' holiday-scheduling backlogs. Key local cost realities: Georgia Secretary of State LLC filing is **$100**, general liability insurance for carpet cleaning runs **$600–$1,400/year**, and Google Local Services Ads in Atlanta average **$18–$35 per qualified lead** in this category.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Operational Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. File Georgia LLC (**$100**) with registered agent.
2. Obtain Atlanta business license (varies by jurisdiction, **$50–$250**).
3. Secure general liability + commercial auto insurance (**$600–$1,400/yr**).
4. Purchase equipment: truck-mount or portable extractor (**$8,000–$22,000**), chemicals, van.
5. Set pricing tiers: residential **$0.30–$0.40/sq ft**, commercial **$0.20–$0.28/sq ft**, move-out specials **$119–$159**.

### Phase 2: Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–4)
6. Launch 5–8 page website with **programmatic service-area pages** for Buckhead, Midtown, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Alpharetta, and 20+ additional ZIP-level targets.
7. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (photos, services, Q&A, booking link).
8. Deploy review-generation workflow (target **15+ reviews in first 60 days**).
9. Set up Google Local Services Ads (**$500 setup**, budget **$1,500–$2,500/month**).

### Phase 3: Lead Generation & B2B Pipeline (Weeks 4–8)
10. Build referral partnerships with **8–12 property management firms** in Buckhead/Midtown (target 3 signed referral agreements).
11. Launch Nextdoor + Meta local campaigns (**$800–$1,500/month**).
12. Distribute door hangers in high-turnover rental ZIPs (30305, 30309, 30324).

### Phase 4: Scale & Optimization (Months 3–6)
13. Track cost-per-booked-job; target **under $45** blended CAC.
14. Add second van once monthly revenue exceeds **$18,000**.
15. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into SEO content expansion (neighborhood guides, before/after galleries).
