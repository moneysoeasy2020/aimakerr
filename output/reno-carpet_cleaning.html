## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with Nevada Secretary of State (~$425) and obtain Washoe County business license.
2. Secure **$1M general liability + commercial auto insurance** ($1,800–$3,200/yr).
3. Purchase truck-mount or high-end portable unit ($12,000–$28,000) plus chemicals and spotting kit.
4. Get IICRC certification (~$500–$900) — a major trust differentiator in Reno.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
5. Launch a 6-page website with online booking (freelancer, ~$1,200).
6. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (categories, services, photos, service-area map of Reno/Sparks).
7. Build 25+ local citations (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor).
8. Set up review-request automation (SMS after each job).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
9. Launch Google Ads: **$1,200–$1,800/mo** on "carpet cleaning Reno," "move-out cleaning Sparks," "pet stain removal Reno."
10. Partner with **10–15 property managers** and 3–5 real estate agents for referral flow.
11. Publish 2 GEO-optimized local articles/week ("How much does carpet cleaning cost in Reno?", "Best carpet cleaning for rentals in Sparks").

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 9–16)
12. Target **12–15 jobs/week** to break even; track cost-per-lead (target <$35) and cost-per-acquisition (target <$85).
13. Systematize upsells (protectant, pet treatment, tile add-on) to lift avg ticket to **$280+**.
14. Add a second technician/van once you sustain 18+ jobs/week for 6 consecutive weeks.
