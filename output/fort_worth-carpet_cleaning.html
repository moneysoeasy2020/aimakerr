## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Step 1: Legal & Licensing (Fort Worth Specifics)
- **Entity:** Register an LLC with the Texas Secretary of State ($300).
- **Permits:** Obtain a **Fort Worth General Business License** (via the city's online portal, approx. $75).
- **Insurance:** Secure a $1M General Liability + Commercial Auto policy. *Critical for truck-mounted units.* Estimated cost: **$1,800–$2,400/year**.
- **Sales Tax:** Register with the Texas Comptroller. Note: Carpet cleaning is generally a service, but you must collect tax on **repairs/installation** if offered.

### Step 2: Equipment Sourcing (Q4 2026 Supply Chain)
- **Truck-Mounted Unit:** Purchase a used **HydraMaster or Prochem** unit (2020–2022 model) for **$12,000–$18,000**. New units are $28k+ and have long lead times.
- **Van:** A used Ford Transit or Chevy Express (2018–2020) with under 80k miles: **$18,000–$24,000**.
- **Chemicals & Accessories:** Budget **$1,500** for initial stock (Chemspec, Pro's Choice).
- **Total Equipment CapEx:** **$31,500 – $43,500**.

### Step 3: Digital Asset Deployment (The GEO Playbook)
- **Website:** Build a 5-page site with **LocalBusiness Schema** and **Service Schema**. Ensure the NAP (Name, Address, Phone) matches exactly across all platforms.
- **Google Business Profile (GBP):** Create and verify. Add **Fort Worth-specific landmarks** in the description (e.g., "Serving the TCU campus area and West 7th").
- **Programmatic SEO:** Create 15–20 city-specific landing pages for suburbs (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in **Saginaw, TX**", "Carpet Cleaning in **Benbrook, TX**"). Use AI to draft, but manually edit for local relevance.
- **Review Generation:** Set up an automated SMS/email request after every job. Aim for **25 reviews in the first 90 days**.

### Step 4: Operational Launch (Q4 2026)
- **Pricing Strategy:** Set a **"Fort Worth Standard"** price of $199 for 3 rooms. This undercuts franchises by $50–$70 but maintains a 60% margin.
- **Scheduling:** Use **Jobber** or **Housecall Pro** ($50–$100/mo). Do not use a custom-built CRM; the maintenance cost is too high.
- **Service Area:** Define a 25-mile radius from downtown Fort Worth. Charge a **$35 trip fee** for addresses outside this zone (e.g., Aledo, Haslet).

### Step 5: Marketing Execution (First 90 Days)
