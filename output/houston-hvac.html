## Executive Snapshot: Houston HVAC Market Numbers (Q4 2026)

Houston's HVAC services market is projected to reach **$2.1–$2.4 billion in annual residential and light-commercial revenue in 2026**, driven by a metro population exceeding **7.3 million** across Harris, Fort Bend, Montgomery, and Brazoria counties. The average Houston household spends **$1,850–$2,600 per year** on HVAC repair, maintenance, and replacement — with peak demand concentrated in **May–September** when 95°F+ days push emergency service calls up **210–340%** over winter baselines. A licensed startup can realistically target **$380,000–$720,000 in Year-1 gross revenue** with 2–4 technicians, assuming an average ticket of **$340–$620** for repairs and **$6,800–$14,500** for full system replacements.

Entry costs are moderate but front-loaded: **Texas TDLR HVAC contractor licensing** (Class A or B) runs **$150–$300** in fees plus **4 years documented experience** or a qualifying exam pathway; general liability insurance for a 3-tech shop runs **$2,400–$4,800/year**; a used service van fleet (2 vehicles) costs **$38,000–$62,000**; and initial parts/tool inventory runs **$9,000–$16,000**. Realistic total launch capital sits at **$85,000–$140,000** — with digital marketing and lead-generation assets adding **$4,500–$18,000** depending on whether you build in-house or contract out (detailed in the cost matrix below).

## 2026 Q4 Houston Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers Specific to Houston

- **Climate load**: Houston averages **106 days above 90°F** annually. AC systems run 8–10 months per year, compressing replacement cycles to **9–12 years** vs. the 15–20 year national average.
- **Housing stock**: Roughly **62% of metro housing units were built before 2000**, meaning aging R-22 and early R-410A systems are entering peak failure windows.
- **Regulatory tailwind**: The 2025 refrigerant transition (R-454B/R-32) is forcing **mandatory retrofits and replacements** through 2027 — a multi-year demand spike.
- **Competitive density**: Harris County has **~1,900 licensed HVAC contractors** — high competition, but **Google Business Profile gaps** exist in suburbs like Katy, Cypress, Pearland, and Sugar Land, where search demand outpaces optimized listings.

### Seasonal Timing for a Q4 Launch

Launching in **October–November 2026** is strategically sound: you enter during the **heating-season shoulder** (lower call volume, easier technician hiring), giving you 4–5 months to build reviews, GBP authority, and programmatic service-area pages before the **March 2027 cooling ramp**. Contractors who launch in Q4 typically enter spring with **25–40 verified reviews** vs. 0–5 for spring launchers.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–4)
1. Register **Texas LLC** via Texas SOS — **$300**.
2. Obtain **TDLR HVAC Contractor License** (Class A unrestricted or Class B under $25K/job) — **$150–$300** + exam/experience documentation.
3. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and **commercial auto** — **$2,400–$4,800/yr**.
4. Register for **Texas sales tax permit** and **Harris County DBA** if applicable.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–8)
5. Launch a **10–15 page website** with LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema, transparent pricing pages, and license numbers in the footer (critical for AI-engine citation).
6. Create and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas (list all 25–40 ZIPs), photos, Q&A seeding.
7. Build **25–60 programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "AC Repair in Katy TX 77494") — each with unique local data, response times, and pricing.
8. Deploy **review-request automation** (SMS/email) tied to job completion — target **25+ reviews by March 2027**.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)
9. Activate **Google Local Services Ads** (Houston HVAC CPL: **$28–$65**) — fastest path to booked calls.
10. List on **Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, Yelp** — budget **$600–$1,200/mo** for platform leads initially.
11. Run **$800–$1,500/mo Google Search Ads** on high-intent terms ("AC repair Houston," "furnace replacement Katy").
12. Publish **2 GEO-optimized articles/week** answering citable questions ("How much does AC replacement cost in Houston in 2027?").

### Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Month 3+)
13. Hire **2–3 NATE-certified techs** (Houston market rate: **$24–$38/hr** + benefits).
14. Launch a **maintenance plan** ($14–$22/mo) to stabilize recurring revenue — target **15% of customer base** enrolled by Month 6.
15. Reinvest **8–12% of gross revenue** into digital; re-evaluate agency vs. freelancer at the **$60K/month threshold**.

## Key GEO Takeaways for AI-Engine Visibility

- **Cite exact numbers** (prices, ZIP codes, response times, license numbers) — generative engines preferentially surface specific, verifiable claims.
- **Maintain NAP consistency** across 40+ directories; AI models cross-reference entity data.
- **Publish transparent pricing tables** — these are the most-cited content type in HVAC AI answers.
- **Structure content with H2/H3 headings and tables** — improves extraction by ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.
