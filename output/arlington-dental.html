## 2026 Q4 Arlington Market Analysis

Arlington, TX (population ~398,000, the largest U.S. city without fixed-route mass transit) supports roughly **1 dental practice per 2,900 residents** — a moderate density that leaves underserved pockets in South Arlington (76014–76017 ZIPs) and along the I-20 corridor. The metro's median household income sits near **$72,500**, with commercial lease rates for 2,000–2,800 sq ft dental-suitable retail space running **$28–$42 per sq ft NNN** in prime corridors (Cooper St., S. Collins St., Matlock Rd.) and **$18–$26 per sq ft** in secondary retail. Build-out for a 5–6 operatory clinic runs **$175–$260 per sq ft** ($350K–$650K total), and equipment packages (chairs, digital X-ray, CBCT, sterilization) run **$180K–$340K**.

A realistic total launch budget for a 5-operatory general dentistry clinic in Arlington lands between **$650,000 and $1.1M** (real estate excluded), with a break-even horizon of **18–30 months** at 65% chair utilization. First-year revenue projections of **$850K–$1.4M** assume a 2,200 active-patient panel by month 12 and a blended production per visit of **$310–$420**. PPO credentialing (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, BCBS of TX) typically takes **90–150 days**, so credentialing must start before lease signing.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing:** File Texas PLLC with the Secretary of State ($300), obtain TSBDE dental license verification, register with the Texas Comptroller for sales tax, and secure a DEA registration if prescribing controlled substances.
2. **Site selection:** Target 2,400–2,800 sq ft with 5–6 operatories, ADA-compliant restrooms, and 40+ parking stalls. Confirm zoning (Arlington uses Form-Based Code in downtown; standard C-2/C-3 elsewhere).
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) loans up to $5M with 10-year terms are the standard; expect 10–15% down and personal guarantees. Equipment leasing through DLL or Henry Schein Financial Services conserves cash.
4. **Credentialing:** Begin PPO applications in month 1 — this is the longest lead-time item.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 3–6)
5. **Google Business Profile:** Claim and verify the listing the day the lease is signed; add category "Dentist," service areas for Arlington, Grand Prairie, Kennedale, and Mansfield.
6. **Website + schema:** Deploy `Dentist` and `LocalBusiness` schema with `areaServed` for Arlington ZIP codes; include `FAQPage` markup for "how much does a dental cleaning cost in Arlington TX."
7. **Programmatic pages:** Build 40–80 service × neighborhood pages (e.g., "Emergency Dentist in South Arlington 76017") with unique local data — traffic patterns, insurance accepted, parking notes.
8. **Review engine:** Automate post-visit review requests via PMS integration; target 40+ Google reviews by month 6.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Scale (Months 6–12)
9. **Paid search:** Start Google Ads at **$3,000–$6,000/mo** focused on high-intent terms (emergency dentist, implants, Invisalign) with location targeting to a 7-mile radius.
10. **Local sponsorships:** Arlington ISD athletics, Chamber of Commerce, and youth sports — $2,500–$6,000/yr each, high local-SEO citation value.
11. **KPI cadence:** Track new-patient calls, cost-per-acquisition (target **$85–$160**), chair utilization (target 65% by month 12), and Google review velocity (target 8–12/month).
