## 2026 Q4 Austin Market Analysis (Read This First)

Austin's dental market in Q4 2026 is a **high-demand, high-competition, high-rent** environment. As of Q4 2026, the Austin–Round Rock–Georgetown metro has an estimated population of **~2.55 million**, with roughly **1,150–1,250 active dental practices** — a ratio of about **1 dentist per 1,900 residents**, tighter than the Texas average of ~1 per 2,300. New-patient acquisition cost (cost-per-booked-appointment) in Austin runs **$85–$220** for general dentistry and **$250–$600** for cosmetic/implant keywords, driven by bid competition from DSOs (Aspen, Smile Brands, MB2). Average general-dentistry revenue per chair in Austin is **$480,000–$720,000/year**; a 4-chair buildout typically reaches break-even in **18–30 months** at 65%+ chair utilization.

**Realistic startup capital ranges for Austin, Q4 2026:** a **de novo 3–4 chair clinic** costs **$650,000–$1.4M** all-in (buildout $180–$350/sq ft; equipment $220k–$480k; working capital 6–9 months). A **practice acquisition** of an existing 4-chair clinic runs **$850k–$2.2M** (typically 65–80% of trailing-12-month collections). A **startup/scratch DSO-style micro-clinic (2 chairs)** can launch for **$320k–$520k**. Lease rates in prime Austin retail corridors (Domain, South Congress, Mueller, Bee Cave) are **$38–$62/sq ft NNN**; suburban (Round Rock, Cedar Park, Kyle) **$26–$38/sq ft NNN**. Expect **$55k–$140k** in 2026 Q4 digital launch spend (website, GBP, paid, SEO, CRM) — detailed below.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

**Phase 0 — Feasibility (Weeks 1–3)**
- Run a **demand-density map**: 3-mile radius patient counts, competitor chair counts, and insurance mix. Austin's north corridor (Pflugerville, Round Rock) is **underserved relative to south Austin**.
- Secure **DSO/partner financing** or SBA 7(a) — expect 10–15% down, 7–10 year terms.

**Phase 1 — Entity & Compliance (Weeks 2–6)**
- Texas dental license + **TDA** membership; register **PLLC** with Texas SOS ($300 filing).
- HIPAA compliance stack, malpractice insurance ($3k–$8k/yr), and Texas sales-tax exemption for dental devices.

**Phase 2 — Location & Buildout (Weeks 4–20)**
- Target **1,800–2,600 sq ft** for 3–4 chairs. Negotiate **TI allowance of $40–$90/sq ft** — standard in Austin Q4 2026.
- Equipment lead times: **10–16 weeks** for chairs/imaging — order by Week 6.

**Phase 3 — Digital Launch (Weeks 6–14)**
- Website live by Week 10; **Google Business Profile verified** by Week 8 (critical — Austin map-pack rankings take 60–120 days to stabilize).
- Launch **programmatic SEO matrix**: 8 procedures × 12 Austin-area cities = 96 pages.
- Begin review-generation system on Day 1 of patient intake.

**Phase 4 — Paid + Open (Weeks 12–18)**
- Google Ads live 3 weeks pre-opening (soft-open list building).
- Target **CPL $85–$220**; expect **25–40 new patients/month** by Month 3.

**Phase 5 — Scale (Months 4–12)**
- Add chairs 3–4 by Month 8 if utilization >70%.
- Layer **AI-search visibility** (GEO): structured data, entity-rich content, and citation consistency across Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc.
