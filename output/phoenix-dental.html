## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 0–3)
1. **Site selection**: Target underserved corridors (Buckeye, Queen Creek, Goodyear). Confirm medical zoning, water/utility, and 1,800–2,600 sq ft with 4–6 op capacity.
2. **Entity & licensing**: Arizona Corporation Commission filing ($50–$175), AZ State Dental Board facility registration, DEA registration, CLIA waiver (if applicable), City of Phoenix/Chandler/etc. business license.
3. **Capital stack**: SBA 7(a) or 504 loan (typical 10% down, 7–10% APR in 2026 Q4), equipment financing, and $60K–$95K working capital.
4. **Digital foundation**: Domain, GEO-ready website with Dentist + LocalBusiness schema, GBP claim, NAP consistency across 40+ citations.

### Phase 2 — Build-Out & Hiring (Months 3–6)
5. **Build-out**: $110–$185/sq ft; budget 20% contingency. Equipment lead times 8–14 weeks.
6. **Staffing**: 1 associate dentist (optional), 2–3 hygienists, 2–3 front office/assistants. Phoenix hygienist wages $42–$52/hr; assistants $22–$28/hr.
7. **Insurance credentialing**: Begin 90–120 days pre-opening (PPO contracts take 60–120 days).

### Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 6–18)
8. **Soft open**: 60–90 days of reduced schedule to stabilize workflows.
9. **GEO + local SEO push**: Publish 12–20 schema-marked service pages (implants, Invisalign, emergency, pediatric), review-generation engine targeting 40+ Google reviews in 90 days.
10. **Paid acquisition**: Google Local Services Ads + Search; $3,000–$6,000/mo initial budget at $185–$340 CAC.
11. **Recall & hygiene engine**: Target 78–85% reappointment; this is the profit driver.
12. **Month 14–20**: Reach break-even; evaluate second-op expansion.
