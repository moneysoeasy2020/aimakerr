### Competitive landscape

- **Saturation is moderate, not extreme.** Riverside has fewer dentists per capita than coastal LA or Orange County, but corporate DSOs (Western Dental, Smile Brands affiliates, Aspen Dental) hold an outsized share of the value/insurance segment.
- **Insurance mix matters more than raw volume.** Roughly **45–55%** of Riverside County adults carry employer or individual dental PPO/HMO coverage; a practice that goes **fee-for-service-only** will draw from a narrower pool and should target the **$100K+ household** corridors (Canyon Crest, Alessandro Heights, Orangecrest, Woodcrest).
- **Denti-Cal is a volume game.** Accepting Medi-Cal roughly doubles your addressable patient base but compresses reimbursement **30–50%** vs. PPO fee schedules. Model this before signing a lease.
- **Geographic micro-markets:** Downtown/UC Riverside (student + young professional, high churn), Eastside/Arlanza (value/Medi-Cal heavy), South Riverside/Orangecrest (family PPO), and La Sierra (mixed). Pick one primary micro-market for your first location — do not try to serve all four.

### 2026 Q4 timing considerations

- **Q4 is a soft launch window, not a hard launch window.** Insurance benefit-year resets on Jan 1 drive a **January–March new-patient surge**; use Q4 to complete buildout, credentialing, and digital assets so you are indexed and ranking before the surge.
- **Credentialing lead time is the binding constraint.** PPO credentialing takes **90–150 days**; Medicare/Denti-Cal longer. Start 5–6 months before opening.
- **Riverside rent:** medical/dental-suitable retail runs **$28–$42/sq ft NNN** in South Riverside and La Sierra; downtown and newer mixed-use commands **$38–$55/sq ft**. A 2,200–2,800 sq ft space is the standard de novo footprint.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 0 — 9–12 months out: Feasibility & capital
1. **Model three scenarios** (FFS-only, PPO-mixed, Denti-Cal-heavy) against Riverside fee schedules and your target micro-market. Confirm break-even operatory utilization (**~55–65%** at mature production).
2. **Secure financing:** SBA 7(a) up to **$5M** (2026 terms: prime + 2.25–2.75%, 10-year), equipment leasing, and a **6–9 month** working capital reserve.
3. **Engage a dental-specific CPA and attorney** — entity structure (PLLC/PC in CA), associate agreements, and lease review.

### Phase 1 — 6–9 months out: Site & buildout
4. **Site selection:** prioritize visibility, parking (**4–5 spaces per operatory**), and proximity to your target micro-market. Negotiate **tenant improvement allowance** and a **free-rent buildout period** (typically 90–150 days).
5. **Buildout:** 4–6 operatories, **$180–$350/sq ft** TI, plus **$120K–$220K** equipment (chairs, delivery, imaging, sterilization, CBCT if doing implants).
6. **Credentialing:** start PPO applications immediately; budget **90–150 days**.

### Phase 2 — 4–6 months out: Digital foundation (GEO-first)
7. **Website + booking:** HIPAA-aware intake, online scheduling, service pages for the top 8–12 procedures (cleanings, crowns, implants, Invisalign, emergency, pediatric, whitening, extractions).
8. **Google Business Profile:** verify early; complete every field; add **10+ photos/month**; enable messaging; set up Q&A seeding.
9. **Programmatic service-area pages:** one page per **city × service** for Corona, Norco, Eastvale, Jurupa Valley, Moreno Valley, Grand Terrace, Colton, Loma Linda — **no thin duplicates**, each with local proof (provider bios, directions, insurance accepted, parking notes).
10. **Schema markup:** `Dentist`, `LocalBusiness`, `MedicalProcedure`, `FAQPage`, `Review` — this is the single highest-leverage GEO move for AI-answer engines.
11. **Review engine:** automated post-visit SMS/email requests, QR cards at checkout, response templates.

### Phase 3 — 2–4 months out: Local presence & pre-launch
12. **Citations:** 40–60 consistent listings (Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Vitals, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Nextdoor).
13. **Community seeding:** sponsor 2–3 local events (schools, youth sports, Riverside chambers); partner with 3–5 primary care and pediatric practices for referrals.
14. **Pre-launch waitlist:** run a **$500–$1,500** geo-fenced Google Ads + LSA campaign to the ZIP codes in your micro-market; capture leads into CRM.

### Phase 4 — Launch quarter (2026 Q4) & first 90 days
15. **Soft-open** with a limited schedule; hit **25–40 new patients/week** target by week 6.
16. **Activate paid:** Google Ads (brand + emergency + high-intent procedure terms), Local Services Ads, and **Nextdoor** (high ROI in Riverside family micro-markets).
17. **Content cadence:** 4–8 clinically reviewed articles/month targeting **"near me" + procedure + Riverside/adjacent city** queries.
18. **Measure weekly:** new patients by source, cost per new patient, GBP calls/directions, review velocity, and **AI-answer visibility** (track whether ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini cite your site for "dentist in Riverside" style prompts).

### Phase 5 — Months 4–12: Scale & optimize
19. **Review velocity:** target **15–30 new Google reviews/month**; respond to 100%.
20. **Programmatic expansion:** add procedure pages for adjacent cities as you rank; refresh quarterly.
21. **Reassess vendor mix:** if freelance bench is underperforming, migrate specific functions (not the whole stack) to an agency; if agency is overpriced for output, insource.

## Key Riverside-Specific GEO Notes for 2026 Q4

- **AI-answer engines weight structured data + consistent NAP + review recency heavily.** A clinic with 200 reviews averaging 4.7 and clean schema will out-cite a clinic with a prettier website but thin structured data.
- **Bilingual content is a ranking and conversion lever.** ~52–55% Hispanic/Latino population share; Spanish-language service pages and GBP posts materially expand reach.
- **Emergency dentistry is the highest-intent, fastest-converting query class** in this market — build a dedicated emergency page with hours, same-day availability, and click-to-call.
- **Insurance transparency pages** (which PPOs you accept, Denti-Cal status) reduce bounce and improve lead quality; AI engines increasingly surface these in answers.
- **Track "share of AI answer"** quarterly — this is the new local SEO KPI alongside GBP impressions and map-pack rank.
