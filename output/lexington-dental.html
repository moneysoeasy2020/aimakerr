A de novo general practice typically requires **$650,000–$1,100,000** in total startup capital (build-out, equipment, working capital), with **$180,000–$420,000** in realistic Year 1 operating losses before breakeven at **month 14–22**. ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months -9 to -6)
1. **Entity formation & licensing** — KY Board of Dentistry licensure, LLC formation, DEA/CDS registration. Budget **$3,500–$7,000**.
2. **Site selection** — Target 1,800–2,600 sq ft in ZIPs 40509, 40513, or 40503. Negotiate TI allowance ($40–$75/sq ft typical).
3. **Financing** — SBA 7(a) or dental-specific lenders (Bank of America Practice Solutions, Live Oak). Expect **7.5–9.5% APR** in Q4 2026.

### Phase 2 — Build-Out & Digital Foundation (Months -6 to -2)
4. **Construction** — 12–16 weeks, **$180–$320/sq ft**. Secure equipment financing separately (chairs, imaging, sterilization: **$220,000–$420,000**).
5. **Website + GBP** — Launch by **October 1**. Include online booking, insurance verification, and service-area schema.
6. **Programmatic SEO** — Build 40–60 pages targeting "dentist in [Lexington ZIP/neighborhood]" and service modifiers (emergency, cosmetic, pediatric).

### Phase 3 — Q4 2026 Launch (Oct–Dec)
7. **Google Ads** — Start at **$3,500–$5,000/mo**; bid on high-intent terms. Expect **$180–$340 CAC** initially, dropping to **$110–$190** by month 6.
8. **Review pipeline** — Automate post-visit requests; target **25+ Google reviews by Dec 31** (critical for map pack ranking).
9. **Insurance credentialing** — Complete Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, and MetLife panels **before** opening (takes 90–120 days).

### Phase 4 — Scale (2027)
10. **Content engine** — 2 blog posts/week + 1 video/month. Focus on Lexington-specific topics (UK employees, Keeneland seasonal demand, Fayette County school programs).
11. **Referral program** — Local physician/dental specialist network; budget **$500–$1,200/mo** in relationship marketing.
12. **Re-evaluate vendors** — At month 12, benchmark freelancer performance against agency proposals. Most successful Lexington clinics keep freelance SEO through **$1.5M revenue** before considering an agency.
