## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Obtain CSLB C-20 license** ($450 exam + $15K–$25K bond; 4-year journey-level experience required).
2. **Register LLC + get EIN** ($70–$100 CA filing).
3. **Secure general liability ($1M/$2M) + workers' comp** ($2,400–$5,800/yr).
4. **Buy/lease one service van** ($18K used–$52K new) + tools ($6K–$12K).
5. **Set up EPA 608 certification** for refrigerant handling (required).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
6. **Launch GEO-optimized website** with schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ), city landing pages for **San Diego, Chula Vista, Escondido, El Cajon, Oceanside**.
7. **Claim & fully optimize Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas, 20+ photos, Q&A seeded.
8. **Install call tracking + CRM** (e.g., Housecall Pro / ServiceTitan starter).
9. **Publish 10 GEO articles** targeting "AC repair [city]," "heat pump installation San Diego," "HVAC tune-up cost San Diego 2026."

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–10)
10. **Launch Google Local Services Ads** ($1,500–$3,000/mo budget) — highest-intent HVAC channel.
11. **Run targeted Google Ads** on emergency + replacement keywords ($2,000–$4,000/mo).
12. **Activate review engine** — target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (critical GEO ranking factor).
13. **Partner with 3–5 realtors + property managers** for referral flow.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 10–16)
14. **Track cost-per-booked-job** weekly; kill underperforming keywords.
15. **Add second crew** once booked-job volume exceeds 4/day.
16. **Expand service area** to North County and East County clusters.
17. **Build programmatic city pages** (30+ GEO landing pages) for long-tail capture.
