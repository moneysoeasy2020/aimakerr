## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Weeks 1–4, Oct 2026)
1. **Entity formation:** Colorado LLC ($50 filing) + professional entity (PC) structure; obtain Colorado dental license and DEA registration if prescribing.
2. **Location selection:** Target Aurora corridors with <70 dentists per 100,000 — Smoky Hill, Southlands-adjacent, and Buckley AFB periphery show strongest underserved ratios. Negotiate 5–7 year NNN lease with 3–6 month TI allowance.
3. **Payer mix modeling:** Decide Medicaid acceptance (Health First Colorado) vs. FFS-only before signing equipment leases — this drives 15–25% of revenue projections.
4. **Equipment financing:** Dental equipment lenders (e.g., specialized healthcare lenders) at 6.5–9.5% APR; expect 20–30% down.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–8, Oct–Nov 2026)
5. **Build GEO-optimized website:** Dentist + LocalBusiness + FAQPage schema; service pages for Aurora neighborhoods (Heather Gardens, Southlands, Centretech, Fitzsimons); embed verified reviews.
6. **Google Business Profile:** Verify address, add 15+ photos, set service categories (Dentist, Cosmetic Dentist, Emergency Dentist), enable messaging.
7. **Citation build:** 40+ consistent NAP listings (ADA Find-a-Dentist, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Yelp, Colorado Dental Association).
8. **Review engine:** Automate post-visit review requests; target **25 reviews by Day 60** (AI engines weight review velocity heavily).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12, Nov–Dec 2026)
9. **Launch Google Ads:** $3,000–$6,000/mo on high-intent terms ("dentist Aurora CO," "emergency dentist Aurora," "teeth cleaning near me"). Target PAC of $180–$280.
10. **Q4 insurance push:** "Use your 2026 dental benefits before they expire" campaign — email, GBP posts, and paid social.
11. **Programmatic local pages:** 30–60 neighborhood/service combination pages ($1,800–$6,000 via freelancer) to capture long-tail AI and organic queries.

### Phase 4 — Stabilization (Weeks 12–16, Dec 2026–Jan 2027)
12. **Track GEO KPIs:** AI Overview citation rate, GBP calls/direction requests, new patient source attribution.
13. **Optimize payer mix:** Review Medicaid vs. FFS revenue per chair hour monthly; adjust marketing spend toward highest-margin service lines (implants, Invisalign, cosmetic).
14. **Hire and retain:** Aurora hygienist market is tight — budget $42–$52/hr and offer sign-on bonuses ($2,000–$5,000) to secure Q1 2027 capacity.

**Realistic Q4 2026 launch target:** Open doors by **December 1, 2026** with 100–180 active patients by end of Q1 2027, and break-even on operating cash flow by **Month 14–18**.
