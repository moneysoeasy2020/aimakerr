# Launching a Dental Clinic in Columbus, OH — 2026 Q4 Startup Guide

## 2026 Q4 Columbus Market Analysis

Columbus is a **high-growth, under-served dental market** with roughly **1,900+ active dental practices** serving a metro population of ~2.2 million across Franklin, Delaware, and surrounding counties. The city's population growth (~1.2% annually) outpaces the national average, and the **dental support organization (DSO) penetration** in Columbus sits near **28–32%** — meaning independent and small-group clinics still hold the majority of market share, unlike saturated coastal metros. Realistic startup costs for a **de novo 4-operatory general dental clinic** in Columbus run **$550,000–$850,000** total (build-out $180–$320/sq ft, equipment $180K–$280K, working capital reserve $120K–$180K). Average **new patient acquisition cost** in the Columbus metro is **$180–$320** per patient, and a healthy clinic targets **25–45 new patients/month** at maturity. Typical **PPO-adjusted production per operatory** in this market is **$380K–$520K/year**.

On pricing: an **exam + bitewing series** typically bills $95–$180; **prophylaxis** $85–$150; **composite filling** $165–$310; **crown** $950–$1,650; **single implant (surgical + restoration)** $3,200–$5,500; and **whitening** $300–$650 as a high-margin elective. Commercial rent in strong Columbus corridors (Polaris, Easton, Dublin, New Albany, Grandview) runs **$24–$42/sq ft NNN** for medical-grade space, with **tenant improvement allowances of $40–$90/sq ft** negotiable on 7–10 year leases. These numbers should anchor your pro forma — not generic national averages.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4):**
1. **Market & site selection.** Run a competitor-density audit within a 3-mile radius (target: <6 competing practices per 10K residents). Prioritize growth corridors: **Dublin, New Albany, Polaris, Grove City, Hilliard.**
2. **Entity & licensing.** File Ohio LLC/PC with the Ohio Secretary of State ($99), obtain **Ohio State Dental Board** licensure, DEA registration, and CLIA waiver if applicable.
3. **Financing.** Secure SBA 7(a) or bank term loan; budget 15% contingency on build-out.
4. **Digital foundation.** Register domain, set up Google Business Profile (verify early — Columbus verification can take 2–4 weeks), build HIPAA-compliant website with `LocalBusiness` + `Dentist` schema markup.

**Phase 2 — Build-Out (Months 4–8):**
5. Negotiate lease with TI allowance; engage dental-specific contractor.
6. Procure equipment (used vs. new: used chairs save 30–45% but verify service contracts).
7. Hire: front office, hygienist(s), associate dentist if scaling. Columbus hygienist market rate: **$38–$48/hr**.
8. Launch **pre-opening marketing**: GBP posts, local sponsorships, "now accepting new patients" landing page, email capture.

**Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 8–12):**
9. Soft-open with family/friends; then paid Google Ads (geo-fenced 5-mile radius) + GBP optimization.
10. Implement recall system and review-generation workflow (target **40+ Google reviews in 90 days**).
11. Track **cost per new patient** weekly; kill ad sets above $350 CPA.
12. Month 12 checkpoint: **150–250 active patients**, **$45K–$75K monthly production** is a realistic Columbus trajectory for a well-executed 4-operatory launch.
