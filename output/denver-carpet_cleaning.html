## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & compliance:** File Colorado LLC ($50), Denver business license ($50/yr), sales tax permit (free), general liability insurance ($85–$160/mo for solo operator).
2. **Equipment decision:** Used van-mount ($6,500–$11,000) vs. portable ($2,800–$4,500). For Denver's mix of single-family homes and downtown condos, a **van-mount + portable backup** covers 90% of jobs.
3. **IICRC certification:** Water Damage Restoration Technician (WRT) + Carpet Cleaning Technician (CCT) — $1,100–$1,600 total, 5–7 days. Display cert numbers on all assets.
4. **Brand & van wrap:** $1,800–$3,200 for a partial wrap (full wrap $4,500+). Van wrap generates 3–5 inbound calls/week in dense Denver corridors (Colfax, Broadway, Colorado Blvd).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
5. **Website launch:** 6-page core (Home, Services, Pricing, Service Areas, About, Contact) + **programmatic layer** of 40–60 pages targeting `[service] + [neighborhood]` (e.g., "carpet cleaning Capitol Hill Denver"). Target $1,200–$2,800 via verified freelancer.
6. **Google Business Profile:** Complete every field, add 15+ photos, enable messaging. GBP drives 40–55% of local service leads in Denver.
7. **Citation build:** 25–35 consistent NAP listings (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Thumbtack, Denver-specific directories). $250–$500 one-time.
8. **Review engine:** Automated post-job SMS review requests (target 4.8★+ with 40+ reviews by Day 90).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–10)
9. **Google Local Services Ads:** Denver LSA carpet cleaning runs **$18–$42 per qualified lead** — start with $1,500/mo budget, prioritize LSA over traditional PPC for Q4.
10. **Google Ads (Search):** $800–$1,200/mo targeting high-intent + seasonal terms ("holiday carpet cleaning Denver," "salt stain removal").
11. **Programmatic SEO activation:** Publish 3–5 neighborhood pages/week; internally link to service and pricing pages. Expect first rankings in 45–75 days.
12. **Commercial outreach:** Direct-mail and email to 100 DTC/RiNo office managers in October (year-end budget angle).

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 10–13)
13. **GEO/AI-answer optimization:** Add FAQ schema, pricing tables, and "Denver" entity mentions to every page. Track referrals from ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews (target 10%+ of leads by Month 6).
14. **Second truck decision:** Trigger at 65%+ utilization of first truck for 3 consecutive weeks (typically Month 5–7 at $30K+ monthly revenue).
15. **Seasonal bridge:** Launch a February–March "post-holiday refresh" campaign at $149 for 3 rooms to smooth the trough.
