# Launching an HVAC Services Business in Plano, TX
### 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Plano's HVAC market is one of the most competitive in the DFW metroplex, but demand remains structurally strong. The city's 2026 estimated population is ~292,000 across roughly 108,000 occupied housing units, with a median household income near $105,000 and a median home age of ~28 years — meaning a large share of the housing stock is entering the 20–35 year window where full system replacements (not just repairs) become routine. Collin County's 2026 HVAC replacement market is estimated at $340M–$410M annually, with Plano capturing roughly 18–22% of that spend. A realistic residential HVAC replacement in Plano runs **$8,500–$14,500** for a 3-ton system (equipment + labor + permit), while a standard service call is **$95–$185** and a seasonal maintenance plan sells at **$180–$320/year**.

Startup costs to launch a compliant, insured, licensed Plano HVAC operation range from **$42,000–$78,000** (lean, owner-operator, one truck) to **$140,000–$210,000** (two-truck crew with a dispatcher). The two non-negotiable Texas requirements are an **TDLR Air Conditioning & Refrigeration Contractor License** (Class A or B) and **$2M general liability insurance** — both are prerequisites before you can legally pull permits in Plano. Your realistic path to $1M annual revenue is 14–22 months if you win the local search and review-game early; it is 30–40 months if you rely on word-of-mouth alone.
