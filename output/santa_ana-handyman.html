## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity:** Form an LLC with the CA Secretary of State (**$70 online filing**, ~$800/yr minimum franchise tax).
2. **License:** Apply for CSLB **C-33 (Painting) or B (General Building)** if doing jobs >$500; otherwise operate strictly under the handyman exemption.
3. **Insurance:** General liability (**$600–$1,400/yr** for a solo operator) + workers' comp if hiring.
4. **Santa Ana Business License:** File with the City of Santa Ana Finance Department (**~$50–$250/yr**, tiered by gross receipts).
5. **Bond:** $25,000 contractor bond if CSLB-licensed (**~$150–$400/yr** premium).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
6. **Domain + website:** Target `santaana-handyman.com`-style exact-match or brand+service domain.
7. **Google Business Profile:** Verify at a real Santa Ana address (home-based OK if compliant). Add **all 20+ service categories**, 10+ photos, and a Q4 promo post.
8. **Programmatic SEO pages:** Build 20–40 pages targeting `[service] in [neighborhood]` — e.g., *"drywall repair in Floral Park," "handyman in 92707," "ADU contractor in East Santa Ana."*
9. **Spanish landing page:** Non-negotiable given 55%+ Spanish-speaking households.

### Phase 3 — Trust & Reviews (Weeks 4–8)
10. **Review engine:** Automate SMS review requests post-job. Target **25+ Google reviews by end of Q4** (critical for Local Pack + AI answer citation).
11. **Local citations:** Yelp, Nextdoor, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Santa Ana Chamber of Commerce.
12. **Portfolio:** 10 before/after photo sets (Santa Ana jobs preferred — geographic relevance boosts local ranking).

### Phase 4 — Paid + B2B (Weeks 6–12)
13. **Google Local Services Ads:** Budget **$800–$1,500/mo**; LSA leads convert 3–5x better than cold PPC for home services.
14. **Landlord/property-manager outreach:** Santa Ana's ~54% rental stock is your highest-LTV B2B channel. Target 15–20 local property managers with a flat-rate maintenance offer.
15. **Nextdoor + community:** Sponsor a Santa Ana neighborhood event (**$250–$600**) for local brand lift.

### Phase 5 — Q4 2026 Seasonal Push (Weeks 10–13)
16. **Holiday repair bundle:** "Pre-holiday home refresh" package (paint touch-ups + fixture installs + drywall) at **$450–$750** — high-margin, fast-turn.
17. **Year-end tax-season angle:** Market to landlords needing **before-Dec-31 repair write-offs**.
