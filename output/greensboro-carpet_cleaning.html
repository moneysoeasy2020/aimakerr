## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & compliance:** File NC LLC ($125), obtain Greensboro business privilege license, EIN, and open a business bank account.
2. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto + optional pollution rider — budget **$1,800–$3,200/year**.
3. **Equipment decision:** Used van-mounted unit ($6,000–$12,000) vs. portable ($2,500–$4,500). For Greensboro's mix of single-family homes and apartments, **van-mounted wins on ticket size and speed**.
4. **Chemicals & supplies:** $600–$1,100 initial stock; establish a vendor account (Interlink, Jon-Don).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5, parallel)
5. **Google Business Profile:** Claim, verify, and complete 100% — categories, service areas (Greensboro, High Point, Jamestown, Summerfield, Oak Ridge), photos, Q&A. This is your **highest-ROI asset** in a market where map-pack visibility drives 40–60% of inbound calls.
6. **Website:** 5–8 core pages + booking form + click-to-call. Target load under 2.5s.
7. **Programmatic service-area pages:** Build 20–40 pages (service × neighborhood: "carpet cleaning in Irving Park," "pet stain removal in Sunset Hills"). Use unique local details per page — never templated filler.
8. **Citations:** Consistent NAP across 25–35 directories (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
9. **Review engine:** Automated post-job review requests; target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (the local trust threshold).
10. **Google Local Services Ads:** Apply early — verification takes 2–4 weeks; budget **$600–$1,200/mo** to start.
11. **Partnerships:** Pitch **8–12 property managers** and 3–5 realtors serving UNCG/NC A&T rental corridors; offer turnover pricing at $0.22–$0.30/sq ft.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)
12. **Track unit economics weekly:** cost per lead, close rate (target 35–50%), average ticket, review velocity.
13. **Add a second revenue line:** upholstery, tile & grout, or post-construction cleaning — each lifts average ticket **$80–$200**.
14. **Reinvest at $12K/month:** hire a part-time tech ($18–$24/hr) before adding paid spend.
