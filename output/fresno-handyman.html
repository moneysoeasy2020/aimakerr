# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Fresno, CA

Fresno's handyman and contractor market in Q4 2026 is defined by a housing stock of roughly **195,000 occupied units**, of which approximately **62% were built before 1990** — driving steady demand for repair, remodel, and aging-in-place work. The Fresno metro population sits near **1.02 million**, with a median household income around **$72,000** and a homeownership rate near **54%**. ## Licensing, Legal & Insurance (Fresno-Specific)

- **CSLB licensing:** Jobs **$500+** (labor + materials) require a California contractor license. Handyman exemption caps at **$500 per job**. Decide early: stay handyman (<$500 jobs) or get licensed (B-General or specialty).
- **Fresno business license:** City of Fresno business tax certificate required; Clovis has a separate registration.
- **Insurance:** General liability **$1M/$2M** runs **$1,200–$3,200/year** for a solo operator; workers' comp required with employees.
- **Bond:** $25,000 contractor bond (CSLB) for licensed contractors.
- **LLC formation:** ~$70 CA filing fee + **$800/year** franchise tax (first-year exemption may apply).
