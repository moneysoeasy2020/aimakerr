The metro area's ~870,000 residents across East Baton Rouge, Ascension, Livingston, and West Baton Rouge parishes support roughly **340–380 licensed HVAC contractors**, leaving meaningful whitespace in the **North Baton Rouge, Zachary, and Denham Springs corridors** where response times routinely exceed 4 hours during peak cooling season (May–September, when 78% of revenue is booked). ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–6)
1. Register LLC with **Louisiana Secretary of State** ($100 filing, ~3 business days).
2. Obtain **LSLBC Mechanical Contractor license** if targeting jobs >$7,500 (exam scheduling runs 4–8 weeks).
3. Secure **EPA Section 608 Universal certification** ($350–$600 course).
4. File **East Baton Rouge Parish occupational license** ($50–$150) + sales tax registration.
5. Bind **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and commercial auto — expect **$2,800–$4,600/yr** for one truck.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 4–10)
6. Launch website with **programmatic service-area pages** for Baton Rouge, Zachary, Central, Denham Springs, Baker, and Prairieville.
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, 20+ photos, Q&A seeded).
8. Deploy **review-generation SMS automation** (target: 40+ reviews by month 6).
9. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** ($18–$42 per qualified lead in this market) before Google Ads.

### Phase 3 — Operations & Fleet (Weeks 6–14)
10. Acquire one used service van ($14,000–$26,000) + tooling ($4,500–$8,000).
11. Stock R-410A and R-454B inventory; register with **Johnstone Supply** and **Baker Distributing** (Baton Rouge branches).
12. Implement dispatch software (**ServiceTitan** entry tier ~$400/mo or **Housecall Pro** ~$189/mo).

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 10–16)
13. Launch **maintenance-plan offer** ($189 intro) to build recurring base.
14. Run **geo-targeted direct mail** to ZIPs 70810, 70816, 70817 (homeowner density, system age >10 yrs).
15. Establish **2–3 commercial referral partnerships** (property managers, restaurant groups).

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 5–12)
16. Add second truck at **$18k–$22k/mo revenue threshold**.
17. Hire **one EPA-certified tech** ($52,000–$68,000 base + spiffs in this market).
18. Expand programmatic SEO to **90+ pages** targeting long-tail queries ("AC not cooling in Zachary LA").
