# Launching a Carpet Cleaning Business in Corpus Christi, TX
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Corpus Christi's carpet cleaning market is defined by a **~$180–$320 average residential job ticket** (3–4 rooms + hallway), a **$55–$85/hour** prevailing labor rate for certified technicians, and **~$0.28–$0.42 per sq ft** for commercial hot-water extraction. The metro (Nueces, San Patricio, Aransas counties) supports roughly **115–135 active carpet cleaning operators**, but fewer than 20 hold IICRC certification — a meaningful differentiation lever. Startup capital realistically lands between **$18,500 (used van + portable unit)** and **$62,000 (new van + truck-mount + branded wrap)**; break-even typically occurs at **22–30 jobs/month** at a $240 blended ticket.

Corpus Christi's **~28% renter-occupied housing rate**, ~$58,000 median household income, and heavy concentration of property managers in the **Southside, Calallen, and Padre Island** ZIPs (78412, 78414, 78418) create a predictable B2B referral base. Expect **$1,400–$2,800/month** in realistic digital marketing spend to reach 25+ jobs/month within 6 months.
