# Launching an HVAC Services Business in Irving, TX
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Irving, TX (Dallas County) is a high-opportunity HVAC market: roughly **256,000 residents** across ~**95,000 households**, a **median household income near $78,000**, and a climate that demands year-round HVAC attention — summer highs routinely exceed **95–100°F** and winter freezes (e.g., the 2021 Uri event) drive emergency heating calls. A realistic residential HVAC service startup should budget **$85,000–$160,000** in total launch capital, with **$35,000–$55,000** tied up in vehicles, tools, refrigerant recovery equipment, and licensing before the first invoice.

Realistic 2026 Q4 pricing for Irving: diagnostic/service call **$89–$149**, standard residential AC tune-up **$129–$249**, refrigerant recharge (R-410A, per lb) **$45–$95**, common capacitor/contactor repairs **$180–$420**, full system replacement **$6,500–$14,500** (16 SEER2–18 SEER2 split systems), and commercial RTU service contracts **$1,200–$3,600/year**. Target **8–14 service calls/week per technician** at a **$185–$260 average ticket** to reach break-even within **9–14 months**.
