# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Houston, TX
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Houston's handyman and contractor market is projected at **$1.9B–$2.2B in annual residential service spend** entering Q4 2026, driven by 2.3M+ occupied housing units across Harris, Fort Bend, and Montgomery counties. The median age of Houston housing stock is **~38 years**, meaning repair, retrofit, and deferred-maintenance demand is structurally high. A solo handyman targeting **$95–$145/hour** (Houston metro average for licensed general handyman work in Q4 2026) can realistically hit **$8,500–$14,000/month gross** within 90 days of launch, assuming 60–70% billable utilization. A two-crew operation targeting **$22,000–$38,000/month** is achievable by month 9–12 with disciplined lead flow.

Realistic startup capital for a compliant Houston handyman operation ranges **$4,200–$11,500** depending on vehicle status, tool inventory, and licensing tier. Texas does **not** require a state handyman license for jobs under **$50,000** (per Tex. Occ. Expect **$1,800–$3,400/year** for GL + commercial auto + tools coverage through a Texas-specialized carrier.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. **File Texas LLC** via SOSDirect — **$300** filing fee, 2–3 business days.
2. **Obtain EIN** (free, IRS online).
3. **Secure GL insurance** — $1M/$2M via Texas carrier (Hiscox, Next, or local broker) — **$1,200–$2,400/yr**.
4. **Register Harris County DBA** if operating under a trade name — **$15–$25**.
5. **Open business banking** (Mercury, Novo, or local credit union) — free to $25/mo.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–4)
6. **Deploy website** with 8 core pages + **programmatic service-area pages** for 30–40 Houston ZIPs (77002–77099 priority, plus Katy/Sugar Land/Cypress).
7. **Claim and optimize Google Business Profile** — primary category "Handyman," secondary "Contractor." Upload 15+ geo-tagged job photos in week 1.
8. **Build citation stack**: Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor Business, BBB, Houzz, Porch — **~$0–$150 total**.
9. **Install schema markup** (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage) — critical for GEO/AI-search visibility.

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation Engine (Weeks 4–8)
10. **Launch Google Local Services Ads** — Houston handyman LSA cost-per-lead averages **$22–$48**; budget **$1,200–$2,000/mo**.
11. **Set up review-request automation** (Jobber or Housecall Pro) — target **15 reviews in 60 days**, **40 in 180 days**.
12. **Pitch 10–15 property management firms** in November — Q4 is their vendor-selection window.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Retention (Weeks 8–16)
13. **Add a second technician** once monthly gross exceeds **$16,000** for two consecutive months.
14. **Introduce membership plan** ($29–$49/mo "Home Care Club") — boosts LTV 2.5–3.5x.
15. **Expand programmatic pages** to 60–80 ZIPs by month 6; track ranking in Bing Webmaster + Google Search Console.
