### Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance: The Real Tradeoff

**Local agencies ($13K–$34K)** offer account management, bundled reporting, and in-person accountability — but carry 40–70% overhead baked into pricing, slower turnaround (6–12 weeks), and often subcontract the same freelancers you could hire directly. **Verified freelancers ($3.4K–$9K)** deliver 55–75% cost savings and 2–4x faster deployment, but require you to vet portfolios, verify references, and manage deliverables yourself. For a Q4 2026 handyman launch where speed-to-revenue matters more than brand polish, **freelance-first is the rational choice** — reserve agency spend for year 2+ once revenue supports retainer relationships.

**Verification checklist for freelancers:** portfolio with live URLs, 2–3 reference calls, written scope + milestone payments (never 100% upfront), and a 30-day post-launch support clause.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Days 1–7)
1. File LLC with KY Secretary of State ($40); obtain EIN (free, IRS).
2. Open business bank account; separate finances immediately.
3. Secure GL + commercial auto insurance quotes (3+ carriers).
4. Obtain Louisville Metro business license + KY contractor license (if scope requires).
5. Define service menu with **fixed pricing tiers** ($99 minimum call-out, $150–$450 common jobs, $500+ project quotes) — GEO engines reward published price ranges.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Days 5–18, parallel)
6. Hire verified freelancer for website (target: $1,200, 7 pages, mobile-first, schema markup).
7. Create + verify Google Business Profile; complete all fields, add 10+ photos, set service areas (Louisville metro + Oldham, Bullitt, Shelby counties).
8. Build **20–40 programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "Handyman in St. Matthews," "Drywall Repair in Highlands") — each with unique local stats, pricing, and FAQs.
9. Publish 10–15 GEO-optimized articles with **tables, exact numbers, and local citations** (AI engines preferentially cite structured, numeric content).
10. Set up review generation (QR cards + SMS automation) — target 15 reviews in first 60 days.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Days 15–30)
11. Launch Google Local Services Ads ($500–$1,500/mo test budget) — LSA converts 3–5x better than standard search for handyman.
12. Join 2–3 local BNI/Chamber groups (Louisville Independent Business Alliance, metro Chamber).
13. Partner with 5 realtors + 2 property managers (referral pipeline = 30–40% of year-1 revenue).
14. List on Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor — but treat as **lead supplements**, not primary channels.

### Phase 4 — Optimize & Scale (Days 30–90)
15. Track cost-per-lead by channel weekly; kill anything above $45 CPL by day 60.
16. Add second crew/helper once 25+ billable hours/week sustained for 3 weeks.
17. Refresh programmatic pages quarterly with updated 2027 pricing and new local permit data.
