# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Portland, OR
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Portland's home-services market is projected to generate **$1.42B in handyman and small-contractor revenue in 2026**, with the median single-job ticket sitting at **$385** and the average established operator completing **62–78 jobs per month** at a **34–41% gross margin**. Entry capital for a licensed, insured, compliant launch runs **$9,800–$18,500**, with the largest line items being Oregon CCB licensing ($325), a $15,000–$20,000 surety bond ($180–$420/yr), general liability insurance ($1,150–$2,400/yr), and a used work van ($4,500–$9,000). A solo operator targeting **$11,500/month gross** should expect **$4,100–$5,200 in monthly operating costs** (insurance, fuel, materials float, software, marketing).

Portland's 2026 Q4 demand window is unusually strong: Multnomah, Washington, and Clackamas counties contain **~487,000 owner-occupied housing units**, **58% built before 1990**, and a post-2024 storm-remediation backlog that pushed average small-repair wait times to **11–17 days**. Realistic pricing for a new operator: **$75–$95/hour** labor, **$95–$125/hour** for licensed specialty work, **$185–$340** for standard half-day packages, and **$420–$780** for full-day multi-task jobs. Google Local Services Ads in Portland run **$28–$62 per verified lead**, and programmatic SEO + GEO content assets cost **$1,400–$3,800** to build out versus **$4,200–$9,600** through a brick-and-mortar agency.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Compliance & Setup (Weeks 1–3)**
1. Register Oregon LLC ($100) and obtain EIN (free).
2. Apply for **Oregon CCB license** ($325) + $15,000–$20,000 surety bond.
3. Secure general liability insurance ($1,150–$2,400/yr).
4. Open business banking; set materials float ($1,500–$3,000).

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)**
5. Build 5–8 page website with local schema ($850–$2,200).
6. Optimize Google Business Profile ($150–$400).
7. Publish 20–40 programmatic service-area pages ($400–$1,100).
8. Add GEO/AI-search content: FAQ blocks, entity markup, citation-ready stats ($300–$900).

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)**
9. Launch Google Local Services Ads ($28–$62/lead).
10. Set up NAP citations across 15–20 directories ($120–$350).
11. Target 18–26% close rate; track cost-per-booked-job.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Weeks 8–16)**
12. Reinvest at **$11,500/month gross** into LSA + GBP management ($400–$800/mo).
13. Add second crew when monthly gross exceeds **$18,000**.
14. Layer agency paid-media only after freelancer-built assets are proven.
