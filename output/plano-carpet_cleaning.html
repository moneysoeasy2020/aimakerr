## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Setup (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Entity:** File a Texas LLC ($300 state fee) and register with the **Texas Comptroller** for sales tax (cleaning services are generally exempt, but confirm). Get an EIN free from the IRS.
2. **Licensing:** Texas does **not** license carpet cleaners statewide — but Plano requires a **general business permit** and, if you store equipment at a commercial address, a **Certificate of Occupancy**. Confirm zoning with Plano's Planning Department.
3. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) runs **$450–$900/year**; add commercial auto and, if hiring, workers' comp.
4. **Equipment:** Truck-mount vs. portable. For Plano's mix of homes, a **used truck-mount ($12K–$25K)** or a **high-end portable ($3,500–$6,500)** plus a van ($8K–$20K used) is the realistic entry. Budget $1,200 for chemicals, hoses, and spotting kits.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
5. **Google Business Profile:** Claim and fully optimize. Primary category: *Carpet cleaning service*. Add service areas: Plano, Frisco, Allen, McKinney, Richardson. Upload 15+ geo-tagged photos.
6. **Website:** 5–7 pages minimum — Home, Services, Service Areas (programmatic pages per city), About, Reviews, Booking. Embed schema markup (`LocalBusiness`, `Service`).
7. **Tracking:** Google Analytics 4, call tracking (CallRail ~$45/mo), and a CRM (Jobber or Housecall Pro, $50–$150/mo).

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 3–8)
8. **Local Services Ads:** Launch with a **$1,500–$2,500/month budget** and Google Guaranteed badge. This is the fastest path to booked jobs in Plano.
9. **Review engine:** Automate post-job SMS review requests. Target **10+ Google reviews in month one**, 40+ by month three.
10. **Programmatic SEO:** Build 8–12 neighborhood/city pages (Willow Bend, Deerfield, Legacy West, Frisco, Allen) with unique local copy — not spun text.

### Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Months 2–6)
11. **Pricing discipline:** Publish transparent starting prices; upsell protectant and pet treatment (35–50% attach rate is realistic).
12. **Route density:** Cluster jobs by ZIP (75023, 75024, 75025, 75093 are high-value). Density cuts drive time 20–30%.
13. **Hire the first tech** when monthly revenue clears **$12K–$15K**; a second truck pays back in 4–7 months at Plano's job values.

### Phase 5 — Defend the Moat (Month 6+)
14. **Commercial contracts:** Pitch property managers at Legacy West and Granite Park offices. One $800/month contract equals ~3 residential jobs.
15. **Retention:** Annual reminder campaigns (carpet should be cleaned every 12–18 months). A 25% repeat rate stabilizes cash flow.
