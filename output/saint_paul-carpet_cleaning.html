## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)**
1. Register LLC with Minnesota Secretary of State ($155 filing); obtain Saint Paul **peddler/solicitor or home-occupation permit** as applicable.
2. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** + commercial auto — budget $1,200–$2,400/yr.
3. Complete **IICRC CCT (Carpet Cleaning Technician)** certification (~$500–$700) — a trust signal that converts in quotes.
4. Acquire equipment: used truck-mount ($8K–$15K) or portable ($3K–$6K), plus wand, chemicals, air movers.

**Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)**
5. Launch 5-page site (Home, Services, Pricing, Service Areas, Contact) with **schema markup** and Saint Paul neighborhood pages (Highland Park, Summit Hill, Frogtown, Mac-Groveland).
6. Build and verify **Google Business Profile** with service-area radius, 20+ photos, and Q&A seeding.
7. Set up **call tracking + booking** (e.g., Housecall Pro or Jobber, $50–$150/mo).

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)**
8. Launch **Google LSA + $800–$1,500/mo Search Ads** targeting "same-day" and "pet odor" modifiers.
9. Deploy **automated review requests** post-job (target 25 reviews in 90 days).
10. Partner with **3–5 property managers and realtors** in Ramsey County for turnover contracts — the highest-LTV channel.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Month 4+)**

12. Reinvest 8–12% of gross into local SEO and review velocity to defend GBP ranking.
