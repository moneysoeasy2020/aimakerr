## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register:** Indiana LLC ($95 online), EIN (free), Fort Wayne business license, Allen County DBA if applicable.
2. **Insurance:** General liability ($500K–$1M) + commercial auto; budget **$1,200–$2,400/year**.
3. **Equipment:** Used truckmount ($8K–$18K) or portable ($2K–$4K); chemicals, hoses, wand, spotting kit ($1,500–$2,500).
4. **Banking & software:** Business checking, scheduling/CRM (Jobber or Housecall Pro, $50–$150/mo).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
5. **Website:** 5–7 pages with online booking, service-area pages, schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service).
6. **Google Business Profile:** Verify, add 20+ photos, set service area to Fort Wayne + suburbs (New Haven, Leo-Cedarville, Huntertown).

10. **Google Ads:** $600–$1,000/mo on "carpet cleaning Fort Wayne" + "pet stain" + "move out."
11. **Local partnerships:** Realtors, property managers, apartment complexes (offer 10% referral).
12. **Direct mail / door hangers:** Target 2,000 homes in high-turnover ZIPs (46805, 46807, 46815).

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–12)

14. **Commercial push:** Bid office parks, clinics, churches.

16. **Review target:** 100+ Google reviews by month 12.
