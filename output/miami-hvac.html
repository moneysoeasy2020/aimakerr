## Executive Summary: Miami HVAC Market Snapshot (2026 Q4)

Miami-Dade County's HVAC services market is projected to reach **$1.42 billion in annual revenue by Q4 2026**, driven by year-round cooling demand (avg. 2,100+ cooling degree days), a housing stock of **~1.02 million occupied units**, and a replacement cycle accelerated by post-2023 refrigerant transition (R-454B/R-32). Residential service call average ticket sits at **$285–$420**; full system replacements (3-ton, SEER2 14.3+) run **$7,800–$14,500 installed**. Commercial maintenance contracts in Miami average **$1,200–$4,800/unit/year**.

Startup capital to launch a licensed, bonded, insured HVAC contractor in Miami-Dade realistically ranges **$48,000–$95,000** (van, tools, recovery equipment, EPA 608 certification, FL DBPR license, insurance, working capital). Break-even for a lean 2-technician operation typically occurs at **month 9–14** with a book of ~140–180 recurring maintenance agreements. Miami-Dade currently has **~1,180 licensed HVAC contractors** (DBPR CAC/CMC licenses), yielding roughly **1 contractor per 865 occupied housing units** — a moderately competitive but underserved-after-hours market where emergency/24-7 response commands **$150–$300 diagnostic premiums**.

3. **Capital stack:** Target **$48K–$95K**; secure a $25K–$50K business line of credit for seasonal working capital.
4. **Equipment:** Used van ($18K–$32K) or lease; recovery machine, vacuum pump, gauges, torches, nitrogen, leak detectors, ladders — **$6,500–$11,000**.
