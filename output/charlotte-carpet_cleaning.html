## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Register the business** with NC Secretary of State (LLC, ~$125) and obtain a Mecklenburg County privilege license.
2. **Secure insurance**: general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto. Budget **$1,200–$2,400/year**.
3. **Set pricing floor** at $145 minimum; publish transparent ranges ($165–$420 residential).
4. **Claim Google Business Profile** immediately — this is your #1 GEO asset. Complete every field, add 10+ photos, set service area to Charlotte + 6 surrounding ZIPs.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–4)
5. **Build a GEO-structured website** with: service pages, a "Carpet Cleaning in [Neighborhood]" template, FAQ schema, LocalBusiness schema, and NAP consistency.
6. **Deploy 15–25 programmatic ZIP landing pages** (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in 28205," "Move-Out Carpet Cleaning South End"). Each page needs unique local context — not spun text.
7. **Clean up citations** across 25 directories (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, etc.).
8. **Set up review capture** — automated SMS/email request post-job. Target 25 verified Google reviews in 90 days.

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 3–6)
9. **Launch Google Local Services Ads** — highest-intent channel in this niche. Budget **$800–$1,500/month** to start; LSA leads convert at 2–3x standard PPC in carpet cleaning.
10. **Build property-manager outreach list** — target 40–60 apartment complexes and STR operators in Uptown, South End, NoDa, University City. Offer volume pricing ($0.22–$0.26/sq ft).
11. **Partner with 3–5 local movers and realtors** for referral flow during lease turnover.

### Phase 4 — Optimize & Scale (Weeks 6–12)
12. **Track cost-per-booked-job** weekly. Kill any channel above $65 CPL after 30 days.
13. **Publish 2 GEO content pieces/month** ("How much does carpet cleaning cost in Charlotte?", "Move-out cleaning checklist for Charlotte renters") — these feed AI answer engines.
14. **Add second truck/technician** only when monthly gross exceeds $18,000 and booking utilization hits 75%.
