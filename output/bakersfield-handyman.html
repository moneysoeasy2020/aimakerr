- **Homeownership:** ~62% — higher than California average, favoring repair/remodel spend
- **Climate factor:** Extreme summer heat (100°F+ days) accelerates HVAC, roofing, and exterior wear — a recurring revenue tailwind
- **Competition density:** ~340 licensed contractors in Kern County per CSLB data; handyman-specific competition is thinner but fragmented

### Seasonal Reality (Q4 Launch)
Q4 is **not peak season** for exterior work, but it is peak season for:
- Holiday prep repairs and punch-list jobs
- Interior painting, drywall, and flooring
- Rental turnover work (Bakersfield has a ~45% renter share in core ZIPs)

Launching in Q4 lets you build reviews and Google Business Profile authority **before the March–September surge**, when demand for exterior and remodel work rises 30–40%.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with California Secretary of State (**$70–$100**)
2. Obtain Bakersfield business license (**~$100–$250** depending on classification)
3. Apply for CSLB contractor license if jobs exceed **$500 in labor + materials** (handyman exemption threshold)
4. Secure **$25,000 contractor bond** + general liability insurance (**$1,200–$2,800/yr**)

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
5. Register domain + build 5–7 page website with service-area pages for **Bakersfield ZIPs (93301, 93304, 93305, 93306, 93309, 93311, 93312, 93313, 93314)**
6. Claim and optimize Google Business Profile (categories, service areas, photos, Q&A)
7. Build 25–40 local citations (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Kern County directories)
8. Set up review request automation (target **15+ reviews in 60 days**)

### Phase 3 — Paid & Local Visibility (Weeks 4–8)
9. Launch Google Local Services Ads (**$300–$800/mo test budget**)
10. Wrap vehicle (**$2,500–$4,000**) — highest-ROI local ad for service businesses
11. Join Nextdoor + 2 local Bakersfield business groups for referral flow

### Phase 4 — Revenue Ramp (Months 2–6)
12. Target **3–5 jobs/week** at $65–$95/hr equivalent
13. Build repeat-client list (property managers, realtors — Bakersfield has ~1,200 active agents)
14. Reinvest 10–15% of revenue into programmatic SEO service pages (per-ZIP, per-service)
15. Hit **$8,500–$14,000/month** by month six, then expand crew
