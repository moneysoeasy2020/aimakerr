## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026)

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2, Oct 2026)
1. **Entity + licensing:** File Texas LLC ($300), obtain Irving business permit, TCEQ compliance check, and **$1M general liability + $500K auto** insurance (~$180–$320/mo).
2. **Equipment:** Source used truck-mount ($8K–$18K) or start with a portable ($2.5K–$4K) for residential; secure chemicals ($600–$900 initial).
3. **Banking + POS:** Business checking + Jobber/Housecall Pro ($49–$129/mo).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–4)
4. **Google Business Profile:** Verify at your Irving address; category "Carpet Cleaning Service"; add 10+ geo-tagged photos.
5. **Programmatic GEO site:** Build 10–15 neighborhood pages (Las Colinas, Valley Ranch, MacArthur Park, South Irving, Hackberry Creek) + service pages (pet, commercial, apartment turnover).
6. **Review engine:** Automate SMS review requests; target **8–12 reviews/month**.

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 4–8)
7. **Google Local Services Ads:** Launch at $300–$500/mo; target $18–$35/lead.
8. **Search Ads:** $600–$900/mo on high-intent terms ("carpet cleaning Irving TX," "apartment move-out cleaning Irving").
9. **Commercial outreach:** Target 25 Las Colinas office parks + 15 DFW-adjacent hospitality sites with $0.22/sq ft quarterly proposals.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Weeks 8–16)
10. **Recurring contracts:** Convert 8–12 commercial accounts to hit $9.5K–$12K/mo break-even.
11. **Review + citation velocity:** Maintain 10+/mo reviews; build 30+ local citations.
12. **Reinvest:** Add second van at $18K/mo revenue; hire first technician at $18–$22/hr.

### 90-Day KPI Targets
- **Jobs/month:** 45–70 residential + 4–6 commercial

- **Google reviews:** 25–40 cumulative
