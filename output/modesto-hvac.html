## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. **License & insurance:** Obtain CSLB C-20 (HVAC) license; carry $1M–$2M general liability + workers' comp. Budget **$1,800–$3,200** for licensing, bonds, and insurance setup.
2. **Entity & banking:** Form LLC ($70–$100 CA filing), open business banking, set up merchant processing (2.6–3.5% + $0.10/swipe).
3. **Vehicle & tools:** One used service van ($18,000–$32,000) + recovery/install tool kit ($4,500–$8,000). Lease-to-own is common for Q4 cash preservation.
4. **Google Business Profile:** Create and verify — **this is your #1 GEO asset**. Complete every field, upload 10+ photos, set service areas (Modesto, Ceres, Turlock, Riverbank, Salida).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–8)
5. **Website:** 5–8 pages minimum — Home, Services, Service Areas, About, Reviews, Contact. **Deploy LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema markup** (critical for generative engine citation).
6. **Service-area pages:** 10–15 pages targeting "HVAC repair in [city]" — Ceres, Turlock, Oakdale, Patterson, Newman, Waterford, Hughson, Ripon, Manteca, Escalon.
7. **Review engine:** Automated post-job review requests via SMS. Target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** — the local pack threshold.
8. **GEO content:** Publish 3–5 explainer articles ("Why R-454B matters in Modesto," "Title 24 duct rules for Stanislaus County") — this is what generative engines cite.

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 6–12)
9. **Google Local Services Ads:** Launch with $1,500–$3,000/mo budget. HVAC LSAs in the Modesto market run **$25–$70 per qualified lead**.
10. **Google Ads (search):** $1,200–$2,500/mo on high-intent terms ("AC repair Modesto," "furnace replacement near me"). Expect **$40–$90 cost-per-lead**.
11. **Referral network:** Partner with 5–10 local realtors, property managers, and home inspectors — HVAC referrals convert at **30–45%** vs. 8–15% for paid.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–12)
12. **Second van + installer** when monthly revenue exceeds $45K.
13. **Maintenance agreements:** Sell $180–$280/yr tune-up plans — recurring revenue smooths the seasonal curve and drives replacement leads.
14. **Programmatic SEO expansion:** Add 20–30 more service-area pages as you validate which cities convert.
