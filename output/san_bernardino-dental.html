## 2026 Q4 San Bernardino Market Analysis

San Bernardino, CA (San Bernardino County seat, ~222,000 residents) sits within a metro of roughly 4.6 million people and a county of ~2.2 million. As of Q4 2026, the Inland Empire dental market is projected to grow at **4.1–4.8% annually**, with average dental spend per household in the metro at **$1,150–$1,400/year** (below the California average of ~$1,650, but offset by higher Medi-Cal and Denti-Cal enrollment — roughly **38–42% of San Bernardino County residents are enrolled in Medi-Cal**, a key payer mix consideration). Commercial rent for a 1,800–2,500 sq ft dental suite in high-traffic corridors (Hospitality Lane, Highland Ave, Waterman Ave, and near Loma Linda/Redlands borders) runs **$28–$42/sq ft NNN annually**, translating to **$4,200–$8,750/month**. Dental office build-out (4–6 operatories, plumbing, vacuum, X-ray shielding) runs **$180–$320/sq ft**, or **$325,000–$800,000** total. Equipment (chairs, delivery units, CBCT, digital X-ray, sterilization) adds **$120,000–$350,000**; a full 6-op buildout with CBCT and intraoral scanners lands at **$450,000–$650,000** all-in before working capital.

A new clinic in San Bernardino should budget **$650,000–$1.1M in total startup capital** (build-out + equipment + 6 months working capital + marketing), with realistic breakeven at **14–22 months** and a target of **1,200–1,800 active patients by month 18**. Marketing spend for a new practice is typically **6–10% of projected gross revenue** — for a $1.2M Year-1 target, that's **$72,000–$120,000 annually** ($6,000–$10,000/month). PPO fee schedules in the Inland Empire average **78–88% of UCR** for the top 5 carriers (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Guardian, Aetna), and Denti-Cal reimbursements run **40–55% of UCR** — so payer mix strategy directly determines whether the clinic hits profitability in Year 1 or Year 3.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & compliance:** Form CA Professional Dental Corporation (must be dentist-owned), register with the Dental Board of California, secure DEA registration (Schedule II–V for sedation), and obtain a fictitious business name with San Bernardino County Clerk.
2. **Site selection:** Target 1,800–2,500 sq ft in a 3-mile radius of your target patient base; verify zoning (C-1/C-2), parking ratio (min 4/1,000 sq ft), and ADA restroom compliance.
3. **Payer contracts:** Apply to Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Guardian, and Denti-Cal 120–150 days before opening — credentialing is the longest lead-time item.
4. **Digital foundation:** Secure domain, launch HIPAA-compliant website (Wix/Squarespace are *not* HIPAA-compliant without BAA — use a dental-specific platform), set up Google Business Profile, and claim Yelp/Healthgrades/Zocdoc listings.

### Phase 2 — Build-Out & Hiring (Months 4–9)
5. **Build-out:** Dental-specific contractor with CA plumbing and radiology shielding experience; budget 90–120 days and 15% contingency.
6. **Equipment:** CBCT, 4–6 chairs, digital X-ray, sterilization; lease vs. buy analysis (leasing preserves ~$80K–$150K working capital).
7. **Team:** Hire 2 RDA/RDH, 1 front-office coordinator, 1 office manager; San Bernardino RDA wages run **$22–$28/hr**, RDH **$48–$58/hr**, office manager **$28–$36/hr**.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Scale (Months 9–18)
8. **Soft launch:** 4–6 weeks of friends/family + discounted hygiene to build 100+ reviews before paid spend.
9. **Paid acquisition:** Google Ads ($4,000–$7,000/mo), Local Services Ads, and Meta retargeting; track cost-per-new-patient (target **$85–$180** in San Bernardino).
10. **GEO/AI-search optimization:** Structure content for AI Overviews and LLM answers — publish "dentist in San Bernardino" service pages, FAQ schema, and location-specific pages (e.g., "emergency dentist near Loma Linda," "Denti-Cal dentist San Bernardino").
11. **Measure & iterate:** Monthly KPI review — new patients, cost-per-acquisition, production per op, and recall rate (target 75%+).
