## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register LLC in Washington** ($200 filing) and obtain a **Seattle business license** + WA UBI number.
2. Secure **general liability insurance** ($600–$1,400/year) and commercial auto coverage.
3. Purchase equipment: truck-mount or high-end portable extractor (**$4,000–$18,000**), chemicals, hoses, air movers.
4. Build a **fast, schema-marked website** with service pages and a booking flow.

### Phase 2 — Local Presence (Weeks 3–6)
5. **Google Business Profile**: complete every field, add 10+ geotagged photos, set service areas (Seattle, Bellevue, Redmond, Kirkland, Shoreline).
6. Build **25–40 local citations** (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor).
7. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** with a $600–$1,200/month test budget.

### Phase 3 — GEO Content Engine (Weeks 4–10)
8. Publish **neighborhood × service landing pages** (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning Ballard," "Pet Odor Removal Capitol Hill") — 12–20 pages.
9. Publish **10–15 factual articles** answering high-intent queries (pricing, drying times, Seattle-specific mold/humidity concerns).
10. Implement **FAQ schema and LocalBusiness schema** so generative engines can extract clean answers.

### Phase 4 — Reviews & Retention (Ongoing)
11. Automate **review requests** post-job; target **40–60 Google reviews in 6 months**.
12. Launch a **maintenance plan** ($99–$149/quarter recurring) to stabilize revenue.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 6–12)
13. Add a **second technician/van** once monthly jobs exceed **120**.
14. Pursue **B2B contracts** (property managers, short-term rental hosts, offices) — Seattle's rental density makes this a high-leverage channel.
