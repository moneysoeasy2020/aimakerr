## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–4, $8,500–$14,000)**

2. Obtain DORA HVAC contractor license (exam + fees: $400–$900).
3. Secure general liability + workers' comp insurance ($3,200–$5,800/yr).
4. Bonding ($500–$1,200).

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–10, $11,500–$21,000)**
5. Build conversion-optimized website with service-area pages for the top 20 Denver ZIPs.

8. Deploy GEO/AI-search optimization — structured data, FAQ schema, and citation-worthy local content to surface in ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews.

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 8–16, $1,500–$3,000/mo)**
9. Activate Google Local Services Ads (highest-intent channel in Denver).
10. Build a review-generation loop (target 40+ reviews by month 6).
11. Launch seasonal emergency-repair ad campaigns timed to Nov–Feb heating peak.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Months 5–12)**
12. Hire 1–2 NATE-certified techs ($62K–$78K base in Denver).
13. Add commercial contracts (property management, multifamily) for recurring revenue.
14. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into SEO/content to compound organic leads.
