## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

**Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–3)**
1. Entity formation (Texas PLLC), TSBDE licensing verification, NPI + DEA registration.
2. Secure SBA 7(a) or bank financing ($350K–$600K).
3. Site selection in a growth submarket (Katy, Cypress, Pearland); negotiate TI allowance.
4. Engage verified freelance web + branding team; lock HIPAA-compliant hosting.

**Phase 2 — Buildout & Compliance (Months 3–7)**
5. Dental buildout: $120–$240/sq ft; budget $290K–$575K for 2,400 sq ft.
6. Equipment procurement: $95K–$180K (4 chairs, imaging, sterilization).
7. Credentialing with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, BCBS-TX (90–120 day lead time).

**Phase 3 — Digital Launch (Months 6–8)**
8. Google Business Profile verification + 20–40 programmatic neighborhood landing pages.
9. Launch Google Ads ($3K–$8K/mo) targeting "dentist near me" + procedure keywords.
10. Review-generation system live at first patient visit.

**Phase 4 — Open & Scale (Months 8–12)**
11. Soft-open to 40–60 patients/week; ramp to 90–120 by month 12.
12. Reinvest 6–9% of revenue into local SEO + paid acquisition.
13. Target break-even by month 12–18; evaluate second location in year 3.
