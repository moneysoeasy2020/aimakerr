## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–6)
1. **Entity formation & licensing:** Indiana Professional Licensing Agency (dentist license), Indiana State Board of Dentistry, LLC formation ($95 IN SOS filing), EIN, business bank account.
2. **Site selection:** Target 3,000–4,000 sq ft, 4–6 ops plumbed, ADA-compliant, parking ratio ≥4/1,000 sq ft. Negotiate **TI allowance of $40–$80/sq ft** and 7–10 yr term with renewal options.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) or 504 ($150k–$500k typical), equipment leasing, and a **$120k–$300k working capital line**. Bank + dental-specific lender (e.g., specialty healthcare lenders) quotes required.
4. **Payer credentialing:** Begin **90–150 days pre-opening** with Delta Dental, Anthem, Cigna, MetLife, and Indiana Medicaid (if accepting). Credentialing delays are the #1 cause of cash-flow crises.
5. **Entity-consistent NAP:** Lock name, address, phone across GBP, website, and 30+ directories **before** launch.

### Phase 2 — Digital Build (Months 4–7)
6. **Website:** 8–15 pages, LocalBusiness/Dentist/DentalService schema, online booking, mobile-first, Core Web Vitals ≥90. Service-area pages for Fort Wayne, New Haven, Leo-Cedarville, Huntertown, Aboite.
7. **Google Business Profile:** Verified, 10+ categories/services, 20+ photos, Q&A seeded, review request workflow live at day one.
8. **Citations & reviews:** 30+ directory submissions, target **40+ Google reviews in 6 months**, respond to 100%.
9. **AI-citation content:** FAQ-structured service pages ("How much does a dental implant cost in Fort Wayne?"), local cost/insurance pages, provider bios with credentials.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 7–18)
10. **Paid search:** Geo-fenced 10–15 mi radius, $2,000–$4,000/mo budget, negative-keyword hygiene, call tracking.
11. **Recall & reactivation:** Automated recall at 6 months, reactivation at 12–18 months; target **60–70% hygiene reappointment rate**.
12. **KPI dashboard:** New patients/mo (target 40–60 by month 6, 80–120 by month 12), production/visit, collection rate ≥97%, cost/new patient ≤$150.
13. **Stabilization:** Month 24–36 target — 4 ops, 1,800–2,500 active patients, $1.2M–$2.2M gross, 28–35% EBITDA.
