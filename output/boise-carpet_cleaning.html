## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Financial Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with **Idaho Secretary of State** ($100 filing).
2. Obtain **Idaho Sales Tax Permit** (chemicals/services taxability — confirm with Idaho State Tax Commission).
3. Secure **general liability + commercial auto insurance** ($1,800–$3,200/yr for a solo operator).
4. Open business banking; set aside **15–20%** for quarterly taxes.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Operations (Weeks 2–4)
- **Truck-mount setup:** used cargo van ($18k–$32k) + truck-mount unit ($8k–$14k) + hoses/wands/chemicals ($2k–$3k).
- **Lean alternative:** portable extractor ($2.5k–$4.5k) + used SUV/van ($10k–$16k) for condo/apartment niche.
- Lock chemical supplier (Boise has regional janitorial distributors); negotiate net-30.

### Phase 3 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
1. Build GEO-optimized site with **service-area pages** for Boise, Meridian, Nampa, Eagle, Garden City, Kuna.
2. Claim + fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (photos, services, Q&A, booking link).
3. Deploy **review automation** (SMS request 2 hrs post-job).
4. Publish **schema markup** (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ) — critical for AI answer engines.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–12)
- **Local pack domination:** target *"carpet cleaning Boise"*, *"pet odor removal Boise"*, *"move-out cleaning Meridian"*.
- **Property manager outreach:** pitch 8–12 Meridian/Nampa managers with move-out volume pricing.
- **Paid ads:** start $500–$900/mo Google Local Services Ads (pay-per-lead).
- **Partnerships:** real estate agents, apartment complexes, BSU-area landlords.

### Phase 5 — Scale & Optimize (Months 4–12)
- Track **cost per booked job** (target <$55).
- Add second van at **$12k–$15k monthly revenue** threshold.
- Expand programmatic pages to **15–25 service-area + service-type combinations**.
- Build recurring maintenance plans (quarterly commercial contracts).
