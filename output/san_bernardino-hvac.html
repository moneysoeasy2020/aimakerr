# Launching an HVAC Services Business in San Bernardino, CA
### 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

San Bernardino's HVAC market in Q4 2026 is defined by extreme seasonal demand swings and a heavily underserved residential base. The metro area (population ~2.2 million) contains roughly **410,000 occupied housing units**, of which approximately **62% were built before 1990** — meaning aging ductwork, R-22-to-R-410A/R-454B retrofits, and frequent capacitor/compressor failures are recurring revenue engines. Average residential AC replacement jobs in San Bernardino run **$5,800–$9,400 installed**, while diagnostic/service calls average **$95–$165**, and dual-pack (heat + AC) replacements commonly hit **$9,000–$14,500**. Summer peak temps routinely exceed **105°F**, driving emergency call premiums of **$150–$300** above standard rates.

Startup capital for a lean licensed operation (one service van, tools, EPA 608 certification, C-20 license, insurance, and a basic digital footprint) realistically lands at **$38,000–$62,000**. Expect **$4,500–$8,000** of that to go to licensing, bonding, and CSLB fees, **$18,000–$28,000** for a used van and tool loadout, and **$2,800–$6,500** for your initial digital assets (website, GBP optimization, local SEO, and review infrastructure). Customer acquisition cost in this market runs **$85–$220 per booked job** through paid local search, versus **$0–$40** through organic/GBP-driven inbound once established.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Obtain **CSLB C-20 (Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating & Air-Conditioning)** license — exam + $400 application + $15K bond.
2. Secure **EPA Section 608** certification (all technicians).
3. Register business entity (LLC), get San Bernardino business license, general liability ($1M–$2M) + workers' comp.
4. Open business banking and set up invoicing (e.g., Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan Lite).

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6)**
5. Build a 6-page website with **service-area pages** for San Bernardino, Redlands, Rialto, Fontana, Colton, Highland.
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, hours, photos, Q&A).
7. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** (screened, pay-per-lead) — high-intent HVAC leads in this market.
8. Install review-generation automation; target **25+ reviews in 90 days**.

**Phase 3 — Operations & Launch (Weeks 5–8)**
9. Buy/outfit one service van; stock common parts (capacitors, contactors, filters, refrigerant).
10. Set pricing tiers: diagnostic $99–$149, tune-up $129–$199, emergency premium +$150.
11. Launch with a **heat pump rebate landing page** targeting TECH Clean California + 25C credits.

**Phase 4 — Growth (Months 3–12)**
12. Add second van at ~$35K/month revenue.
13. Layer programmatic SEO pages: "AC repair in [city]," "heat pump installation [city]," "furnace tune-up [city]" across 12–18 Inland Empire cities.
14. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into local search ads; track CAC per channel.
