## 2026 Q4 Henderson Market Analysis

Henderson, NV enters Q4 2026 with roughly **345,000 residents across ~135,000 households** and a median household income near **$95,000**—about 20% above the national median, which supports premium pricing on finish work. The local housing stock skews newer (median home age under 25 years) and heavily owner-occupied, with concentrated demand in **Green Valley, Anthem, Seven Hills, MacDonald Ranch, and Lake Las Vegas**. Realistic 2026 Q4 pricing for a solo operator: **$95–$125/hour** for general handyman work, **$85–$150** for flat-rate jobs (TV mounting $120–$250, furniture assembly $75–$180, drywall patch $150–$400), and **$300–$900** for multi-task "honey-do" packages. Contractor-licensed remodel work (kitchens, baths, additions) typically prices at **$75–$140/sq ft** for mid-grade finishes.

Labor is the binding constraint: Clark County's construction trades remain tight, and Nevada requires **contractor licensing (NSCB) for jobs at or above $1,000 in combined labor and materials**—a threshold that catches most "handyman" jobs, so plan for license classification early. Startup capital for a lean, compliant launch (license, insurance, tools, vehicle wrap, website, and 90 days of working capital) realistically runs **$12,000–$28,000**, with break-even at **$9,000–$14,000/month gross revenue** for a solo operator.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Register LLC with Nevada Secretary of State (**$425** filing + **$150** annual list).
2. Obtain Henderson business license (**~$100–$300** depending on classification) and Clark County requirements.
3. Secure NSCB contractor license if targeting jobs ≥$1,000 (application + exam + fees: **$600–$1,500**).
4. Bind general liability (**$500K–$1M**, **$600–$1,400/year**) and commercial auto.

**Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–8)**
5. Build website with service-area pages for each Henderson neighborhood (programmatic SEO: "handyman in [neighborhood]").
6. Launch and verify Google Business Profile; add 10+ geotagged project photos.
7. Deploy Local Services Ads with Google Guaranteed verification.
8. Publish 4 cornerstone guides (cost guides, permit FAQs, HOA rules) targeting AI-search citations.

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)**
9. List on Thumbtack, Angi, and Nextdoor (Henderson neighborhood groups convert well).
10. Partner with 3–5 local realtors and property managers for recurring punch-list work.
11. Run geo-fenced Google Ads (**$1,500–$3,000/mo** starting budget; target $40–$90 cost-per-lead).

**Phase 4 — Scale (Q1 2027)**
12. Add a second technician once monthly gross exceeds **$18,000**.
13. Layer programmatic SEO pages (100+ neighborhood × service combinations) and review-generation automation.
