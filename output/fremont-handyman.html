## Licensing, Legal & Compliance (Non-Negotiable)

- **CSLB rules:** Jobs **$500+ (labor + materials)** require a licensed contractor. Handyman work under $500 per job is exempt — but most Fremont operators still pursue a **B-General Building** or specialty license to scale.
- **Business license:** City of Fremont business tax certificate + Alameda County registration.
- **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M minimum) + workers' comp if hiring.
- **Bond:** $25,000 CSLB contractor bond (2026 requirement).
- **LLC formation:** ~$70–$800 depending on filing route; California LLC annual tax $800.
