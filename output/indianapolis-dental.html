## 2026 Q4 Indianapolis Market Analysis

**Demand fundamentals**
- Marion County population: ~975,000; Indianapolis–Carmel–Anderson MSA: ~2.15 million.
- Adults 65+ (highest dental utilization per capita): ~15.5% of metro — growing ~2% annually.
- Employer-sponsored dental coverage penetration: ~62% of metro workforce; Medicaid adult dental in Indiana is **limited** (emergency-only for most adults), so commercial PPO and self-pay mix matters.
- Uninsured/self-pay adults: ~9–11% of metro — a viable membership-plan segment.

**Competitive landscape**
- Active general dentists in metro: **~1,100–1,200**; DSO-affiliated share is rising and now estimated at **35–45%** of practices.
- High-saturation corridors: Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Broad Ripple, Mass Ave. Lower-competition, high-growth ZIPs: **Westfield, Whitestown, McCordsville, Franklin Township (46259), Avon/Brownsburg edges**.
- Average Google review count for top-quartile Indy general practices: **180–420**; median new practice reaches ~60 reviews in year one with active solicitation.

**Cost inputs specific to Indy**
- Commercial lease (medical/dental suite, Class B suburban): **$22–$34/sq ft NNN**; downtown/Mass Ave premium: **$38–$52/sq ft**.
- Dental build-out: **$180–$320/sq ft** (4–6 operatories typical for de novo).
- Equipment package (4 chairs, imaging, sterilization, IT): **$280,000–$420,000** new; **$140,000–$230,000** refurbished.
- Dental assistant wage: **$19–$24/hr**; hygienist: **$38–$46/hr** (tight labor market — budget signing bonuses).
- Indiana corporate/LLC filing: **$95**; dental board licensure and facility permits: **$1,200–$3,500**.

**Payer and pricing reality**
- PPO reimbursement in Indy runs roughly **75–85% of UCR fees**; model 55–65% PPO, 20–30% FFS/self-pay, remainder Medicaid-emergency if accepted.
- Break-even for a 4-chair practice typically occurs at **$95,000–$130,000 monthly collections**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (90 Days Pre-Launch + 90 Days Post)

### Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1–30)
1. **Entity & licensure:** Form Indiana LLC ($95), obtain NPI, enroll in CAQH, apply for Indiana dental license and DEA if prescribing. Budget $1,200–$3,500 for permits.
2. **Site selection:** Target 1,800–2,400 sq ft in a high-growth ZIP (46259, 46074, 46062). Negotiate TI allowance of $40–$70/sq ft and a 7–10 year term with a 90-day rent abatement.
3. **Financial model:** Build a 3-year P&L assuming $95K–$130K monthly collections at break-even; secure $450K–$750K via SBA 7(a) (typical 10-year term, prime + 2.75%) plus equipment financing.
4. **Brand & domain:** Secure .com domain, trademark search (USPTO TESS), and lock brand assets.

### Phase 2: Build & Digital Assets (Days 31–60)
5. **Build-out & equipment:** Award build-out contract ($180–$320/sq ft); order equipment ($280K–$420K new or $140K–$230K refurbished).
6. **Website launch:** 5–8 pages with Dentist + LocalBusiness schema, online booking (NexHealth, LocalMed, or similar), and HIPAA-compliant forms. Freelance cost: $2,800–$6,500.
7. **Google Business Profile:** Create and verify; add services, hours, geo-tagged photos, and Q&A. Freelance: $350–$900.
8. **Citations & local SEO:** 40+ directory listings, NAP consistency, and location landing pages for Westfield, Carmel, Fishers, and Avon. Freelance: $1,200–$2,800.

### Phase 3: Pre-Launch Marketing (Days 61–90)
9. **Review infrastructure:** Deploy SMS/email review requests (HIPAA-safe vendor) — target 40–60 reviews in first 90 days. Freelance: $500–$1,100.
10. **Paid acquisition:** Launch Google Local Services Ads (requires license + insurance verification) and Meta lead ads. Freelance setup: $700–$1,600; ad spend $1,500–$4,000/month.
11. **Membership plan:** Launch in-house plan ($29–$39/month) to capture the 9–11% uninsured segment.
12. **Community seeding:** Partner with 3–5 local employers and 2–3 pediatrician/primary-care referral sources.

### Phase 4: Post-Launch Optimization (Days 91–180)
13. **Track KPIs:** New patients/month (target 60–90), cost per new patient ($85–$180 via LSA), review velocity, and collections per chair ($22K–$32K/month).
14. **Content cadence:** 2 blog posts/month targeting "dentist near me [ZIP]" and procedure queries; freelancer cost $300–$600/month.
15. **Quarterly audit:** Review GBP insights, ad ROAS, and SEO rankings; adjust retainer scope.
