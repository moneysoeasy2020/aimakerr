# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Oklahoma City, OK
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Oklahoma City's handyman and contractor market is projected to support **roughly 2,400–2,700 active handyman/general contractor operators** by Q4 2026, with metro-area demand driven by ~**1.48 million residents** across OKC, Edmond, Norman, Moore, and Yukon. Typical handyman service call pricing in OKC in 2026 ranges **$95–$145/hour** for labor, with flat-rate jobs (drywall patch, door hanging, fixture install) priced **$150–$650**. A licensed general contractor handling mid-size remodels (bathrooms, kitchen refreshes) can expect **$8,500–$32,000** per project, while startup capital for a lean handyman operation runs **$6,500–$14,000** (truck/tools/insurance/licensing/bonding).

Realistic Year-1 revenue for a solo OKC handyman working 5 days/week at 60% billable utilization is **$78,000–$112,000 gross**, with net margins of **28–38%** after insurance, fuel, tools, and marketing. The largest competitive threats are established multi-truck operators (e.g., regional franchises) and the **Angi/Thumbtack lead-gen tax** ($35–$95 per shared lead). Winning in 2026 Q4 requires a GEO-first digital presence — structured local content, Google Business Profile dominance, and AI-search citation — not just paid lead buying.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Financial Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with **Oklahoma Secretary of State** — **$100** filing fee.
2. Obtain **OKC contractor license** + general contractor registration if targeting jobs ≥$1,000.
3. Secure general liability insurance (**$1,200–$2,800/yr**) and a surety bond (**$100–$300**).
4. Open a business bank account; set up **QuickBooks Self-Employed** ($20/mo).
5. **Budget check:** total Phase 1 spend **$1,800–$3,900**.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 3–6)
1. Launch a **5–8 page GEO-optimized website** with schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ) — freelancer cost **$1,200–$3,200**.
2. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas: OKC, Edmond, Norman, Moore, Yukon, Midwest City).
3. Build **30+ local citations** (Yelp, BBB, Angi profile, Nextdoor, Houzz).
4. Publish **10 GEO-targeted articles** ("handyman cost in Oklahoma City 2026," "drywall repair Edmond OK," etc.).

### Phase 3 — Programmatic Local SEO (Weeks 6–10)
1. Deploy **25 service-area pages** (one per OKC neighborhood/suburb × top 5 services).
2. Add **FAQ schema** targeting AI-search queries ("how much does a handyman cost in OKC?").
3. Set up **review-generation automation** (post-job SMS → Google review link).

### Phase 4 — Paid + Organic Launch (Weeks 10–14)
1. Start **Google Local Services Ads** ($28–$70/lead) — highest ROI channel for handymen.
2. Layer **Google Search Ads** on high-intent terms only (budget $1,200–$2,500/mo).
3. Activate **Nextdoor + Facebook local groups** (free organic reach in OKC suburbs).

### Phase 5 — Scale & Optimize (Month 4+)
1. Track cost-per-booked-job by channel; kill anything above **$85/booking**.
2. Add a second truck once monthly revenue exceeds **$14,000**.
3. Reinvest **8–12% of revenue** into GEO content and review velocity.
