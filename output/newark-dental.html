Newark, NJ supports roughly **380–420 active dental practices** serving a city of ~305,000 residents across 13,000+ acres — a ratio of about **1 dentist per 1,400 residents**, well below the NJ state average of 1 per 1,100, signaling real capacity headroom. Launch costs for a **2,000–2,500 sq ft, 4–5 operatory ground-floor clinic** in a high-traffic corridor (Ironbound, Downtown/University Heights, or Forest Hill) run **$620,000–$1,150,000** all-in, with build-out at **$180–$320/sq ft**, equipment at **$210,000–$340,000**, and working capital reserves of **$120,000–$180,000**.

Newark's median household income (~$46,000) means **Medicaid (NJ FamilyCare) acceptance and sliding-scale pricing are non-negotiable** for volume; private-pay and cosmetic services drive margin.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Months 1–6, target completion Sept 2026)

1. **Entity & compliance** — NJ LLC or PA filing ($125–$300), NPI registration, NJ dental license verification, DEA if prescribing, malpractice insurance ($3,500–$8,000/yr).
2. **Site selection** — Target 2,000–2,500 sq ft, ground floor, 25+ parking stalls or transit-adjacent (Newark Penn Station, Light Rail). Negotiate 7–10 year lease with 3-month free build-out period.
3. **Financing stack** — SBA 7(a) ($350K–$750K at 2026 rates ~8.5–10.5%), equipment leasing ($210K–$340K over 60 months), NJ EDA small business programs.
4. **Build-out & equipment** — Dental-specific contractor; 4–5 ops with digital X-ray, CBCT (optional $85K–$140K), chairs ($18K–$32K each), sterilization.
5. **Digital foundation** — Website + GEO content, GBP verified, 40+ citations, review system live **before** opening day.

### Phase 2: Launch (Month 7 — October 2026)

6. **Soft open** — 2 weeks, family/friends/referral pricing, gather 25–40 Google reviews.
7. **Insurance credentialing** — File with Delta Dental NJ, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, and **NJ FamilyCare MCOs (Horizon, Amerigroup, WellCare)** 90–120 days pre-launch — this is the #1 launch-delay cause.
8. **Paid launch** — $3,500–$5,500/mo Google Ads (geo-fenced 3–5 mile radius), $800–$1,500/mo Meta for Ironbound language-targeted campaigns.
9. **Q4 benefit-season push** — "Use your 2026 benefits before Dec 31" campaigns; emergency same-day slots advertised.

### Phase 3: Growth (Months 8–18)

10. **GEO expansion** — Publish 2–4 local landing pages/month (e.g., "dentist in Ironbound," "Spanish-speaking dentist Newark," "emergency dentist near Newark Penn"). Target AI Overview citations via FAQ schema and entity-consistent citations.
11. **Review velocity** — 20–40 new Google reviews/month; respond to 100%.
12. **Recall & reactivation** — Automated SMS/email; target 70%+ recall rate by month 12.
13. **Service-line expansion** — Add ortho (clear aligners), implants, or pediatrics based on demand data at month 12–15.

### Phase 4: Optimization (Months 19–24)

14. **Chair utilization audit** — Target 75%+; add associate dentist at 80%.
15. **Payer mix review** — Rebalance Medicaid vs. PPO vs. FFS to hit 22–28% EBITDA.
16. **Second-location feasibility** — Evaluate at $1.4M+ revenue run-rate.
