A single-chair startup build-out in a leased 1,200–1,800 sq ft suite runs **$180,000–$420,000** in total capital, with core dental equipment (chair, delivery unit, intraoral X-ray, autoclave, compressor) costing **$65,000–$140,000** and tenant improvements at **$55–$120 per sq ft**. Cleveland commercial lease rates for medical/dental suites in Q4 2026 average **$22–$38 per sq ft NNN** in inner-ring suburbs (Lakewood, Parma, Cleveland Heights) and **$30–$52 per sq ft** in premium corridors like Beachwood, Westlake, and the Cleveland Clinic–adjacent University Circle.

## 2026 Q4 Cleveland Market Analysis

### Demographics & Demand Signals
- **Metro population:** ~2.1M (Cuyahoga, Lorain, Medina, Lake, Geauga, Summit-adjacent counties).
- **Median household income (Cuyahoga County):** ~$63,000–$68,000; **Beachwood/Westlake corridors:** $110,000–$145,000.
- **Insurance mix:** Ohio Medicaid covers adult dental (limited benefits) — roughly **18–22% of Cuyahoga County residents** are Medicaid-eligible, a meaningful volume driver if you accept it.
- **Senior demand:** Adults 65+ represent ~17% of the metro — a strong driver for dentures, implants, and periodontal maintenance.

### Competitive Landscape
- **Dental Service Organizations (DSOs)** control an estimated **28–35%** of Cleveland-area practices and dominate paid search; expect CPCs of **$6–$14** for "dentist near me" and **$9–$22** for "dental implants Cleveland."
- **Independent differentiation** wins on: same-week emergency availability, transparent pricing, sedation options, and Spanish/Ukrainian/Russian-language service (Cleveland has notable Eastern European and Hispanic populations in Parma and Clark-Fulton).
- **Google Business Profile density:** In dense ZIPs (44107 Lakewood, 44129 Parma, 44118 Cleveland Heights), expect **15–40 competing GBP listings** within 3 miles — local pack ranking requires review velocity of **8–15 new reviews/month** to stay competitive.

### 2026 Q4 Timing Advantages
- Q4 lease negotiations favor tenants (year-end vacancy pressure) — **5–12% concession** on base rent is negotiable.
- Equipment vendors run Q4 financing promos (0% for 12–24 months common) ahead of tax year-end.
- January is peak "new year, new dental benefits" search season — launching digital assets in **October–November** positions you to capture January demand.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Entity & Compliance (Weeks 1–6)
1. Form **Ohio LLC** ($99 filing) or PLLC; register with Ohio Secretary of State.
2. Obtain **Ohio State Dental Board** licensure and **DEA registration** (if prescribing).
3. Secure **malpractice insurance** ($1,800–$5,500/yr) and general liability.
4. Apply for **NPI, Medicare/Medicaid enrollment** (4–8 week lead time — start early).
5. Open business banking; set up merchant processing (target 2.2–2.6% + $0.10).

### Phase 2 — Site & Build-Out (Weeks 4–20)
6. Lease 1,200–1,800 sq ft in target ZIP (negotiate TI allowance of **$25–$60/sq ft**).
7. Engage dental equipment rep; finance at Q4 promo rates.
8. Complete build-out; schedule **Ohio Department of Health** inspection and radiation safety registration.

### Phase 3 — Digital Assets (Weeks 6–14, parallel)
9. Commission website + GBP from a **verified freelancer** (see matrix).
10. Build **programmatic SEO pages**: "dentist in [Lakewood / Parma / Cleveland Heights / Westlake]" × service (implants, Invisalign, emergency, pediatric) — target 40–120 pages.
11. Set up review automation + call tracking.

### Phase 4 — Pre-Launch Marketing (Weeks 14–18)
12. Launch Google Ads at **$2,500–$4,000/mo** targeting high-intent terms; geo-fence 5-mile radius.
13. Seed **20–30 reviews** from soft-launch patients before public opening.
14. Claim/optimize all directory listings (Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Yelp, Angi).

### Phase 5 — Open & Scale (Weeks 18–26)
15. Grand-opening offer (new-patient exam + X-rays at **$59–$99**) to drive first-visit volume.
16. Track **cost per new patient** — target **$85–$180** blended; kill any channel above $250.
17. Add second operatory at **month 9–12** once hygiene schedule hits 75% utilization.

### 2026 Q4 KPI Targets
- **New patients/month by month 6:** 35–60
- **Google review count by month 12:** 120–200
- **Local pack visibility:** top 3 in 3+ target ZIPs by month 9
- **Blended cost per new patient:** ≤ $180
