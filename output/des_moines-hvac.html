## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Licensing & Compliance (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Iowa mechanical contractor license** via Iowa Division of Labor — $150–$400 filing.
2. **EPA Section 608 certification** (required for refrigerant handling) — $150–$350 exam.
3. **General liability + commercial auto insurance** — $2,800–$5,200/yr for two trucks.
4. **Des Moines business license** + Polk County registration — $100–$250.
5. **Surety bond** (if required for commercial work) — $250–$600.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6)
1. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories: HVAC contractor, furnace repair, AC repair).
2. Launch **GEO-optimized website** with service-area pages for Des Moines, West Des Moines, Ankeny, Urbandale, Waukee.
3. Build **30+ local citations** (Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Thumbtack).
4. Set up **call tracking + CRM** (Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan starter tier).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–12)
1. **Google Ads** — target "AC repair Des Moines," "furnace replacement near me," emergency keywords; budget **$1,500–$3,000/mo**.
2. **Seasonal tune-up offers** ($89–$129) to build recurring customer base before winter.
3. **Partnerships** with 3–5 local realtors and property managers (referral fee 8–12%).

### Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Months 4–12)
1. Hire first **EPA-certified technician** ($28–$38/hr in Des Moines market).
2. Add **second truck** once weekly call volume exceeds **12 jobs**.
3. Layer in **maintenance agreements** ($14–$19/mo) for recurring revenue — target **15–20% of customer base** by month 12.
