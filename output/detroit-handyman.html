## 2026 Q4 Detroit Market Analysis

Detroit's handyman and contractor market is projected to reach **$1.42 billion in annual revenue** across Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties by Q4 2026, driven by **41,000+ aging housing units** (pre-1970 construction) requiring repair and **$890M in committed residential rehab funding** from the Detroit Housing & Revitalization Department's 2025–2027 allocation. The median Detroit homeowner spends **$2,850–$4,200 annually** on maintenance and repairs, while landlord clients in the 48201, 48202, and 48207 ZIP codes average **$6,100–$8,400 per rental unit per year** in turnover and compliance repairs. Entry-level handyman pricing in Detroit runs **$65–$95/hour**, with licensed contractor rates at **$95–$145/hour**; specialized jobs command flat fees (e.g., drywall repair $350–$650, bathroom tile $1,800–$4,200, deck rebuild $4,500–$9,500).

Startup capital for a compliant Detroit handyman operation ranges **$18,500–$34,000** (license, insurance, tools, vehicle wrap, permits), with realistic break-even at **4–7 months** if you secure 8–12 recurring landlord accounts or 15+ residential clients monthly. Michigan's **Homeowner Construction Lien Recovery Fund** and Detroit's **Building Safety & Engineering** permit portal (online since 2024) reduce administrative friction — but the **Michigan Occupational Code (PA 299)** requires residential builder licensure for projects exceeding **$600 in labor and materials**, a threshold that catches most new operators off guard.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–3) — $2,400–$4,100
1. **Register LLC** with Michigan LARA — $50 filing fee.
2. **Obtain Residential Builder License** (required for jobs >$600 labor+materials) — $195 application + exam.
3. **General liability insurance** — $1,100–$2,400/year for $1M/$2M coverage (Detroit-specific carriers: MEEMIC, Frankenmuth).
4. **Michigan workers' comp** — required at first employee; solo operators can file exemption.
5. **Detroit business license** + **Building Safety registration** for permit-pulling authority.

### Phase 2: Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5) — $3,000–$7,200
6. **Domain + hosting** ($180/year) — choose `.com` with "detroit" or service keyword.
7. **Google Business Profile** — verify at physical address; add 12+ service categories and 20+ photos.
8. **Website with GEO schema** — LocalBusiness + Service + FAQPage markup; embed Detroit ZIP service areas (48201–48228).
9. **Programmatic service pages** — one page per service × neighborhood (e.g., "Drywall Repair in Corktown," "Deck Building in Palmer Park").
10. **Review engine** — automated SMS request 24 hours post-job; target 25 reviews by month 3.

### Phase 3: Lead Generation (Weeks 4–12) — $800–$1,800/month
11. **Google Local Services Ads** — pay-per-lead ($28–$65/lead in Detroit metro); requires license + insurance verification.
12. **Angi/Thumbtack/Nextdoor** — $400–$900/month blended; negotiate lead caps.
13. **Landlord outreach** — target 40+ property management firms in Wayne/Oakland County; pitch recurring maintenance contracts at **$450–$750/month retainer** per portfolio.
14. **Door hanger + neighborhood saturation** in 3 target ZIPs (48214, 48207, 48224 — high owner-occupancy, aging housing stock).

### Phase 4: Operations & Scale (Months 3–6)
15. **Jobber or Housecall Pro** — $49–$129/month scheduling/invoicing.
16. **Hire first helper** at month 4–5 when bookings exceed 22 jobs/month.
17. **Add second service category** (e.g., painting or flooring) once 40+ reviews and 4.7+ star average achieved.
