## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity & licensing**: File Oklahoma LLC ($100), obtain **Oklahoma Board of Dentistry** license verification, register with **Tulsa Health Department** for X-ray equipment, secure DEA registration if prescribing.
2. **Site selection**: Target **1,800–2,500 sq ft** in a **medical-zoned corridor** with 25+ parking spaces; negotiate **6–12 months free rent** on a 7–10 year NNN lease (standard Tulsa concession in Q4).
3. **Financing**: SBA 7(a) loans up to **$5M** at Tulsa-area lenders (avgreens 10.5–11.5% in Q4 2026); equipment financing at 7–9% APR.
4. **Digital foundation**: Domain, HIPAA-compliant website, Google Business Profile verified (allow **2–3 weeks** for Tulsa verification), local citations (Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Tulsa Chamber).

### Phase 2 — Buildout & Hiring (Months 3–6)
5. **Buildout**: 10–14 weeks typical in Tulsa; budget **15% contingency**.
6. **Hiring**: 1 hygienist, 2 assistants, 1 front-office coordinator; Tulsa dental labor market is **moderately tight**—post 8–10 weeks pre-opening.
7. **Insurance credentialing**: Begin **90–120 days pre-opening**; Medicare/Medicaid credentialing in Oklahoma takes **60–90 days**.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 6–12)
8. **Soft launch**: 4–6 weeks of reduced hours to stabilize workflows.
9. **Marketing activation**: GBP posts 2x/week, review generation target **25+ reviews in 90 days**, Google Ads at **$2,500–$4,000/mo** targeting "dentist near me" + Tulsa neighborhood terms.
10. **Q4 capture**: Launch **October 2026** to capture insurance-benefit-expiration demand; run "use-it-or-lose-it" campaigns in November.

### Phase 4 — Optimization (Month 12+)
11. **KPI review**: Track **cost-per-new-patient ($95–$180)**, **no-show rate (<8%)**, **production per operatory ($28,000–$42,000/mo)**.
12. **GEO/AI search visibility**: Optimize for AI-generated local recommendations by maintaining **consistent NAP data**, **structured schema markup**, and **review velocity**—generative engines weight these heavily for "best dentist in Tulsa" queries.
