# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Madison, WI
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Madison, Wisconsin's 2026 Q4 handyman market is defined by a **$78–$125/hour blended labor rate** for licensed handyman work, **$95–$165/hour** for specialty contractor trades (electrical, plumbing rough-in, tile), and a **$250–$600 minimum job ticket** that most established operators now enforce to protect margin. The metro (Dane County) carries roughly **1,900–2,200 active licensed contractors** across all trades, but fewer than **~380 firms** self-identify as general handyman/remodel-support services — a genuine supply gap given **~290,000 occupied housing units** in Dane County, of which **~62% were built before 2000** and now generate steady repair demand.

Startup capital for a lean, license-compliant launch in Madison runs **$8,500–$22,000** depending on vehicle and tool inventory; a fully equipped two-person operation with branded truck wrap and digital assets lands at **$28,000–$45,000**. First-year revenue targets for a solo operator should be **$95,000–$160,000** at 60–70% billable utilization, with **$14,000–$26,000** allocated to a realistic 12-month digital acquisition budget. The single biggest 2026 Q4 risk is **not demand — it's licensing and insurance compliance**, which we address in the execution plan below.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 0 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–3)

1. **Entity & registration.** File a Wisconsin LLC with the **WI Department of Financial Institutions** (~$130 online filing); obtain an **EIN** from the IRS (free).
2. **Local licenses.** Register with the **City of Madison Clerk** for a Direct Seller/contractor permit as applicable, and confirm Dane County and adjacent municipality (Middleton, Sun Prairie, Fitchburg, Verona) requirements — they vary.
3. **Insurance (do not skip).** General liability **$1M/$2M** ($900–$2,200/yr for a solo handyman) and workers' comp if you hire. Many property managers require a **certificate of insurance** before dispatching work.
4. **Credentialing.** Wisconsin does **not** license "handyman" as a standalone trade, but **electrical, plumbing, and HVAC work requires a state credential** — scope your service menu to stay inside the line, or subcontract licensed trades.
5. **Banking & bookkeeping.** Business checking + a bookkeeping tool (QuickBooks/Wave); set aside **25–30%** of revenue for taxes.

### Phase 1 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–6)

6. **Domain + website.** Buy the domain (~$15–$40/yr); build a 5–7 page site with **service pages, service-area pages, pricing transparency page, and a booking form**. Target **$1,200–$3,200** via verified freelancer.
7. **Google Business Profile.** Claim/verify, complete every field, upload 15–25 photos, set service areas to Madison + 8–12 surrounding communities. This is your #1 GEO asset.
8. **Programmatic service-area pages.** Generate 20–40 pages (e.g., "Handyman in [Neighborhood/Suburb]") with **unique local content** — permit notes, housing-stock specifics, response-time promises. Avoid thin duplication.
9. **Review engine.** Automated post-job review requests; target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** at a 4.8+ average. Reviews are heavily weighted in AI-generated local recommendations.
10. **Tracking.** Call tracking number, GA4, and a simple CRM (Jobber/Housecall Pro, **$50–$150/mo**).

### Phase 2 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)

11. **Launch Google Ads** on high-intent terms ("handyman Madison," "drywall repair Madison") at **$1,500–$3,000/mo** starting budget; expect **$35–$70 cost-per-lead** in this DMA.
12. **Property-manager outreach.** Pitch 10–15 local property managers and small landlords with a COI, response SLA, and volume rate card — this is the highest-LTV channel in Dane County.
13. **Neighborhood seeding.** Door hangers + Nextdoor presence in 3–5 target ZIPs; track with unique promo codes.
14. **Content for AI search (GEO).** Publish 2–4 authoritative articles/month answering real questions ("How much does drywall repair cost in Madison?", "Do I need a permit for a deck in Dane County?"). Structured data (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ schema) is mandatory.

### Phase 3 — Optimize & Scale (Months 4–12)

15. **Unit economics review monthly.** Track cost-per-booked-job by channel; kill anything above **$250 CPBJ** for standard work.
16. **Hire the first helper** at ~$22–$28/hr when utilization exceeds 70%; this is the margin inflection point.
17. **Expand service menu** into higher-margin project work (bathroom refresh, deck repair, rental turnovers) once review volume exceeds 50.
18. **Reinvest 8–12% of revenue** into digital assets — this keeps AI-search visibility compounding while competitors buy shared leads.
