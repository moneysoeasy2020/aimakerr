### The Agency vs. Freelancer Breakdown — Read This Carefully

**Brick-and-mortar Buffalo agencies** ($2,500–$6,000/mo retainers) bundle strategy, design, ads, and "account management." You pay for overhead: office space on Elmwood or Delaware Ave, account managers, and sales staff. For a startup doing **$8K–$12K/month in revenue**, that retainer eats **20–40% of gross** — unsustainable.

**Verified freelancers** (check: Upwork "Top Rated Plus," LinkedIn history, portfolio of local service clients, references you actually call) deliver the same deliverables at **40–70% less**. The trade-off: **you must manage them**. Budget 3–5 hours/month for oversight.

**Decision rule:** Hire a freelancer for website, GBP, and ads management. Hire a local agency **only** if you're spending **$4,000+/month on ad spend** and need multi-channel strategy — not before month 12.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Insurance Foundation (Weeks 1–2)

1. **Form an LLC** with NY Department of State — **$200 filing fee** + $25 biennial statement. Use a registered agent ($50–$150/yr) if you don't want your home address public.
2. **Register for NY sales tax** (materials resale) and obtain an **EIN** (free, IRS).
3. **Home improvement contractor registration** — New York requires registration with the **NYS Department of State Division of Consumer Protection** for home improvement work over **$200** (fee ~$50, renewal every 2 years). Erie County may add local licensing — verify with the **City of Buffalo Permits & Inspections** office.
4. **General liability insurance** — **$1,100–$1,900/year** for a solo handyman ($1M/$2M limits). Add **workers' comp** the moment you hire (~$1,200–$2,500/yr per employee in NY).
5. **Open a business bank account** — free at most credit unions; avoid monthly-fee banks.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)

1. **Claim and optimize your Google Business Profile** — this is your #1 lead source in Buffalo. Category: "Handyman" + secondary "Contractor." Service area: Buffalo, Amherst, Cheektowaga, Tonawanda, Kenmore, Williamsville, West Seneca, Orchard Park.
2. **Build a 5–7 page website** with **LocalBusiness + Service schema markup** (critical for GEO/AI-search visibility). Pages: Home, Services, Service Areas, About, Reviews, Contact, Quote Request.
3. **Set up a review engine** — automated SMS request 24 hrs after job completion. Target **40+ Google reviews in 90 days**; this alone moves you into the Buffalo Maps 3-pack.
4. **Vehicle wrap** — highest-ROI local ad in a drive-heavy metro. **$1,800–$3,200**.

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 4–8)

1. **Google Local Services Ads** — pay-per-lead ($28–$65 in Buffalo), Google Guaranteed badge. Start with **$500–$800/month budget**.
2. **Google Search Ads** — target "handyman Buffalo," "contractor Amherst NY," "deck repair Buffalo" with **$600–$1,000/month** and tight geo-radius (15 miles).
3. **Realtor referral program** — Buffalo has ~1,200 active agents; a pre-listing repair partnership generates repeat B2B volume. Offer a 5–10% referral fee (disclose per NY rules).
4. **Nextdoor + local Facebook groups** — free, high-intent, Buffalo-specific (e.g., "Buffalo Homeowners," neighborhood groups in Elmwood, North Buffalo, Kenmore).

### Phase 4 — Operational Scale (Months 3–6)

1. **Hire your first helper** at **$22–$28/hr** once you're booking 4+ jobs/week consistently.
2. **Standardize quoting** — flat-rate pricing sheets cut sales time 40% and improve close rates.
3. **Add a second service line** (e.g., snow removal in winter, basement finishing) to smooth seasonal revenue.
4. **Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into marketing** — the benchmark for local service businesses scaling past $15K/month.
