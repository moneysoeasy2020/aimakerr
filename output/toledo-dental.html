**Demand fundamentals**

- **Population:** ~641,000 metro (Lucas, Wood, Fulton, Ottawa counties); ~268,000 in Toledo city proper.
- **Dental insurance penetration:** ~68% commercial/Medicaid mix; Ohio Medicaid adult dental expansion keeps ~22–26% of the market Medicaid-eligible — a meaningful volume channel if you credential early.
- **Age skew:** 14.2% of metro residents are 65+, above the national 12.8% — strong demand for implant, denture, and periodontal services.
- **Competitive density:** ~1 dentist per 2,050 residents metro-wide (below national average), but **hyper-concentrated in Sylvania, Perrysburg, and West Toledo** — suburban saturation is real; **East Toledo, North Toledo, and Oregon** are underserved.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-launch (Months 1–3)
1. **Entity formation & licensing:** Ohio Secretary of State LLC ($99), Ohio State Dental Board licensure, Toledo-Lucas County Health Dept. facility permit.
2. **Site selection:** target 2,000–2,600 sq ft in Oregon/East Toledo or Maumee; negotiate 5-year NNN with 3-month free rent.
3. **Equipment:** lease 4–5 A-dec/Pelton operatory packages ($95K–$140K financed at 7–9%).
4. **Domain + GBP:** secure exact-match domain (e.g., `[neighborhood]dental.com`); claim Google Business Profile **before** opening (pre-launch listing allowed).

### Phase 2 — Digital foundation (Months 3–5)
5. **Website:** 10-page build with `Dentist` schema, `LocalBusiness` markup, online booking (NexHealth/Curve), and service-area pages for **Toledo, Oregon, Maumee, Perrysburg, Sylvania, Waterville**.
6. **Programmatic geo-pages:** 30–50 pages targeting `[service] + [neighborhood]` (e.g., "emergency dentist Oregon OH") — the single highest-ROI GEO play in this market.
7. **Review engine:** automate post-visit SMS requests; target **40–60 Google reviews in first 6 months**.

### Phase 3 — Paid + local authority (Months 5–9)
8. **Google Ads:** $2,500–$4,000/mo starting budget; isolate emergency + Invisalign campaigns; expect CPL $45–$75.
9. **Local citations:** Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Angi, Toledo Blade business directory, Lucas County Dental Society.
10. **Community GEO:** sponsor 2–3 local events (Toledo Zoo, Metroparks) — improves AI-citation signal for "best dentist in Toledo" queries.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 9–12)
11. **Add specialty services** (implants, ortho) once 150+ active patients.
12. **Programmatic expansion** to 100+ geo-pages across the 6-county metro.
13. **Reinvest 8–12% of revenue** into digital; expect **180–260 new patients in year one** at $145–$260 CAC.

## Key 2026 Q4 Risks & Mitigations

- **PPO reimbursement compression:** model 55/45 PPO/FFS mix; credential with Delta Dental of Ohio and Medical Mutual early.
- **Medicaid credentialing lag (90–120 days):** apply in Phase 1, not after opening.
- **Agency overspend:** the $85K–$140K brick-and-mortar agency path is **2.2–3.7× the cost** of a verified freelance stack for equivalent output at this scale.
- **AI-search visibility:** ensure every geo-page has structured data, NAP consistency, and 300+ words of unique local content — this is what drives citation in AI Overviews and ChatGPT/Perplexity local answers.
