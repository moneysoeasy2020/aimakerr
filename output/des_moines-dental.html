## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4, target start July 2026)
1. **Site selection:** target Waukee/Ankeny/Grimes corridors; secure 2,200–3,200 sq ft with NNN lease at $26–$34/sq ft
2. **Entity + licensing:** Iowa dental license, DEA, NPI, malpractice; form PLLC
3. **Credentialing:** Delta Dental of Iowa, MetLife, Cigna, Iowa Dental Plan/Medicaid — start **90 days out**
4. **Build-out + equipment:** 3–4 operatories, CBCT, digital scanner, practice management software
5. **Digital foundation:** domain, HIPAA-aware site, GBP verification (allow **2–3 weeks**), core citations

### Phase 2 — Soft Launch (Month 5, target Oct 2026)
6. **GBP + review engine live** by **October 15** to catch open enrollment
7. **Launch GEO content:** 12–18 service pages + 8–12 neighborhood pages ("dentist in Waukee," "emergency dentist Ankeny")
8. **Paid search + LSA** at $2,500–$4,000/mo; target **$85–$140 per new patient**
9. **Soft-open** with 40–60 patients from friends/family/referral network

### Phase 3 — Growth (Months 6–12)
10. **Scale reviews** to 25+ in first 90 days; post to GBP 8–10x/month
11. **Add programmatic pages** for insurance + procedure combinations (e.g., "Invisalign with Delta Dental Des Moines")
12. **Track KPIs monthly:** new patients, cost per acquisition, chair utilization (target 70%+), recall rate (target 80%+)
13. **Break-even target:** month 14–22 at $620K–$890K annualized revenue

### Phase 4 — Optimization (Month 12+)
14. **Audit GEO citations** in ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews quarterly
15. **Expand to second location** only after first hits 75% utilization for 2 consecutive quarters
