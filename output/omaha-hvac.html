### Agency vs. Freelance — Explicit Comparison

**Brick-and-mortar Omaha agencies** offer accountability, in-person meetings, and bundled teams, but carry **35–60% overhead premiums**, slower turnaround (2–3 week content cycles), and often subcontract programmatic SEO to junior staff. Best fit: operators with $500K+ revenue who need a single point of contact.

**Verified freelance experts** (vetted via portfolio audits, Upwork Top Rated Plus, or Clutch-verified references) deliver **50–65% cost savings**, faster iteration, and direct access to the practitioner. Risk: continuity. Mitigate with written SOPs, 30-day notice clauses, and Escrow-backed milestones. Best fit: launch-phase operators under $400K revenue who need maximum capital efficiency.

**Recommended hybrid:** Freelance web build + freelance programmatic SEO + part-time local SEO consultant, reserving agency retainers for Year 2 once cash flow stabilizes.
