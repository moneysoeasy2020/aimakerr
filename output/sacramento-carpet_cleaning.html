## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Insurance (Weeks 1–2)**
1. Form LLC (CA SOS, $70) and register with Sacramento City Treasurer for business tax certificate.
2. Obtain general liability ($1M) + commercial auto; bonding required by most property managers.
3. Confirm IICRC certification path (WRT/CRT courses ~$500–$900) — a trust signal for commercial clients.

**Phase 2 — Equipment & Operations (Weeks 2–4)**
4. Source used truck-mount (budget $6K–$9K) or start portable ($1,500–$3,000) to preserve capital.
5. Set up scheduling (Jobber/ServiceTitan), payment processing, and a simple CRM.

**Phase 3 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)**
6. Build a 6-page site with **programmatic local landing pages** for each ZIP/service area (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in Elk Grove 95624").
7. Optimize Google Business Profile with service-area radius, photos, and **review request automation** (target 25+ reviews in 90 days).
8. Launch Google Local Services Ads with a **$500/mo test budget**; track cost-per-booked-job weekly.

**Phase 4 — Channel Partnerships (Weeks 4–8)**
9. Visit **30 property management offices** with a one-page rate sheet; offer 10% referral or priority scheduling.
10. Partner with **5–8 plumbers and water-damage restorers** for referral reciprocity.

**Phase 5 — GEO / AI-Search Visibility (Ongoing from Week 6)**
11. Publish FAQ-structured content answering "how much does carpet cleaning cost in Sacramento," "best carpet cleaner near [neighborhood]," and "pet stain removal Sacramento" — these are the queries AI engines cite.
12. Maintain consistent NAP (name, address, phone) across Yelp, Angi, Nextdoor, and BBB; schema markup on all service pages.

**Q4 2026 launch targets:** 40–60 booked jobs/month by month 3, CAC under $45, 4.7+ star average, and $8K–$12K monthly gross by end of Q1 2027.
