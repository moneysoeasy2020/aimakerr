# How to Launch a Handyman & Contractor Business in Los Angeles, CA
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition

Los Angeles County's home-services market is projected at **$4.1B–$4.6B in annual consumer spend** across handyman, repair, and light-remodel categories in 2026, with roughly **38,000–42,000 active licensed contractors** competing in the county (CSLB-registered). A solo handyman in LA can realistically charge **$75–$125/hour** (or **$95–$150/hour** for a licensed general contractor doing specialty work), with a typical first-year revenue target of **$85,000–$140,000** at 60–70% billable utilization. Startup capital ranges from **$6,500 (solo, truck-based)** to **$28,000 (with a small crew, insurance, and bonding)**.

The single largest cost driver in 2026 is **CSLB licensing and compliance**—the California Contractors State License Board requires a **$25,000 bond**, a **$450–$650 exam fee**, and a **$400–$600 application fee** for a General B (or specialty C-class) license. Expect **$2,800–$4,200** in total licensing, bonding, and insurance setup for a licensed contractor, or **$1,200–$1,800** for a handyman operating under the **$500 material-and-labor threshold** that does not require a contractor license in California.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Entity:** File an LLC with the CA Secretary of State — **$70** online filing fee.
2. **License decision:** Operate as a handyman under the **$500 threshold** OR pursue a **CSLB B or C-class license** (recommended for scaling past $150K revenue).
3. **Bond + insurance:** **$25K contractor bond** (~$300–$500/yr), **$1M general liability** (~$1,200–$2,400/yr), workers' comp if hiring.
4. **Local permits:** LA City Business Tax Registration Certificate (**$50–$300**), plus LADBS permits for structural/electrical/plumbing work.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6)
1. **Google Business Profile** — verified, category "Handyman," service areas mapped to **top 15 LA ZIPs** (90025, 90034, 90064, 90210, 90402, etc.).
2. **Website** — 5–7 pages: home, services, service-area hub, about, reviews, contact, booking.
3. **Programmatic SEO** — generate **service × neighborhood** pages (e.g., "drywall repair in Sherman Oaks") targeting **120–200 long-tail queries**.
4. **GEO/AI-search optimization** — schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ), FAQ blocks answering "how much does X cost in LA," and citation consistency across 40+ directories.

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 5–12)
1. **Google Local Services Ads** — start at **$1,500–$2,500/mo** budget; LSA leads convert 3–5x better than Angi for handyman.
2. **Google Ads** — $1,200–$2,000/mo on high-intent keywords ("handyman Santa Monica," "drywall repair LA").
3. **Review engine** — target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**; automate post-job SMS requests.
4. **Referral partnerships** — 5–10 real estate agents and property managers (each can drive **$2K–$8K/mo** in recurring work).

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 4–12)
1. **Crew expansion** — add 1–2 W-2 or 1099 helpers at **$22–$30/hr**.
2. **Service mix shift** — move toward **ADU prep, wildfire-repair, and insurance-adjacent** work (2–3x margin vs. general handyman).
3. **Reputation moat** — 100+ reviews, before/after galleries, and neighborhood-specific case studies for GEO citation.
4. **Financial targets** — Month 6: **$9K–$14K/mo revenue**; Month 12: **$18K–$28K/mo** (solo-to-small-crew trajectory).
