## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)
- Register LLC with **DC Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection (DLCP)** — filing fee **$99–$220**.
- Obtain **DC Basic Business License (BBL)** for cleaning services: **$150–$400**.
- Secure general liability insurance (**$600–$1,200/yr**) and commercial auto.
- Register with DC Office of Tax and Revenue; obtain Clean Hands certification.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Operations (Weeks 3–5)
- Source truck-mount extractor (used: **$4,500–$9,500**) or commercial portable (**$2,200–$4,000**).
- Build service tiers: Standard ($285–$340), Deep Clean ($380–$480), Commercial (per sq ft).
- Set up booking via Jobber or Housecall Pro (**$49–$169/mo**).

### Phase 3 — Digital Assets & GEO (Weeks 4–7)
- Launch website with **LocalBusiness + Service schema**, service-area pages for DC wards, Arlington, Alexandria.
- Optimize Google Business Profile; target **"carpet cleaning [neighborhood] DC"** entities.
- Publish 10 FAQ pages answering AI-search queries (pricing, pet stains, drying time).
- Deploy review-generation flow targeting **25 reviews in 90 days**.

### Phase 4 — Paid Acquisition (Weeks 6–10)
- Google Local Services Ads (**$800–$1,500/mo**) + Search Ads (**$1,200–$2,000/mo**).
- Target ZIP clusters: 20001–20016 (DC), 22201–22207 (Arlington).
- Expected CAC: **$45–$85**; target **4–6x LTV** via recurring maintenance plans.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 3–6)
- Add second van at **$18K–$22K** incremental.
- Pursue property-manager contracts (apartment turnover, 30–60 units/mo).
- Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into GEO content and local link building.
