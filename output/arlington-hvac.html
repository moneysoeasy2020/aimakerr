## 2026 Q4 Arlington Market Analysis

**Demand fundamentals (Q4 2026):**
- Arlington population: **~398,000** (2025 Census estimate trajectory), median household income **$67,800–$71,200**.
- Housing stock: **~62% single-family detached**, with a large cohort of **1970s–1990s builds** — prime replacement age. Roughly **38–44% of Arlington's single-family homes are 25+ years old**, driving steady condenser/furnace replacement demand.
- Climate load: DFW cooling-degree-days average **~2,700–2,900/year**; Arlington sees **100+ days above 90°F** in a typical year. Heating is secondary but real (January lows avg **35–37°F**), so dual-fuel and heat pump conversions are growing.
- **Q4 seasonality:** October–November is the "shoulder" — tune-ups, heat pump changeouts, and pre-winter furnace checks. Expect **20–30% lower call volume than July peak** but **higher average ticket** on replacement jobs as homeowners use fall promotions.
- Competitive density: Arlington has **strong incumbent presence** (national franchises + 40+ local independents). Differentiation hinges on **response time (<90 min), transparent pricing, and bilingual service** (Arlington is ~30% Hispanic/Latino).

**Regulatory snapshot (verify before launch):**
- **TDLR Air Conditioning & Refrigeration license** required (contractor + technicians).
- **EPA Section 608 certification** for refrigerant handling (mandatory for all techs).
- **Arlington trade permit** for HVAC replacement/install; permit fees typically **$85–$220** per residential job.
- **General liability + commercial auto + workers' comp** — budget **$4,200–$7,800/year** for a 2-truck operation.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Secure **TDLR license** + confirm **EPA 608** for all techs.
2. Bind insurance (GL, auto, workers' comp) — **$4,200–$7,800/yr**.
3. Register LLC (Texas), EIN, business bank account.
4. Set up **Google Business Profile** with verified Arlington address, service area, categories, and 15+ photos.

**Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)**
5. Launch website with **LocalBusiness + Service schema**, service pages, and financing widget.
6. Deploy **programmatic SEO**: 40–60 pages covering "AC repair," "furnace install," "heat pump," × Arlington neighborhoods (North Arlington, Pantego border, Dalworthington Gardens, etc.).
7. Activate **Local Services Ads** (highest-intent channel) + Google Ads for emergency keywords.

**Phase 3 — Demand Capture & Reviews (Weeks 5–10)**
8. Install review-generation automation; target **8–12 reviews/month**.
9. Publish 4–6 GEO-optimized FAQ/blog pieces (e.g., "How much does AC replacement cost in Arlington in 2026?").
10. Set up call tracking + CRM; benchmark **cost per booked call ($140–$310)**.

**Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 10–16)**
11. Add second truck once **booked-call rate > 55%** and margin holds **28%+**.
12. Expand programmatic pages to **80–120**; layer bilingual (Spanish) content for Arlington's ~30% Hispanic market.
13. Review channel ROI monthly; reallocate spend toward the **lowest cost-per-booked-call** source.

**Year-1 targets:** 480–620 tickets, **$1.1M–$1.6M revenue**, **28–34% gross margin**, **120–180 Google reviews**, and a digital acquisition cost under **$210 per booked call** blended.
