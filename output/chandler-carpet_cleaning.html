## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–2)

1. **Register LLC** with Arizona Corporation Commission — **$50 filing fee**.
2. **Get an AZ Transaction Privilege Tax (TPT) license** — required for service businesses; Chandler city + state licensing.
3. **Business insurance**: general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto — **$1,200–$2,400/year**.
4. **IICRC certification** (Carpet Cleaning Technician) — **$500–$700**; strongly boosts trust and GEO citations.

### Phase 2: Equipment & Setup (Weeks 2–4)

- **Truck-mount (used)**: $8,000–$18,000
- **High-end portable (backup/small jobs)**: $2,500–$5,000
- **Chemicals, hoses, wand, spotting kit**: $800–$1,500
- **Van + wrap**: $3,000–$6,000 (wrap) + vehicle cost
- **Total lean setup**: **$18,000–$35,000**

### Phase 3: Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–5)

1. **Google Business Profile** — claim, verify, add 10+ photos, service areas (Chandler, Sun Lakes, Ocotillo, Ahwatukee border).
2. **Website** — 5–7 pages with localized service pages (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in Ocotillo," "Pet Odor Removal Chandler").
3. **Schema markup** — LocalBusiness + Service schema for GEO/AI-search visibility.
4. **Review system** — automated SMS request post-job; target **20 reviews in 60 days**.

### Phase 4: Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)

1. **Google Local Services Ads** — pay-per-lead, strong for carpet cleaning; budget **$500–$1,000/mo**.
2. **Google Ads** — start at **$15–$25/day** on high-intent keywords ("carpet cleaning Chandler").
3. **Neighborhood targeting** — Ocotillo, Fulton Ranch, Sun Groves, Downtown Chandler.
4. **Partnerships** — Realtors, property managers, HOAs (Chandler has 200+ HOAs).

### Phase 5: Q4 Holiday Push (Weeks 8–12)

- **Holiday prep package** (3 rooms + hallway + free deodorizer) at **$299**.
- **Commercial Q4 budget pitch** to Chandler office parks and retail centers.
- **Referral program**: $25 credit per referral.
