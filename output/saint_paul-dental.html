## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

1. **Entity & licensing (Weeks 1–4):** File Minnesota PLLC with the Secretary of State ($155), obtain MN Board of Dentistry license and clinic registration, secure DEA and Medicare/Medicaid enrollment.
2. **Site selection (Weeks 2–8):** Target 55104/55130 or Highland Park; negotiate 7–10 year lease with TI allowance of **$35–$65/sq ft**.
3. **Build-out & equipment (Weeks 6–22):** Dental chairs **$28,000–$55,000** each; CBCT **$65,000–$120,000**; sterilization and cabinetry **$40,000–$70,000**. Budget 16–20 weeks for permitting in Saint Paul.
4. **Credentialing (Weeks 4–20):** Submit to Delta Dental MN, HealthPartners, BCBS MN, and MetLife simultaneously — expect 90–150 days.
5. **Digital launch (Weeks 10–20):** GBP verification, website with LocalBusiness + Dentist schema, service-area landing pages for each Saint Paul neighborhood, review-generation workflow.
6. **Paid + organic ramp (Weeks 18–26):** Google Ads at **$2,500–$4,500/month** targeting "dentist near me Saint Paul" and neighborhood terms; programmatic SEO pages for "emergency dentist 55104," "cosmetic dentist Highland Park," etc.
7. **Soft open → full open (Week 24–28):** Target **35–50 new patients in month 1**, scaling to **60–90/month** by month 6.
