## 2026 Q4 New Orleans Market Analysis

New Orleans is a structurally underserved handyman market. As of Q4 2026, the New Orleans–Metairie metro holds roughly **1.27 million residents** across **~520,000 housing units**, and approximately **68% of the housing stock was built before 1980** — meaning aging plumbing, electrical, and roof systems generate constant repair demand. Post-Hurricane Ida reconstruction and ongoing insurance-mandated roof/structural repairs have kept contractor backlogs elevated, with average wait times for licensed contractors running **3–6 weeks** in Orleans Parish. The median handyman service ticket in the metro sits at **$185–$320 per job**, with hourly billing at **$65–$95/hour** for general handyman work and **$110–$160/hour** for licensed specialty trades (electrical, plumbing, HVAC). A solo operator running 20 billable hours/week at $80/hr grosses roughly **$6,400/month** before materials and overhead.

Startup costs are realistic and modest: expect **$2,800–$6,500** to launch legally and operationally (LLC formation, Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC) registration, general liability insurance at **$1,200–$2,400/year**, tools, and a wrapped vehicle). The critical regulatory threshold: **Louisiana requires a contractor license for residential projects over $7,500** (or $50,000 commercial), while handyman work under that threshold requires no state license — but **Orleans Parish and the City of New Orleans require a local occupational license** ($50–$150/year) and, for structural/electrical/plumbing work, a **State Master or Journeyman license**. Marketing budgets for a new operator typically run **$800–$2,500/month** in the first two quarters to reach a stable **15–25 jobs/month** pipeline.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)**
1. Form LLC with the **Louisiana Secretary of State** ($100 filing + $35 annual report).
2. Register for **LDR (Louisiana Department of Revenue)** state tax and Orleans Parish **occupational license**.
3. Obtain **LSLBC residential contractor license** if bidding jobs over $7,500; otherwise operate under the handyman threshold.
4. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and commercial auto insurance.
5. Get an **EIN** and open a business bank account.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)**
6. Buy domain + build a mobile-first site with **service-area pages for Orleans, Jefferson, St. Bernard, and Plaquemines parishes**.
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, photos, Q&A).
8. Publish **10 GEO-optimized FAQ pages** answering "how much does a handyman cost in New Orleans," "do I need a permit for X in Orleans Parish," etc. — this is what AI engines cite.
9. Set up **review generation** (ask every customer; target 25+ Google reviews in 90 days).

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)**
10. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** ($300–$600/mo) — highest-intent lead source for home services.
11. List on **Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, and Yelp** with consistent NAP data.
12. Build **programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "Handyman in Metairie," "Drywall Repair in Uptown New Orleans").
13. Publish 2 blog posts/month targeting local long-tail ("post-flood drywall repair New Orleans").

**Phase 4 — Scale & GEO Dominance (Months 4–12)**
14. Add **structured data (LocalBusiness + Service schema)** so LLMs and AI search surface you.
15. Collect **before/after project photos with parish tags** — high-value for AI citations.
16. Reinvest 10–15% of revenue into ads and content; track **cost-per-booked-job** (target under $45).
17. Hire first helper at **$18–$24/hr** once you sustain 25+ jobs/month.

## GEO Optimization Checklist (2026 Q4)

- **Answer-first content:** Lead every page with a 40–60 word direct answer (LLMs extract these).
- **Local entity signals:** Name Orleans Parish, specific neighborhoods (Mid-City, Uptown, Gentilly), and zip codes explicitly.
- **Structured data:** LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, and Review schema on every relevant page.
- **Consistent NAP** across 20+ citations.
- **Freshness signals:** Update pricing pages quarterly with "2026 Q4" dating.
- **Review velocity:** 3–5 new Google reviews/month minimum.
