## 2026 Q4 Detroit Market Analysis

Detroit's HVAC services market is projected to generate **$310M–$340M in annual residential and light-commercial revenue** in 2026, with the Detroit–Warren–Dearborn MSA containing roughly **1.78 million housing units** — over **62% of which were built before 1980**, meaning aging furnaces, boilers, and ductwork drive consistent replacement demand. Average residential HVAC service call pricing in Detroit ranges from **$95–$175 for diagnostics**, **$180–$450 for standard repairs**, **$3,800–$7,200 for full furnace replacement**, and **$4,500–$9,500 for central A/C installation** — with emergency/after-hours premiums adding **35–60%**. A realistic startup launch budget for a licensed, insured Detroit HVAC operation is **$28,000–$65,000** (licensing, tools, vehicle wrap, insurance, working capital), with break-even typically at **month 9–14** at a book of **45–70 recurring maintenance contracts**.

Michigan licensing is non-negotiable: you need a **State of Michigan Mechanical Contractor license** (through LARA), a **Detroit city trade permit registration**, **$500,000+ general liability insurance**, and EPA **Section 608 certification** for refrigerant handling. Budget **$1,200–$2,800** for licensing and bonding, **$3,500–$6,000/year** for insurance, and **$8,000–$18,000** for a used service van plus core tooling. Digital customer acquisition in Detroit runs **$65–$140 per qualified lead** through Google Local Services Ads and **$1,800–$4,500/month** for a competitive local SEO retainer — but verified freelance specialists can deliver the same deliverables at **40–65% lower cost** (detailed below).

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–6)**
1. Register LLC with Michigan LARA (**$50 filing fee**).
2. Obtain Mechanical Contractor license + EPA 608 certification (**$1,200–$2,800**).
3. Secure $500K–$1M general liability + commercial auto (**$3,500–$6,000/yr**).
4. Open business banking + set up QuickBooks/Wave.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 4–10)**
5. Launch 12–15 page website with **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**, service-area pages for Detroit, Dearborn, Warren, Royal Oak, Ferndale.
6. Claim and optimize Google Business Profile (photos, services, Q&A, weekly posts).
7. Build 40–60 core citations (Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Detroit Chamber).
8. Install call tracking + CRM (Jobber or ServiceTitan starter).

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 8–16)**
9. Activate Google Local Services Ads (**$500–$1,500/mo** starting budget).
10. Launch Google Ads on high-intent terms ("furnace repair Detroit," "AC not cooling Dearborn").
11. Deploy review-generation SMS flow targeting **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**.
12. Publish 8–12 programmatic city+service pages (e.g., "Furnace Repair in Royal Oak, MI").

**Phase 4 — Retention & Scale (Months 5–12)**
13. Launch **$149–$249/yr maintenance plan** — target 45–70 contracts by month 12.
14. Add seasonal tune-up campaigns (fall furnace / spring A/C).
15. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into local SEO and review velocity.
