## 2026 Q4 Fort Worth Market Analysis

**Demand drivers (verified, current):**
- **Population growth:** Fort Worth added ~25,000–30,000 residents/year 2023–2025; Tarrant County now exceeds **2.2 million**. Fastest dental-demand growth is in ZIPs **76177, 76247, 76179 (Alliance/North FW)** and **76008/76087 (Aledo/Willow Park)**.
- **Insurance mix:** ~**62–68%** of Fort Worth adults carry dental benefits (employer PPO dominant); Medicaid adult dental in Texas remains limited, so DSO and PPO-friendly positioning matters.
- **Competitive density:** ~**700–760 dental offices** in Tarrant County. Corporate/DSO penetration is roughly **22–28%** — lower than Dallas (~35%), leaving room for independent positioning.
- **Labor:** Hygienist wages **$38–$46/hr**, dental assistants **$19–$25/hr**; hygienist shortage is the #1 operational constraint — budget signing bonuses ($2,000–$5,000).

**2026 Q4 timing note:** Q4 is the strongest **case-acceptance window** (patients maximizing annual insurance maximums before Dec 31). Launching build-out in Q4 2026 targets a **Q2 2027 opening** — align marketing spend ramp to January (new benefit year) and back-to-school (August).

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 → Q2 2027 Opening)

**Phase 1 — Entity & Site Selection (Weeks 1–6, Q4 2026)**
1. Form **Texas PLLC** (dentist must be majority owner under Texas Dental Practice Act); file with TX SOS ($300), EIN same week.
2. Texas State Board of Dental Examiners facility registration + **DEA registration** ($888/3 yrs) + **DPS controlled-substance registration**; budget 8–12 weeks lead time.
3. Secure letter of intent on 2,200 sq ft NNN space in a target ZIP; negotiate **$60–$90/sq ft TI allowance** and 90-day rent abatement.
4. Open business banking + apply for **SBA 7(a)** ($50K–$5M; 2026 rates ~prime + 2.25–2.75%) or equipment financing.

**Phase 2 — Build-Out & Equipment (Weeks 6–20)**
5. Dental-specific architect + contractor; Texas requires plumbing/medical gas permits — expect **14–18 weeks** construction.
6. Equipment order (4 chairs, pan/CBCT, sterilization, IT): **$180K–$320K**; negotiate installation + 5-year service.
7. Practice management software (Dentrix, Open Dental, or Curve) + clearinghouse + HIPAA BAAs.

**Phase 3 — Digital Launch (Weeks 10–24, overlapping)**
8. Website live **8 weeks pre-opening** with `Dentist` + `LocalBusiness` schema, service-area pages for Fort Worth ZIPs, and online booking.
9. **Google Business Profile** verified at opening address (allow 2–3 weeks); seed 15–25 reviews in first 90 days via post-visit SMS.
10. Google Ads launch **2 weeks pre-opening**: target "dentist near me," "emergency dentist Fort Worth," "dental implants Fort Worth" — budget **$3,000–$5,000/mo** initially.
11. Citations: Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Dental Insider, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect.

**Phase 4 — Open & Optimize (Q2 2027 onward)**
12. Track **cost per new patient** (target $150–$350 general, $400–$900 implant) and **lifetime value** ($1,800–$3,200 per PPO patient).
13. Add programmatic service × neighborhood pages (e.g., "emergency dentist Alliance Fort Worth") once GBP shows map-pack traction.
14. Quarterly review of agency/freelancer ROI; hold vendors to **new-patient attribution**, not impressions.

**GEO/AI-search note:** Structure site content with FAQ schema, named-author clinical bios, and citation-ready stats (fees, insurance accepted, hours) — generative engines in 2026 pull these directly into local recommendations. Ensure your GBP, website, and Yelp list **identical NAP + hours**.
