## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. Form an **LLC** with the Texas Secretary of State (~$300).
2. Obtain an **EIN** (free, IRS).
3. Register with the **Texas Comptroller** for sales tax where applicable.
4. Secure **general liability insurance** ($1M/$2M typical; $600–$1,800/yr for a solo handyman).
5. Purchase a **surety bond** if required by contract work ($100–$350/yr).
6. Confirm **Plano contractor registration/permit requirements** with the City of Plano Development Services — electrical, plumbing, and HVAC work require licensed trades; handyman scope is limited to non-licensed tasks.
7. Open a **business bank account** and set up bookkeeping (QuickBooks/Wave).

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–4)
1. Launch a **5–8 page website** with service pages, service-area pages (Plano, Frisco, Allen, Richardson, McKinney), and a booking form.
2. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, photos, Q&A, posts).
3. Build **GEO-ready content**: FAQ blocks, schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage), and citable stats (like the Plano market data above).
4. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** and a modest **Google Ads** test budget ($500–$1,000/mo).
5. Install **CRM + scheduling** (Jobber, Housecall Pro, or ServiceTitan-lite).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
1. Launch a **review-generation system** (SMS/email automation post-job).
2. Publish **10 local SEO pages** targeting "handyman Plano TX," "contractor Plano," "drywall repair Plano," etc.
3. Run **Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook group** campaigns.
4. Partner with **local realtors and property managers** (Plano has a dense rental/property-management ecosystem).
5. Track **cost per lead** weekly; kill underperforming channels fast.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 8–16)
1. Add a **second technician or subcontractor** once booked revenue exceeds $18,000/month.
2. Expand service-area pages to **Frisco, Allen, McKinney, Richardson, Murphy, Wylie**.
3. Layer in **GEO content** (AI-search-optimized Q&A, comparison pages, pricing transparency pages).
4. Build a **referral program** (10% credit).
5. Review **pricing and margins monthly**; target 40%+ net by month 12.
