## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing Foundation (Weeks 1–3)

1. **Register your LLC** with the California Secretary of State ($70 online filing).
2. **Obtain your Long Beach Business License** via the city's online portal ($85–$310/year; contractor classification requires proof of CSLB license).
3. **Secure your CSLB license** — a **C-36 (Painting), B (General Building), or C-33 (Painting/Decorating)** classification depending on scope. Note: **jobs under $500 total (labor + materials) do not require a CSLB license**, but most Long Beach property managers require one anyway.
4. **Carry insurance:** General liability **$1M/$2M minimum** ($1,200–$2,400/year) plus workers' comp if hiring.
5. **Set up a business bank account and a simple bookkeeping stack** (Wave or QuickBooks Simple Start, $0–$30/month).

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 3–6)

6. **Claim and fully optimize your Google Business Profile** — categories: *Handyman*, *Contractor*, *Drywall Contractor*. Add 15–20 geo-tagged project photos, service areas (Long Beach + Signal Hill + Lakewood), and Q&A pre-population.
7. **Launch a 6-page core site:** Home, Services, Service Areas, About, Reviews, Contact — built on WordPress or Webflow by a verified freelancer ($600–$1,800).
8. **Deploy programmatic service-area pages** for 20–40 Long Beach neighborhoods (Belmont Shore, Bixby Knolls, Los Altos, Naples, Rose Park, Cambodia Town, etc.) — each with **unique local proof** (permit notes, neighborhood-specific repair patterns).
9. **Build 25–40 local citations** (Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Houzz, Thumbtack, Long Beach Chamber).

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation Engine (Weeks 6–10)

10. **Turn on Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$1,200/month starting budget) — highest-intent leads at **$28–$52 each**.
11. **Launch a review-generation loop:** automated SMS request 24 hours post-job; target **25 Google reviews by day 90** (the Long Beach ranking threshold for map-pack visibility).
12. **Build referral partnerships** with 5–8 local realtors, property managers, and hardware stores (ACE on 4th Street, Friedman's in Bixby Knolls).

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 4–6)

13. **Add a second revenue line** — e.g., ADU consulting or landlord turnover packages — to lift average ticket from ~$380 to ~$1,100.
14. **Track cost-per-booked-job** weekly; kill any channel above **$95/booking**.
15. **Reinvest 8–12% of gross revenue** into content and paid acquisition through month 12.

**Realistic 6-month financial snapshot:** $9K–$14K startup spend → $14K–$22K/month gross by month 6 → **35–48% net margin** for a solo operator with one helper.
