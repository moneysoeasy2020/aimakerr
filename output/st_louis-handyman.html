# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in St. Louis, MO
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

St. Louis' handyman and home-services market is projected to generate roughly **$410M–$460M in annual consumer spend across the metro in 2026**, with the average independent handyman billing **$65–$95 per hour** and small remodeling jobs (drywall, deck repair, bathroom refresh) priced at **$1,800–$12,500 per project**. The metro's ~1.1M occupied housing units — roughly **63% built before 1980** — create a durable repair-and-renovation backlog that favors licensed, insured operators with strong local search visibility.

Startup capital requirements are modest but non-trivial: a **Missouri LLC filing costs $50 online**, a St. Louis City business license runs **$50–$150 annually** depending on classification, general liability insurance averages **$1,200–$3,200/year** for a solo operator, and a reliable used work van plus tool kit typically runs **$9,000–$18,000**. Most first-year operators should budget **$14,000–$28,000 in total launch costs** and target **$8,000–$15,000 in monthly revenue by month 6** to reach break-even on a solo-operator model.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2)
1. File **Missouri LLC** online ($50) via Secretary of State.
2. Obtain **St. Louis City or County business license** ($50–$150).
3. Register with **Missouri Department of Revenue** for sales/use tax where applicable.
4. Secure **general liability insurance** ($1,200–$3,200/yr) — required for most property manager referrals.
5. Confirm **contractor licensing thresholds** — Missouri has no statewide general contractor license, but St. Louis City and County require registration for structural/electrical/plumbing work. Handyman-scope work (under $600/job in some jurisdictions) has lighter requirements; verify current 2026 rules with the city.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
1. Register domain + business email (~$30/yr).
2. Launch **5–8 page website** with service pages, service-area pages for top 6 ZIPs, and LocalBusiness schema.
3. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, hours, photos, Q&A, service areas).
4. Build **30+ local citations** (Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor, BBB, Nextdoor, Thumbtack).
5. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** — pay-per-lead, typically **$18–$45/lead** in St. Louis handyman category.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
1. Launch **Google Ads** on high-intent keywords ("handyman near me," "drywall repair St. Louis") — budget **$800–$1,500/mo**.
2. Post **weekly GBP updates** and solicit reviews after every job (target 25+ reviews by day 90).
3. Join **Nextdoor** and 2–3 neighborhood Facebook groups in target ZIPs.
4. Partner with **2–3 local property managers** and real estate agents for referral flow.

### Phase 4 — Q4 Optimization (Weeks 8–13)
1. Publish **seasonal service pages** (weatherization, gutter cleaning, holiday punch lists).
2. Run **retargeting ads** to site visitors ($300–$500/mo).
3. Track **cost-per-booked-job** weekly; kill underperforming keywords.
4. Build **email/SMS list** from every quote for winter reactivation.
