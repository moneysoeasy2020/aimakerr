## Executive Snapshot: Atlanta Handyman Market (2026 Q4)

Atlanta's handyman and general contractor market is projected to generate **$1.42 billion in annual revenue across the 12-county metro** in 2026, with the City of Atlanta proper accounting for roughly **$410–$480 million** of that total. The metro's **2.1 million occupied housing units** — with a median home age of **38 years** — drive consistent demand for repair, maintenance, and light remodeling work. Average handyman service tickets in Atlanta run **$185–$340 per job**, with hourly rates of **$65–$110/hour** for licensed general contractors and **$45–$75/hour** for non-licensed handymen (Georgia does not license "handyman" work under **$2,500 per job**, but requires a **Georgia Residential Basic Contractor (RBC) license** for anything above that threshold or involving structural, electrical, or plumbing work).

Startup capital for a lean Atlanta handyman operation runs **$8,500–$22,000** (vehicle, tools, insurance, licensing, bonding, and initial marketing), while a full contractor build-out with a crew of 2–3 runs **$35,000–$65,000**. Expect a **6–9 month path to $10K/month revenue** if you execute the digital asset plan below, with **$4,200–$7,800/month in realistic Year 1 marketing spend** depending on whether you use local agencies or verified freelancers.

- **Q4 is Atlanta's "pre-holiday punch list" season**: homeowners book gutter cleaning, HVAC tune-ups, drywall repair, and paint touch-ups before Thanksgiving and December hosting. Booking volume historically spikes **28–42%** between October 15 and December 5.

- **Aging housing stock**: 61% of City of Atlanta homes were built before 1990 — steady recurring repair demand.
- **Corporate relocation inflow**: Atlanta added **~38,000 net new households** in 2025–2026, many needing move-in punch-list work.

- **Average customer acquisition cost (CAC)** for Atlanta handyman businesses: **$95–$180** via paid ads, **$28–$65** via organic/GBP.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Licensing Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Register LLC** with Georgia Secretary of State — **$100 filing fee**.
2. **Obtain Atlanta business license** (City of Atlanta Occupational Tax Certificate) — **$50–$400** based on projected revenue.
3. **Secure Georgia Residential Basic Contractor (RBC) license** if jobs exceed $2,500 — exam + **$100–$300** fees.
4. **General liability insurance** — **$650–$1,400/year** for $1M/$2M coverage (essential for Atlanta residential work).
5. **Surety bond** — **$100–$250/year** for $10K bond.
6. **EIN + business banking** — free.

### Phase 2: Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–4)
1. Hire **verified freelance web developer** ($650–$1,400) for a 5–7 page local-SEO site.
2. Set up **Google Business Profile** with service-area targeting (Atlanta, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Buckhead, Midtown, East Atlanta).
3. Deploy **10 programmatic service-area landing pages** (e.g., "Handyman in Buckhead," "Drywall Repair Sandy Springs").
4. Install **review automation** (SMS request after job completion) — target **15+ reviews in first 60 days**.
5. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** — Atlanta LSA cost-per-lead runs **$28–$65** for handyman category.

### Phase 3: Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
1. **Google Local Services Ads** — budget **$800–$1,500/month** for first 90 days.
2. **Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook groups** — free, high-conversion in Atlanta's intown neighborhoods (Virginia-Highland, Grant Park, Kirkwood).
3. **Partnership outreach** — 10 real estate agents, 5 property managers, 3 HOAs per week.
4. **Yelp + Angi free listings** — claim and optimize (paid tiers optional after 90 days).

### Phase 4: GEO/AI Search Optimization (Weeks 6–12)
1. **Structured data (LocalBusiness + Service schema)** on every page — critical for AI Overview citation.
2. **FAQ blocks** answering "How much does a handyman cost in Atlanta?" with 2026 Q4 pricing.
3. **Consistent NAP** across 40+ citations (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Thumbtack, Houzz, Nextdoor).
4. **Publish 2 local case-study pages/month** ("Drywall repair in Midtown Atlanta — $340 job") to feed generative engines.

### Phase 5: Scale & Optimize (Month 4+)
1. Track **CAC by channel** — target blended CAC under **$75**.
2. Reinvest **15–20% of revenue** into marketing until $30K/month, then **10–12%**.
3. Add **second crew** when booked jobs exceed **22/week**.
4. Consider **boutique Atlanta agency** only at **$25K+/month revenue**.
