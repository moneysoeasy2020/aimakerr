## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)**
1. Entity formation (Nebraska LLC/PC), NPI, DEA, and Nebraska dental license verification.
2. Secure financing: SBA 7(a) or equipment leasing; budget $450K–$720K total.
3. Site selection in NW Lincoln/Highlands or south Lincoln corridor; negotiate 7–10 year NNN lease.
4. Engage architect + contractor for **$85K–$140K** buildout; confirm radiology/plumbing compliance.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 3–5)**
5. Build website with service + location pages, online scheduling, and dental schema markup.
6. Claim and optimize Google Business Profile; seed 40+ local citations.
7. Launch review-generation workflow (target 25+ reviews pre-opening).

**Phase 3 — Go-to-Market (Months 5–6)**
8. Launch Google Ads (target $2,500–$4,000/mo spend; $95–$180 CAC benchmark).
9. Publish 12 GEO-optimized service/location content pages.
10. Activate insurance credentialing (PPO panels take 60–120 days — start early).

**Phase 4 — Stabilize & Scale (Months 6–12)**
11. Monitor new-patient CAC monthly; reallocate ad spend to top-performing service lines.
12. Add recall/reactivation campaigns; target 35–45% hygiene reappointment rate.
13. Evaluate second-operatory expansion or associate hire at 1,200+ active patients.
