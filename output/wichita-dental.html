## 2026 Q4 Wichita Market Analysis

**Demand fundamentals**
- Sedgwick County population: ~525,000; Wichita MSA ~650,000.
- Adults 18+ without a dental visit in past 12 months: **~38%** (Kansas Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System trend), above the national ~33% — indicates under-served recall demand.
- Insurance mix: Delta Dental of Kansas, Blue Cross Blue Shield of Kansas, and MCNA/Envolve (Medicaid) dominate. **~22–26%** of Wichita children are Medicaid-enrolled — a meaningful volume segment if you credential with KanCare MCOs.
- Competitive density: roughly **1 dentist per 1,650 residents** metro-wide, but **1 per 1,100–1,300** in affluent ZIPs (67206, 67230, 67235) — expect CPC pressure in those areas.

**2026 Q4 timing factors**
- Q4 is the **peak "use-it-or-lose-it" insurance window** (Oct–Dec). New clinics opening in Q4 capture patients with remaining annual maximums — a genuine first-quarter revenue accelerant.
- Kansas dental staffing: hygienist vacancy rate ~**11–14%**; budget **$38–$46/hr** for hygienists and **$22–$28/hr** for expanded-duty assistants in Wichita (below national averages, a local cost advantage).
- Google local pack for "dentist Wichita" shows **8–14 map-pack competitors** in core queries — GBP optimization is the highest-ROI early channel.

**Phase 1 — Pre-launch (Months 1–3)**
1. Entity formation (Kansas LLC or PA), NPI, DEA if prescribing, Kansas Dental Board licensure verification.

3. Equipment procurement (refurbished chairs can save 40–50%).
4. Credential with Delta Dental of Kansas, BCBS KS, and 2–3 KanCare MCOs (allow 90–120 days).

**Phase 2 — Digital foundation (Months 2–4)**
5. Launch website with LocalBusiness + Dentist schema, service-area pages for Wichita + Derby, Andover, Maize, Goddard.
6. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (categories, services, Q&A, photos weekly).
7. Build 40–60 local citations (Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, BBB, Wichita Chamber).
8. Install review-request automation; target **25+ reviews in first 90 days**.

**Phase 3 — Demand capture (Month 3 onward)**
9. Launch Google Ads on high-intent terms ("dentist near me Wichita," "emergency dentist Wichita," "dental implants Wichita") — expect **$8–$22 CPC** in this DMA.
10. Publish 8–12 programmatic service+city pages (e.g., "teeth whitening Derby KS").
11. Run a soft-opening insurance-maximization campaign (Oct–Dec) targeting patients with unused benefits.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Months 6–12)**
12. Add recall/reactivation workflows; target **70%+ hygiene reappointment rate**.
13. Layer reputation ads and local service ads (Google LSA) once review count exceeds 50.
14. Review CAC monthly; target blended new-patient cost **under $150** by month 9.
