Typical residential pricing in Q4 2026 runs **$0.28–$0.45 per square foot**, with a 3-room minimum job landing at **$135–$210**; commercial jobs (offices, restaurants, property management turns) average **$0.18–$0.30/sq ft** with recurring contract value of **$600–$2,400/month** per mid-size account.

Peak demand windows are **April–June (spring allergen season)** and **October–December (holiday hosting + year-end lease turns)**, which makes a Q4 2026 launch strategically timed: you enter during the second seasonal surge with 90 days to build reviews before spring. ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & compliance:** Register LLC with NM Secretary of State (~$50). Obtain Albuquerque Business Registration (free–$35). Secure NM Gross Receipts Tax permit (CRS) via NM Taxation & Revenue.
2. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) at **$600–$1,200/yr**; add commercial auto for the van.
3. **Equipment decision:** Used van-mounted unit ($8K–$18K) vs. portable ($2.5K–$5K). Q4 lean start: portable + strong upsell, upgrade in Q2.
4. **Chemicals & supplies:** $800–$1,500 initial stock (pre-spray, rinse, protectant, spotting kit, air movers).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
5. **Google Business Profile:** Create, verify, add 10+ geo-tagged job photos, set service area to your 5 target ZIPs, enable messaging and booking.
6. **Website:** 5 pages (Home, Services, Commercial, Service Areas, Contact) with **LocalBusiness schema, Service schema, and FAQ schema** — this is what GEO engines cite.
7. **Citations:** Consistent NAP on Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB, and 15+ local directories.
8. **Review engine:** Automated post-job SMS/email request; target **15 reviews in first 60 days** (4.8★+ threshold).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
9. **Google Local Services Ads:** $500–$800/mo budget; LSA leads convert at 25–35% for carpet cleaning.
10. **Property-management outreach:** Pitch 10–15 PM firms (Westside/Rio Rancho) with a turn-key pricing sheet ($0.22–$0.28/sq ft, 48-hr turnaround guarantee).
11. **Nextdoor + neighborhood targeting:** Hyperlocal posts in Rio Rancho and NE Heights groups.

### Phase 4 — Scale & GEO Capture (Weeks 8–12)
12. **Content for AI citation:** Publish 6–10 programmatic pages (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in [ZIP/Neighborhood]") with local pricing tables and FAQ blocks — these get surfaced by AI Overviews and Perplexity.
13. **Recurring contracts:** Convert 20–30% of residential clients to annual maintenance plans ($99–$149/yr membership with discounted cleans).
14. **KPI review:** Target **$8K–$14K month-3 revenue**, CAC under $85, and 40+ reviews by day 90.
