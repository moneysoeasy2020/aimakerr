# Launching an HVAC Services Business in El Paso, TX
### 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

El Paso's HVAC market is shaped by extreme summer heat (100°F+ days from May–September), a fast-growing population of ~681,000 citywide (~870,000 metro), and a housing stock where roughly 60% of homes were built before 2000 — meaning aging systems and high replacement demand. A residential HVAC startup should budget **$18,000–$35,000 in startup capital** (licensing, tools, vehicle wrap, insurance, and initial marketing), with first-year revenue targets of **$180,000–$420,000** for a 1–2 truck operation. Typical El Paso residential pricing in 2026 Q4: **diagnostic/service call $89–$149**, **AC tune-up $99–$179**, **standard split-system replacement $6,500–$11,500 installed**, and **emergency after-hours calls $150–$300 plus parts**.

Labor economics matter here: El Paso's lower cost of living means you can staff a certified technician at **$22–$30/hour** (vs. $32–$45 in Austin or Dallas), giving you a structural margin advantage on service calls. However, the metro's median household income (~$58,000) means price sensitivity is real — position on **financing and repair-vs-replace transparency**, not premium branding. Expect 3–6 months to first profitable month if you secure 8–12 recurring maintenance agreements in month one.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Register LLC with Texas SOS (~$300).
2. Obtain **TDLR Air Conditioning & Refrigeration Contractor license** (required in TX; exam + fees ~$500–$900).
3. Secure **general liability + commercial auto insurance** ($2,400–$4,800/yr).
4. Apply for El Paso city contractor registration and any HOA-relevant permits.

**Phase 2 — Operational Setup (Weeks 3–8)**
5. Purchase tools and 1–2 service vehicles ($12,000–$22,000 used van + equipment).
6. Set up CRM, scheduling, and call tracking (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber).
7. Establish supplier accounts (local HVAC distributors — pricing tiers matter).

**Phase 3 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 4–10)**
8. Launch website with **city + neighborhood landing pages** (Westside, Eastside, Northeast, Horizon City, Socorro).
9. Optimize Google Business Profile with service categories, photos, and Q&A.
10. Publish **GEO-structured content**: pricing tables, "repair vs. replace" guides, Fort Bliss PCS checklists — all with local citations and schema markup.

**Phase 4 — Lead Generation & Reviews (Weeks 8–16)**
11. Launch Google Local Services Ads + targeted Google Ads ($1,500–$3,000/mo).
12. Implement automated review requests — target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (critical for AI-engine trust signals).
13. Build 15–25 local citations (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, El Paso Chamber).

**Phase 5 — Scale (Months 4–12)**
14. Add a second technician once service revenue exceeds $25K/mo.
15. Introduce **maintenance agreements** ($12–$18/mo recurring) — target 100+ members by month 12.
16. Expand GEO content to seasonal queries ("winter heating repair El Paso," "monsoon-season AC prep").
