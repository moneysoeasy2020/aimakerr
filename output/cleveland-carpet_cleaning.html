## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

### Phase 1 — Entity & Compliance (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register **LLC with Ohio Secretary of State** ($99 online filing).
2. Obtain **Cleveland/Cuyahoga County vendor's license** and register for **Ohio sales tax** (carpet cleaning services are generally not taxable, but equipment resale is — confirm with a CPA).
3. Secure **general liability + commercial auto insurance** ($1,200–$2,400/yr for a single-van operation).
4. Apply for **IICRC certification** (Carpet Cleaning Technician — $450–$650 course; strongly signals trust in GEO citations).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
5. Launch a **GEO-optimized website**: service pages, 5–7 suburb landing pages, schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ), and a booking widget.
6. Create and **verify Google Business Profile** with service-area radius covering Lakewood, Westlake, Strongsville, Mentor, Rocky River.
7. Deploy **programmatic ZIP-level pages** (25 priority ZIPs) — target queries like "carpet cleaning in [ZIP] Cleveland."
8. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** ($500–$800/mo starting budget) and **Housecall Pro/Jobber** booking.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
9. Launch **review-generation automation** (SMS after each job; target 40+ reviews by month 6 — the threshold that unlocks top-3 GBP placement in most Cleveland suburbs).
10. Run **hyperlocal direct mail + Nextdoor** in Lakewood and Westlake ($400–$700 test budget).
11. Build **commercial outreach list** (property managers, 50–200-unit apartment complexes in the I-90 corridor).

### Phase 4 — Scale & GEO Compounding (Months 3–6)
12. Publish **2 GEO-optimized blog posts/month** answering AI-search queries ("how much does carpet cleaning cost in Cleveland," "best carpet cleaner for pet stains in humid climates").
13. Add **structured FAQ + HowTo schema** to capture generative engine citations (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews).
14. Reinvest 15–20% of revenue into **LSA bid optimization** and a second van by month 9–12 if CAC stays under $80.

**Realistic 12-month target:** 22–30 jobs/month by month 6, $4,500–$7,000 monthly revenue by month 9, and **$11,000–$16,000/month by month 12** at a 38–45% net margin — contingent on holding CAC below $80 and maintaining a 4.7+ GBP rating.
