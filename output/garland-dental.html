## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

**Phase 1 — Site & Entity (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Form **PLLC** in Texas; register with TSBDE (Texas State Board of Dental Examiners); secure DUNS + NPI.
2. Confirm **HIPAA-compliant** software stack (EHR, scheduling, payment).
3. Lease in a high-traffic corridor: **Firewheel Pkwy, Broadway Blvd, or near I-635** — target 1,800–2,600 sq ft, $28–$42/sq ft NNN.
4. File assumed name + local permits with City of Garland.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–8)**
5. Launch website with **service-area pages** for Garland, Rowlett, Sachse, Mesquite, Richardson.
6. Claim + fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, services, photos, Q&A, booking link).
7. Deploy **review engine** (post-visit SMS) — target 40+ reviews by day 90.
8. Build **Spanish-language** mirror pages and GBP posts.
9. Set up **call tracking + CRM** to measure CAC by channel.

**Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 6–12)**
10. Launch **Google Ads** on high-intent terms: "emergency dentist Garland," "dentist near me Garland," "Spanish speaking dentist Garland."
11. Run **Q4 insurance-maximization campaign** (Nov–Dec) — "Use your 2026 benefits before they expire."
12. Partner with 5–8 local PCPs, pediatricians, and pharmacies for referral loops.
13. Seed **in-house membership plan** for uninsured patients.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Month 4+)**
14. Add service lines by demand: **emergency → cosmetic (Invisalign/veneers) → sedation → implants**.
15. Reinvest 6–9% of revenue into marketing; track **cost-per-new-patient** weekly.
16. Expand to second location once **$1.2M+ annual collections** and 1,500+ active patients.
