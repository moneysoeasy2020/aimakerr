### Local Agency vs. Verified Freelance — Explicit Comparison

**Brick-and-mortar agency (Portland metro):**
- **Pros:** Single point of accountability, in-person meetings, bundled services, local reputation stake.
- **Cons:** 2.5–4× cost, slower iteration, junior staff often execute the work, 12-month lock-in contracts common, upsell pressure on retainers.
- **Best for:** Operators planning to scale to 3+ vans within 12 months and wanting hands-off management.

**Verified freelance experts (Upwork Top Rated, Contra verified, or Portland-specific SEO consultants with public case studies):**
- **Pros:** 60–75% lower cost, direct access to the practitioner, faster turnaround, easy to test-and-replace.
- **Cons:** Requires you to vet rigorously (demand 2+ home-services case studies, verify GBP results, check references), no single accountable party, risk of scope creep.
- **Best for:** Lean launches under $50K capital where every dollar must produce a booked job.

**Verdict for a 2026 Q4 Portland launch:** A hybrid — hire a verified freelancer for website + GBP + local SEO ($1,800–$3,200 total setup, $600–$900/mo retainer), and reserve agency budget only if you exceed 2 vans or $250K revenue.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Operational Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register LLC** with Oregon Secretary of State — **$100**.
2. **Obtain Portland Business License** (Revenue Division) — **$100 + annual fee**.
3. **Register with Oregon CCB** if subcontracting — **$325** two-year.
4. **Secure insurance:** general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto + workers' comp — **$2,400–$4,200/year**.
5. **IICRC certification** (Carpet Cleaning Technician + Water Restoration) — **$1,200–$1,800** — differentiates you in Portland's informed buyer market.
6. **Equipment decision:** used truck-mount ($12K–$22K) vs. portable ($3K–$6K) vs. van + portable hybrid. For Portland's older homes and multi-story STRs, truck-mount wins on ticket size.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–5)
7. **Domain + hosting** — $15–$40/mo.
8. **Website build** via verified freelancer — 5–7 pages, service-area pages for Portland, Beaverton, Gresham, Hillsboro, Lake Oswego, Tigard.
9. **Google Business Profile** — claim, verify, add 20+ photos, service list, booking link.
10. **Review system** — configure SMS review requests post-job; target 25 reviews in 90 days.
11. **Google Ads** — start with $1,200–$1,800/mo on exact-match "carpet cleaning [city]" + "pet odor removal Portland."

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)
12. **Property manager outreach** — target 40–60 small PM firms in Multnomah/Washington/Clackamas; offer 10% portfolio discount + 24-hr turnaround.
13. **STR turnover contracts** — pitch 30–50 STR operators via Hostfully/Guesty directories and local Facebook host groups.
14. **Neighborhood seeding** — door hangers in Sellwood, Hawthorne, Alberta Arts (high owner-occupancy, older carpet).
15. **Referral program** — $25 credit per referred job; track via Jobber.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 4–12)
16. **Add second technician** at ~$85K revenue threshold.
17. **Programmatic SEO pages** — one page per Portland neighborhood + service combo (e.g., "pet odor removal in Sellwood").
18. **Review velocity** — maintain 8–12 new Google reviews/month.
19. **Commercial expansion** — target 3–5 recurring contracts by month 9 ($1,100–$4,800 each).
