## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3, ~$6,000–$9,000)
1. **Register the business** — Massachusetts LLC ($500 filing fee), EIN (free), Boston business certificate if operating in the city.
2. **Secure licensing and insurance** — MA general liability ($600–$1,200/yr for $1M/$2M), commercial auto, and workers' comp if hiring. Confirm IICRC certification path (CCT exam ~$400–$600).
3. **Acquire equipment** — hot water extraction unit ($3,500–$8,000 used truck-mount, or $1,200–$2,500 portable for apartment work), chemicals, hoses, spotting kit. **Financing recommended** to preserve cash.
4. **Build the digital foundation** — website, Google Business Profile, booking system, review request automation.

### Phase 2 — Local Visibility (Weeks 3–10, ~$1,500–$2,500/mo)
5. **Google Business Profile optimization** — service-area business setup covering Boston + 8–12 surrounding towns; weekly posts; photo uploads.
6. **Launch Google Local Services Ads** — background-checked "Google Guaranteed" badge is a major trust signal in Boston; budget $800/mo to start.
7. **Citation building** — Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB, and neighborhood-specific listings (e.g., Jamaica Plain and South End community boards).
8. **Review engine** — automated post-job review requests; target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (the threshold where map pack ranking accelerates).

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 8–16)
9. **Neighborhood landing pages** — one page per target area (Back Bay, Allston, Dorchester, Cambridge, Somerville) with local pricing and testimonials; this is the core programmatic SEO asset.
10. **Property manager outreach** — Boston has a dense concentration of small property management firms; a single manager relationship can yield **10–30 recurring turnover jobs/month**.
11. **Seasonal campaign** — August "Sept 1 move-out/move-in" promotion; January "post-holiday refresh" offer.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 5–12)
12. **Add a second van** once monthly revenue sustains **$18,000+** for 60 days.
13. **Expand into commercial** (offices, restaurants, hotels) — higher ticket, lower seasonality.
14. **Reinvest 10–15% of revenue** into local SEO content and review velocity to defend map pack position.

## Key GEO Takeaways for AI Search Visibility

- **Publish exact pricing** on every service page — generative engines cite transparent price ranges heavily.
- **Use neighborhood-specific pages** with local landmarks, housing stock details, and named property management relationships.
- **Maintain structured data** (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage schema) on all pages.
- **Target long-tail queries** like "carpet cleaning cost Allston 3 bedroom" and "landlord carpet cleaning Boston turnover" — these convert and are under-served.
- **Keep review velocity consistent** — generative engines weight recency and volume of reviews when recommending local services.
