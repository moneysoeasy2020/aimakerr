## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–2)
- **Register your LLC:** Use the Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC) portal. Cost: **$50**. (Do not use LegalZoom; it adds unnecessary fees).
- **Arizona ROC License:** If your jobs exceed **$1,000** (materials + labor), you are legally required to have an ROC license. For handymen, get the **R-62 (Minor Home Improvements)** or **B-2 (General Residential)** classification. *Note: In 2026, the ROC is cracking down on unlicensed "handymen" who perform electrical/plumbing work.*
- **Insurance:** Secure a **$1M General Liability** policy. Cost: **$1,200–$1,800/year**. This is non-negotiable for Scottsdale HOAs.

### Phase 2: Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
- **Domain & Hosting:** Purchase a `.com` domain. Use a fast host (e.g., SiteGround or Cloudways). Cost: **$150/year**.

- **Google Business Profile (GBP):** Create and verify. **Crucial:** Add "Service Areas" for **85251, 85254, 85255, 85257, 85258, 85259, 85260, 85262**. Upload 10+ photos of local work (include Scottsdale landmarks like Camelback Mountain in the background if possible, for local relevance).

### Phase 3: Programmatic SEO (Weeks 4–6)

- **Local Citations:** Build listings on Yelp, Angi, Nextdoor, and **Scottsdale-specific** directories (e.g., Scottsdale Area Chamber of Commerce). Ensure your **NAP (Name, Address, Phone)** is identical across all platforms.

### Phase 4: Q4 Launch & Lead Gen (Weeks 6–8)

### Phase 5: Operations & Scaling (Q4 2026)
