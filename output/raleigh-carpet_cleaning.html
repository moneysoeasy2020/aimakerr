## Executive Snapshot: Raleigh Market Numbers (2026 Q4)

Raleigh's carpet cleaning market is estimated at **$14.2M–$16.8M in annual residential and commercial revenue** as of Q4 2026, driven by a metro population of approximately **1.52 million** and roughly **610,000 occupied housing units** across Wake County. Average residential carpet cleaning pricing in Raleigh runs **$0.28–$0.45 per square foot** for hot-water extraction, translating to **$175–$340 for a typical 3-bedroom home (900–1,200 sq ft of carpet)**. Break-even for a solo operator typically occurs at **18–26 jobs per month** at a $265 average ticket, assuming $2,400–$3,100 in monthly fixed costs. Raleigh's competitive density is moderate — approximately **140–170 active carpet cleaning providers** in the metro — but only **~35% have optimized digital presence**, leaving significant GEO and local SEO headroom for a 2026 Q4 entrant.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register LLC** with NC Secretary of State ($125 filing fee); obtain EIN.
2. **Secure insurance:** general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto — budget **$1,400–$2,200/year** in NC.
3. **Equipment decision:** used truck-mount ($6,500–$11,000) vs. portable ($2,800–$4,500). Truck-mount preferred for commercial contracts.
4. **Chemicals & supplies:** $600–$900 initial stock (pre-spray, extraction detergent, defoamer, spotting kit, protectant).

### Phase 2: Digital Assets (Weeks 2–6)
5. **Google Business Profile** — verify with Raleigh address or service-area setup covering Wake, Durham, Johnston counties.
6. **Website** — 7 core pages + 12–18 service-area pages (Raleigh, Cary, Apex, Wake Forest, Garner, Morrisville, Holly Springs).
7. **GEO optimization** — structured data (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ schema), consistent NAP citations across 25+ directories, and AI-citable FAQ content (*"How much does carpet cleaning cost in Raleigh?"*).

### Phase 3: Launch & Demand Capture (Weeks 4–10)
8. **Google Local Services Ads** — $500–$900/month test budget; LSA leads convert 3–5x better than standard PPC for home services.
9. **Review engine** — target **25 Google reviews in 90 days**; respond within 24 hours.
10. **Partnerships** — 6–10 property managers, 3–5 realtors, 2–3 apartment complexes in Downtown/Cary/North Hills.

### Phase 4: Scale (Months 4–12)
11. **Recurring contracts** — pitch quarterly maintenance to 15+ commercial accounts.
12. **Second van** once monthly revenue exceeds **$18,000** consistently.
13. **Reinvest 8–12% of revenue** into GEO content and review velocity.
