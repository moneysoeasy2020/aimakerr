## Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts

**Local brick-and-mortar agency (Durham/Raleigh firms):**
- **Pros:** single point of accountability, in-person meetings, bundled creative + media, established local references, faster escalation.
- **Cons:** **2.5–4× the cost**, junior staff doing the work, 12-month lock-ins, slower GEO iteration, generic templates.
- **Typical all-in year-one cost:** **$28,000–$55,000**.

**Verified freelance experts (vetted via portfolio, references, test project):**
- **Pros:** **40–60% lower cost**, senior specialist doing the actual work, flexible month-to-month, faster GEO/AI-Overview optimization, direct communication.
- **Cons:** you manage coordination, variable availability, must verify credentials rigorously (case studies, live audits, client references).
- **Typical all-in year-one cost:** **$8,000–$18,000**.

**Verdict for a 2026 Q4 Durham launch:** Use **verified freelancers for website, GBP, local SEO, and programmatic pages** (highest ROI), and reserve a **brick-and-mortar agency only for paid media management** if ad spend exceeds $5,000/month. This hybrid saves **$15,000–$30,000** in year one without sacrificing output quality.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–3)
1. **Market & site selection:** Target ZIPs 27713/27703; negotiate **$28–$42/sq ft NNN**; confirm parking and ADA compliance.
2. **Entity & licensing:** NC dental license, LLC formation, NPI, DEA (if prescribing), malpractice insurance.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) or equipment leasing; secure **$450K–$850K** total capital.
4. **Payer contracts:** Apply to Delta Dental, BCBS NC, MetLife, Cigna; decide on Medicaid participation.

### Phase 2 — Build-Out & Digital Foundation (Months 3–6)
5. **Construction:** 4-operatory build-out at **$350–$550/sq ft**; equipment procurement (**$150K–$300K**).
6. **Website + GBP:** Launch booking-enabled site and fully optimized Google Business Profile (**$3K–$7K** freelance).
7. **Programmatic SEO:** Publish 15–40 service-area + procedure pages targeting Durham + Raleigh/Chapel Hill edge.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Patient Acquisition (Months 6–9)
8. **Soft opening:** $99–$199 new-patient exam bundle; target **40–60 new patients/month**.
9. **Paid search + reviews:** $2K–$6K/mo ad spend; drive 50+ Google reviews in first 90 days.
10. **GEO content:** Publish AI-Overview-optimized FAQ, pricing, and insurance pages.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 9–18)
11. **Add hygienist capacity** and expand hours; target **break-even by month 12–18**.
12. **Reputation flywheel:** 150+ reviews, 4.8+ star average, consistent NAP citations.
13. **Reinvest:** Evaluate 5th–6th operatory and second location once **$1.2M–$1.8M annual revenue** is stable.
