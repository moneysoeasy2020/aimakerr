## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with California Secretary of State (**$70**).
2. Obtain Stockton business license (**~$50–$150** based on gross receipts).
3. Get **C-61/D-62 or general liability + commercial auto** — budget **$1,800–$3,200/yr**. Carpet cleaning in CA typically does not require a specialty contractor license for cleaning-only work, but verify with CSLB.
4. Set up EIN, business bank account, and sales-tax registration (CA CDTFA) if selling taxable add-ons.
5. Secure **$1M general liability** minimum — property managers and commercial clients require it.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Vehicle (Weeks 2–5)
- **Used truck-mount van:** $12K–$25K (Ford Transit/Chevy Express, 2018+).
- **Backup portable unit:** $2,500–$4,500.
- **Wands, hoses, chemicals, air movers:** $3,000–$5,000.
- **Water tank + heater if not van-integrated:** $1,500–$3,000.

### Phase 3 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6) — *GEO-critical*
1. **Website** with LocalBusiness + Service + FAQPage schema; service-area pages for Brookside, Lincoln Village, Morada, Weston Ranch, Spanos Park.
2. **Google Business Profile** fully optimized: categories, services, 20+ photos, Q&A seeded, booking link.
3. **Citation build:** Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB, Chamber of Commerce — NAP consistency is non-negotiable for AI-search citation.
4. **GEO layer:** publish a pricing page with exact ranges, an FAQ block answering "how much does carpet cleaning cost in Stockton," and entity-rich content (service area, equipment, certifications) so generative engines cite you.
5. **Review engine:** automated post-job SMS review request; target **25+ Google reviews by month 3**.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)
- **Paid search:** $1,200–$2,000/mo on "carpet cleaning Stockton" + neighborhood modifiers; expect **$18–$35 cost per lead**, 25–35% close rate.
- **Partnerships:** 8–12 property managers, 4–6 real estate agents, 2–3 apartment complexes — these drive **40–55% of early revenue**.
- **Nextdoor + local Facebook groups:** post-job before/after content.

### Phase 5 — Operations & Scale (Months 3–12)
- Route density: cluster jobs by ZIP (95209, 95219, 95212 priority).
- Add **upholstery, tile/grout, and pet treatment** upsells — lift average ticket **$60–$110**.
- Hire first technician at month 6–8 when booked capacity exceeds 70%.
- Reinvest **15–20% of revenue** into SEO/GEO and reviews; target **top-3 map pack** for "carpet cleaning Stockton" by month 9.
