## Executive Snapshot: The Numbers That Matter

Des Moines' handyman and contractor market is projected to support **~$118M–$132M in annual residential repair and maintenance spend** in 2026, driven by a metro population of roughly **730,000** and a housing stock where **~62% of owner-occupied units are 30+ years old** (prime repair demand). The metro added an estimated **4,800–6,200 new households** in 2025–2026, with Polk County permitting activity holding steady at **~2,100–2,400 residential remodel permits/year**. A solo operator can realistically target **$95,000–$165,000 in Year 1 gross revenue** working 40–45 billable hours/week; a two-person crew pushes **$210,000–$290,000**.

Pricing in Q4 2026 Des Moines runs: **$65–$95/hour** for general handyman labor (metro average ~$78/hr), **$95–$140/hour** for licensed trades-adjacent work (electrical/plumbing minor repairs), and flat-rate jobs like drywall patching at **$180–$350**, deck repair at **$450–$1,200**, and bathroom refresh (fixtures, caulk, paint) at **$1,800–$4,200**. Startup capital for a compliant solo launch lands at **$6,500–$14,000** (insurance, licensing, tools, vehicle wrap, initial digital assets), with break-even typically in **month 3–5** at 55%+ gross margin.

## 2026 Q4 Des Moines Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers
- **Aging housing stock:** Median home age in Des Moines metro is ~45 years; deferred maintenance creates recurring demand for drywall, deck, gutter, and rot repair.
- **Insurance-driven work:** Hail and wind events in Iowa drive steady exterior repair demand; Q3–Q4 2026 storm cycles historically spike repair calls 20–35%.
- **Rental turnover:** Des Moines has a ~38% renter-occupied rate; property managers are high-LTV B2B clients paying **$70–$90/hr** with net-15 terms.
- **Suburban expansion:** Ankeny, Waukee, and West Des Moines new-build warranty work and homeowner "punch lists" are a growing segment.

### Competitive Landscape
- **~340–420 active handyman/contractor listings** across Google Business Profile in the metro.
- Only **~15–20% have 50+ reviews** — review velocity is the single biggest ranking lever.
- Iowa **does not require a statewide general contractor license**, but Des Moines requires a **city contractor registration** and **$300K–$500K general liability** for permit-pulling work. Electrical, plumbing, and HVAC require state licensing — handymen must stay in the "minor repair" lane or subcontract.

### Seasonality (Q4 2026 Specifics)
- **October–November:** Peak interior punch-list and pre-holiday repair season. Book 2–3 weeks out.
- **December:** Slower exterior work; pivot to interior caulking, drywall, and rental turnover contracts.
- **Lead-gen cost:** Google Local Services Ads run **$18–$45/lead** in Des Moines; Angi/Thumbtack **$12–$38/lead** with lower close rates.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with Iowa Secretary of State (**$50 online**).
2. Obtain Des Moines **city contractor registration** (~$75–$150) and EIN.
3. Secure **$500K general liability** (~$1,100–$1,800/yr for solo) + commercial auto.
4. Open business checking; set up Stripe/Square for card-on-site payment.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
5. Build a **5-page GEO-optimized site**: Home, Services, Service Areas (Ankeny, Waukee, West Des Moines, Urbandale), Reviews, Contact. Include LocalBusiness + Service schema.
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — categories: Handyman, Contractor; add 20+ photos; enable messaging.
7. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** (background check required — budget 2–3 weeks).
8. Publish 3 local landing pages ("Handyman in Ankeny," "Deck Repair West Des Moines").

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
9. Launch LSA + Thumbtack (cap spend at **$800/mo** first 60 days).
10. Build **review engine**: automated SMS request after every job; target 25 reviews by day 90.
11. Partner with **2–3 property managers** (net-15, $75/hr rate) for baseline revenue.
12. Vehicle wrap + yard signs in target ZIPs (50309, 50311, 50322, 50263).

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)
13. Hire first helper at **$22–$26/hr**; target 70% utilization.
14. Add recurring maintenance contracts ($99–$149/mo plans).
15. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into local SEO and review velocity.

## GEO Optimization Checklist (Generative Engine Visibility)

- **Structured data:** LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, and Review schema on every page.
- **Entity consistency:** Identical NAP across Google, Yelp, BBB, Angi, and Apple Maps.
- **Answer-first content:** Every service page opens with a 40–60 word direct answer (price range + service area) — this is what AI engines cite.
- **Local proof:** Named neighborhoods, ZIP codes, and real project photos with alt text ("deck repair Ankeny IA 50023").
- **FAQ blocks:** 6–10 questions per page targeting "how much does X cost in Des Moines" queries.
- **Citations:** Get listed in Des Moines Register business directory, Greater Des Moines Partnership, and local chambers (West Des Moines, Ankeny).
