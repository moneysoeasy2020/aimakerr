# Launching an HVAC Services Business in Memphis, TN
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Memphis HVAC demand is driven by extreme summer heat (30–40 days above 95°F annually) and a housing stock where roughly 60% of single-family homes were built before 1990 — meaning aging systems, frequent refrigerant retrofits, and high replacement volume. A realistic 2026 Q4 launch budget for a licensed, insured, two-truck residential HVAC operation in Shelby County runs **$48,000–$95,000** in startup capital, with **$18,000–$32,000** of that allocated to licensing, insurance, tools, and digital assets. Residential service call pricing in Memphis averages **$89–$149 diagnostic**, **$250–$650** for common repairs (capacitor, blower motor, contactor), and **$4,800–$9,500** for a 3-ton system replacement including labor and permit.

Market context: the Memphis MSA (Shelby, DeSoto, Fayette, Tipton, Crittenden counties) has approximately **1.34 million residents** and **~340,000 occupied housing units**, with HVAC contractor density lower per capita than Nashville or Atlanta — a genuine gap in the **Cordova, Bartlett, Collierville, Germantown, and Arlington** suburban corridors where median household incomes ($78K–$128K) support premium maintenance plans. Q4 is the ideal launch window: shoulder-season demand lets you build review volume and Google Business Profile authority before the January–February heating spike and the May–September cooling surge that generates 65–70% of annual revenue.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with Tennessee Secretary of State (**$300 filing fee**, ~$50/yr annual report).
2. Obtain **Memphis/Shelby County business license** (~$50–$150 based on receipts).
3. Secure **Tennessee HVAC contractor license** via state Board for Licensing Contractors (exam + experience requirements; verify current 2026 fees, typically **$250–$400**).
4. Purchase **general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto + workers' comp** — expect **$3,500–$7,500/yr** for a two-truck operation.
5. Register with **TVA/MLGW** as needed for rebate program participation (efficiency upsell).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6)
6. Launch website with **service-area pages for Cordova, Bartlett, Collierville, Germantown, Arlington, Germantown, Midtown, East Memphis** (programmatic, 20–40 pages).
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, services, photos, Q&A, booking link).
8. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** (background check + license verification required).
9. Deploy **review request automation** (SMS after every job; target 40+ reviews in first 90 days).
10. Install **call tracking + CRM** (Housecall Pro or Jobber; $99–$299/mo).

### Phase 3 — Operations & Fleet (Weeks 4–8)
11. Outfit **1–2 service vans** (used: $18K–$32K each; tools/inventory $6K–$12K).
12. Stock Q4-priority parts: capacitors, contactors, igniters, flame sensors, thermostats, R-454B retrofit kits.
13. Hire **1 lead tech (EPA 608 certified) + 1 apprentice**; Memphis market wages $22–$34/hr lead, $15–$19/hr apprentice.
14. Set flat-rate pricing book aligned to Memphis benchmarks above.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)
15. Run **$1,500–$3,000/mo Google Ads** targeting "AC repair Memphis," "furnace repair near me," "HVAC replacement Collierville."
16. Launch **maintenance plan offer ($149–$199/yr)** to build recurring revenue before summer.
17. Pursue **realtor and property manager partnerships** (Memphis has high rental density — 45%+ renter-occupied in some ZIPs).
18. Target **60+ Google reviews and 15+ LSA leads/week by end of Q1 2027**.

### Phase 5 — Scale (2027)
19. Add second crew once monthly revenue exceeds **$45K**.
20. Expand programmatic pages to DeSoto County (MS) and Tipton County.
21. Layer agency support only after $500K annualized revenue.
