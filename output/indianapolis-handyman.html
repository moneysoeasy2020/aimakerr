# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Indianapolis, IN
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

**Executive summary with hard numbers:** Indianapolis (Marion County) supports roughly **4,300–5,200 active handyman/contractor establishments**, and the metro's housing stock sits at approximately **430,000 owner-occupied units**, with a median single-family home age of **~45 years** — a repair-heavy, deferred-maintenance market. The Indiana Builders Association and local remodeler chapters consistently report that **60–70% of Indianapolis handyman revenue comes from homes built before 1990**. Realistic 2026 Q4 pricing: **$95–$145/hour** for licensed handyman labor, **$75–$110/hour** for general labor/helper rates, **$250–$450** for a standard "punch list" day rate, and **$1,800–$4,500** for mid-scope kitchen/bath refresh projects. Startup capital for a lean, legally compliant launch (LLC, insurance, tools, vehicle wrap, website, GBP) runs **$6,500–$14,000**.

**Licensing and compliance reality in Indianapolis:** Indiana does **not** require a statewide general contractor license, but Marion County and the City of Indianapolis require a **Contractor Registration** through the Department of Business and Neighborhood Services (BNS) for most residential work, and **lead-safe (RRP) certification** is federally required for pre-1978 homes — which represent a large share of Indianapolis's Near Eastside, Irvington, and Old Northside inventory. Expect **$150–$400/year** for BNS registration, **$1,200–$2,800/year** for general liability + workers' comp (depending on payroll), and **$250–$400** for EPA RRP certification. Budget these as non-negotiable line items before marketing spend.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2)
1. Form an **Indiana LLC** ($95 state filing fee).
2. Register with **Indianapolis BNS** as a contractor ($150–$400).
3. Secure **general liability insurance** ($1,200–$2,800/yr) and workers' comp if hiring.
4. Obtain **EPA RRP lead-safe certification** ($250–$400) — required for pre-1978 homes.
5. Open a business bank account and set up simple bookkeeping.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
6. Build a **5–7 page website** with service-area pages for Indianapolis, Lawrence, Speedway, Beech Grove, and Carmel/Fishers (high-income adjacency).
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas, hours, photos, Q&A.
8. Implement **LocalBusiness + Service schema** markup (GEO-critical).
9. Set up **review automation** (post-job SMS/email request).

### Phase 3 — Trust & Content (Weeks 4–6)
10. Shoot **before/after portfolio photos** across 3–5 job types.
11. Publish **3–5 local content pieces** ("Indianapolis handyman pricing 2026," "winter freeze repair checklist," "pre-1978 lead-safe repairs").
12. Build **20–30 local citations** (BBB, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, Chamber).

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–10)
13. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** + targeted Search ads ($800–$2,000/mo).
14. Activate **Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook groups** (high ROI in Indianapolis suburbs).
15. Partner with **2–3 local realtors and property managers** for recurring punch-list work.

### Phase 5 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 10–16)
16. Track **cost-per-lead and close rate** weekly; cut underperforming channels.
17. Add a **second service van** once monthly revenue exceeds $18,000–$22,000.
18. Refresh GBP photos and reviews monthly — **GEO ranking is review-velocity sensitive**.
