## Executive Snapshot: The Numbers First

Jacksonville is the largest city by land area in the contiguous U.S. (~875 sq mi) with a population of roughly **1.02 million** (Duval County), and a metro area of **~1.75 million**. The housing stock is old and heavily owner-occupied: **~62% owner-occupied** with a median home age of **~35 years**, and roughly **58% of homes were built before 2000** — a structural tailwind for repair, remodel, and punch-list demand. The Jacksonville MSA added **~12,000–15,000 net new residents annually** through 2025–2026, and single-family permit activity remains elevated (JAX is consistently a top-20 U.S. metro for new residential permits).

**Realistic 2026 Q4 Jacksonville pricing you can launch on:** handyman hourly labor **$65–$95/hr** (billed), minimum service call **$95–$150**, half-day package **$350–$550**, full-day **$650–$950**. Contractor-side project ranges: bathroom refresh **$8,500–$18,000**, kitchen remodel **$28,000–$65,000**, deck build **$6,500–$18,000**, drywall repair **$350–$1,200**, interior painting **$2,800–$6,500** (whole-home), and fence install **$28–$48/linear ft**. A lean solo launch can be capitalized for **$12,000–$22,000** all-in (licensing, insurance, tools, vehicle wrap, digital assets); a two-crew operation runs **$45,000–$80,000**.

## 2026 Q4 Jacksonville Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers (Verified, Current)
- **Hurricane-season repair backlog:** Northeast Florida's 2024–2025 storm cycles (Helene, Milton, Debby) left a multi-year tail of roof, fence, drywall, and water-intrusion repair work. Q4 2026 sits inside the peak post-season repair window (Oct–Dec).
- **Aging housing stock:** Median home age ~35 years means a recurring stream of rot repair, subfloor, plumbing rough-in, and electrical fixture upgrades.
- **Insurance-driven remediation:** Florida carriers increasingly require 4-point and wind-mit inspections; handymen and licensed contractors who can execute the resulting punch lists capture high-intent demand.
- **Population inflow:** Relocation-driven "settle-in" work (TV mounting, shelving, assembly, minor remodel) is a reliable recurring revenue lane.

### Competitive Landscape
- **Fragmented and under-digitized.** Most Jacksonville handymen operate on word-of-mouth + Facebook. Fewer than ~30% have a Google Business Profile with 50+ reviews. **This is your wedge.**
- **Licensing nuance (critical):** Florida does **not** require a state license for handyman work under the **$2,500 threshold** per job (labor + materials). Above that, or for structural/electrical/plumbing/roofing, you need the appropriate **DBPR** license (e.g., Certified/Local Contractor, or specialty license). Jacksonville/Duval issues **local competency licenses** through the Building Inspection Division. **Verify current thresholds with DBPR before quoting** — this is the single biggest compliance risk in this market.
- **Insurance floor:** General liability **$1M/$2M** is table stakes; most property managers and HOAs in JAX (especially in Ponte Vedra, Nocatee, and Mandarin) require it before you touch a job.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Confirm licensing threshold** with Florida DBPR and Jacksonville Building Inspection Division; decide handyman (<$2,500/job) vs. licensed contractor track.
2. Register **LLC** with Florida Sunbiz (~$125), get **EIN**, open business bank account.
3. Secure **general liability $1M/$2M** + commercial auto (~$1,800–$3,600/yr combined).
4. Buy domain, set up **Google Workspace** email, and a business phone line (Google Voice or OpenPhone).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–4)
5. Launch **5–8 page website** with LocalBusiness + Service schema, mobile-first, <2.5s load.
6. Create and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (correct primary category: "Handyman" or "Contractor"; add all services, 10+ geo-tagged photos, service areas).
7. Build **15–25 programmatic service-area pages** targeting JAX neighborhoods: Mandarin, Riverside/Avondale, San Marco, Southside, Arlington, Orange Park, Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, Fleming Island, Beaches.
8. Publish **5–10 cost-guide articles** ("Bathroom remodel cost Jacksonville 2026") — these are prime GEO citation fodder for AI answer engines.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 3–8)
9. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** (screened, pay-per-lead) + a modest **Google Ads** search campaign ($1,000–$2,000/mo).
10. Deploy **review automation**; target **40+ Google reviews in 90 days**.
11. List on **Thumbtack, Angi, Nextdoor** (Nextdoor is disproportionately strong in JAX neighborhoods).
12. Wrap the vehicle; print QR-code review cards.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Month 3+)
13. Add **property-manager and realtor referral** outreach (JAX has dense PM networks in Southside and the Beaches).
14. Introduce **maintenance packages** ($149–$299/mo recurring) for stable cash flow.
15. Reinvest to **second crew** once booked utilization exceeds ~75%.
