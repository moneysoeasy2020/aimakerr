### Agency vs. Freelance — Decision Framework

**Choose brick-and-mortar agency if:** you need integrated strategy, multi-channel coordination, and can absorb $3,500–$6,000/month retainers with 6-month commitments.

**Choose verified freelance experts if:** you need specific deliverables (a website, GBP optimization, LSA setup) and can manage vendors directly. Verify via: (1) portfolio of Cleveland or Midwest service businesses, (2) Upwork "Top Rated Plus" or Contra verified status, (3) references from 2+ HVAC clients, (4) written scope with deliverable dates.

**Hybrid recommendation for HVAC startups:** Freelance for website, GBP, and programmatic SEO (one-time builds); agency or dedicated freelancer for ongoing paid search management once monthly ad spend exceeds $4,000.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. Register LLC with Ohio Secretary of State (**$99**).
2. Obtain Ohio HVAC contractor license via OCILB (**$200 exam + $150 license fee**); verify local Cleveland/Cuyahoga registration requirements.
3. Secure EPA Section 608 certification (**$400–$600** course + exam).
4. Bind general liability (**$1,200–$2,400/year** for $1M/$2M) and commercial auto insurance.
5. Open business banking; set up QuickBooks or Wave.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 3–8)
1. Launch 6-page website with service-area pages for Cleveland, Parma, Lakewood, Euclid, and Cleveland Heights (**$1,800–$4,200 freelance**).
2. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile — categories, services, photos, Q&A (**$400–$750**).
3. Build 40–80 programmatic suburb pages targeting "[service] + [suburb]" queries (**$1,200–$2,800**).
4. Set up Google Local Services Ads (HVAC category) — budget **$2,500–$4,000/month** initial spend.
5. Install scheduling software (Housecall Pro or Jobber, **$280–$520/month**).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)
1. Launch LSA + Google Ads campaigns targeting emergency and replacement keywords.
2. Deploy review-generation SMS flow post-service (target 25+ reviews in 90 days).
3. Print door hangers and van wraps for hyperlocal brand recognition (**$1,800–$3,200**).
4. Establish 3 commercial maintenance contracts (target: $8,000–$12,000 recurring annual revenue).

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 4–12)
1. Hire first technician once monthly booked revenue exceeds **$38,000**.
2. Add second van at **$72,000/month** revenue threshold.
3. Layer in programmatic SEO expansion (80→200 suburb pages) and retargeting.
4. Target: **$1.4M–$2.1M annual revenue** by month 18 with 3 technicians.
