## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 0 — Pre-Launch (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Legal entity & licensing:** Form LLC (CA Secretary of State, ~$70–$90). Obtain **C-20 Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating & Air-Conditioning Contractor license** via CSLB (application + exam + fingerprinting; budget 4–8 weeks lead time). Register with **Fremont Business Tax** and Alameda County.
2. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto + workers' comp — budget **$2,800–$5,500/yr** for a 2-person shop.
3. **Bonding:** CSLB contractor bond ($25,000) — ~$300–$500/yr.
4. **Banking & accounting:** Business checking, payment processor (Stripe/Square), bookkeeper.

### Phase 1 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
5. **Google Business Profile:** Claim, verify, add service areas (Fremont + Newark, Union City, Milpitas), hours, photos, and service categories. *This is your #1 GEO asset.*
6. **Website:** 6–8 pages — Home, Services (AC repair, furnace, heat pump install, maintenance), Service Areas, About, Reviews, Contact. Embed **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**, FAQ schema, and NAP consistency.
7. **Citations:** Build 30+ consistent listings (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Chamber of Commerce).
8. **GEO content layer:** Publish 5–8 Fremont-specific FAQ/answer pages (*"How much does heat pump installation cost in Fremont, CA?"*) formatted for AI extraction — direct answers, tables, citations.

### Phase 2 — Lead Generation (Weeks 4–8)
9. **Review engine:** Automate post-job SMS review requests; target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**.
10. **Google Ads:** Start at **$1,500–$2,500/mo** on high-intent terms (*AC repair Fremont, furnace repair Fremont, heat pump installer*). Cap CPC bids given $18–$42 market.
11. **Local partnerships:** Realtors, property managers, HOAs in Irvington/Centerville/Niles; offer co-branded tune-up promos.

### Phase 3 — Scale & Retention (Months 3–12)
12. **Service agreements:** Sell **$199–$349/yr maintenance plans** — recurring revenue + review pipeline.
13. **Fleet & staffing:** Add second van at ~$180k–$250k revenue run-rate; hire EPA-certified tech.
14. **GEO monitoring:** Track AI Overview/Perplexity citations monthly; refresh FAQ content quarterly.
15. **Q4 heating push:** Oct–Dec furnace tune-up campaigns + heat-pump rebate education content.
