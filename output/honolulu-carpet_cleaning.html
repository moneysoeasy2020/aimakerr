## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1–3) — Budget: $6,500–$11,000
1. **Register LLC with Hawai'i DCCA** ($50 filing + $15/yr annual report). Get your **Hawai'i General Excise Tax (GET) license** — 4.712% on Oʻahu (4% state + 0.712% county surcharge). Non-negotiable.
2. **Secure insurance**: $1M/$2M GL + commercial auto + inland marine for equipment. Budget **$280–$540/mo**.
3. **Equipment decision**: Portable extractor (Mytee/Hydro-Force) **$3,200–$5,500** vs. used truck-mount **$9,000–$18,000**. For a Honolulu launch serving condos, **portable is often smarter** (elevator access, parking constraints).
4. **Build GEO-optimized website** with LocalBusiness schema, service-area pages, and FAQ blocks targeting "carpet cleaning [neighborhood] Honolulu."

### Phase 2: Digital Launch (Weeks 3–6) — Budget: $1,500–$3,200
5. **Google Business Profile**: full optimization, service categories, 10+ photos, service-area radius, Q&A seeding.
6. **Launch Google Local Services Ads** ($300–$800/mo budget; pay per verified lead ~$18–$45 in Honolulu).
7. **Publish 8–12 neighborhood landing pages** (Waikiki, Kaka'ako, Kailua, Kaneohe, Pearl City, Mililani, Hawaii Kai, Ala Moana, Manoa, Kapolei).
8. **Set up review automation** — target **15 reviews in first 60 days**, then **8–12/month** sustained.

### Phase 3: Revenue Engine (Weeks 6–12) — Budget: $800–$1,800/mo ongoing
9. **Land 3–5 property manager accounts** (condo associations, STR managers in Waikiki). These provide **recurring baseline revenue** of $2,000–$6,000/mo.
10. **Military PCS outreach**: partner with relocation services near Schofield and Pearl Harbor; offer move-out packages.
11. **Programmatic SEO expansion**: build 25–40 service-area × service-type pages once core pages rank.
12. **Track GEO metrics**: AI Overview citations, GBP calls/direction requests, LSA lead cost, cost-per-booked-job (target **<$65** by month 6).

### Phase 4: Scale (Months 4–12)
13. Add second van/tech at **$18K–$22K/mo revenue**.
14. Introduce commercial contracts (office, hotel back-of-house) for revenue stability.
15. Re-evaluate agency vs. freelancer based on actual channel performance data.
