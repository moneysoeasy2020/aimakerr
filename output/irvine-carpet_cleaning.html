## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Operational Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & licensing:** File LLC with CA Secretary of State (~$70–$100). Register for Irvine business license (check current city fee schedule — typically $100–$300 based on gross receipts).
2. **Bonding & insurance:** CA requires a **$25,000 surety bond** for carpet cleaning contractors under the Business and Professions Code; carry **$1M general liability + commercial auto**. Budget $3,000–$6,000.
3. **Equipment:** Purchase hot-water extraction unit (truck-mount used: $12K–$22K; portable: $3K–$6K), wands, hoses, spotting kit, air movers, dehumidifier.
4. **Software stack:** Jobber or Housecall Pro for scheduling, invoicing, and review requests.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–6, parallel)
5. **GEO-optimized website:** Service pages for each core offer (residential, move-out, pet odor, commercial, post-construction), each with Irvine-specific pricing, neighborhoods served, and FAQ schema.
6. **Google Business Profile:** Full optimization — categories, service areas (Irvine, Tustin, Lake Forest, Newport Beach), photos, Q&A seeding, review request automation.
7. **Programmatic landing pages:** Build 20–40 pages targeting "carpet cleaning in [neighborhood]" (Northwood, Woodbridge, Turtle Rock, Westpark, Great Park, etc.) with unique local content — not spun templates.
8. **Review velocity system:** Automate SMS review requests post-job; target **15–25 Google reviews in first 90 days**.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)
9. **Google Ads:** Launch with $30–$60/day budget targeting high-intent keywords ("carpet cleaning Irvine," "move out carpet cleaning Irvine"). Expect $28–$55 CPL and 20–35% close rate.
10. **Property manager outreach:** Target 15–25 Irvine property management firms and HOA managers with a commercial rate sheet and same-week scheduling promise.
11. **Referral partnerships:** Realtors, movers, and apartment leasing offices — offer 10–15% referral fees.

### Phase 4 — Scale & GEO Compounding (Months 4–12)
12. **Content compounding:** Publish 2–4 GEO articles monthly (cost guides, neighborhood guides, pet-odor science, HOA compliance) to capture AI Overview and Perplexity citations.
13. **Review moat:** Reach **75–120 reviews** by month 12; respond to 100% of reviews.
14. **Second van:** Add capacity when monthly revenue hits **$25K–$35K** and utilization exceeds 70%.
15. **Commercial contracts:** Convert 3–5 recurring HOA/property manager accounts for revenue stability.
