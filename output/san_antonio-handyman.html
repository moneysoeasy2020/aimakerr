## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Step 1: Legal & Licensing Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
- **Entity Formation:** Register an LLC with the Texas Secretary of State (**$300 filing fee**).
- **TDLR Licensing:** Determine if your work requires a specific trade license (e.g., HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing). For general handyman work, you are not required to hold a state license, but you **must** register with the TDLR if you perform work over **$50,000** in a single project or if you are doing specific regulated trades.
- **Insurance:** Secure General Liability ($1M/$2M) and Commercial Auto. San Antonio providers like **Texas Mutual** or **Hiscox** offer policies starting at **$1,100/year** for solo operators.
- **Bond:** Obtain a **$10,000 surety bond** (required for certain municipal permits in San Antonio).

### Step 2: Digital Asset Deployment (Weeks 3–4)
- **Domain & Hosting:** Purchase a .com domain (**$12/year**) and managed WordPress hosting (**$25/month**).
- **Website Structure:** Build a 5-page site (Home, Services, About, Reviews, Contact) with **GEO-optimized schema markup** (LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ schema). Include a "San Antonio Service Areas" page listing specific neighborhoods (Stone Oak, Alamo Heights, Helotes).
- **Google Business Profile (GBP):** Create and verify your GBP. Upload **10+ geo-tagged photos** of your work in San Antonio. Set your service area to a **15-mile radius** from your home base to avoid spam filtering.

### Step 3: GEO Content & AI Optimization (Weeks 5–6)
- **AI Overview Targeting:** Write 3 long-form blog posts (1,200+ words) answering specific San Antonio queries: *"How much does it cost to repair drywall in San Antonio?"* and *"What permits do I need for a fence in Bexar County?"*
- **Citation Building:** List your business on **Yelp, Angi, Nextdoor, and the San Antonio Chamber of Commerce**. Ensure your Name, Address, and Phone (NAP) are identical across all platforms.
- **Review Generation:** Implement an automated SMS review request system. Target **25 Google reviews in the first 60 days**. Respond to every review within 24 hours.

### Step 4: Lead Generation & Sales (Weeks 7–8)
- **Google Local Services Ads:** Apply for LSA (requires background check and insurance verification). Set a weekly budget of **$350**. This is the highest-intent lead source in San Antonio.
- **Direct Mail:** Target **500 homes** in the **Stone Oak** and **Alamo Ranch** zip codes (78258, 78253) with a "New Neighbor Handyman Special" postcard. Cost: **$0.45 per piece**.
- **Partnerships:** Visit **5 local real estate offices** (e.g., Keller Williams Heritage, Coldwell Banker D'Ann Harper) and offer a **10% referral fee** for their clients' repair needs.

### Step 5: Q4 Scaling & Operations (Weeks 9–12)
- **Software Stack:** Use **Jobber** or **Housecall Pro** ($49–$99/month) for scheduling, invoicing, and payment processing.
- **Pricing Strategy:** Set your hourly rate at **$75/hour** for standard repairs. Offer a **$99 "Handyman Diagnostic"** for the first hour to lower the barrier to entry.

**Q: Do I need a license to be a handyman in San Antonio?**
A: Texas does not require a state license for general handyman work.
