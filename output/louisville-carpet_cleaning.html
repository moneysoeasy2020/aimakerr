## 2026 Q4 Louisville Market Analysis

Louisville's carpet cleaning market is a **$38–52M annual opportunity** across roughly 1.28 million residents in Jefferson County and 340,000+ occupied housing units — approximately **62% of which contain wall-to-wall carpet** (higher than the national average due to older housing stock in the Highlands, Okolona, and Shively corridors). The metro is served by an estimated **140–170 active carpet cleaning operators**, but fewer than 15% have optimized digital presence, creating a wide GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gap for a new entrant. Standard residential cleaning jobs in Louisville price at **$175–$320 for 3 rooms** (avg. $210), **$0.28–$0.45/sq ft** for whole-home work, and **$45–$75 per room** for add-ons like pet treatment or Scotchgard. Commercial contracts (office parks in the East End, medical offices near Norton Healthcare) run **$0.18–$0.32/sq ft** with recurring 90-day cycles worth **$1,800–$6,500/year per account**.

Realistic startup capital for a Louisville launch is **$18,500–$42,000** depending on truck-mount vs. portable equipment, and a disciplined operator can reach **$9,000–$14,000/month gross by month 6** with 55–70 jobs and a 2.8–3.4 average ticket. Break-even typically lands between **months 4 and 7** if digital acquisition costs are controlled — which is where the cost matrix below matters most.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Equipment Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
- Register LLC with Kentucky Secretary of State (**$40 online**), obtain Louisville Metro business license (**$50–$150**), and secure general liability insurance (**$85–$180/month**).
- Choose equipment: **truck-mount ($14,000–$28,000)** for scale or **portable heated unit ($3,500–$6,800)** for lean launch. Add a used cargo van (**$9,000–$16,000**) if truck-mount.
- Budget **$1,200–$2,000** for chemicals, hoses, wands, and a rotary extractor.

### Phase 2 — Digital GEO Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
- Build a **GEO-optimized website** with LocalBusiness + Service schema, FAQ blocks, and neighborhood landing pages — this is what AI engines cite when someone asks "best carpet cleaning in Louisville."
- Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, 20+ photos, Q&A seeding).
- Deploy **programmatic neighborhood pages** targeting: Highlands, St. Matthews, Okolona, Jeffersontown, Shively, Middletown, Prospect, and 18–25 more.
- Set up **call tracking + CRM** ($60–$140/month) to attribute every lead source.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
- Launch **Google Ads** at **$1,200–$2,000/month** spend targeting high-intent keywords; expect **$55–$95 cost per booked job** in Louisville.
- Activate **review engine**: automated SMS requests post-job; target **4.8+ stars and 40 reviews by month 6**.
- Pursue **B2B outreach** to 60–80 property managers, apartment complexes (Louisville has 400+ multifamily properties), and medical offices.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Retention (Months 3–9)
- Introduce **recurring maintenance plans** (quarterly at 15% discount) — target 25% of customers on plan by month 9.
- Expand to **commercial contracts** once residential hits 50 jobs/month.
- Reinvest **8–12% of revenue** into GEO content and review velocity to defend AI-search visibility.

### Phase 5 — 2026 Q4-Specific Timing Notes
- **Q4 is peak season** in Louisville (holiday hosting + end-of-year commercial deep cleans). Launch by **early October** to capture Thanksgiving/December demand.
- Lock ad budgets before **November CPC inflation** (Louisville carpet cleaning CPCs rise **12–18%** in Nov–Dec).
- Winterize portable equipment and market **post-holiday stain removal** packages for January pipeline.
