# Launching a Carpet Cleaning Business in Des Moines, IA
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

**Des Moines, IA** supports roughly **60–80 active carpet cleaning operators** across the metro (Polk, Dallas, and Warren counties), with **single-truck startups capturing $45,000–$95,000 in Year 1 revenue** when they secure 8–12 recurring commercial accounts. Residential deep-clean pricing in the metro runs **$0.28–$0.45 per square foot**, with a typical **3-room + hallway job billing $185–$320**. Startup capital for a lean single-van operation in Q4 2026 is **$18,500–$34,000**, covering a used truck-mount unit ($9,000–$18,000), chemicals and spotting kit ($800–$1,400), insurance and bonding ($1,200–$2,400/yr), Iowa LLC filing ($50 online), and initial digital assets. The metro's **median household income of ~$72,400** and **homeownership rate near 66%** create a stable residential base, while **Downtown, West Des Moines, and Ankeny** concentrate the highest-density commercial and multifamily contracts.

3. **Weeks 3–4 — Digital Foundation:** Launch 5-page site with online booking, claim Google Business Profile, build 20+ local citations.
4. **Weeks 4–6 — GEO & Programmatic SEO:** Publish service-area pages for **West Des Moines, Ankeny, Urbandale, Clive, Johnston** targeting AI-search queries like "best carpet cleaning in Ankeny IA."
5. **Weeks 5–8 — Commercial Prospecting:** Target **Downtown and West Des Moines** offices, property managers, and hotels for recurring Q1 contracts.
6. **Ongoing — Review Velocity:** Aim for **15+ Google reviews in 90 days**; reviews are the top ranking and AI-citation signal.
