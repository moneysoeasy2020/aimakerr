## 2026 Q4 Las Vegas Market Analysis

### Demand fundamentals
- **Climate-driven urgency:** Las Vegas averages **~80 days/year above 100°F**; AC failure is a health emergency, not a comfort issue. Emergency dispatch conversion rates run 2–3x national averages.
- **Housing stock age:** A large share of Clark County's single-family stock was built **1995–2007** — systems are now 19–31 years old, well past the 12–15 year replacement window. Replacement demand is at a structural peak.
- **New construction pockets:** Summerlin West, Skye Canyon, and Cadence (Henderson) drive new-install and warranty work; older core neighborhoods (Spring Valley, Sunrise Manor, North Las Vegas) drive repair/replace work.
- **Seasonality inversion:** Unlike most US markets, Las Vegas has a **second demand peak in winter** (heat pump heating, ~35–45°F lows) — a dual-season model smooths cash flow.

### Competitive landscape
- **~600–800 licensed HVAC contractors** operate in Clark County; the top 10 firms capture an estimated **35–45%** of residential replacement revenue.
- **Google Local Services Ads (LSA)** and **Google Guaranteed** dominate high-intent "AC repair near me" queries; LSA cost-per-lead in Las Vegas runs **$45–$120** for HVAC, among the highest in the US.
- **Programmatic/local SEO gap:** Most incumbents have thin, templated city pages. A well-built programmatic layer (neighborhood × service × intent) is a genuine 2026 Q4 opening.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 0 — Pre-Launch (Months -4 to 0, i.e., now through Q4 2026)
1. **File NSCB Class C-21 application** — secure a qualifying party (owner or hired qualifier). Budget **$1,400–$2,600**; expect **60–120 days**.
2. **Form LLC in Nevada** ($425 state fee + ~$150 registered agent/yr) and obtain **Clark County business license**.
3. **Secure insurance:** general liability ($1M/$2M), commercial auto, workers' comp — **$2,500–$6,000/yr**.
4. **EPA Section 608 certification** (required for refrigerant handling) — $300–$600.
5. **Purchase/lease service van** — used cargo van **$18,000–$32,000** or lease **$450–$750/mo**; stock with gauges, vacuum pump, recovery machine, torches, and parts inventory (**$3,500–$7,000**).

### Phase 1 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 1–4 post-license)
6. **Launch website** with service-area pages, online booking, and **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**.
7. **Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas (Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Spring Valley), photos, Q&A.
8. **Set up CRM + dispatch** (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber) — **$99–$299/mo**.
9. **Install call tracking and review-generation automation.**

### Phase 2 — Demand Capture (Weeks 4–12)
10. **Launch Google LSA + Google Ads** — start at **$3,000–$5,000/mo** ad spend; target "AC repair," "emergency HVAC," "furnace repair" by neighborhood.
11. **Deploy programmatic local pages** — neighborhood × service × intent (e.g., "AC repair Summerlin," "emergency HVAC Henderson").
12. **Build review velocity** — target **15–25 Google reviews in first 90 days**; respond to 100%.

### Phase 3 — Scale & Retention (Months 3–12)
13. **Launch maintenance plan** ($149–$299/yr) to build recurring revenue and smooth seasonality.
14. **Add commercial/light-commercial tier** (property managers, HOAs, strip retail) — higher ticket, less price sensitivity.
15. **Track unit economics:** target **CAC $150–$350**, **average ticket $450–$900**, **gross margin 45–60%** on service, **25–35%** on replacement.

## Key 2026 Q4 Risks & Mitigations
- **License delay:** Do not market or contract work before NSCB issuance — fines and license revocation risk. File early.
- **Peak-season labor shortage:** Recruit **2–4 weeks before** summer; certified techs command **$28–$45/hr** in Las Vegas.
- **Ad cost inflation:** LSA/Google Ads HVAC CPCs in Las Vegas are among the nation's highest — budget for **$45–$120/lead** and validate close rates weekly.
- **Programmatic SEO quality:** Avoid thin duplicate pages; pair every programmatic page with unique local data (neighborhood, climate zone, common system types) to survive 2026 search quality updates.
