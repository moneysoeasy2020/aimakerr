# Launching an HVAC Services Business in Raleigh, NC
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Raleigh's HVAC market is one of the most attractive in the Southeast: the metro population sits near **1.5 million** (Raleigh-Cary CSA), the region adds roughly **60–70 net new residents per day**, and the climate delivers **~3,400 cooling degree days and ~2,900 heating degree days annually** — meaning nearly every household runs HVAC year-round. Residential HVAC replacement in Raleigh runs **$6,500–$14,000** (system-dependent), repair calls average **$250–$650**, and seasonal maintenance plans price at **$120–$220/year per household**. A well-run startup targeting 300 maintenance agreements and 8–12 installs/month can realistically reach **$1.2M–$2.4M annual revenue** by month 18.

Startup capital requirements are equally concrete. Expect **$45,000–$85,000** to launch a licensed two-truck operation: **$12,000–$25,000** for a used truck and tool loadout, **$8,000–$15,000** for initial refrigerant recovery equipment, gauges, and inventory, **$3,500–$6,000** for NC licensing, bonding, and insurance (general liability + commercial auto), and **$10,000–$20,000** for the first 90 days of digital marketing. The Raleigh market supports **premium pricing** — homeowners here pay 8–12% above national HVAC averages — but competition is dense: **380+ licensed HVAC contractors** operate in Wake County, so differentiation through digital presence and response time is non-negotiable.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Register LLC with NC Secretary of State (**$125**).
2. Obtain NC HVAC contractor license (Class I/II/III per N.C. Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating & Fire Sprinkler Contractors) — exam + fees **$200–$600**.
3. Secure general liability (**$1M/$2M**, ~$1,200–$2,800/yr) and commercial auto.
4. Register for NC sales tax and E-Verify; open business banking.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–6)**
5. Launch 6–8 page website with online booking (Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan starter).
6. Build **Google Business Profile** with service areas: Raleigh, Cary, Apex, Wake Forest, Garner, Holly Springs.
7. Publish **30–60 programmatic service-area pages** ("AC Repair in [City]" with unique local data).
8. Install schema (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage) and call tracking.

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)**
9. Launch Google Local Services Ads (**$35–$90/lead**) + Search campaigns.
10. Deploy review-generation SOP — target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (critical GEO trust signal).
11. Publish 8–12 AI-optimized FAQ/blog pieces answering "how much does HVAC replacement cost in Raleigh," "best heat pump for NC climate," etc.

**Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Months 3–12)**
12. Hire first licensed tech (**$28–$38/hr** in Raleigh market) + apprentice.
13. Launch maintenance plan program at **$149–$199/yr** — recurring revenue + install pipeline.
14. Add second truck at month 9–12 when install backlog exceeds 6 weeks.
15. Refresh GEO content quarterly; monitor AI-search citations monthly.

**Financial milestones:** Break-even at **~$38K–$45K monthly revenue** (months 6–9); $1M run-rate by month 14–18; 20%+ net margin achievable with maintenance-plan mix above 25% of revenue.
