## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–6)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Form Maryland LLC (~$300), obtain **Maryland State Board of Dental Examiners** license, DEA registration if prescribing, and Baltimore City Use & Occupancy permit.
2. **Site selection:** Target 1,800–2,600 sq ft, 3–4 operatories. Lease rates: **$28–$42/sq ft NNN** in Canton/Fells; **$18–$26/sq ft** in Dundalk/Highlandtown.
3. **Equipment:** 3 chairs + digital X-ray + sterilization = **$180,000–$260,000** (new); **$95,000–$140,000** (refurbished/verified vendor).
4. **Buildout:** **$120,000–$210,000** depending on plumbing/dental gas retrofit.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 4–7, parallel)
5. **Domain + website** with online booking (NexHealth / LocalMed integration).
6. **Google Business Profile** — verify, add services, photos, Q&A; target "dentist [neighborhood]" queries.
7. **Programmatic SEO pages:** 12–20 neighborhood landing pages (e.g., "Dentist in Highlandtown," "Emergency Dentist Canton") with schema markup (Dentist, LocalBusiness, FAQPage).
8. **Review engine:** automated post-visit SMS/email → target **40+ Google reviews in first 90 days** (top-3 pack threshold in Baltimore).

### Phase 3 — Launch & Scale (Months 7–18)
9. **Insurance credentialing** (Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MD Medicaid) — start 90 days pre-launch; Medicaid credentialing takes **90–150 days**.
10. **Paid search:** $2,500–$4,000/mo Google Ads on high-intent terms ("emergency dentist Baltimore," "dentist accepting Medicaid Baltimore").
11. **Recall & reactivation** system by month 9; hygiene-driven recurring revenue is the margin engine.
12. **Track KPIs:** new patients/mo (target 60–90 by month 12), cost-per-new-patient (target <$180), Google review velocity.
