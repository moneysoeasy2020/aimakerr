## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Entity & Compliance (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Form Texas PLLC** via Texas SOS — **$300 filing fee**, 2–5 business days expedited.
2. **Obtain DPS dental license verification** and register with **Texas State Board of Dental Examiners (TSBDE)**; facility registration required before patient contact.
3. **Secure DEA registration** ($888/3 years) and **Texas Controlled Substances** registration if prescribing.
4. **Open business banking + merchant processing** — negotiate **2.1–2.6% + $0.10** on card-present dental transactions.
5. **HIPAA Security Risk Assessment** — budget **$1,500–$3,500**; required before EHR go-live.

### Phase 2 — Location & Build-Out (Weeks 3–16)
6. **Lease negotiation** — target **$28–$42/sq ft NNN** on SPID or Saratoga; secure **6–9 months free rent** and **$40–$75/sq ft TI allowance** as standard 2026 concessions.
7. **Equipment procurement** — A-dec or Planmeca chairs (**$8,000–$14,000 each**), **CBCT ($65,000–$120,000)**, intraoral scanners (**$18,000–$35,000**), sterilization (**$12,000–$22,000**).
8. **EHR/PMS selection** — Dentrix, Open Dental, or Eaglesoft; **$400–$900/mo** per provider seat.

### Phase 3 — Digital Launch (Weeks 8–14, overlapping)
9. **Google Business Profile** — verify at physical address; complete 100% of fields; upload 20+ photos; enable messaging. **GBP drives 40–60% of new-patient calls** for Corpus dental clinics.
10. **Website + programmatic SEO** — build 40–60 geo-service pages (e.g., "emergency dentist Portland TX," "pediatric dentist Calallen") targeting **Nueces, San Patricio, Aransas, Kleberg, and San Patricio County** sub-markets.
11. **Paid acquisition** — Google Ads (Search + Local Services Ads) at **$2,500–$5,000/mo**; LSA cost-per-lead in Corpus dental runs **$35–$90**.
12. **Review engine** — automated post-visit SMS review requests; target **25+ Google reviews in first 90 days**.

### Phase 4 — Ramp & Optimize (Months 4–12)
13. **Insurance credentialing** — begin 90–120 days pre-opening with Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife, BCBS TX; Medicaid enrollment via HHSC takes **60–90 days**.
14. **Staffing** — hygienist (**$38–$48/hr**), dental assistant (**$18–$24/hr**), front office (**$17–$22/hr**) in Corpus market.
15. **KPI tracking** — new patients/month (**target 60–90 by month 6**), case acceptance (**target 65–75%**), hygiene reappointment rate (**target 85%+**), cost-per-new-patient (**target <$180**).
