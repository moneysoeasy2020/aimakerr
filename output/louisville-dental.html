## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)

1. **Entity & licensing (weeks 1–3):** File **PLLC** with KY Secretary of State ($40). Obtain **Kentucky Board of Dentistry** license verification and **DEA registration** ($888/3 yrs). Register with **Jefferson County Public Health** for facility permit.
2. **Site selection (weeks 2–8):** Target 1,800–2,400 sq ft in **40245 (Middletown)**, **40299 (J-Town)**, or **40206 (Nulu)**. Medical-grade lease rates: **$22–$34/sq ft NNN**.
3. **Build-out (weeks 6–20):** Budget **$185–$340/sq ft**. Dental-specific plumbing, vacuum, and radiology shielding add **$28K–$55K** vs. general medical.
4. **Equipment (weeks 8–16):** 4-chair operatory package (chairs, delivery, imaging, CBCT, sterilization) = **$145K–$235K**. Henry Schein, Patterson, and **Louisville-based Dentsply Sirona** regional reps all quote.
5. **Financing (weeks 4–10):** **Bank of America Practice Solutions**, **Live Oak Bank**, and **Huntington** (regional) offer 10-yr SBA 7(a) at **8.9–11.2% APR** (Q4 2026). Expect 80–90% LTV on equipment, 70–80% on build-out.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 3–5)

6. **Website live** by week 14 (before build-out completes) with **online booking** + insurance verification widget.
7. **Google Business Profile** verified + optimized (service areas: 40245, 40299, 40206, 40222, 40241).
8. **Programmatic SEO pages** published: `[service] + [ZIP]` (e.g., "emergency dentist 40245") — 60–120 pages.
9. **Review engine** live before first patient (target: 15 reviews by day 60).

### Phase 3 — Soft Open & Ramp (Months 5–9)

10. **Soft open** with 40–60 pre-booked patients from waitlist (build waitlist via Google Ads + GBP during build-out).
11. **Google Ads launch:** $4K–$8K/mo, geo-targeted 8-mile radius, separate campaigns for **emergency**, **cosmetic**, **pediatric**, **Medicaid**.
12. **Insurance credentialing** (start month 3): Delta Dental KY, Anthem, Humana, Cigna, MetLife, Passport/Molina. Timeline **90–150 days** — start early.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 9–14)

13. **Add hygienist + second op** at month 10–11 (breakeven typically at 1,100–1,400 active patients).
14. **Programmatic SEO expansion** to adjacent counties (Oldham, Bullitt, Floyd IN).
15. **Target metrics at month 12:** 1,200 active patients, $1.15M–$1.65M collections, 22–28% EBITDA margin.
