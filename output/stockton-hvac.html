## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. Obtain **C-20 Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating and Air-Conditioning Contractor license** via CSLB (application ~$450, exam scheduling 4–8 weeks).
2. Secure **$25,000 contractor bond** and general liability + workers' comp insurance (~$3,500–$6,500/yr).
3. Register business entity (LLC ~$800 CA filing), EIN, seller's permit, Stockton business license (~$100–$300).
4. Open business banking; set up ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro ($150–$400/mo).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–8)
5. Launch 6-page website with online booking (freelance, $1,500–$2,500).
6. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (categories, service areas, photos, Q&A).
7. Build 25–40 programmatic service-area pages (e.g., "AC Repair in Lodi," "Furnace Installation in Manteca").
8. Set up call tracking + review automation.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)
9. Launch Google Local Services Ads + Search Ads ($1,500–$3,000/mo spend, target $45–$90 cost-per-lead).
10. Run seasonal tune-up offer ($99 fall/winter special) to build review base — target **40+ reviews in 90 days**.
11. Partner with 3–5 local realtors and property managers for referral pipeline.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Month 4+)
12. Add second truck once monthly call volume exceeds 120.
13. Layer programmatic SEO expansion into adjacent cities (Tracy, Manteca, Lodi).
14. Reassess agency hire at $750K+ annual revenue.
