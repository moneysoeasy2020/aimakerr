### Regulatory Reality (Critical for 2026)

- **Lead-safe certification (EPA RRP)** — mandatory for pre-1978 homes (a large share of Milwaukee stock). Cost: **~$250–$400** for the cert course.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026)

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register **LLC with Wisconsin DFI** (~$130 online) and obtain **EIN** (free).
2. File **City of Milwaukee DNS Home Improvement Contractor registration** (~$100–$150).
3. Secure **general liability insurance** ($1M/$2M): **$550–$1,100/year** for solo handyman in Milwaukee.
4. Complete **EPA RRP lead-safe cert** if targeting pre-1978 homes (~$250–$400).
5. Open business banking; set aside 25–30% for taxes.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
6. Buy domain + hosting (~$120/year); build **5-page GEO site** with LocalBusiness + Service schema.
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (service categories, 15+ photos, Q&A).
8. Publish **10 GEO-format articles** (Q&A headers, cited Milwaukee stats, price tables).
9. Build **20 programmatic service-area pages** (Milwaukee + Wauwatosa, West Allis, Greenfield, Shorewood, etc.).
10. Set up **review-generation SMS/QR** flow — target 25 reviews in 90 days.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–10)
11. List on **Thumbtack, Angi, Nextdoor** (budget $300–$600/mo for leads initially).
12. Join **Milwaukee-area neighborhood Facebook groups** and local contractor networks.
13. Run **$400–$800/mo Google Local Services Ads** once GBP is verified.
14. Track cost-per-booked-job; kill channels above **$65/lead**.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Weeks 10–16)
15. Add a second crew or subcontractor at **>70% utilization**.
16. Expand programmatic pages to **50+ zip codes**.
17. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into GEO content and review velocity.
