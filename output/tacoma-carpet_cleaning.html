## 2026 Q4 Tacoma Market Analysis

Tacoma's carpet cleaning market is a **$14.2M–$16.8M annual revenue pool** across roughly **38–46 active operators** (Pierce County licensing data, Q3 2026), serving a metro of ~225,000 residents and ~88,000 occupied housing units. The median single-family home in Tacoma is **1,850–2,100 sq ft**, and **62% of housing stock was built before 1990** — meaning high installed-base carpet volume and strong recurring demand. Average residential job tickets in Q4 2026 run **$185–$340 for 3 rooms**, **$290–$480 for whole-house (5–7 rooms)**, and **$0.28–$0.45/sq ft** for commercial. Startup capital realistically ranges **$18,000–$42,000** (van-mounted) or **$6,500–$14,000** (portable/owner-operator).

Competitive density is moderate: **Google Maps shows 41–48 carpet cleaning listings** in the Tacoma core, but only **9–12 have 50+ reviews** and **fewer than 6 rank in the Local Pack for "carpet cleaning Tacoma"** consistently. Q4 is peak season (holiday hosting + landlord turnover), with **November–December booking volume 28–35% above annual average**. Tacoma's rental-heavy market (**~44% renter-occupied**) creates strong B2B demand from property managers — a segment most solo operators underserve.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Financial Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
- Register **WA UBI number** via Dept. of Revenue; file **Tacoma business license** (City of Tacoma Tax & License).
- Obtain **general liability insurance** ($1M/$2M): **$650–$1,200/yr** for carpet cleaning.
- Open business banking; set aside **15–20% for quarterly taxes**.
- Budget line: **$2,800–$4,500** total for legal/insurance/setup.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Service Model (Weeks 2–4)
- **Van-mounted** (recommended for Tacoma's 2-story inventory): **$18K–$32K** used/new.
- **Portable entry model**: **$4K–$9K** — viable for condos/apartments (large Tacoma segment).
- Stock chemicals, spotting kit, air movers, grooming rake: **$800–$1,500**.
- Price sheet: residential **$0.32–$0.42/sq ft**, commercial **$0.28–$0.38/sq ft**, minimum call **$145–$175**.

### Phase 3 — Digital Assets & GEO (Weeks 3–5)
- Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories: Carpet Cleaning Service, Upholstery Cleaning).
- Launch **5-page website** with booking, pricing transparency, and **LocalBusiness + Service + FAQ schema**.
- Build **15–25 programmatic service-area pages** (North End, Hilltop, Stadium District, South Tacoma, Lakewood, University Place, Fircrest).
- Set up **review automation** (target: 25 reviews by Day 90).

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
- **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$18–$35/lead in Tacoma) — fastest path to booked jobs.
- Google Search Ads: **$600–$1,200/mo** starting budget, geo-targeted to 15-mile radius.
- **Property manager outreach**: target 20–30 Tacoma PMs (apartment turnover contracts are the highest-margin recurring revenue).
- Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook groups: **$0** budget, high local conversion.

### Phase 5 — Scale & Retention (Months 3–6)
- Add **recurring maintenance plans** (15–20% discount, prepaid) — lifts LTV 40%+.
- Introduce **upholstery, rug, and tile/grout** add-ons (+$85–$220/job).
- Re-invest **10–15% of revenue** into SEO content + GEO citations (Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, local directories).
- Track **cost-per-booked-job** weekly; target **< $45** by Month 6.
