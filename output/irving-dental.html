## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (2026 Q4 Launch)

### Phase 0 — Pre-Launch (Months -6 to -3)
1. **Entity formation:** Texas PLLC (dental), DSO vs. solo decision, TSBDE licensure verification.
2. **Site selection:** Target 75038/75039; negotiate TI allowance ($40–$90/sq ft typical in Las Colinas).
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) or dental-specific lenders (Bank of America Practice Solutions, Live Oak); budget 12–18 months working capital.
4. **Equipment RFP:** 3 quotes minimum; lease vs. buy analysis on imaging (CBCT, intraoral scanners).

### Phase 1 — Build & Digital Foundation (Months -3 to 0)
5. **Build-out:** 90–120 day timeline typical; permit through City of Irving.
6. **Website + GBP:** Launch 6–8 weeks pre-opening; schema markup, service pages, 4 geo-pages (75038/75039/75061/75063).
7. **PMS + phone system:** Open Dental or Dentrix; HIPAA-compliant VoIP with call tracking.
8. **Insurance credentialing:** Begin 90–120 days out — PPO contracts take 60–90 days.

### Phase 2 — Launch (Month 0–3)
9. **Soft opening + GBP posts:** Weekly posts, review solicitation from day 1 (target 25+ reviews by day 90).
10. **Google Ads:** Launch "dentist near me Irving," "emergency dentist Irving," "cosmetic dentist Las Colinas" — $2,500–$4,000/mo ad spend.
11. **Local citations:** Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Dental Insider, Bing Places — NAP consistency audit.

### Phase 3 — Scale (Months 3–12)
12. **Geo-page expansion:** Add service×ZIP matrix (e.g., "Invisalign Valley Ranch," "emergency dentist 75061").
13. **Review velocity:** 15–25 new reviews/month; respond within 24 hrs.
14. **Content engine:** 2–4 dental education posts/month targeting Irving-specific queries.
15. **Retention systems:** Recall automation, recall-to-review pipeline, referral program.

### Phase 4 — Optimize (Month 12+)
16. **CAC audit:** Target blended CAC <$280 by month 12.
17. **Add specialty services** (ortho, implants, sedation) once general dentistry hits 60%+ chair utilization.
18. **Consider second location** (Grand Prairie or Coppell) only after 18 months of stable EBITDA.
