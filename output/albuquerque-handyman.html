## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register LLC** with NM Secretary of State — **$50 online**.
2. **Obtain NM CRS (Construction Industries Division) registration** — required for jobs **over $5,000** in labor/materials; handyman exemption applies below threshold. Fee ~**$150–$300**.
3. **General liability insurance:** $1M/$2M — **$1,100–$2,400/yr** (Next, Thimble, or local broker).
4. **Business license:** City of Albuquerque Business Registration — **$35–$75**; Bernalillo County if applicable.
5. **EIN (free)**, business bank account, and simple bookkeeping (QuickBooks Self-Employed ~$20/mo).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
1. **Google Business Profile** — verify address, set service area (Albuquerque + Rio Rancho + Corrales), upload 15+ job photos weekly.
2. **Website** — 5–7 pages: Home, Services (Handyman, Drywall, Painting, Assembly, Weatherization), Service Areas, Reviews, Contact. Embed click-to-call and booking widget (Jobber or Housecall Pro — **$39–$169/mo**).
3. **Citations:** Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor Business, BBB, NM Home Builders Assoc.
4. **Review engine:** automated post-job SMS requesting Google reviews — target **25 reviews in 90 days**.

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 4–8)
1. **Google Local Services Ads** — pay-per-lead, ~**$18–$45/lead** in ABQ for handyman category; requires license + insurance verification.
2. **Google Search Ads** — start **$800–$1,200/mo** on 15–20 high-intent keywords ("handyman Albuquerque," "drywall repair NE Heights").
3. **Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook groups** — free; post weekly before/after photos.
4. **Property manager outreach** — 10 emails/week to ABQ rental managers; offer turnover-repair packages.

### Phase 4 — Operations & Pricing (Ongoing)
- **Minimum service call:** $125–$150.
- **Half-day / full-day bundles:** $385 / $695.
- **Maintenance plan:** $149/quarter (2-hour visit) — recurring revenue anchor.
- Target **60–70% booked capacity** by month 6.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 6–12)
- Add 1 helper at **$22–$28/hr** once revenue exceeds **$9,000/mo**.
- Layer in **Q4 holiday lighting** and **spring gutter/stucco** seasonal campaigns.
- Reinvest 8–10% of revenue into ads + content.
