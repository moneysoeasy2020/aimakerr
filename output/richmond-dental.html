A de novo general dentistry clinic typically requires **$550,000–$850,000** in total startup capital (build-out, equipment, working capital), with **$350,000–$600,000** of that in hard costs. Expect **12–18 months** from lease signing to break-even, with average general dentistry revenue per chair of **$350,000–$500,000/year** at maturity.

Realistic Richmond pricing: **dental chair + delivery unit packages run $35,000–$65,000 each** (2–4 chairs recommended), **CBCT/panorex imaging $45,000–$120,000**, and **tenant build-out at $150–$300/sq ft** for a 2,000–3,000 sq ft space. Commercial rents in prime Richmond corridors run **$28–$42/sq ft NNN** (Short Pump/West Broad) versus **$18–$26/sq ft NNN** in the East End and Southside. A **single-location digital marketing retainer** in this market ranges **$2,500–$6,000/month**, and a **new website with local SEO foundation** costs **$6,000–$18,000** depending on vendor type.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)**
1. **Market & site selection**: Target a 3-mile radius with **>15,000 residents, <10 dentists, and median income >$70k**. Short Pump and western Chesterfield score highest; East End offers Medicaid volume.
2. **Entity & licensing**: Virginia SCC LLC filing (~$100), Virginia Board of Dentistry licensure, DEA registration, malpractice insurance.
3. **Financing**: SBA 7(a) up to **$5M**; equipment leasing covers 60–80% of chair/imaging costs.
4. **Build-out**: Budget **$300k–$550k** for 2,500 sq ft; permit timelines in Richmond run **8–14 weeks**.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 3–6)**
5. **Website + GBP**: Launch 90 days before opening; claim Google Business Profile, build 15+ locally optimized pages, schema markup for `Dentist` and `LocalBusiness`.
6. **GEO/AEO layer**: Publish FAQ-structured content targeting "dentist in [neighborhood]" queries; ensure AI Overviews can cite your practice (clear headings, structured data, authoritative citations).
7. **Review engine**: Pre-seed 25–40 reviews from soft-launch patients before public opening.

**Phase 3 — Launch & Scale (Months 6–18)**
8. **Paid acquisition**: Start Google Ads at **$3,000–$6,000/month** targeting high-intent keywords; layer Meta for implant/ortho lead gen.
9. **Recall & reactivation**: Automate recall at 6 months; target **>65% hygiene reappointment rate**.
10. **Specialty expansion**: Add implants or ortho by Month 12–15 once general dentistry hits **$400k/chair annualized**.
