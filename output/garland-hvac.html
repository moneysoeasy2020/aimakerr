2. Obtain TDLR HVAC contractor license + EPA 608 certs.
3. Secure GL + commercial auto insurance.
4. Open business banking + set up payment processing (Stripe/Square).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)
8. Launch Google Local Services Ads (pay-per-lead, ~$18–$40/lead in DFW).
9. Start a modest Google Ads campaign (**$1,500–$2,500/mo**) targeting "AC repair Garland" and "furnace repair near me."
10. Publish 2 GEO-optimized articles/month answering local queries ("How much does AC replacement cost in Garland?").

### Phase 4 — Retention & Scale (Month 4+)
11. Launch a **$99–$149/yr maintenance plan** — recurring revenue and replacement pipeline.
12. Add a second technician once monthly revenue clears **$30,000**.
13. Expand service-area pages to 8–10 adjacent ZIPs.
