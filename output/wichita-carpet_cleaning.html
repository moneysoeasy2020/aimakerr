## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Financial Foundation (Weeks 1–2)**
1. Register LLC with Kansas Secretary of State (**$160** filing fee).
2. Obtain Wichita business license (~$50–$100) and Sedgwick County sales tax permit (carpet cleaning services are generally not taxable in KS — confirm with a CPA).
3. Secure general liability insurance (**$600–$1,200/yr**) plus commercial auto.
4. Open a business bank account; set aside 25% for quarterly taxes.

**Phase 2 — Equipment & Operations (Weeks 2–4)**
5. Purchase equipment: used truck-mount ($8K–$18K) or portable ($2.5K–$5K), plus wand, hoses, chemicals ($800–$1,500 initial stock).
6. Set pricing tiers: standard ($0.32/sq ft), deep clean ($0.42/sq ft), pet treatment (+$45–$75), commercial contract (custom).
7. Build a simple booking flow (Jobber or Housecall Pro, **$39–$99/mo**).

**Phase 3 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 3–6)**
8. Launch a 5–7 page website with **LocalBusiness + Service schema**, service-area pages for Wichita, Derby, Andover, Maize, and Goddard, and FAQ blocks written in Q&A format (AI engines cite these directly).
9. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile: 10+ photos, service list, booking link, weekly posts.
10. Set up review automation — target **15+ Google reviews in 60 days** (review count is the #1 Maps ranking lever in Wichita).

**Phase 4 — GEO & Lead Generation (Weeks 5–12)**
11. Publish 2–3 locally specific blog posts monthly (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning Costs in Wichita by Neighborhood, 2026") — these earn AI Overview citations.

**Phase 5 — Scale (Months 4–12)**
