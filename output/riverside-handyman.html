## Local Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts

**Brick-and-mortar agencies (Riverside/Inland Empire):**
- **Pros:** Local accountability, bundled services, in-person meetings, established referral networks with realtors and property managers.
- **Cons:** Retainers of **$1,500–$4,000/month** are common; markups of **2–4x** on freelance-equivalent work; long contracts (6–12 months); slower iteration on programmatic SEO pages.
- **Best for:** Established contractors doing **$250K+/year** who need full-service brand management.

**Verified freelance experts (Upwork, Contra, Fiverr Pro, local referrals):**
- **Pros:** Project-based pricing (**$400–$1,200** for a full site), direct communication, faster turnaround, GEO-friendly (you can iterate landing pages weekly for neighborhood-level queries like "handyman near UC Riverside" or "drywall repair Wood Streets Riverside").
- **Cons:** Requires vetting (portfolio, reviews, test task), no bundled accountability, you manage the project.
- **Best for:** New handyman startups in **$0–$150K/year** range; highest ROI in year one.

**Verdict for Q4 2026 Riverside launch:** Start with **verified freelancers for website + GBP optimization (~$800–$1,600 total)**, self-manage reviews and LSA, and revisit agency retainers only after hitting **$15K/month gross**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & compliance:** File Riverside business license, form LLC, secure general liability insurance, confirm CSLB handyman-exemption scope (or begin license application if targeting larger jobs).
2. **Vehicle & tooling:** Outfit van/truck with core kit (~$2,500–$5,000 if starting fresh).
3. **Brand basics:** Logo, business cards, uniform, phone number (local 951 area code), and a Google Workspace email.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
4. **Website:** 5–8 pages — Home, Services (drywall, plumbing fixtures, assembly, painting, minor electrical), Service Areas (Riverside neighborhoods: Wood Streets, Canyon Crest, La Sierra, Arlington, Eastside), About, Contact, Reviews.
5. **Google Business Profile:** Full optimization — categories, service areas, hours, photos, Q&A seeding, and weekly posts.
6. **Programmatic local pages:** Generate 15–25 neighborhood + service combo pages (e.g., "Fence Repair in La Sierra, Riverside") to capture long-tail GEO queries.
7. **Review engine:** Set up automated SMS/email review requests post-job; target **15+ Google reviews in 60 days**.

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 6–10)
8. **Google Local Services Ads:** Apply (background check + insurance verification required); budget **$300–$600/mo** initially.
9. **Referral partnerships:** Walk into **10–15 Riverside realtors, 5 property managers, and 3 senior-living communities** — handyman demand from these channels converts at 3–5x cold leads.
10. **Nextdoor + local Facebook groups:** Post before/after jobs weekly; Riverside neighborhood groups are high-intent.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 10–16)
11. **Track cost-per-lead** by channel; kill underperformers below **$40 CPL** threshold.
12. **Expand service radius** to Corona, Norco, and Eastvale once Riverside capacity hits 70%.
13. **Add a second tech** when monthly gross exceeds **$14K** consistently.
14. **Refresh programmatic pages quarterly** with seasonal keywords (Q4: "holiday lighting installation," "gutter cleaning before rain season," "heater repair").
