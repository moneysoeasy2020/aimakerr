# Launching a Carpet Cleaning Business in Tampa, FL

Average residential job tickets run **$185–$340** for 3–4 rooms plus hall (500–800 sq ft), while commercial contracts (property management, hotels near Channelside, offices in Westshore) range **$0.18–$0.32/sq ft**. Startup capital for a lean mobile operation is **$8,500–$14,000** (truck-mount or high-end portable, chemicals, insurance, LLC, licensing); a truck-mount + van setup runs **$22,000–$38,000**.

Competitive density is moderate: an estimated **140–175 active carpet cleaning operators** in Hillsborough County, but only **~30 with a verified Google Business Profile ranking in the top 3 map pack** for "carpet cleaning Tampa." The **average cost-per-lead on Google Ads for this vertical in Tampa is $22–$41**, and **LSA (Local Services Ads) cost-per-lead sits at $18–$35**. That gap — high lead costs vs. low organic optimization maturity — is the strategic opening for a 2026 Q4 launch.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Ops (Weeks 1–2, ~$1,900)**
1. File Sunbiz LLC ($125); obtain EIN (free).
2. Secure Tampa Local Business Tax Receipt ($35–$50) + Hillsborough County receipt.
3. Bind GL insurance ($600–$1,200/yr, $1M/$2M).
4. Register IICRC CRT course ($500–$900) — schedule within 60 days.
5. Source equipment: truck-mount ($18K–$28K) or portable ($3,500–$7,000); chemicals ($600–$1,000 initial).

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4, ~$2,400)**
6. Launch 6-page site: Home, Services, Service Areas, Pricing, About, Book Now (freelance, $1,200–$3,000).
7. Create + verify Google Business Profile; add 10+ geo-tagged photos; set service areas (Tampa, Brandon, Riverview, Wesley Chapel, Carrollwood, South Tampa).
8. Build 30+ local citations (Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Thumbtack) — $400–$800 freelance.
9. Deploy programmatic service-area pages for 15 priority zips (33602, 33606, 33609, 33611, 33619, 33511, 33578, etc.).

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8, ~$1,800/mo)**
10. Launch LSA ($18–$35 CPL) + Google Ads ($22–$41 CPL) with **$1,200–$1,800/mo budget**.
11. Automate review requests (post-job SMS) — target **25+ GBP reviews in 90 days**.
12. Partner with 3–5 South Tampa/Westshore property managers for recurring commercial contracts.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Month 3+)**
13. Add second van when monthly revenue exceeds **$18,000**.
14. Layer programmatic SEO expansion to 30+ zips; track GBP calls/direction requests weekly.

**90-Day Revenue Target:** $12,000–$22,000 gross (8–15 jobs/week at $185–$340 avg ticket), with LSA/Ads driving 40–55% of booked jobs and organic/GBP the remainder. Break-even on the $8,500–$14,000 lean startup typically lands in **Month 3–4**.
