## GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Playbook

To get cited by AI answer engines in 2026 Q4, your digital assets must be **structured, factual, and locally specific**:

1. **FAQ schema on every service page** — target queries like "how much does AC replacement cost in Corpus Christi."
2. **Publish local data** (cooling degree days, permit costs, neighborhood-specific content for Calallen, Flour Bluff, Portland, Padre Island).
3. **Consistent NAP + entity signals** — Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp.
4. **Review velocity:** 15–25 new Google reviews/month in months 1–6 (automated SMS requests post-service).
5. **Citable content formats:** pricing tables, comparison pages, "cost of X in Corpus Christi" articles — AI engines preferentially cite these.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
- Form LLC (**$300–$500**), obtain EIN, open business banking.
- Secure TDLR license application + EPA 608 certification.
- Bind liability + commercial auto insurance (**$2,400–$6,000/yr**).
- Register City of Corpus Christi contractor permit.
- **Budget:** $4,500–$8,000

### Phase 2 — Digital Build (Weeks 2–5, parallel)
- Commission freelance web developer + SEO specialist (**$3,000–$6,000**).
- Build Google Business Profile, LSA application, 40+ citations.
- Publish 10 GEO-optimized service pages with FAQ schema.
- **Budget:** $3,500–$7,000

### Phase 3 — Equipment & Fleet (Weeks 3–6)
- Lease 2 used service vans (**$900–$1,400/mo each**) or buy used (**$18,000–$32,000 each**).
- Stock tools + refrigerant + parts inventory (**$6,000–$12,000**).
- **Budget:** $8,000–$15,000 (lease path) or $40,000+ (buy path)

### Phase 4 — Hiring & Systems (Weeks 5–8)
- Hire 1–2 EPA-certified techs (**$22–$32/hr** in Corpus Christi market).
- Deploy field service software (ServiceTitan/Jobber/Housecall Pro — **$150–$500/mo**).
- Set up dispatch, invoicing, review automation.
- **Budget:** $3,000–$6,000

### Phase 5 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–10)
- Launch Google LSA + Google Ads (**$1,500–$3,000/mo** initial spend).
- Push maintenance-contract signups ($149–$249/yr) to smooth cash flow.
- Target 15–25 Google reviews/month.
- **Budget:** $2,500–$4,500

### Phase 6 — Scale to Peak (Weeks 10–16 → into Q2 2027)
- Add third truck if utilization >75%.
- Layer in commercial contracts (small retail, restaurants).
- Transition to agency retainer for SEO/ads.
- **Target by month 6:** $35K–$50K/month revenue, 60+ reviews, LSA top-3 placement.
