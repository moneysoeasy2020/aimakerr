# Launching an HVAC Services Business in Madison, WI
## 2026 Q4 GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Madison, WI's HVAC market generates an estimated **$180M–$220M in annual residential and light-commercial revenue** across roughly **140–160 licensed contractors** serving a metro population of ~690,000. A cold-climate market with 6,500+ heating degree days means furnace and boiler service demand dominates October–March, while AC installs cluster May–August. A realistic 2026 Q4 launch requires **$85,000–$140,000 in startup capital** for a two-truck residential operation (vehicle, tools, EPA 608 certification, insurance, Wisconsin HVAC contractor registration, and 90 days of working capital).

Entry-level residential service call pricing in Madison runs **$95–$145 diagnostic**, **$180–$320 for standard furnace tune-ups**, **$4,800–$9,500 for a 96% AFUE furnace replacement**, and **$5,200–$11,000 for a central AC install**. Commercial RTU work starts at **$450–$900 per service visit**. Targeting a **22–28% gross margin on service, 30–38% on installs**, a lean two-truck shop needs **$38,000–$52,000 monthly revenue** to break even including owner draw.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Capital (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with Wisconsin DFI (**$130**).
2. Obtain HVAC contractor registration + EPA 608 (**$600–$1,200**).
3. Secure general liability + commercial auto (**$3,800–$6,500/yr**).
4. Open business banking + line of credit (**$25k–$50k** target).
5. Purchase/lease two service vans (**$28,000–$52,000** total).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
6. Launch 6-page website with **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**, service-area pages for Madison, Sun Prairie, Fitchburg, Middleton, Verona, Waunakee.
7. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (categories, service areas, 10+ photos, Q&A).
8. Build **20–40 programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "Furnace Repair in Sun Prairie, WI").
9. Set up Google Local Services Ads + call tracking (**$400–$900/mo** initial budget).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
10. Launch emergency no-heat landing page by **Nov 1** — this is your Q4 revenue engine.
11. Deploy review-request automation (target **25+ Google reviews by Dec 31**).
12. Run **$1,500–$2,500/mo** Google Ads on high-intent keywords ("furnace repair Madison," "no heat emergency").
13. Partner with 3–5 local property managers for recurring maintenance contracts.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 3–6)
14. Add second truck once monthly revenue exceeds **$45,000**.
15. Launch maintenance-plan subscription (**$14–$22/mo**) to stabilize cash flow.
16. Expand programmatic pages to **60–80** targeting suburban ZIPs.
17. Review ad spend monthly; target **$55–$90 cost per booked call**.
