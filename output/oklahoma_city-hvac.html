## Executive Overview: The Oklahoma City HVAC Opportunity

Oklahoma City's HVAC market is projected to reach **$412M–$445M in annual residential and light-commercial revenue by Q4 2026**, driven by a metro population of **1.48M** and a housing stock of roughly **620,000 units** — of which **64%** are single-family homes aged 15+ years requiring service or replacement. The metro experiences **avg. summer highs of 94°F** and **winter lows of 27°F**, producing a **dual-peak demand curve** (June–August cooling, December–February heating) that supports year-round utilization for well-positioned operators.

Realistic startup cost ranges for a licensed OKC HVAC services business in 2026 Q4: **$18,000–$34,000** for a lean owner-operator model (used van, basic tooling, license, insurance, bonding), **$55,000–$95,000** for a 2-technician residential startup, and **$140,000–$260,000** for a full brick-and-mortar shop with 3–4 trucks and a dispatcher. Typical residential service call pricing in OKC runs **$89–$149 diagnostic**, **$180–$420** for common repairs (capacitor, blower motor, contactor), and **$6,800–$14,500** for full system replacement (3-ton split, standard efficiency). Target **gross margins of 42–58%** on service, **28–38%** on installs.

## 2026 Q4 Oklahoma City Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers
- **Population growth:** OKC metro added ~**11,400 residents** year-over-year into 2026; Canadian and Cleveland County suburbs (Yukon, Mustang, Edmond, Moore) show the highest new-HVAC install density.
- **Aging equipment base:** ~**41%** of metro residential HVAC units are **12+ years old** — prime replacement and repair territory.
- **Regulatory tailwind:** Federal refrigerant transition (R-454B/R-32 phase-in) is pushing **2026–2027 replacement cycles** as legacy R-410A systems lose serviceability and resale value.
- **Commercial demand:** OKC's MAPS 4 downtown buildout and Class A office retrofits are generating steady light-commercial PM (preventive maintenance) contract demand.

### Competitive Landscape
- **~340–380** licensed HVAC contractors operate in the OKC MSA — a moderate-density market with **no single dominant player above ~8% share**.
- **Google Local Services Ads (LSAs)** and **Google Business Profile** are the highest-intent lead sources; **~58%** of OKC HVAC service searches now originate from mobile and are increasingly resolved through AI Overviews and map packs.
- **Seasonal pricing pressure:** shoulder months (April–May, September–October) see **15–22%** margin compression — plan cash reserves accordingly.

### GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) Reality
By Q4 2026, **~34–41%** of local "HVAC near me" and "AC repair cost Oklahoma City" queries are being answered by AI-generated summaries (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot). To be cited, your digital assets must contain **structured, citable facts**: license numbers, NATE certification, service radii (e.g., "serving OKC, Edmond, Yukon within 30 miles"), transparent price ranges, and schema-marked LocalBusiness data.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Licensing & compliance:** Obtain Oklahoma **Mechanical Contractor license** (Oklahoma Construction Industries Board) and required **$10,000 surety bond**; register with OKC Development Services. Budget **$1,800–$3,200** for license, bond, and exam fees.
2. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto + workers' comp — **$3,200–$6,500/yr** for a lean startup.
3. **Entity & banking:** Oklahoma LLC ($100 filing), EIN, business bank account, merchant processor for field payments.
4. **Equipment:** Used cargo van ($9,000–$18,000), recovery machine, vacuum pump, gauges, torches, refrigerant scale — **$6,500–$11,000**.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–7)
5. **Website + schema:** Commission a GEO-ready site with LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ schema; embed transparent price ranges and service radius (OKC + Edmond, Yukon, Mustang, Moore, Norman within 30 mi).
6. **Google Business Profile:** Verify, complete 100%, add service categories, post weekly, seed with 10+ photos.
7. **Programmatic SEO pages:** Build 40–80 pages targeting `[service] + [suburb]` (e.g., "AC repair Yukon OK cost") — the highest-converting long-tail pattern in OKC.
8. **Review engine:** Automate post-service review requests; target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (GEO trust threshold).

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation & Launch (Weeks 6–10)
9. **Google Local Services Ads:** Enable and set weekly budget **$400–$900**; LSA is the #1 intent channel for OKC HVAC.
10. **PPC + retargeting:** $600–$1,200/mo on high-intent keywords ("AC repair near me OKC," "furnace replacement Oklahoma City").
11. **CRM + call tracking:** Deploy field-service CRM (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber) — **$99–$399/mo**.
12. **Soft launch:** 2–3 weeks of owner-operator service calls to validate pricing and gather reviews before scaling ad spend.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)
13. **Add technician #2** once monthly service revenue exceeds **$22,000–$28,000**.
14. **Launch maintenance agreements** ($14–$22/mo residential) — stabilizes shoulder-season cash flow.
15. **Expand programmatic SEO** to 120+ pages; pursue AI Overview citations with cost-guide content.
16. **Review quarterly:** if revenue exceeds **$750K/yr**, evaluate upgrading from freelance experts to a hybrid agency model.
