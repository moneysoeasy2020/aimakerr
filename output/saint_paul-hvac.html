## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Licensing & Compliance (Weeks 1–6)
1. Secure **Minnesota HVAC contractor license** (Dept. of Labor and Industry) + **$25,000 recovery fund** contribution.
2. Obtain **EPA Section 608 Universal certification** for all technicians.
3. Register **LLC with Minnesota Secretary of State** ($155 filing) and Saint Paul business license.
4. Bind **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and commercial auto insurance.
5. Confirm **A2L refrigerant handling training** for 2026 compliance.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–8)
1. Launch a **GEO-optimized website**: service-area pages for Saint Paul neighborhoods (Highland Park, Summit Hill, Midway, Como, Dayton's Bluff), FAQ schema, LocalBusiness schema, and pricing transparency blocks.

3. Publish **6 GEO articles** in the first 60 days (e.g., "Furnace Replacement Cost in Saint Paul 2026," "Cold-Climate Heat Pump Guide for Ramsey County").
4. Set up **call tracking** and CRM (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber — $150–$400/mo).

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 6–12)
1. Activate **Google Local Services Ads** ($800–$1,500/mo ad spend).
2. Launch **Google Search Ads** on high-intent terms ("furnace repair Saint Paul," "AC install Ramsey County") — $1,200–$2,500/mo.
3. Implement **review generation workflow** — target 40+ Google reviews by month 6.
4. Secure **15–25 local citations** (BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Ramsey County directories).

### Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Months 3–12)
1. Hire **1 lead installer + 1 apprentice** by month 4.
2. Launch **maintenance plan** ($180–$340/year) to build recurring revenue — target 120 plan members by month 12.

4. Track KPIs monthly: cost-per-lead (**target <$65**), close rate (**target 32–45%**), average ticket (**target $380+**), and review velocity (**8+/month**).

### Phase 5 — GEO Compounding (Months 6–18)
1. Publish **2–4 GEO articles monthly** with Saint Paul-specific data, pricing tables, and citations.
