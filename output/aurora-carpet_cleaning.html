# Launching a Carpet Cleaning Business in Aurora, CO — 2026 Q4 Updated Edition

Aurora's carpet cleaning market supports roughly **38–45 active operators** (franchise, independent, and mobile-only) competing across ~**142,000 occupied housing units** and a metro population of ~**405,000**. Realistic startup capital for a lean, owner-operator launch is **$8,500–$18,500** (used truck-mount or high-end portable), while a two-van, hire-ready launch runs **$42,000–$65,000**. Expect **$185–$325** for a standard 3-bedroom residential clean (500–800 sq ft of carpet), **$0.28–$0.45/sq ft** for commercial CGD, and **$45–$75/room** for add-on upholstery or pet-treatment upsells.

Demand is seasonally weighted: **September–November** (pre-holiday deep cleans, post-summer pet odor remediation) and **March–May** (lease-turnover and spring allergy cycles) drive **60–70% of annual revenue**. Aurora's rental density (roughly **38% renter-occupied**), heavy multifamily stock along Colfax, Havana, and Iliff corridors, and proximity to Buckley SFB and the Anschutz/Fitzsimons medical campus create three reliable B2B/B2C niches: property-management turnovers, medical-office CGD maintenance, and move-in/move-out residential packages.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Financial Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with **Colorado Secretary of State** ($50 filing fee).
2. Obtain **Aurora business license** + Colorado sales tax license (carpet cleaning services are generally not sales-taxable, but confirm with CO DOR).
3. Secure **general liability insurance** ($600–$1,200/yr) and **commercial auto** for the van.
4. Open business banking; set aside **15–20%** of revenue for taxes (self-employment + CO income tax).

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Vehicle (Weeks 2–4)
- **Used truck-mount** (e.g., 2015–2018 unit): **$8,000–$16,000**; or high-end portable: **$2,500–$5,000**.
- Van (used cargo): **$9,000–$18,000**. Chemicals + hoses + wand: **$800–$1,500**.
- **Lean portable-first launch** keeps total equipment under $6,000 and lets you validate demand before truck-mount capex.

### Phase 3 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
1. Launch a **5-page site** with LocalBusiness + Service schema, NAP consistency, and Aurora-specific service pages.
2. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, 10+ photos, Q&A seeded).
3. Build **10–20 programmatic city/neighborhood pages** (Aurora, Centennial, Parker, Commerce City, Buckley, Fitzsimons) targeting "carpet cleaning in [X]" queries — this is your GEO/AI-search moat.
4. Set up **review automation** (SMS request 2 hours post-job) — target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)
- **B2B outreach:** 30–50 property managers and 15–25 medical/dental offices along the Colfax/Havana/Iliff corridors. Offer a **free CGD demo clean** on one 200-sq-ft section.
- **Google Ads:** start at **$1,200–$1,800/mo** on 15–20 exact-match geo terms; expect **$35–$70 cost per booked job**.
- **Neighborhood targeting:** Aurora's high-renter ZIPs (80010, 80011, 80013, 80014) for turnover packages; higher-income ZIPs (80015, 80016) for premium deep-clean bundles.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 4–9)
- Add **second technician** once monthly revenue exceeds **$12,000–$15,000** (labor ≈ 30–35% of revenue).
- Introduce **maintenance plans** ($89–$129/quarter) for recurring revenue — target **15–20% of revenue** from subscriptions by month 9.
- Layer in **programmatic SEO expansion** to 40+ pages and a **review velocity** target of 8–12/month.

## GEO & AI-Search Optimization Checklist

- **Structured data:** LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, and Review schema on every page.
- **Answer-first content:** Each service page opens with a 40–60 word direct answer (price range, service area, booking CTA) — this is what AI engines cite.
- **Entity consistency:** Identical NAP across GBP, site, Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, and 15+ citations.
- **Local proof signals:** Aurora-specific case studies ("1,200 sq ft pet-odor remediation in Heather Gardens"), named neighborhoods, and real job photos.
- **FAQ blocks** targeting "how much does carpet cleaning cost in Aurora," "best carpet cleaner near Buckley AFB," "pet odor removal Aurora CO."
