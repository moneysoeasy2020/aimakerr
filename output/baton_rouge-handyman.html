## 2026 Q4 Baton Rouge Market Analysis

Baton Rouge's handyman and contractor market is currently serving a metro population of approximately **870,000** across East Baton Rouge, Ascension, Livingston, and West Baton Rouge parishes, with a **homeownership rate near 62%** and a median single-family home age of **38 years** — the single largest driver of repair and remodel demand. The East Baton Rouge Parish housing stock alone contains roughly **185,000 occupied housing units**, of which an estimated **58% were built before 2000**, meaning deferred-maintenance work (drywall, carpentry, plumbing rough-ins, deck repair, and post-hurricane/storm remediation) is a recurring, non-discretionary revenue stream. Post-Ida and post-Francine insurance-driven repair work continues to feed demand into Q4 2026, and the region's **~$62,000 median household income** supports a healthy mid-market for $150–$500 jobs.

Realistic 2026 Q4 Baton Rouge pricing: **handyman hourly rates of $65–$110/hr** (licensed trades at the top of that band), **minimum service call fees of $95–$150**, **drywall repair at $3.50–$7.00/sq ft**, **interior paint at $2.50–$4.50/sq ft**, **deck repair at $35–$65/linear ft**, and **bathroom remodel projects at $12,000–$35,000**. A solo operator targeting **$14,000–$22,000/month in gross revenue** at a **45–55% gross margin** is a realistic 12-month trajectory. Startup capital ranges from **$4,500 (lean, truck-and-tools)** to **$28,000 (fully branded, insured, and digitally marketed)**. Louisiana requires a **State Licensing Board for Contractors (LSLBC)** license for residential work over **$7,500** (commercial over **$50,000**), plus a **$10,000–$15,000 general liability policy** running **$1,200–$2,800/year** and **workers' comp at roughly $8–$18 per $100 of payroll** for carpentry classification.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2, $1,200–$2,400)**
1. Register LLC with Louisiana Secretary of State (**$100** online filing).
2. Obtain EIN (free, IRS), open a business bank account.
3. Apply for **LSLBC residential license** if jobs exceed $7,500 (application + exam fees: **$300–$600**).
4. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** and commercial auto insurance.
5. Register with **Louisiana Workforce Commission** for unemployment tax and obtain a **Baton Rouge/EBR Parish occupational license** (**$50–$150**).

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–5, $1,500–$3,000)**
6. Launch a 5-page website with **schema markup** (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage) — critical for GEO/AI-search visibility.
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** with 10+ photos, service categories, and parish-level service areas (Baton Rouge, Zachary, Central, Baker, Denham Springs).
8. Build **15–25 programmatic service-area pages** (e.g., "Drywall Repair in Central, LA") targeting long-tail queries.
9. Set up **call tracking** and a booking form with SMS auto-response.

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12, $800–$2,000/mo)**
10. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, $25–$75/lead in Baton Rouge) before standard Google Ads.
11. Deploy a **review-generation SMS flow** — target 25 Google reviews in 90 days (critical ranking factor).
12. Publish **8–10 GEO-optimized service pages** answering AI-search queries: "How much does drywall repair cost in Baton Rouge?" etc.
13. Join **local trade networks** (BR Area Chamber, Nextdoor business, Angi/Thumbtack as lead-fill only).

**Phase 4 — Scale & Systematize (Months 4–12)**
14. Add a second crew or subcontractor once booked revenue exceeds **$16,000/month**.
15. Build a **maintenance-plan subscription** ($99–$199/quarter) for recurring revenue.
16. Invest in **AI-search visibility** — structured FAQs, citation building (Yelp, BBB, Houzz), and consistent NAP data across 20+ directories.

## GEO Optimization Checklist (2026 Q4)

- **Entity clarity:** Ensure your business name, address, phone, and service areas are identical across every citation.
- **Answer-first content:** Every page should lead with a direct, quotable answer (AI engines extract these).
- **Local proof:** Embed Baton Rouge-specific data — parish names, storm-history references, local pricing.
- **Structured data:** FAQPage + LocalBusiness schema on all service pages.
- **Review velocity:** 4+ new Google reviews/month maintains AI-citation eligibility.
