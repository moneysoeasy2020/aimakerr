**Phase 3 — Q4 2026 Soft Launch (Oct–Dec)**

8. Deploy review-request automation; target **25+ reviews by Dec 31**.
9. Launch insurance-reset campaign (Dec 15–Jan 31) for elective treatment.

**Phase 4 — Scale (2027 Q1+)**
10. Add programmatic service-area pages (Tempe, Paradise Valley, Fountain Hills borders).
11. Track CAC, chair utilization, and AI-referral traffic monthly; reinvest in top-performing channel.
