## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Months 1–4, Budget: $285K–$475K)
1. **Entity & licensing** — TN dental license, DEA registration, LLC formation ($310 TN filing), malpractice insurance ($3,200–$5,800/yr)
2. **Site selection** — Target **1,800–2,600 sq ft** in Germantown/Collierville ($28–$38/sq ft NNN) or Frayser/Whitehaven for Medicaid volume ($16–$22/sq ft NNN)
3. **Buildout & equipment** — 3-chair buildout $145K–$220K; dental equipment (chairs, X-ray, sterilizers) $95K–$145K
4. **Digital foundation** — Engage verified freelance web + SEO specialist; launch GBP 90 days pre-opening

### Phase 2: Soft Launch (Months 5–6, Budget: $45K–$70K)
5. **GBP optimization** — 10+ geo-tagged photos weekly, service categories, Q&A seeding
6. **Review engine** — Target **40+ Google reviews at 4.8+ stars** by month 6 (critical ranking threshold in Memphis)
7. **Insurance credentialing** — Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, BCBS TN, TennCare (allow 90–120 days)

### Phase 3: Growth (Months 7–18, Budget: $2,300–$5,500/mo digital)
8. **Local SEO content** — 4–6 geo-targeted pages/mo (e.g., "dentist in Germantown TN," "emergency dentist near University of Memphis")
9. **Google Ads** — $1,800–$3,200/mo starting budget, geo-fenced to 7-mile radius
10. **Programmatic location pages** — 12–18 suburb pages (Collierville, Bartlett, Cordova, Southaven MS, Olive Branch MS)
11. **Reputation compounding** — 15–25 new reviews/month; respond within 24 hours

### Phase 4: Scale (Months 19–30)
12. **Second location evaluation** at $850K+ annual collections
13. **Specialty service lines** — implants, ortho, sedation (each adds $180K–$420K annual revenue potential)
14. **DSO partnership evaluation** — only if valuation exceeds 6.5× EBITDA
