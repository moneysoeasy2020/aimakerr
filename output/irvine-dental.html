## 2026 Q4 Irvine Market Analysis

Irvine is one of the most saturated yet highest-value dental markets in Orange County. As of Q4 2026, the city has roughly **110–130 active dental practices** serving a population of approximately **315,000 residents** across ~120,000 households, with a median household income near **$125,000–$130,000** and a median age of ~35. Irvine's population skews toward families and tech/professional workers, with a high rate of employer-sponsored PPO dental coverage (broadly 65–75% of insured adults in the city). Expect **startup capital requirements of $450,000–$850,000** for a 1,800–2,500 sq ft build-out (tenant improvements at $150–$250/sq ft, plus $180,000–$350,000 in equipment including chairs, digital X-ray, CBCT, and sterilization), and **lease rates of $38–$60/sq ft NNN** in retail/medical-zoned corridors (Irvine Spectrum, Woodbridge, Northwood, Westpark, Great Park). Realistic year-one revenue for a 4–5 operatory general practice is **$850,000–$1.4M**, with break-even typically in **months 14–22**.

Procedure pricing benchmarks in Irvine (2026 Q4, cash/PPO-negotiated ranges): new patient exam + X-rays **$150–$350**; adult prophylaxis **$120–$220**; composite filling **$250–$450**; crown **$1,400–$2,200**; root canal (molar) **$1,100–$1,800**; single implant (surgical + restoration) **$3,500–$6,000**; Invisalign full case **$4,500–$7,500**; whitening **$350–$650**. Marketing and digital launch budgets should be planned at **$8,000–$25,000** for initial asset build (website, GBP, listings, content) and **$3,000–$8,000/month** ongoing for SEO/PPC to compete in a market where "dentist Irvine" and "Irvine dental implants" are high-CPC terms ($12–$35 per click for implants).

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

1. **Entity & licensing (Weeks 1–4):** Form PLLC/PC in California, register with the Dental Board of California, obtain DEA and NPI, secure malpractice insurance ($2,500–$6,000/yr), and set up business banking. Irvine business license via city portal.
2. **Site selection & build-out (Weeks 4–20):** Target medical-zoned retail in Woodbridge/Northwood/Great Park; negotiate TI allowance ($30–$60/sq ft common). Dental build-out runs 90–150 days.
3. **Digital foundation (Weeks 8–14):** Launch website with LocalBusiness/Dentist schema, service-area pages for Irvine ZIPs (92602, 92603, 92604, 92606, 92612, 92614, 92618, 92620), and Google Business Profile verification (allow 2–4 weeks).
4. **Pre-launch marketing (Weeks 12–20):** Google Ads on high-intent terms, 20–30 review seeding via early patients, local sponsorships (Irvine schools, youth sports), and insurance credentialing with Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, MetLife (start 90 days early — credentialing takes 60–120 days).
5. **Soft open → full launch (Weeks 20–24):** Target 40–60 new patients/month by month 3 via PPC + GBP; layer programmatic SEO content (procedure × neighborhood pages) for organic compounding by months 6–12.
6. **Optimize (Months 6–18):** Track cost-per-new-patient (target $150–$350), recall rate (target 70%+), and production per operatory ($35,000–$55,000/month mature).
