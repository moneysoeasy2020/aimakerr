## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2)
- Register an LLC with the **Ohio Secretary of State ($99 online)**; obtain an Ohio vendor's license and Cincinnati business tax account.
- Secure **general liability insurance ($450–$900/year)** and commercial auto for the van — landlords and property managers will ask for a COI before booking.
- Confirm IICRC certification path (Carpet Cleaning Technician course, **$495–$650**) — it is a trust signal in Cincinnati property-management RFPs.

### Phase 2 — Equipment & Vehicle (Weeks 2–4)
- Budget **$7,000–$15,000** for a used truck-mount or **$2,500–$4,500** for a portable + heated extraction setup. Buy used from regional dealers in Columbus or Dayton to avoid Cincinnati markup.
- Stock chemicals, spotting kits, air movers, and a moisture meter (**$600–$1,100**).

### Phase 3 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
- Launch a **5–8 page site** with service pages, pricing ranges, and a booking form.
- Build **30–60 programmatic service-area pages** targeting neighborhoods (Oakley, Hyde Park, Anderson Township, Mason, West Chester) — this is where GEO wins: AI answer engines cite specific, structured local pages.
- Optimize Google Business Profile with service categories, Q4 hours, and photo cadence (3–5/week).

### Phase 4 — Review & Lead Engine (Weeks 5–8)
- Deploy an SMS review-request flow triggered 2 hours post-job. Target **25 reviews by day 60, 60 by day 120** — the threshold where Cincinnati map-pack visibility inflects.
- Set Google Ads at **$600–$1,200/month** initially, geo-fenced to a 12-mile radius; expect **$18–$38 cost per lead** and 22–30% close rate.

### Phase 5 — B2B Channel (Weeks 8–16)
- Pitch **15–25 property managers and 10–15 commercial offices** in the Blue Ash, Kenwood, and downtown corridors. Commercial contracts at **$0.28–$0.45/sq ft** stabilize Q4 revenue and reduce ad dependency.
