## 2026 Q4 Baton Rouge Market Analysis

Baton Rouge's dental services market is projected to generate approximately **$310M–$340M in annual revenue** across roughly **420–470 active dental practices** (general, specialty, and DSO-affiliated) serving an East Baton Rouge Parish population of about **455,000** and a broader metro of **870,000+**. The parish has roughly **1 dentist per 1,850 residents** — below the national average of ~1 per 1,600 — leaving measurable capacity gaps in the **Mid City, Zachary, Central, and Prairieville corridor** growth zones. Average patient acquisition cost (PAC) for a new general dentistry patient in this market runs **$180–$320** via paid search and **$95–$160** via organic/local SEO, with DSOs (Heartland Dental, Aspen, Sage) driving up bid competition on "dentist near me" queries by an estimated **22–30% YoY**.

Realistic startup capital for a **3–4 operatory general dentistry clinic** in Baton Rouge ranges from **$480,000–$850,000** (build-out at $180–$260/sq ft, equipment $140K–$260K, working capital 6–9 months). Lease rates run **$22–$38/sq ft NNN** in Class A retail medical corridors (Bluebonnet, Perkins Rowe, Siegen Lane) and **$14–$22/sq ft** in secondary nodes (Florida Blvd, Airline Hwy). A solo practitioner can reach breakeven at **1,100–1,400 active patients**; a two-doctor practice at **2,200–2,800**. Q4 2026 timing is favorable: insurance re-credentialing cycles and calendar-year maximums create a **September–December patient-inflow surge** of 15–25% industry-wide, and Louisiana's **expanded Medicaid adult dental benefit** (rolling out through 2026) adds a new reimbursement stream for preventive and restorative codes.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)**
1. Entity formation (LLC/PC in Louisiana), NPI, and **Louisiana State Board of Dentistry** licensure verification.
2. Secure financing: SBA 7(a) up to $5M; Louisiana Economic Development small-business incentives; equipment leasing at 6–9% APR.
3. Site selection: prioritize **1,800–2,600 sq ft** in high-traffic medical retail; negotiate TI allowance of $40–$80/sq ft.
4. Credential with Delta Dental, Blue Cross LA, Cigna, and MetLife (90–150 day lead time — start immediately).

**Phase 2 — Build & Digital Foundation (Months 4–7)**
5. Build HIPAA-compliant website with **schema markup** (Dentist, LocalBusiness, FAQ) — critical for AI/GEO citation.
6. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile; target 40+ local citations.
7. Launch review-generation workflow (target 10–15 reviews/month).
8. Publish 8–12 GEO-optimized service pages (implants, Invisalign, emergency dentistry) with Baton Rouge-specific data.

**Phase 3 — Launch & Scale (Months 7–12)**
9. Soft launch with 200–400 patient records from prior relationships/referrals.
10. Activate Google Ads (target $3K–$5K/mo) and track PAC weekly.
11. Hire front-office + hygienist by month 9; add second doctor at ~1,800 active patients.
12. Q4 2026: run insurance-maximization campaigns (Sept–Nov) and New Year benefit-reset campaigns (Dec).
