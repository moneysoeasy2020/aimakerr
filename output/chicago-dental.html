## 2026 Q4 Chicago Market Analysis

**Demand-side metrics (Chicago-specific):**
- **Population:** ~2.66M city / ~8.6M metro; ~13.5% uninsured adults citywide, higher on the South and West Sides — sliding-scale and Medicaid acceptance (Illinois Medicaid dental for adults was restored and expanded) is a meaningful differentiator.
- **Competition:** ~6,800 metro practices; **Google Maps "dentist near me" local pack** in dense areas (Lincoln Park, Wicker Park, Lakeview) is contested by 15–30 listings per query. Suburban collar counties (Cook outside Chicago, DuPage, Will) show 20–35% lower cost-per-acquisition.
- **Commercial rent:** $28–$55/sq ft NNN in strong retail corridors (Lincoln Park, West Loop, River North); $18–$30/sq ft in emerging corridors (Avondale, Bronzeville, Pilsen).
- **Labor:** Hygienist wages $42–$55/hr; dental assistants $20–$27/hr; associate dentist guarantees $140K–$180K.
- **Payer mix reality:** PPO-heavy market; Delta Dental of Illinois, Cigna, MetLife, and BCBS IL dominate. Fee-for-service-only practices need affluent ZIP codes (60614, 60610, 60611) or a strong specialty niche.

**GEO/AI-search context (Q4 2026):** A growing share of "dentist near me" and "emergency dentist Chicago" queries now resolve through AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity rather than blue links. Generative engines pull from **Google Business Profile, structured schema, review corpora, and third-party citations (Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Yelp)** — meaning your local entity footprint matters more than raw backlink volume. Prioritize: consistent NAP across 40+ directories, `Dentist` + `MedicalBusiness` schema, FAQ content answering insurance/emergency/pricing queries verbatim, and a review velocity of **8–15 new Google reviews/month**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Entity & Compliance (Months 0–2)**
1. Form an Illinois LLC or PC; register with IDFPR for dental licensure and controlled-substance/DEC registration.
2. Secure commercial lease (target 1,800–2,400 sq ft; negotiate TI allowance of $40–$80/sq ft).
3. Obtain City of Chicago business licenses, zoning clearance, and dental office build-out permits (plan 8–14 weeks in Chicago).
4. Set up NPI, dental insurance credentialing (start immediately — takes 90–150 days), and CAQH profile.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 1–3, parallel to build-out)**
5. Launch website with `Dentist` + `LocalBusiness` + `FAQPage` schema; publish insurance, pricing, and emergency pages in plain Q&A format for AI extraction.
6. Create and fully optimize Google Business Profile (categories, services, photos weekly, Q&A seeding).
7. Build 40+ consistent citations (Yelp, Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect).
8. Deploy review-request automation at first patient visit; target 8–15 reviews/month.

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Months 3–6)**
9. Launch Google Ads on high-intent terms (emergency dentist, implants, Invisalign) with $2,500–$5,000/mo budget; track cost-per-booked-appointment (target $60–$150).
10. Publish 2–4 GEO-optimized local pages/month (neighborhood + service, e.g., "Emergency Dentist in Wicker Park").
11. Activate recall/reactivation campaigns via practice management software.

**Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 6–12)**
12. Review CAC by channel monthly; reallocate from underperforming paid search to SEO/GEO if CPAs exceed $200.
13. Add a second hygienist at ~800 active patients; evaluate Medicaid/sliding-scale mix for volume stability.
14. Re-audit AI-search visibility quarterly (test ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews for "dentist in [neighborhood]" and fix entity/citation gaps).

**Key 2026 Q4 KPIs to track:** active patient count, cost per new patient ($80–$180 blended), Google review count/rating (target 4.8+ with 150+ reviews by month 12), local pack ranking for 5 target queries, and website-to-booking conversion rate (target 4–8%).
