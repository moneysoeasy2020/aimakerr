## Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts

**Local brick-and-mortar agencies (Indianapolis):**
- **Retainers:** $1,500–$3,500/month for full-service local SEO + PPC management.
- **Pros:** In-person meetings, bundled services, established local relationships, accountability via local reputation.
- **Cons:** Overhead passed to you (30–50% premium), junior staff often execute, 6–12 month contracts common, slower iteration.
- **Best for:** Operators with $5K+/month marketing budgets who want hands-off management.

**Verified freelance experts (Upwork, vetted local SEO consultants):**
- **Rates:** $45–$110/hour, or $1,200–$2,800 fixed for a launch package (GBP + site + citations + 10 pages).
- **Pros:** 40–60% lower cost, direct access to the practitioner, flexible scope, faster turnarounds.
- **Cons:** Requires vetting (portfolio, reviews, test task), no local office, you manage the project.
- **Best for:** Lean launches ($20K–$35K capital) where every dollar must convert to booked jobs.

**Verdict for 2026 Q4:** A **hybrid model** wins — hire a verified freelancer for GBP + programmatic pages + citations (~$1,500–$2,500), then reinvest saved budget into **Google Local Services Ads** ($800–$1,500/mo), which delivers calls within days in Indianapolis.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Operations (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register an **Indiana LLC** ($95 online via INBiz) and obtain an **EIN** (free).
2. Secure **general liability insurance** ($600–$1,200/yr) — required by most commercial clients and property managers.
3. Confirm **Indianapolis/Marion County** business licensing; no state carpet-cleaning license is required, but **IICRC certification** ($500–$900) is a strong trust signal for AI citations and reviews.
4. Set up a business bank account and payment processing (Stripe/Square, ~2.6–2.9% + $0.10).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–5)
5. Claim and **fully optimize Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas (Indianapolis + 15–20 suburbs), photos weekly, Q&A seeding.
6. Launch a **10–15 page website** with LocalBusiness + Service schema, pricing tables, and suburb service-area pages (programmatic SEO for Carmel, Fishers, Noblesville, Greenwood, Plainfield, Avon).
7. Build **25–40 consistent citations** (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, local chambers).
8. Publish **5–10 GEO-formatted Q&A posts** answering "how much," "best," and "near me" queries with exact Indianapolis pricing.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
9. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** ($800–$1,500/mo) — pay-per-lead, appears above everything else.
10. Deploy **review automation** (SMS after job completion); target **40+ Google reviews in 90 days**.
11. Partner with **3–5 property managers** and 2–3 realtors for recurring turnover work.
12. Wrap the van ($1,800–$3,500) for mobile visibility and local brand recall.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Weeks 8–16)
13. Track **cost-per-booked-job** weekly; kill channels above $70/job.
14. Add **commercial contracts** (offices, apartments, hotels) at $0.18–$0.32/sq ft for revenue stability.
15. Reinvest into **second van** once monthly booked jobs exceed **60–70** and reviews surpass **75**.
