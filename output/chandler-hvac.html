## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–8)
1. Obtain **Arizona ROC C-39 license** (application + exam + bond).
2. Secure **EPA 608 Universal** certification.
3. Register LLC with AZ Corporation Commission; obtain Chandler business license.
4. Bind general liability ($1M/$2M) and commercial auto insurance.
5. Open business banking + merchant processing (target 2.5–2.9% + $0.10).

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 6–14)
6. Launch GEO-optimized website: schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage), location landing pages, service-area pages.
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, services, 10+ photos, Q&A seeding).
8. Build 40–60 programmatic SEO pages targeting "[service] + [Chandler neighborhood]" (e.g., Ocotillo, Sun Lakes, Downtown Chandler, Fulton Ranch).
9. Deploy **call tracking** (CallRail or similar, $45–$95/mo) to attribute leads.
10. Set up Google Local Services Ads (LSA) — budget $1,500–$3,000/mo initially; LSA leads convert 3–5x better than PPC for HVAC.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 12–24)
11. Launch maintenance-contract offer ($149–$249/year) as recurring revenue anchor.
12. Publish 2 GEO blog posts/week targeting AI-search queries ("best AC repair Chandler," "how much does AC replacement cost in Chandler").
13. Build review engine: aim for 25+ Google reviews in first 90 days (SMS follow-up automation).
14. Partner with 3–5 local realtors and property managers for referral pipeline.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Month 6+)
15. Hire first technician (W-2, $55,000–$75,000 + commission).
16. Add second van when monthly revenue exceeds $45,000.
17. Expand programmatic SEO to Mesa, Gilbert, Tempe (adjacent high-value markets).
18. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into digital; track cost-per-lead against $85–$140 LSA benchmark.
