## 2026 Q4 Las Vegas Market Analysis

Las Vegas supports roughly **1,100–1,200 active dental practices** across Clark County, serving a metro population of approximately **2.4 million** — a ratio of about **1 dentist per 2,000 residents**, slightly tighter than the national average. The metro added roughly **35,000–45,000 new residents** in the trailing 12 months, concentrated in Henderson, Summerlin, and the southwest corridor (Enterprise/Spring Valley). Commercial dental space ranges from **$28–$42/sq ft NNN** in suburban retail centers to **$55–$75/sq ft** in high-traffic Summerlin and Green Valley locations. A standard **2,000–2,500 sq ft** build-out (4–5 operatories) runs **$380,000–$650,000** in tenant improvements, plus **$180,000–$320,000** in equipment (chairs, digital X-ray/CBCT, sterilization, imaging).

Typical 2026 Q4 revenue benchmarks for a new Las Vegas general practice: **$55,000–$85,000/month** by month 12, with patient acquisition cost (PAC) of **$180–$340** for new patients via paid search and **$95–$160** via organic/local SEO. Average new-patient value in Las Vegas sits at **$650–$1,100** first-year, with lifetime value of **$2,800–$4,500**. Nevada's dental workforce shortage (roughly **1 dentist per 1,850 residents statewide**) means associate recruitment is competitive — budget **$160,000–$220,000** base plus production bonus for a general dentist, and **$95,000–$130,000** for hygienists (Las Vegas commands a premium due to cost of living and licensing demand).

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 0–4)**
1. Secure entity (NV LLC), NPI, DEA if prescribing, and Nevada State Board of Dental Examiners licensure.
2. Lease negotiation: target **$30–$38/sq ft NNN**; negotiate TI allowance of **$40–$75/sq ft**.
3. Build-out + equipment procurement (lead time 10–16 weeks on chairs/CBCT).
4. Engage verified freelancer for website + GBP setup (budget **$4,000–$8,000**).

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 3–5)**
5. Launch HIPAA-compliant site with LocalBusiness + Dentist schema, service-area pages for Henderson, Summerlin, Spring Valley, North Las Vegas.
6. Google Business Profile verification + 15–25 citation submissions.
7. Programmatic SEO: 40–60 pages (procedure × neighborhood matrix).

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Months 4–9)**
8. Google Ads launch: **$3,000–$6,000/month** spend, geo-fenced to 5-mile radius.
9. Review velocity campaign: target **15–25 new Google reviews/month**.
10. Insurance credentialing (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Guardian) — 60–120 day cycles.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Months 9–18)**
11. Add second hygienist at **60–70% operatory utilization**.
12. Introduce membership plan ($29–$39/month) to capture uninsured (≈18% of Clark County).
13. Reinvest 6–9% of revenue into digital; audit agency/freelancer ROI quarterly.
