### Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts

**Local brick-and-mortar agencies (Glendale/Phoenix):** Expect $2,500–$4,500/month retainers, 6–12 month lock-ins, and account managers who often outsource execution. Advantages: in-person meetings, established local references, bundled services. Disadvantages: 2–3x cost, slower iteration, and you rarely own the assets (website, GBP, ad accounts) outright.

**Verified freelance experts:** Expect $35–$95/hour or $650–$2,400 per project, with milestone-based payments. Advantages: 40–60% lower cost, direct communication with the person doing the work, and full asset ownership. Disadvantages: you must vet rigorously — demand portfolio proof, a live GBP case study, and a 30-day satisfaction clause. **For a sub-$50K startup, freelance + one fractional local SEO consultant ($500–$900/mo) delivers the best ROI.** Use agencies only for paid-search execution once you exceed $4,000/month in ad spend.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3, ~$6,500)**
1. Register LLC with AZ Corporation Commission ($50), obtain EIN, and secure a Glendale business license (~$100–$250).
2. Purchase equipment: used truck-mount ($8,000–$14,000) or portable ($2,500–$4,500), plus chemicals and spotting kit ($600).
3. Secure commercial general liability insurance ($1,200–$2,200/yr) and a commercial auto policy.
4. Establish a verifiable business address in Glendale (home office qualifies if compliant) for GBP verification.

**Phase 2 — Digital Launch (Weeks 3–6, ~$2,800)**
5. Launch a 6-page website with LocalBusiness + Service schema, click-to-call, and online booking (Housecall Pro or Jobber, $49–$129/mo).
6. Create and verify your Google Business Profile in the **Carpet Cleaning** primary category; add 12+ services and 20 geo-tagged photos.
7. Build 30 core citations (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor) with NAP consistency.
8. Publish 5 neighborhood landing pages: **Arrowhead Ranch, Westgate, Historic Downtown Glendale, Sahuaro Ranch, and Deer Valley.**

**Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 6–12)**
9. Enroll in Google Local Services Ads ($500–$1,500/mo budget; pay per verified lead).
10. Launch a review-generation SMS flow — target **25 reviews by Day 90** (the threshold where GBP ranking accelerates).
11. Run a $400–$700/mo Google Search campaign on high-intent terms ("carpet cleaning Glendale AZ," "pet stain removal near me").
12. Partner with 3–5 local property managers and 2 real estate agents for recurring referral flow.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Month 4+)**
13. Add commercial contracts (medical offices, restaurants) at $0.18–$0.32/sq ft.
14. Introduce a maintenance plan ($89–$129/quarter) to lift lifetime value.
15. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into local SEO and review velocity to defend GBP top-3 positioning.
