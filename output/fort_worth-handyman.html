### Demand Drivers (Q4 2026)
- **Housing stock:** ~**310,000 owner-occupied single-family units** in Fort Worth; median home age **38 years** — prime repair-and-rehab territory.
- **Seasonal demand curve:** Q4 spikes in **weatherization, gutter cleaning, fence repair, holiday lighting installs, and interior punch-list work**. October–November bookings typically run **18–25% above Q3 baseline**.
- **Competitive density:** ~**1,150–1,300 active handyman/contractor listings** on Google Business Profile across Fort Worth; only **~22%** maintain 20+ reviews, leaving a wide GEO ranking gap.
- **Regulatory note:** Texas does **not** require a state handyman license for jobs under **$50,000** (Tex. Occ. Code Ch. 1303 applies to A/C; electrical/plumbing require licensed subs). Fort Worth requires a **General Contractor registration** for structural permits and a **$75–$300 annual city permit bond** depending on scope.
