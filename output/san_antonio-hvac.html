San Antonio's HVAC services market generated an estimated **$410M–$460M in annual residential and light-commercial revenue** in 2025, with the metro's 2.6M+ population and 8%+ annual cooling-degree-day growth driving demand. - San Antonio's housing stock skews older: **~42% of homes built before 2000**, meaning legacy R-22 and early R-410A systems are hitting end-of-life replacement windows through 2027.
- **Military City USA**: ~80,000 active-duty personnel at JBSA installations create steady PCS-driven move-in/move-out HVAC inspection demand.
- Median household income (~$62K) supports mid-tier replacement packages; financing partnerships (GreenSky, Synchrony) convert **20–30% more replacement quotes**.

**Competitive landscape:** 400+ licensed HVAC contractors operate in Bexar County. Top players (Airrosti-adjacent regional firms, ARS/Rescue Rooter, local independents) dominate branded search. Your wedge: **response-time guarantees (same-day service before 2 PM), flat-rate pricing transparency, and Spanish-language service capability** (63%+ Hispanic population — bilingual dispatch is a measurable conversion advantage).

**Seasonality:** Q4 (Oct–Dec) is the **best launch window** — heating call volume is light, letting you build review velocity, Google Business Profile authority, and maintenance-contract base before the March–September revenue sprint.

## Local Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts

**Brick-and-mortar SA agencies** (full-service, 10–50 staff): $3,500–$8,000/month retainers, 6–12 month contracts, bundled reporting. Strengths: single point of accountability, local networking, faster in-person onboarding. Weaknesses: **30–45% margin baked into overhead**, junior staff on your account, slow iteration, and often weak on **generative engine optimization (GEO)** — most still optimize for 2019-style keyword density rather than AI-answer citation.

**Verified freelance experts** (vetted via Upwork Top Rated Plus, Clutch, or local SA freelancer collectives): $1,200–$3,500/month for equivalent deliverables, project-based pricing, no lock-in. Strengths: **direct access to the practitioner, 40–60% cost savings, faster GEO/AI-search adaptation** (schema markup, entity optimization, structured Q&A content). Weaknesses: requires you to vet rigorously — demand portfolio proof of **local service business rankings**, ask for GBP case studies, and verify they understand HVAC seasonality.

**Verdict for a 2026 Q4 launch:** Hybrid model. Hire a **verified freelance web/SEO specialist ($2,000–$3,000/mo)** for the first 6 months, then layer a local agency only if you need multi-channel (radio, direct mail, community sponsorship) integration. Budget **$2,400–$3,600/month total** for digital — a brick-and-mortar agency equivalent runs **$5,500–$9,000/month**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Licensing & Compliance (Weeks 1–6)**
1. Obtain **TDLR Air Conditioning & Refrigeration Contractor License** (Class A or B) — exam + $300–$600 fees; 4+ years documented experience required for Class A.
2. Register **Texas Secretary of State LLC** ($300) + **Bexar County DBA** if applicable.
3. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto + workers' comp** — expect **$3,800–$7,500/year** for a 2-tech operation.
4. EPA **Section 608 Universal certification** for all techs (refrigerant handling — non-negotiable).

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 4–10)**
5. Launch website with **service-area pages for 12–18 SA ZIP clusters** (Stone Oak, Alamo Heights, Helotes, Converse, Schertz).
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas, Q&A seeding, 10+ photos.
7. Deploy **schema markup** (LocalBusiness, HVACBusiness, Service) and structured FAQ content for AI-search citation.
8. Enroll in **Google Local Services Ads** (background-checked badge) — highest-intent lead source for HVAC.

**Phase 3 — Revenue Engine (Weeks 8–16)**
9. Launch **maintenance agreement program** ($199–$349/year) — recurring revenue + replacement pipeline.
10. Set up **financing portal** (Synchrony/GreenSky) before first replacement quote.
11. Build review generation SOP: **target 40+ Google reviews by Day 90** (request at invoice + follow-up SMS).

**Phase 4 — Scale (Month 4+)**
12. Add second service vehicle when billable utilization exceeds **65%** for 30 consecutive days.
13. Layer **programmatic SEO** city pages (New Braunfels, Boerne, Seguin) as service radius expands.
14. Track **cost-per-acquired-customer (target: $180–$320)** and **LTV (target: $2,800–$4,500)** — maintain 8:1+ ratio.
