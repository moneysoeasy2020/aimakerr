## 2026 Q4 Oklahoma City Market Analysis

**Demand drivers:**
- **Rental turnover:** OKC has a rental vacancy rate near **7–8%** and a large multifamily stock (especially in Midwest City, Del City, Moore, and south OKC), generating steady move-out cleaning demand.
- **Storm season residue:** Oklahoma's hail and wind season (April–June) drives post-storm interior cleaning and insurance-adjacent restoration referrals through Q4.
- **Aging housing stock:** Median home age in OKC is **~40 years**, meaning mature carpet in need of deep extraction and pet-odor treatment.
- **Pet ownership:** Oklahoma ranks in the top 15 states for dog ownership (~**48–52% of households**), making pet-odor and urine treatment a high-margin upsell ($75–$150 add-on).

**Competitive landscape:** The OKC metro has **roughly 120–160 active carpet cleaning companies** listed on Google, but only **~25–35** maintain strong local SEO presence (10+ reviews/month, optimized GBP). This is a moderately contested but winnable market — far less saturated than Dallas or Austin.

**Seasonality (Q4-specific):** October–December is **pre-holiday deep-clean season** — expect a **15–25% demand lift** in November as households prepare for guests. Lock commercial contracts (property managers, offices) before December budget freezes.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Operational Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Register an LLC** with the Oklahoma Secretary of State — filing fee **$100** (online).
2. **Obtain an OKC business license** through the City of Oklahoma City (Development Services) — verify current fee (~$50–$150 depending on classification).
3. **Secure general liability insurance** — expect **$600–$1,200/year** for a solo carpet cleaning operation; add commercial auto if using a van.
4. **Open a business bank account** and set up a simple bookkeeping tool (QuickBooks or Wave).
5. **Source equipment:** used truck-mount ($8k–$15k) or commercial portable ($2.5k–$5k); budget **$1,200–$2,000** for chemicals, hoses, and wands.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–4)
6. **Register a local-intent domain** (e.g., `okccarpetcare.com`) — $12–$20/year.
7. **Build a 6-page site** (Home, Services, Pricing, Service Areas, About, Contact/Book) with LocalBusiness schema and click-to-call.
8. **Create and verify your Google Business Profile** — primary category "Carpet Cleaning Service," service-area business setup covering OKC, Edmond, Moore, Norman, Yukon, and Midwest City.
9. **Set up a booking + review-request system** (Jobber, Housecall Pro, or NiceJob) — $50–$150/month.

### Phase 3 — Local Visibility (Weeks 3–6)
10. **Build 40–60 local citations** (Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Thumbtack, Chamber of Commerce).
11. **Launch Google Local Services Ads** — pay per verified lead ($18–$45 in OKC); requires license and insurance verification.
12. **Start a Google Ads search campaign** on high-intent terms ("carpet cleaning Oklahoma City," "pet odor removal OKC") at **$15–$30/day**.
13. **Publish 2–3 geo-targeted service pages** (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in Edmond, OK") to capture suburb-level searches.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation & Reviews (Weeks 4–12)
14. **Run a launch offer** (e.g., "$199 for 3 rooms + free deodorizer") to seed reviews — target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**.
15. **Partner with 5–10 property managers** in south OKC and Midwest City for recurring turnover cleans — this is your highest-LTV channel.
16. **Distribute door hangers and van wrap** in target ZIP codes (73159, 73160, 73135) — $400–$900 one-time.
17. **Track cost-per-booked-job weekly**; kill any channel exceeding $60/job by week 8.

### Phase 5 — Q4 Optimization (Weeks 10–14)
18. **Push pre-holiday promotions** in November (deep-clean bundles, gift certificates).
19. **Lock commercial contracts** before December budget cycles close.
20. **Reinvest 20–25% of gross into the winning channel** and begin building a referral program ($25 credit per referral).
