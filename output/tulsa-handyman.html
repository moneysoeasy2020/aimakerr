# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Tulsa, OK
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Tulsa's handyman and general contracting market is projected to support **~1,180 active handyman/contractor establishments** by Q4 2026, serving a metro population of roughly **1.03 million** across Tulsa, Broken Arrow, Owasso, Jenks, and Bixby. The metro's housing stock—**~62% built before 1990**—drives steady demand for repair, remodel, and maintenance work, and the median existing-home age of **~48 years** means deferred-maintenance jobs (deck repair, drywall, plumbing fixtures, door/window replacement) are the volume driver. Expect **average residential handyman tickets of $150–$450 per visit**, with hourly rates of **$65–$95/hr** for licensed trades and **$45–$70/hr** for general handyman labor. A solo operator targeting 25 billable hours/week at a $75 blended rate can realistically gross **$8,000–$9,500/month** within 90 days of launch.

Startup capital for a compliant Tulsa launch runs **$6,500–$14,000**: Oklahoma Secretary of State LLC filing (**$100 online**), Tulsa sales/use tax permit (free), Oklahoma Construction Industries Board (CIB) trade license fees (**$75–$300** depending on specialty), general liability insurance (**$1,200–$2,800/year** for $1M/$2M coverage), a used cargo van or trailer setup (**$3,500–$8,000**), and tools (**$1,500–$3,000**). Digital launch costs—website, Google Business Profile optimization, and local SEO—range **$800–$4,500** depending on whether you use a Tulsa brick-and-mortar agency or a verified freelance specialist (detailed below). Oklahoma does **not** require a statewide general contractor license, but Tulsa and surrounding municipalities require **local registration and permits** for structural, electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work—budget **$150–$400/year** for municipal registrations across Tulsa, Broken Arrow, and Owasso.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2)
1. File **Oklahoma LLC** online via Secretary of State (**$100**).
2. Obtain **EIN** (free, IRS).
3. Register for **Tulsa sales/use tax permit** (free, Oklahoma Tax Commission).
4. Apply for **Oklahoma CIB trade license** if doing electrical/plumbing/mechanical (**$75–$300**).
5. Register with **City of Tulsa** and adjacent municipalities for permit-pulling authority (**$150–$400/yr**).
6. Secure **general liability insurance** ($1M/$2M, **$1,200–$2,800/yr**) and optional **workers' comp** if hiring.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
7. Build a **5–7 page website** with service pages (drywall, deck, plumbing fixtures, door/window, painting, assembly) and a **Tulsa-specific** homepage.
8. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, photos, Q&A).
9. Launch **programmatic SEO** city pages (Tulsa, Broken Arrow, Owasso, Jenks, Bixby, Sand Springs) targeting "[service] + [city]" queries.
10. Set up **review request automation** (SMS/email) to hit **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)
11. List on **Thumbtack, Angi, Nextdoor, and Facebook Marketplace** (budget **$300–$600/mo** for lead credits).
12. Run **Google Local Services Ads** (**$500–$1,200/mo** starting budget) — highest-intent channel in Tulsa.
13. Distribute door hangers and yard signs in **74105, 74114, 74137** (highest job density).
14. Partner with **2–3 Tulsa realtors** and **1 property manager** for recurring turnover work.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Month 4+)
15. Add a **second crew** once monthly gross exceeds **$14,000**.
16. Layer **programmatic SEO expansion** to 15+ surrounding cities.
17. Introduce **maintenance plans** ($99–$199/mo recurring) for predictable revenue.
