# Launching a Dental Clinic in Denver, CO — 2026 Q4 Startup Guide

Denver's dental services market is projected to reach **$1.42 billion in annual consumer spend by Q4 2026**, with roughly **1,180 active dental practices** serving a metro population of **2.98 million**. A new general dentistry clinic in Denver should budget **$485,000–$720,000 in total startup capital** for a 2,200–2,800 sq ft buildout, with **$310,000–$480,000** of that going to construction, equipment, and imaging (CBCT, digital pans, 4–6 operatories). Average Denver commercial dental lease rates sit at **$34–$48 per sq ft NNN** in 2026, and tenant improvement allowances typically cover only **$25–$45 per sq ft** — meaning most of your buildout is out-of-pocket.

Patient acquisition costs in the Denver metro average **$180–$340 per new patient** through paid channels, and the median general dentist production per day in the market is **$4,200–$5,600**. A well-run 4-operatory clinic should target **break-even at 14–18 months** and **$1.1M–$1.6M in annual collections by year two**. ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 Launch)

**Months 1–2: Entity & Financing**
1. File Colorado LLC + obtain **DORA dental licensure** (individual) and clinic registration.
2. Secure SBA 7(a) or equipment financing — Denver lenders (Vectra, FirstBank, Colorado Enterprise Fund) fund **80–90% LTV** on equipment.
3. Lock commercial lease with **TI allowance + free rent (3–6 months)** negotiated.

**Months 2–4: Buildout & Equipment**
4. Engage dental-specific contractor; permit timelines in Denver run **6–10 weeks**.
5. Order CBCT, chairs, and imaging — lead times **8–12 weeks** in 2026.
6. Negotiate PPO credentialing (Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife) — allow **90–120 days**.

**Months 4–5: Digital Launch**
7. Launch website + Google Business Profile **6 weeks before opening**.
8. Run geo-fenced Google Ads ($2,500–$4,000/mo) targeting "dentist near me" + emergency keywords.
9. Seed 25–40 reviews via early soft-launch patients.

**Month 6: Open & Optimize**
10. Target **35–50 new patients/month** in months 1–3, scaling to **80–120/month** by month 12.
11. Track cost-per-new-patient weekly; kill underperforming campaigns at **$350 CPA**.
