The Lucas County housing stock is **62% pre-1980**, meaning high replacement cycle demand and aging ductwork — a structural tailwind for new entrants. Q4 2026 is an **ideal launch window** because Ohio's heating season (Oct–Mar) drives emergency call volume up **40–55%** versus summer, and competitors are capacity-constrained — meaning a new operator with fast response times can capture spillover demand immediately. Expect a **$14,000–$22,000** first-quarter revenue ramp if you invest **$4,500–$9,000** in digital assets pre-launch.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Obtain Ohio HVAC contractor license (Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board) and EPA Section 608 certification.
2. Register LLC in Ohio, secure **$1M–$2M general liability** + commercial auto + workers' comp (~$3,200–$5,800/yr).
3. File Lucas County bond; set up business banking and a call-tracking number.

**Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–6)**
4. Build GBP with service-area radius covering Toledo, Sylvania, Maumee, Oregon, Perrysburg; post 2×/week.
5. Launch 5-page website with online booking, financing widget (e.g., Synchrony), and schema markup for LocalBusiness + Service.
6. Deploy programmatic city × service pages (e.g., "Furnace Repair Sylvania OH") — target **18–24 pages** at launch.

**Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 4–8)**
7. Google Ads: **$1,500–$2,500/mo** on "emergency furnace repair Toledo" + "AC replacement [city]" with call-only extensions.
8. Review engine: SMS request post-job, target **25+ reviews in 60 days** (critical for GBP ranking).
9. Partner with 3–5 local realtors and property managers for recurring maintenance contracts.

**Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)**
10. Add second technician when booked hours exceed **70% capacity**.
11. Introduce maintenance agreements ($14–$19/mo) to stabilize revenue.
12. Reinvest **10–15% of revenue** into content and paid search; expand to commercial quotes.
