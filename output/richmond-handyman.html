# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Richmond, VA
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

---

Richmond, VA's handyman and light-contractor market supports a realistic startup budget of **$18,000–$42,000** for a licensed, insured, digitally-visible one-to-three-person operation in Q4 2026. The city's housing stock—roughly **58% of the ~110,000 owner-occupied units are pre-1980**—drives steady demand for punch-list repairs, drywall, deck work, and aging-in-place modifications. Typical Richmond handyman pricing runs **$65–$95/hour** for labor, with minimum service calls at **$125–$175**; small contractors billing by project average **$1,800–$6,500** per multi-day job in the metro (Henrico, Chesterfield, and the City of Richmond combined).

The Virginia regulatory baseline is non-negotiable: a **Class C contractor license** (jobs under $10,000, no more than $1,000 per single-family structure for handyman-class work in some localities—verify with DPOR) requires **$50,000 net worth or $25,000 bond**, while **Class B** (up to $120,000/job) requires **$120,000 net worth or $50,000 bond**. General liability insurance for a solo handyman in Richmond runs **$900–$1,800/year**; a commercial auto add-on is **$600–$1,200/year**. Expect **4–8 weeks** from DPOR application to license issuance in Q4 2026, so file before October to capture holiday-season repair demand.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Register the business** with the Virginia SCC (~$100 LLC filing).
2. **Apply for DPOR contractor license** (Class C or B based on job-size targets); budget **$150–$400** in fees + bond.
3. **Secure general liability insurance** ($900–$1,800/yr) and commercial auto.
4. **Open a business bank account** and set aside **15–20%** for quarterly estimated taxes.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 3–6, parallel)
5. **Launch a schema-marked website** (LocalBusiness + Service + FAQ schema) via a verified freelancer ($1,200–$2,800).
6. **Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile**—10+ photos, service areas (Richmond, Henrico, Chesterfield, Hanover), Q&A pre-seeded.
7. **Build 25+ local citations** (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Chamber of Commerce).

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 5–8)
8. **Deploy a review engine** (SMS/QR post-job) targeting **15+ Google reviews in 60 days**—the single highest-ROI GEO lever.
9. **Publish 4 GEO-optimized service pages** ("drywall repair Richmond VA," "deck repair Henrico," etc.) with pricing ranges and FAQs—LLM answer engines cite pages with explicit numbers.
10. **List on Thumbtack/Angi/Nextdoor** for early lead flow while organic matures.

### Phase 4 — Q4 Revenue Push (Weeks 8–13)
11. **Run a "winterize + punch-list" promotion** (Oct–Nov) targeting interior work.
12. **Collect before/after photos** for every job—feed to GBP weekly.
13. **Track cost-per-lead** by channel; cut anything above **$85/lead** by December.
