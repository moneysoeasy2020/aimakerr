# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Las Vegas, NV
### 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

---

Las Vegas, NV is one of the most attractive 2026 Q4 markets for handyman and contractor launches because of its unique combination of high homeowner turnover, aging housing stock, and a tourism-driven rental economy. As of Q4 2026, Clark County has roughly **870,000 housing units**, with approximately **58% owner-occupied** and a median single-family home age of **24 years** — meaning a steady pipeline of repair, remodel, and turnover work. The Las Vegas metro also sees an estimated **55,000–65,000 residential resale transactions per year**, plus a short-term rental (STR) market of **~9,500 licensed units** that requires recurring maintenance. Realistic startup capital for a licensed, insured handyman operation is **$8,500–$22,000**, and typical first-year revenue for a solo operator runs **$95,000–$165,000** at blended rates of **$75–$125/hour** for handyman work and **$95–$185/hour** for licensed contractor specialties (electrical, plumbing, HVAC sub-tiers).

Pricing in Las Vegas in Q4 2026 clusters around clear benchmarks: a standard handyman service call is **$95–$150** (first hour), with additional hours at **$65–$95**; small drywall/plaster repairs run **$250–$650**; bathroom vanity and fixture swaps **$400–$1,200**; door and trim replacement **$180–$450 per opening**; and full kitchen/bath remodel management (as a general contractor) **$18,000–$65,000**. Nevada requires a **Nevada State Contractors Board (NSCB)** license for any project over **$1,000 in labor and materials combined**, and a Las Vegas business license plus Clark County contractor permit. Your realistic launch budget should reserve **$2,800–$4,200** for licensing, bonds, and insurance, **$1,500–$3,500** for tools and vehicle branding, and **$1,800–$4,500** for digital assets in the first 90 days.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Register LLC** with Nevada Secretary of State — **$425** (expedited) or **$75** standard.
2. **Obtain NSCB license** — required for jobs over **$1,000** labor+materials. Application, bond (**$1,000–$5,000** depending on classification), and fees total **$800–$1,800**.
3. **Las Vegas business license** + **Clark County contractor permit** — **$200–$600**.
4. **General liability insurance** — **$1,200–$2,800/year** for handyman; higher for GC.
5. **Workers' comp** if hiring — Nevada mandates it at first employee.
6. **EIN, business bank account, payment processor** (Stripe/Square).

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–6)
1. **Domain + hosting** — **$15–$40/mo**.
2. **Website** with service pages, location pages, and **LocalBusiness + Service schema** — freelance build **$900–$2,400**.
3. **Google Business Profile** — claim, verify, optimize with categories, services, photos, Q&A.
4. **Citation build** — Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB, Las Vegas Chamber — **$0–$400** via freelance.
5. **Review engine** — SMS/QR tool **$25–$75/mo**.
6. **Vehicle wrap + yard signs** — **$400–$1,200**.

### Phase 3 — GEO & Local SEO Launch (Weeks 4–10)
1. **Location content clusters** for high-value Las Vegas submarkets: **Summerlin, Henderson, Green Valley, Spring Valley, North Las Vegas, Boulder City, Enterprise, Paradise**.
2. **Service clusters**: drywall repair, fixture install, door/trim, tile, painting, deck repair, STR turnover, pre-sale punch lists.
3. **Structured data**: `LocalBusiness`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, `Review`, `AggregateRating`.
4. **FAQ blocks** answering generative-engine queries: *"How much does a handyman cost in Las Vegas?"*, *"Do I need a licensed contractor in Nevada?"*, *"Best handyman for STR turnover in Las Vegas?"*
5. **Consistent NAP** across all citations — critical for AI entity resolution.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)
1. **Google Local Services Ads** — pay-per-lead, **$18–$45/lead** in Las Vegas handyman vertical.
2. **Google Search Ads** — start **$500–$1,000/mo**, target high-intent keywords.
3. **Thumbtack/Angi** — **$300–$800/mo** lead spend; monitor cost-per-booked-job.
4. **STR partnership outreach** — target **9,500 licensed STR units**; offer turnover maintenance retainers **$150–$400/property/mo**.
5. **Realtor referral program** — **55K–65K annual resales**; offer punch-list packages.

### Phase 5 — Operations & Scale (Weeks 10–16)
1. **Job management software** — Jobber/Housecall Pro **$50–$150/mo**.
2. **Hire first helper** at **$22–$28/hr** once booked revenue exceeds **$18K/mo**.
3. **Add licensed specialty subs** (electrical, plumbing) for upsell margin.
4. **Track KPIs**: cost-per-lead, close rate, average ticket, review velocity, GEO citation share.
