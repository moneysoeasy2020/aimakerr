## 2026 Q4 Corpus Christi Market Analysis

Corpus Christi's handyman and home-repair market is entering Q4 2026 with strong tailwinds. The metro (Nueces, San Patricio, Aransas counties) carries roughly **420,000–430,000 residents** across ~155,000 households, with a **homeownership rate near 62%** — translating to roughly **95,000 owner-occupied units**. The median owner-occupied home value sits in the **$190,000–$215,000** range, and the housing stock skews old: a large share of single-family homes were built before 1990, and coastal humidity plus hurricane exposure (Harvey 2017, and recurring tropical systems) drive steady demand for rot repair, siding, drywall, decking, fencing, and storm-prep work.

Realistic pricing for a solo operator or 2–3 person crew in Corpus Christi: **$65–$95/hour** for general handyman labor; **$75–$125/hour** for licensed trades (electrical/plumbing sub-work); minimum service call **$95–$150**; and common flat-rate jobs — drywall patch **$150–$400**, interior door hang **$125–$250**, ceiling fan install **$150–$300**, fence section repair **$250–$600**, deck board replacement **$300–$900**, and hurricane shutter install **$400–$1,200**. A well-run startup targeting **$8,000–$15,000/month** in revenue by month 6 is a realistic benchmark; net margins of **35–50%** are achievable for owner-operated crews with disciplined job costing.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Financial (Weeks 1–2)**
1. Form LLC with Texas SOS (~$300); obtain EIN.
2. Secure general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto/tools rider ($1,200–$3,000/yr).
3. Open business checking; set up job-costing spreadsheet or Jobber/Housecall Pro ($49–$149/mo).
4. Confirm licensing posture: Texas does **not** require a statewide general contractor license, but electrical/plumbing/HVAC sub-work requires licensed subs — build a vetted sub list.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)**
5. Register domain + hosting; build 5–8 page site (Home, Services, Service Areas, About, Reviews, Contact) with LocalBusiness + Service schema.
6. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (categories, hours, service areas: Corpus Christi, Portland, Robstown, Flour Bluff, Padre Island, Calallen).
7. Clean NAP across 25–40 citations.
8. Launch programmatic service × neighborhood pages (e.g., "drywall repair Flour Bluff," "fence repair Calallen") — 20–50 pages.

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 3–8)**
9. Start Local Services Ads ($500–$1,500/mo test) — highest-intent leads in home services.
10. Add Google Ads search campaign on 15–25 high-intent keywords ($600–$2,000/mo).
11. Implement automated review requests; target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**.
12. Post weekly to GBP and Nextdoor; join Coastal Bend chambers and local contractor groups.

**Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 3–6)**
13. Track cost-per-lead and close rate weekly; kill underperforming keywords.
14. Add recurring-revenue offers (seasonal storm-prep inspections, gutter cleaning, deck sealing).
15. Reinvest 10–15% of revenue into SEO content and review velocity.
16. Target benchmarks: **$8k–$15k/mo revenue by month 6**, **CPL under $60**, **close rate 25–40%**.
