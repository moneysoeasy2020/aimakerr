## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Register LLC (CA Secretary of State, ~$70), obtain Bakersfield business license, apply for **IICRC certification** (CCT) and liability insurance ($1M/$2M, ~$1,200–$2,400/yr).
2. **Equipment:** Truck-mount or high-end portable extractor ($6K–$18K), spotting kit, air movers, chemicals ($800–$1,500 initial).
3. **Brand & domain:** Secure `.com` domain, logo, van wrap ($1,200–$2,800).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
4. **Website:** 5–7 pages + **15–25 programmatic service-area pages** (neighborhood × service).
5. **Google Business Profile:** Verify, add services, photos, service areas (Bakersfield, Oildale, Rosedale, Southwest, East Bakersfield).
6. **Citations:** 25–40 consistent NAP listings.
7. **Review engine:** Automate post-job review requests.

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 6–10)
8. **Google Local Services Ads** + targeted Google Ads ($800–$1,500/mo).
9. **Landlord/property-manager outreach:** 30–50 direct pitches to Kern County PM firms.
10. **Neighborhood GEO content:** Publish 4 answer-engine-optimized posts (pet odor, apartment turnover, allergen deep clean).

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)
11. **Recurring contracts:** Target 8–12 commercial accounts for baseline revenue.
12. **Review moat:** Aim for 50+ Google reviews by month 6.
13. **Reinvest:** Add second tech/van once 75+ jobs/month sustained.
