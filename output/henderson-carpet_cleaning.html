## 2026 Q4 Henderson Market Analysis

Henderson, NV (population ~340,000 across ~125,000 households, per 2024–2025 Census estimates) represents one of the most favorable carpet cleaning launch markets in the Mountain West. The city's median household income sits at roughly **$89,000**—well above the national average—and approximately **62% of housing units are owner-occupied**, with a large share built between 1995 and 2015 (Green Valley, Seven Hills, Anthem, Inspirada). That housing stock means wall-to-wall carpet is still dominant in bedrooms, stairs, and living areas, driving recurring demand. Henderson's dry desert climate (under 5 inches of rain annually) reduces mold-related emergency calls but increases dust and allergen-driven bookings—expect **peak demand in spring (March–May) and fall (September–November)**, with Q4 2026 landing in a moderate seasonal window ideal for launch.

Realistic pricing in Henderson: **$0.25–$0.45 per sq ft** for standard steam cleaning, **$120–$180 for a typical 3-bedroom home (800–1,200 sq ft of carpet)**, **$45–$65 per room** as an à la carte rate, and **$25–$40 add-ons** for pet enzyme treatment or stain protectant. Startup capital for a lean owner-operator (van-mounted or portable unit, chemicals, insurance, licensing, and initial marketing) runs **$12,000–$28,000**. Target a **$6,000–$9,000 monthly revenue** by month 6 to reach profitability against ~$2,800–$4,200 in monthly fixed costs.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Compliance & Setup (Weeks 1–2)**
1. Register LLC with Nevada Secretary of State (**~$425** filing + annual fees).
2. Obtain Henderson business license via the City of Henderson (**~$100–$300**).
3. Secure general liability insurance (**$600–$1,400/yr**) — required by most property managers.
4. Purchase/lease equipment: truck-mount (**$18k–$35k**) or portable (**$3k–$6k**); start portable to preserve capital.

**Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)**
5. Build a 6-page website: Home, Services, Pricing, Service Areas, About, Contact.
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, services, photos, service area = Henderson + Green Valley, Seven Hills, Anthem, Inspirada, MacDonald Ranch).
7. Deploy **10–15 programmatic location pages** (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in Green Valley Ranch, Henderson NV").
8. Set up Google Local Services Ads + a $25/day search campaign.

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)**
9. Launch review-generation workflow (target **15+ Google reviews in 60 days**—drives both map pack and AI citation trust).
10. Partner with 5–10 local property managers and real estate agents (Henderson turnover is high).
11. Publish 2 GEO-optimized blog posts/month (e.g., "How much does carpet cleaning cost in Henderson?").

**Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)**
12. Add a second van/technician once bookings exceed 60% capacity.
13. Introduce maintenance plans (**$99–$149 quarterly**) for recurring revenue.
14. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into ads; track cost-per-booked-job (target **under $45**).
