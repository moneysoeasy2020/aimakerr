## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Equipment (Weeks 1–3)
1. File **Detroit business license** + Wayne County DBA ($100–$175 total).
2. Secure **GL insurance** ($400–$900/yr) and optional bonding.
3. Buy equipment: used truck-mount ($12K–$22K) or pro portable ($3.5K–$6K), plus chemicals, hoses, air movers.
4. Set pricing: **$0.28–$0.38/sq ft residential**, room minimums $89–$129.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
5. Launch a 6-page GEO-optimized site with **LocalBusiness + Service schema**, Detroit ZIP service-area pages, and FAQ blocks structured for AI Overview citation.
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, services, 10+ photos, Q&A seeding).
7. Build **25–30 local citations** (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB, Detroit Chamber).
8. Activate **Google Local Services Ads** — typically $18–$45 per verified lead in this market.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8)
9. Launch **review-generation SMS flow** — target 15 Google reviews in 60 days (critical GEO trust signal).
10. Run **Nextdoor + Facebook local** campaigns in Royal Oak, Ferndale, Grosse Pointe ($300–$600/mo).
11. Partner with **3–5 Detroit-area property managers and realtors** for recurring commercial work.
12. Publish **2 GEO-targeted blog posts/month** ("carpet cleaning cost in [Detroit suburb] 2026") for programmatic local capture.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)
13. Reinvest 15–20% of revenue into **AI-search monitoring** (track citations in ChatGPT/Perplexity for "carpet cleaning Detroit").
14. Add second technician/truck once **22+ jobs/month** is sustained.
15. Layer in **upholstery, tile & grout, and water extraction** upsells (+$60–$120 avg ticket).
