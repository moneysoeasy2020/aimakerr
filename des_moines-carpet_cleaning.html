# Launching a Carpet Cleaning Business in Des Moines, IA
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition

Des Moines' carpet cleaning market is projected to generate **$38–$46 million in annual revenue** across roughly **115–135 active operators** in 2026, with the metro's 720,000+ residents and 310,000+ occupied housing units driving steady demand. A residential standard 3-room clean in Des Moines now prices at **$145–$220**, with average tickets landing near **$185**; commercial contracts (offices, property management turnovers) run **$0.18–$0.32 per square foot**. ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Register LLC in Iowa (~$50), obtain EIN, and secure general liability insurance ($600–$1,200/yr — required by most property managers).
2. Confirm equipment: portable extraction ($3,500–$7,000) or used truck-mount ($12,000–$22,000); budget $1,500 for chemicals, hoses, and spotting kits.
3. Stand up Google Business Profile with service-area settings covering Des Moines, West Des Moines, Ankeny, Urbandale, and Johnston.

**Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–8)**
4. Launch a lean website with **programmatic suburb + service pages** (e.g., "carpet cleaning Ankeny IA," "pet stain removal West Des Moines") — this is your GEO moat.
5. Deploy review-request automation; target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (review velocity is a top AI-citation signal).
6. Build 25–40 consistent local citations (NAP).

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)**
7. Open a Google Local Services Ads account (pay-per-lead, ~$18–$45/lead in this market) alongside a modest $600–$1,000/mo search budget.
8. Direct-outreach property managers in West Des Moines and Ankeny for turnover contracts — target **3–5 recurring accounts** before January.
9. Publish 2 GEO-structured blog/location pages weekly, each citing local specifics (neighborhoods, water hardness, seasonal ice-melt staining).

**Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–12)**
10. Add a second van once monthly gross exceeds $14,000.
11. Layer commercial janitorial cross-sells; push average ticket via protection and pet-treatment upsells.
12. Refresh programmatic pages quarterly with 2027 pricing and local data to maintain AI-engine citations.

**Q4 2026 timing note:** Launch digital assets *now* to capture pre-holiday booking demand, but underwrite the January–February trough with commercial contracts signed in Q4. That single sequencing decision separates Des Moines operators who survive year one from those who don't.
