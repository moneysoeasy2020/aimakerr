## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Q4 2026, Months 1–3)
1. **Secure financing** — SBA 7(a) loans (up to $5M, 10-year terms) plus equipment leasing; Texas dental-specific lenders (Bank of America Practice Solutions, Live Oak Bank) typically approve in 45–75 days.
2. **Site selection** — Target 2,000–2,800 sq ft in 79424/79423 with 25+ parking spots and visibility from a major arterial (Slide Rd, Milwaukee Ave, or 82nd St). Lease rates: **$22–$34/sq ft NNN**.
3. **Entity + licensing** — Texas DPS dental license, TSBDE registration, DBA filing, malpractice insurance (Texas requires no state-level malpractice mandate, but carriers like The Doctors Company quote $3,200–$6,800/yr for a new solo GP).
4. **Credentialing** — Begin Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Guardian, and BCBS TX applications immediately (90–150 day timelines).

### Phase 2 — Build & Digital Foundation (Months 2–5)
5. **Build-out** — 10–16 weeks; budget $220,000–$500,000 depending on chair count and imaging suite.
6. **Equipment procurement** — Chairs ($28,000–$45,000 each), CBCT ($85,000–$140,000), intraoral scanners ($18,000–$32,000), autoclaves, and cabinetry.
7. **Digital asset deployment** — Website, Google Business Profile, programmatic local SEO pages, booking integration, and review system live **before** opening day.
8. **Hire team** — 1 hygienist ($38–$46/hr in Lubbock), 1–2 dental assistants ($18–$24/hr), 1 front-office coordinator ($19–$26/hr).

### Phase 3 — Launch & Patient Acquisition (Months 5–9)
9. **Soft launch** — 4–6 weeks of internal marketing to friends, family, and referral partners (physicians, pediatricians, oral surgeons).
10. **Paid acquisition ramp** — Google Ads + Meta local awareness; target 60–120 new patients/month by Month 9.
11. **Review flywheel** — Automated post-visit review requests; aim for 4.8+ stars and 75+ reviews by Month 12.
12. **Insurance panel activation** — Confirm all five major carriers are live; track in-network vs. out-of-network mix monthly.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 9–12)
13. **Programmatic SEO expansion** — Add 20–40 procedure × neighborhood pages ("dental implants in Southwest Lubbock," "emergency dentist near Texas Tech").
14. **Recall + reactivation systems** — Automated 6-month recall, unscheduled treatment follow-up, and referral incentives.
15. **KPI review** — Track new patients/month, cost per new patient, active patient count, production per chair, and EBITDA margin. Target **1,400+ active patients and 20%+ EBITDA by Month 12**.
