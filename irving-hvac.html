## Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts

This is the most consequential cost decision for a new Irving HVAC operator.

### Local Brick-and-Mortar Agency (DFW-based)
- **Monthly retainer:** $2,500–$6,500
- **Setup/onboarding:** $1,500–$4,000
- **Contract terms:** 6–12 months typical; early termination fees common
- **Overhead baked in:** office lease, account managers, sales staff
- **Speed:** Slower iteration; ticket-based change requests
- **Accountability:** Brand reputation on the line, but you're often a mid-tier account
- **Best for:** Established operators with $3K+/mo marketing budgets and zero time to manage vendors

### Verified Freelance Experts (vetted individually)
- **Monthly retainer:** $900–$2,400 (GBP + local SEO specialist)
- **Project-based setup:** $600–$2,500
- **Contract terms:** Month-to-month typical; 30-day notice
- **Overhead:** Near-zero; you pay for execution
- **Speed:** Direct communication, same-day edits common
- **Accountability:** Verify via case studies, call references, check Upwork/Clutch reviews, confirm they've ranked HVAC clients in DFW specifically
- **Best for:** Lean startups, sub-$3K/mo budgets, operators willing to manage 1–2 contractors directly

**Verdict for a 2026 Q4 Irving launch:** A **verified freelance GBP/local-SEO specialist ($1,200–$1,800/mo)** plus a **one-time website build ($2,400–$4,000)** delivers 80–90% of agency output at **40–55% of the cost**. Reserve brick-and-mortar agencies for when monthly revenue exceeds ~$60K and you need full-funnel management. Always verify credentials: ask for **2–3 HVAC-specific case studies with verifiable GBP ranking screenshots**, confirm they understand TDLR/EPA compliance language, and insist on **client-owned assets** (you own the GBP, site, and citations — never the agency).

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–4)
1. Register LLC with Texas Secretary of State (**$300**).
2. Obtain **TDLR A/C Contractor license** (Class A/B) — study + exam + insurance certificate (**$1,200–$2,400** total).
3. Secure **general liability + commercial auto insurance** (**$1,800–$3,600/yr**).
4. EPA 608 certification for all techs (**$150–$400/person**).
5. Open business banking + set up payment processing (Stripe/Square, ~2.6–2.9% + $0.10).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–6)
6. Register domain + build 5–7 page mobile-first site with **LocalBusiness + Service schema**.
7. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, hours, photos, Q&A).
8. Build **8–12 service-area landing pages** targeting "Irving + [service]" queries (AC repair, furnace install, RTU maintenance, etc.).
9. Submit to **25–40 local citations** (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, DFW directories) — NAP consistency is non-negotiable.
10. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** (background-check verified) at **$500–$1,000/mo** to generate leads while organic ranks.

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation & Reviews (Weeks 4–12)
11. Implement automated **review request system** (post-job SMS/email) — target **4.7★+ and 25+ reviews within 90 days**.
12. Publish **6–10 service guides** optimized for generative engine citation (clear answers, local stats, FAQ schema).
13. Set up call tracking + CRM (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber — **$150–$400/mo**).

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Month 3+)
14. Track **cost-per-lead by channel**; kill underperformers, double down on GBP + LSA.
15. Add **property-management contracts** (Irving's 58% renter market) for recurring revenue.
16. Expand service-area pages into **Las Colinas, Valley Ranch, Coppell, Grand Prairie** once Irving ranks.
17. Re-audit GBP and citations **quarterly** — local rankings decay without maintenance.
