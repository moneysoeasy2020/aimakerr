## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with **Indiana Secretary of State** ($95 online).
2. Obtain **Fort Wayne/Allen County contractor registration** through the city's Building Department.
3. Secure **general liability insurance** ($1M/$2M) — expect **$85–$165/month** for a solo handyman.
4. Get an **EIN** (free, IRS.gov) and open a business bank account.
5. Confirm Indiana does **not** require a statewide handyman license for jobs under $1,000 in labor + materials — but electrical, plumbing, and HVAC work require licensed subcontractors.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4)
1. Buy domain + hosting (**$15–$35/month**).
2. Deploy a 6-page website: Home, Services, Service Areas, About, Reviews, Contact.
3. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — this is your #1 lead source in Fort Wayne.
4. Set up a **review request SMS system** (target: 15 reviews in 60 days).
5. Build **15 programmatic service-area pages** targeting Fort Wayne neighborhoods (Aboite, Waynedale, Pine Valley, Southwest, New Haven, Huntertown, Leo-Cedarville, etc.).

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 3–8)
1. Launch **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$32–$58/lead in Fort Wayne).
2. Add **Google Ads** on 8–12 high-intent keywords ("handyman Fort Wayne," "drywall repair Fort Wayne," "gutter cleaning Fort Wayne").
3. Publish **2 blog posts/week** targeting long-tail GEO queries ("how much does drywall repair cost in Fort Wayne 2026").
4. Set up **instant-response automation** (respond to every lead in under 5 minutes via SMS).

### Phase 4 — Scale & Retention (Months 3–6)
1. Add **seasonal service bundles** (fall gutter + weatherization, spring deck repair).
2. Introduce **maintenance plans** ($49–$99/month recurring) — highest-margin revenue in this category.
3. Build **referral partnerships** with 5–8 local realtors and property managers.
4. Track **cost-per-booked-job** monthly; target under **$85** by month 6.
