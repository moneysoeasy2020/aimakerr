# Launching a Carpet Cleaning Business in Reno, NV
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Reno's carpet cleaning market in Q4 2026 supports roughly **38–45 active residential/commercial operators** serving a metro population of ~510,000 across Washoe County, with average residential job tickets of **$185–$320** for 3–5 rooms and **$0.28–$0.42 per sq ft** for commercial work. Startup capital for a lean owner-operator launch runs **$8,500–$22,000** (truck-mount used vs. new portable), while a fully equipped van-based operation with hot-water extraction runs **$28,000–$45,000**. First-year revenue targets for a solo operator are realistic at **$95,000–$165,000** with a 55–65% gross margin after chemicals, fuel, and labor.

Reno's specific demand drivers matter more than national averages: **short-term rental (STR) turnover cleaning** around Midtown, the University of Nevada Reno (UNR) student housing cycle, and **wildfire smoke remediation** cycles (Jul–Oct) create seasonal spikes that a carpet cleaner can monetize with add-on services like ozone treatment ($75–$150/room) and pet odor enzyme treatment ($45–$90). The average Reno household spends **$210–$480/year** on carpet care, and commercial accounts (casinos support services, medical offices, property managers) represent **40–55% of recurring revenue** for established operators.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Register business**: Nevada LLC ($425 state fee), EIN, business bank account.
2. **License & insure**: NS Contractors Board license ($300–$500), general liability ($600–$1,200/yr), commercial auto.
3. **Equipment**: Used truck-mount ($12k–$22k) or new portable ($4k–$8k); chemicals, hoses, wand, air mover, dehumidifier.
4. **Brand & digital**: Logo, van wrap, 5-page website, Google Business Profile (verify address — use home or virtual office compliant with GBP rules).

### Phase 2 — Local Visibility (Weeks 3–8)
5. **GBP optimization**: Categories, services, photos (10+), Q&A, review requests.
6. **Citations**: Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, BBB, Nextdoor, Reno-Sparks Chamber.
7. **Programmatic SEO**: Build 15–25 landing pages (e.g., "carpet cleaning [ZIP]" for 89501–89523, "pet odor removal Reno", "STR turnover cleaning Midtown").
8. **Google Ads**: $25–$40/day geo-targeted to Reno-Sparks; negative keywords critical.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–16)
9. **Property manager outreach**: Target 20 PM firms; offer trial pricing for first 5 units.
10. **STR partnerships**: Midtown/Downtown hosts — bundle turnover cleaning.
11. **Review engine**: SMS automation post-job; target 50+ reviews by month 6.
12. **Referral program**: $25 credit per referral.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Retention (Months 5–12)
13. **Commercial contracts**: Medical offices, casinos support services, retail.
14. **Add-on services**: Ozone, pet enzyme, upholstery, tile/grout ($0.75–$1.25/sq ft).
15. **Hire first tech** at ~$22–$28/hr when booked 70%+ capacity.
16. **Track KPIs**: CAC, LTV, repeat rate, job ticket, route density.
