Glendale's HVAC market is one of the most competitive in the West Valley, and the numbers matter. ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Licensing & Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. **Arizona ROC licensing:** Obtain **Residential/Commercial HVAC (R-39) contractor license**. Exam + application + bond runs **$1,200–$2,400**.
2. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto + workers' comp = **$3,800–$7,200/year**.
3. **Entity & tax:** AZ LLC ($85), EIN (free), AZ Transaction Privilege Tax license, Glendale business license (~$150–$400).
4. **EPA 608 certification** for refrigerant handling (required for all techs).

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 3–8)
5. **Domain + hosting** ($180–$400/yr), **HVAC LocalBusiness schema**, and **service-area pages** for Glendale + Peoria, Sun City, Youngtown, and North Phoenix.
6. **Google Business Profile**: verified, 10+ categories, service areas, Q&A seeded, 15+ photos.
7. **Programmatic geo-pages**: 20–40 pages targeting "AC repair [neighborhood]" and "HVAC installation [ZIP]" — Glendale ZIPs 85301–85310.
8. **Review engine**: automated SMS review requests post-service (target **40 reviews in 6 months**).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)
9. **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$35–$85/lead in Glendale HVAC) + **Google Ads** ($1,800–$3,200/mo peak).
10. **PMA (maintenance agreement) push**: $189–$279/year plans to smooth off-season revenue.
11. **Rebate positioning**: APS/SRP heat-pump rebate landing page — high-converting in Q4.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Retention (Month 4+)
12. **Dispatch software** (Jobber/ServiceTitan), **call tracking**, and **review-to-revenue dashboard**.
13. **Commercial PMA outreach** to Westgate/59th Ave retail and industrial parks.
14. **Quarterly GEO audit**: refresh schema, GBP posts, and geo-page content for 2027 Q1.
