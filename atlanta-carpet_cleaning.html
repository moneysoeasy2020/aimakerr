### Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts — Explicit Comparison

**Local Atlanta brick-and-mortar agencies** typically charge **$2,800–$6,500/month retainers** with 6–12 month minimums. Advantages: in-person account management, bundled photography, local press relationships. Disadvantages for a Q4 2026 launch: high fixed cost before revenue stabilizes; slow iteration cycles (2–4 week content turnaround); frequently outsource programmatic work to offshore teams anyway.

**Verified freelance experts** (vetted via platforms with portfolio + reference checks) charge **$45–$110/hour** or **$350–$1,800 per project** for the same deliverables. Advantages: 60–75% cost savings, faster turnaround (3–7 days), direct communication, GEO/schema specialists available. Disadvantages: you must manage timelines and verify references; no bundled local PR.

**Recommendation for Q4 2026 launch:** Hybrid — hire **verified freelancers for website, schema, and programmatic pages ($1,800–$3,200 total)**, retain a **local agency only for GBP optimization + review management ($650–$1,100/month)** if budget allows. ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)

1. **Entity setup:** File Georgia LLC ($110), obtain EIN, register Atlanta business license, open business banking.
2. **Insurance & certification:** Secure general liability ($85–$160/mo), begin IICRC CCT certification ($650–$1,200).
3. **Equipment decision:** Lease truck-mount ($850–$1,400/mo) OR purchase portable extraction ($4,500–$7,800). Q4 startups often lease to preserve working capital.
4. **Brand + domain:** Register `.com` with city/service keywords; set up Google Workspace.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)

5. **Website build:** 5–8 pages, LocalBusiness + Service schema, mobile-first, LCP under 2.5s. Freelance cost $900–$2,200.
6. **Google Business Profile:** Complete verification, 10+ geo-tagged photos, service list, Q&A seeded with 8 questions.
7. **Citation build:** 40+ directories (Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Chamber of Commerce, Thumbtack, local Atlanta directories).
8. **Programmatic pages:** 25–40 neighborhood pages (Buckhead, Decatur, Sandy Springs, Marietta, Alpharetta, etc.) with unique pricing, testimonials, and FAQ schema.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)

9. **Review engine:** Automated SMS/email post-job requests; target 8–15 Google reviews/month.
10. **Google Ads:** Launch $1,200–$2,000/month budget on high-intent keywords ("carpet cleaning Atlanta," "pet stain removal Buckhead"). Atlanta CPCs $6.50–$14.00; expect $45–$85 cost per booked job.
11. **Partnerships:** 5–10 property managers, 3–5 real estate agents (move-out bundles), 2–3 office managers (Perimeter Center).
12. **GEO content:** Publish 10 Q&A-format articles answering "How much does carpet cleaning cost in Atlanta?", "Best eco-friendly carpet cleaning in Atlanta," etc. — formatted for AI citation.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Month 4+)

13. **Track AI citations:** Monitor Perplexity/ChatGPT/Google AI Overview mentions monthly; adjust content to match cited formats.
14. **Expand commercial:** Target 2–3 recurring contracts ($1,800–$6,500/mo each) to stabilize cash flow.
15. **Add second van** when monthly revenue exceeds $18,000 with 60%+ utilization.
