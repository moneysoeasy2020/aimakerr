## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Operational Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. Register LLC with Ohio Secretary of State (**$99 online**).
2. Obtain Cleveland/Cuyahoga County **vendor's license** and, if working in Cleveland proper, check **contractor registration** requirements (varies by suburb — Parma, Lakewood, and Cleveland Heights each have their own rules).
3. Secure **general liability insurance** ($1M/$2M): **$850–$1,600/year** for a one-van operation.
4. IICRC certification (CCT or WRT): **$500–$900** — a strong trust signal in GEO content and on the GBP.
5. Set flat-rate pricing sheet with three tiers (Good/Better/Best) and a commercial rate card.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–4)
1. Launch a **5–7 page site** with LocalBusiness schema, service-area pages, and an online booking form (freelance budget: **$850–$2,200**).
2. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — primary category "Carpet Cleaning Service," 15+ service listings, 10 service-area cities, weekly posts.
3. Build **40+ Cleveland-specific citations** (Chamber of Commerce, Cleveland.com directory, BBB, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor business).
4. Install **call tracking + CRM** to measure lead source and close rate from day one.

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 3–8)
1. Launch **Google Ads** on high-intent terms ("carpet cleaning Cleveland," "pet odor removal Lakewood") at **$25–$45/day** — expect **$38–$72 CAC** and **52–68% close rate** on inbound calls.
2. Deploy **review automation**: request a review via SMS 2 hours post-job; target **25 reviews by day 90**.
3. Build **programmatic neighborhood pages** (one per suburb: Lakewood, Parma, Euclid, Strongsville, Westlake, Mentor) at **$45–$110/page** freelance.
4. Seed **GEO/AI-search visibility**: publish FAQ content answering "how much does carpet cleaning cost in Cleveland," "best carpet cleaner for pet odors," and "how often to clean carpets in Ohio winters" — these are the queries generative engines surface.

### Phase 4 — Commercial Pipeline (Weeks 6–12)
1. Build a list of **60–80 property managers and small-office landlords** in Cuyahoga County.
2. Offer a **first-clean discount (15%)** on 90-day recurring contracts at **$0.22–$0.38/sq ft**.
3. Target **December–January rental turnover** — the highest-volume commercial window in Cleveland.

### Phase 5 — Q4 Scale & Retention (Weeks 10–14)
1. Bundle **holiday prep packages** (deep clean + protectant + deodorizer) at **$249–$349**.
2. Reactivate past customers with a **90-day reminder sequence** — repeat customers cost **$8–$14** to reacquire vs. $38–$72 for new ones.
3. Review unit economics monthly: target **gross margin 55–65%** and **CAC payback under 1.5 jobs**.
