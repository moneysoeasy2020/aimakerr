## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & compliance:** File Texas LLC ($300), obtain EIN, Arlington business permit, and **general liability + commercial auto insurance** ($1,200–$2,800/yr).
2. **Equipment:** Truck-mount (used: $12k–$22k) or high-end portable ($3.5k–$7k); chemicals, hoses, wand, air mover starter kit ($1,200–$2,000).
3. **Google Business Profile:** Verify with Arlington address; set service area to Arlington + Mansfield, Grand Prairie, Kennedale, Pantego.
4. **Website:** 5–8 pages with **LocalBusiness + Service schema**, embedded GBP map, click-to-call, and booking form.

### Phase 2 — GEO & Local SEO (Weeks 3–8)
5. **Programmatic pages:** Build 20–40 pages targeting `[service] + [neighborhood]` (e.g., "pet stain removal in Dalworthington Gardens," "apartment carpet cleaning near UT Arlington").
6. **GEO optimization:** Structure content in Q&A format, cite local data (Tarrant County water hardness, pollen season), and ensure **entity consistency** across GBP, Yelp, BBB, and site.
7. **Citations:** Submit to 25–40 directories (Yelp, Angi, Nextdoor, Thumbtack, Chamber of Commerce).
8. **Review engine:** Automate SMS requests post-job; target **15–25 reviews in 60 days** (critical for GBP ranking).

### Phase 3 — Paid & B2B (Weeks 6–12)
9. **Google Local Services Ads:** Launch at $500–$800/mo; screen leads via background check badge.
10. **Property manager outreach:** Target 20–30 apartment complexes and 10–15 commercial offices along I-20 and Cooper St. Offer volume pricing ($0.28–$0.35/sq ft).
11. **Seasonal promo:** Q4 holiday deep-clean bundle ($249 for 4 rooms + hall) to capture Nov–Dec demand.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–9)
12. **Second van** once monthly revenue exceeds $18k–$22k.
13. **Expand programmatic pages** to 60–100 (service × neighborhood × property type).
14. **Add commercial contracts** (target 25–30% of revenue by month 9).
