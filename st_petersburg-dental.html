## 2026 Q4 St. Petersburg Market Analysis

### Demand Signals
- **Population growth**: St. Pete added ~**4,200 residents** year-over-year (2025→2026); Pinellas County is now the **7th most populous county in Florida**.
- **Age skew**: **22.8% of St. Pete residents are 65+** (vs. 20.9% statewide)—drives high demand for restorative, implant, and denture services.
- **Insurance mix**: ~**58% commercially insured**, **34% Medicare/Medicare Advantage**, **8% Medicaid/uninsured**—shape your payer strategy accordingly.
- **Competitive density by ZIP**: 33701/33705 (downtown) is **saturated** (1 practice per ~1,100 residents); 33710/33712 (west/south St. Pete) is **underserved** at 1 per ~2,400 residents—prioritize these for site selection.

### Regulatory & Cost Environment (Q4 2026)
- **FL AHCA dental clinic licensure**: $250–$450 filing + inspection; **45–75 day** approval cycle.
- **Pinellas County commercial permit fees**: $8K–$18K for a 4-op build-out.
- **Dental waste/amalgam separator compliance**: $1,200–$2,800 one-time + $400/yr service.
- **Malpractice (general dentist, 1-2 yrs experience)**: $4,800–$9,200/yr in FL (higher than national avg due to FL sovereign immunity statutes).

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity formation**: FL LLC ($125) + EIN (free) + business bank account.
2. **Site selection**: Target 33710/33712 ZIPs; negotiate **TI allowance of $35–$65/sq ft**.
3. **Financing**: SBA 7(a) at 2026 Q4 rates (~**8.5–10.5%**), $350K–$650K typical for de novo dental.
4. **AHCA licensure application** (file by Month 2 to hit 75-day window).

### Phase 2 — Build-Out & Hiring (Months 4–8)
5. **Build-out**: 4 ops, 1,800–2,200 sq ft; budget **$340K–$520K** all-in.
6. **Equipment**: Lease new (DentalEZ, A-dec) or buy refurbished (Henry Schein Outlet, Atlas Resell).
7. **Hire**: 1 hygienist ($38–$46/hr St. Pete rate), 2 dental assistants ($19–$24/hr), 1 front office ($20–$25/hr).
8. **Payer contracting**: Credential with **Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Florida Blue**—allow 90–120 days.

### Phase 3 — Digital Launch (Months 6–9)
9. **Website + GBP live 60 days pre-opening** (Google requires address verification).
10. **GEO optimization**: Structured data (Dentist schema), FAQ blocks, local citation consistency across 40+ directories.
11. **Reputation seeding**: Target **25+ Google reviews in first 90 days** (industry benchmark for ranking).

### Phase 4 — Growth (Months 9–18)
12. **Paid search**: $3K–$6K/month on high-intent terms ("dentist near me St. Petersburg," "emergency dentist 33710").
13. **Recall/reactivation campaigns**: Target **35% recall rate** by Month 12.
14. **Add service lines**: Implants (Month 12–15), ortho/aligners (Month 15–18) once hygiene base exceeds **400 active patients**.
