## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Register LLC with Oklahoma Secretary of State (~$100). Obtain OKC business license, sales tax permit (Oklahoma Tax Commission), and general liability + equipment insurance (~$1,200–$2,400/yr).
2. **Equipment:** Used van-mounted unit ($8,000–$16,000), portable backup, chemicals, hoses, air movers. Total equipment budget: $11,000–$19,000.
3. **Banking & bookkeeping:** Business account + QuickBooks or Wave.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
4. **Website:** 5–7 pages with service pages, pricing transparency, and schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service).
5. **Google Business Profile:** Complete every field, add 10+ photos, set service areas to your 3–5 target zips.
6. **Citations:** Consistent NAP across 25+ directories (Yelp, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor, Thumbtack).
7. **Programmatic pages:** Build 15–30 service-area pages (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in Edmond, OK") with unique local data — this is your GEO moat.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–8)
8. **Review engine:** Automated post-job SMS requesting Google reviews; target 25 reviews in 90 days.
9. **Local partnerships:** Realtors, property managers, apartment complexes — offer a 10% referral fee or co-branded move-out special.
10. **Paid test:** $400–$800 Google Local Services Ads trial; measure cost-per-booked-job (target under $45).

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)
11. **Add second technician/van** once you sustain 16+ jobs/week for 6 weeks.
12. **Commercial outreach:** Q4 deep-clean proposals to offices, clinics, churches (send by Sept 15).
13. **GEO content refresh:** Publish quarterly local-metric articles and update service-area pages with fresh pricing data.

### Phase 5 — Optimization (Ongoing)
14. Track **cost-per-lead, average ticket, review velocity, and AI-citation appearances** (search your brand in ChatGPT/Perplexity monthly).
15. Reinvest 8–12% of gross revenue into digital assets and review generation.
