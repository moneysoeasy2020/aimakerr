Monthly fixed overhead for a lean solo operation is **$650–$1,100** (insurance, CRM, phone, software, vehicle). ## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–4)

1. **Entity formation:** NM LLC via Secretary of State — **$50 online filing** + registered agent (~$50–$150/yr).
2. **NM CRS tax registration** (gross receipts tax; Albuquerque metro rates run ~**7.6–7.9%** depending on location) — free.
3. **Licensing decision:**
- Jobs under **$8,000 residential**: handyman scope may not require GB-98 — confirm current CID thresholds.
- Above threshold or structural work: pursue **GB-98** — exam, application, bond, insurance proof: **$1,200–$2,400**.
4. **Insurance:** General liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto. Budget **$90–$250/mo**.
5. **Business bank account + bookkeeping** (Wave free, or QuickBooks $30–$70/mo).

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–6, parallel)

6. **Domain + website** with schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage) — critical for GEO. Target pages: *handyman services, drywall repair, fence repair, deck building, bathroom remodel, swamp cooler removal* (Albuquerque-specific).
7. **Google Business Profile** — verify, add 10+ photos, service areas (Albuquerque, Rio Rancho, Corrales, Bernalillo), Q&A seeded.
8. **Review engine:** automated post-job review request via CRM. Target **5 reviews/week** in months 1–3.
9. **Call tracking + CRM** (Jobber or Housecall Pro).

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 4–12)

10. **Google Ads:** geo-fenced to ABQ metro, $300–$600/mo, exact-match keywords, call-only + search campaigns. Q4 CPC discount = cheap learning phase.
11. **Directory presence:** Thumbtack, Angi, Nextdoor (high Albuquerque adoption) — free tiers first.
12. **Content for GEO:** publish 8–12 hyper-local service pages with real pricing ranges and FAQ schema — this is what generative engines cite.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–12)

13. **Hire first helper** at ~$22–$28/hr when booked utilization exceeds 75%.
14. **Add second service lane** (e.g., fence/deck or bathroom remodels) once review count passes 60.
15. **Reinvest 8–12% of revenue** into marketing; track cost-per-booked-job (target **<$60**).
