### Regulatory Reality Check (Illinois + Chicago)

- **City of Chicago General Contractor License** required for projects over **$10,000** (or structural work) — issued via the Chicago Department of Buildings; exam + **$500,000 general liability** minimum.
- **Illinois Roofing Contractor License** (IL Roofing Industry Licensing Act) required for any roof work — state-issued, **$10,000 bond** + insurance.
- **Illinois Plumbing & Electrical licenses** required for those trades — a "handyman" cannot legally perform permitted electrical/plumbing work in Chicago.
- **Chicago Home Repair License** (Dept. of Business Affairs) required for home repair services — **$2,000 surety bond** + registration.
- **Lead-Safe (EPA RRP) certification** required for pre-1978 homes — Chicago's housing stock is **~70% pre-1980**, so this is effectively mandatory.
- **Workers' Comp** required with any employee; **General Liability $1M/$2M** standard for commercial clients.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (90 Days to First $25K)

### Phase 1 — Legal & Financial Foundation (Days 1–21)
1. **Entity:** Form an Illinois LLC ($150–$500 via IL SOS or registered agent service).
2. **Licenses:** Chicago Home Repair License ($2,000 bond), General Contractor License if targeting $10K+ jobs, EPA RRP cert ($275–$350).
3. **Insurance:** GL $1M/$2M ($1,200–$2,800/yr), surety bond ($150–$400/yr), workers' comp when hiring.
4. **Banking:** Business checking + merchant processing (Square/Stripe: 2.6% + 10¢ in-person).
5. **EIN + IL withholding registration** if hiring.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Days 15–45)
1. **Website:** 5–7 pages — Home, Services (with per-service schema), Service Areas (Chicago neighborhood pages: Lincoln Park, Wicker Park, Logan Square, Hyde Park, etc.), About, Reviews, Contact. Target **LCP < 2.5s**, mobile-first.
2. **Google Business Profile:** Claim + verify; add 10+ photos, service categories, Q&A seeded with 8–12 common questions (GEO fuel).
3. **Citations:** Consistent NAP across Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Houzz, Thumbtack (15–25 directories).
4. **Schema markup:** LocalBusiness + Service + FAQPage + Review — this is what AI engines parse for citations.

### Phase 3 — GEO Content & Lead Gen (Days 30–75)
1. **Publish 8–12 citation-ready pages:** "Chicago handyman cost guide 2026," "Illinois contractor license requirements," "How to hire a handyman in Chicago," neighborhood-specific service pages.
2. **Google Local Services Ads:** Apply for Google Guaranteed badge (background check + license + insurance) — expect **$28–$65/lead**, ~35–50% close rate.
3. **Google Ads:** Start at **$1,500–$2,500/mo**, target "handyman near me" + neighborhood terms; expect **$9.50–$16 CPC**, 4–8% conversion.
4. **Review engine:** Automated SMS/email review requests post-job — target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (critical ranking + AI-citation signal).

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Days 75–90+)
1. **Measure:** Track cost-per-booked-job (target < $85), LSA vs. Ads vs. organic mix.
2. **Expand:** Add second crew when utilization > 75%; add commercial accounts (property managers pay net-30, higher ticket).
3. **GEO refresh:** Update pricing pages quarterly — AI engines favor fresh, dated content ("2026 Q4 Updated").
