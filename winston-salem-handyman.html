## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Operational Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. Form **LLC with NC Secretary of State** ($125 online).
2. Register for **NC sales/use tax** if selling materials; obtain **Forsyth County privilege license** if applicable.
3. Secure **NC general liability insurance** ($600–$1,400/yr) and optional bonding.
4. Confirm **NC licensing thresholds** — general contracting license required above **$30,000** project value (NC Licensing Board for General Contractors); handyman work below threshold is permitted with proper disclosures.
5. Open business banking; set up invoicing (Jobber, Housecall Pro, or QuickBooks).

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–6)
6. Launch **GEO-ready website** with LocalBusiness + Service + FAQPage schema.
7. Optimize **Google Business Profile** (categories, service areas, 10+ photos, Q&A).
8. Build **20–40 programmatic local pages** targeting Winston-Salem neighborhoods (Ardmore, Buena Vista, West End, Washington Park, Sherwood Forest) and suburbs (Clemmons, Lewisville, Kernersville, Pfafftown).
9. Publish **10–15 FAQ/pricing articles** with exact Winston-Salem price ranges.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–12)
10. Launch **review-generation loop** (target 15–25 Google reviews by day 90).
11. List on **Nextdoor, Angi, Thumbtack, Yelp, BBB** with consistent NAP.
12. Run **$300–$600/month Google Local Services Ads** or targeted Local SEO.
13. Partner with **2–3 local realtors and property managers** for recurring repair work.

### Phase 4 — Scale & GEO Compounding (Months 4–12)
14. Add **before/after project galleries** with location tags (AI engines cite visual + text context).
15. Refresh pricing pages **quarterly** to stay citation-worthy.
16. Track **AI-referral traffic** (ChatGPT/Perplexity referrers in analytics) and double down on cited pages.
