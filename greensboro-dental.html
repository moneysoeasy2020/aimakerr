## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-launch (Months 1–3)
1. **Entity and licensure:** File NC PLLC with NC Secretary of State ($125), obtain NC State Board of Dental Examiners license, DEA registration, NPI, and malpractice coverage ($2,800–$5,500/yr).
2. **Site selection:** Target 2,200–2,600 sq ft in Lawndale or Battleground corridor; negotiate 7–10 year term with TI allowance of $35–$60/sq ft.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) or bank practice loan at 7.5–9.5% for equipment + build-out; target 10–15% owner equity.
4. **Brand + domain:** Secure .com domain, logo, and HIPAA-compliant website brief.

### Phase 2 — Build-out and digital foundation (Months 3–6)
5. **Construction:** 12–16 week build-out; budget $110–$185/sq ft.
6. **Equipment:** Order chairs, imaging (3D CBCT optional at $65,000–$110,000), sterilization; lead time 8–12 weeks.
7. **Website + GBP:** Launch site 6 weeks pre-opening; verify Google Business Profile with NAP consistency across 40–60 citations.
8. **Insurance credentialing:** Begin 90–120 days pre-opening with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, BCBS NC, and Medicaid (if applicable).

### Phase 3 — Pre-opening marketing (Months 5–7)
9. **Google Ads + LSA:** Launch LSA 4 weeks pre-opening ($1,500–$3,000/mo); search ads at $2,500–$4,500/mo.
10. **Local outreach:** Greensboro Chamber of Commerce, Guilford County Schools, UNCG/A&T/Greensboro College HR benefits, and 15–25 primary care referral relationships.
11. **Review engine:** Automate post-visit review requests; target 40+ Google reviews by month 3 of operation.

### Phase 4 — Opening and stabilization (Months 7–12)
12. **Soft opening:** 4–6 weeks at reduced schedule to stabilize ops.
13. **Grand opening event:** $3,000–$6,000 budget; invite referring physicians and community leaders.
14. **KPI review (monthly):** New patients (target 45–65/mo by month 6), cost-per-new-patient (<$180), show rate (>85%), production per operatory ($28,000–$42,000/mo).

### Phase 5 — Scale (Months 12–24)
15. **Add hygiene capacity** when recall backlog exceeds 3 weeks.
16. **Expand service lines:** Implants, ortho, or sedation once 600+ active patients.
17. **Consider second location** in Northern Guilford/Hwy 220 corridor once first location exceeds $1.6M annual collections.
