## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. **NC HVAC contractor license** — apply through the NC State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating & Fire Sprinkler Contractors. Budget $400–$700 in fees; exam prep $500–$1,500.
2. **LLC formation** — NC Secretary of State, ~$125 filing. Register trade name if different.
3. **Insurance** — general liability ($1M/$2M) + commercial auto + workers' comp. Budget $4,500–$8,000/year.
4. **EPA Section 608 certification** for refrigerant handling (required for all techs).
5. **Business banking, accounting setup** — $150–$400/month bookkeeping.

### Phase 2 — Physical & Operational Setup (Weeks 3–8)
6. **Service vehicle** — used cargo van $28,000–$42,000 + upfit (shelving, racking, inverter) $8,000–$15,000 + wrap $2,500–$4,000.
7. **Tools & diagnostic equipment** — $12,000–$20,000 (gauges, recovery machines, vacuum pumps, combustion analyzer, leak detectors).
8. **Initial parts inventory** — $10,000–$15,000 (capacitors, contactors, motors, filters, common refrigerant).
9. **Dispatch/CRM software** — ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber; $200–$600/month.

### Phase 3 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 4–10) — **GEO-Critical**
10. **Launch GEO-optimized website** with: LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema, service-area pages, FAQ blocks structured for AI extraction, and clear NAP consistency.
11. **Google Business Profile** — complete verification, categories, service areas, photos, Q&A seeding.
12. **Programmatic local pages** — build 20–40 pages targeting Greensboro neighborhoods and adjacent towns (Summerfield, Oak Ridge, Jamestown, McLeansville) with unique local data.
13. **Review engine** — automate post-job review requests; target 40+ Google reviews by month 6 (AI engines weight review volume and recency heavily).
14. **Citation build** — Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Chamber of Commerce; ensure NAP consistency across all.

### Phase 4 — Demand Generation Launch (Weeks 8–12)
15. **Google Local Services Ads** — launch immediately (pay-per-lead, ~$25–$60/lead in Greensboro HVAC).
16. **Google Search Ads** — $1,500–$2,500/month on high-intent terms ("AC repair Greensboro," "furnace replacement near me").
17. **First 3 GEO content pieces** — "HVAC replacement cost in Greensboro 2027," "Heat pump vs. furnace in NC Piedmont," "How to choose an HVAC contractor in Greensboro" — each structured with tables, FAQs, and cited local data.
18. **Partnership seeding** — 5–10 realtor/property manager relationships (referral engine for replacements).

### Phase 5 — Optimization & Scale (Months 4–12)
19. **Monthly GEO audit** — query ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews for "best HVAC Greensboro" and track citation presence; adjust content accordingly.
20. **Review velocity target** — 8–12 new reviews/month; respond to 100%.
21. **Add second technician** at ~$350K–$450K trailing revenue; add second van.
22. **Evaluate agency engagement** at month 12 if revenue supports $3,000+/month retainer.
