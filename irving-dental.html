## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–4)
1. **Entity formation & licensing:** Texas PLLC, DSO or solo structure; TSBDE dental license verification; Irving business permit.
2. **Location selection:** Las Colinas (75039) for premium; MacArthur Blvd corridor (75062) for volume. Lease **$28–$42/sq ft NNN**; 2,200–3,000 sq ft.
3. **Buildout & equipment:** 3–4 operatories; **$180k–$320k** (CBCT, chairs, sterilization, IT).
4. **PPO credentialing:** Begin Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna applications — **90–120 day** cycle.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 3–5)
5. **Website + schema:** Dentist/LocalBusiness JSON-LD, service pages per procedure, NAP consistency.
6. **Google Business Profile:** Verify at physical address; add 20+ photos; enable booking.
7. **Citation building:** 40+ directories (Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Yelp, NPI, BBB).

### Phase 3 — Launch & Demand Capture (Months 5–7)
8. **Google Ads:** Start with $3,000–$5,000/mo; geo-fence Irving + Las Colinas + DFW Airport corridor.
9. **Review engine:** Automated post-visit SMS; target 25+ reviews in 90 days.
10. **Membership plan:** $29–$49/mo for uninsured (~18–20% of market).

### Phase 4 — GEO & Scale (Months 7–12)
11. **AI-search optimization:** Publish procedure-specific FAQ pages, physician bio schema, and local cost guides — these feed AI Overviews and Perplexity citations.
12. **Programmatic local pages:** "Dentist near [Irving neighborhood]" templates (Las Colinas, Valley Ranch, South Irving, Hackberry).
13. **Measure:** Track cost-per-new-patient (<$150 target), GBP calls, and AI-referral traffic via UTM + server logs.
