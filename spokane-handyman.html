## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Compliance (Weeks 1–2, ~$1,800–$3,200)
1. Register **WA UBI number** (free, Dept. of Revenue) and city of Spokane business license (~$110).
2. File **WA general contractor registration** ($120.50/2 yrs) — required above $500/job.
3. Secure **$12,000 contractor bond** ($150–$300/yr) and **GL insurance** ($1,100–$2,400/yr).
4. Open business banking + set up **Jobber or Housecall Pro** ($69–$169/mo).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 2–4, ~$1,500–$3,200)
5. Launch a 6-page site targeting ZIP-level service pages (e.g., "handyman 99205," "deck repair Spokane Valley").
6. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** — categories, service areas (10 ZIPs), 20+ photos, Q&A.
7. Build 30+ local citations (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB).
8. Set up **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$18–$45/lead in Spokane) before broad Google Ads.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 4–8, ~$800–$1,500/mo)
9. Run a **review sprint**: 25+ Google reviews in 60 days via post-job text automation.
10. Launch Google Ads on 5–8 high-intent keywords ($600–$1,200/mo budget).
11. Partner with 3–5 Spokane property managers (rental turnover work = recurring revenue).

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 3–6)
12. Hire first helper at $22–$28/hr; target 65% billable utilization.
13. Add flat-rate pricing menu (increases average ticket 20–30% vs. hourly).
14. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into ads/SEO until you hit $25k/mo, then evaluate agency management.
