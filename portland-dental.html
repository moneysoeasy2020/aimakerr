## 2026 Q4 Portland Market Analysis

Portland's dental services market is projected at **~$1.02B in annual consumer spend** across roughly **1,150 active dental practices** serving a metro population of **2.51M** (Multnomah, Washington, Clackamas, and Clark County, WA). The metro has approximately **1 dentist per 1,850 residents** — slightly above the national average of 1 per 1,600 — meaning competition is moderate but density is highest in the Pearl District, Sellwood, and Beaverton corridors. Average new-patient acquisition cost in the metro sits at **$180–$340 per patient** for general dentistry and **$400–$900** for cosmetic/ortho leads. A de novo 4-operatory clinic requires **$650,000–$1.1M in total capitalization** (build-out, equipment, working capital, and 6-month operating reserve), with **$185–$310 per sq ft** tenant improvement costs in Class B medical-zoned retail.

Realistic startup budget bands for a Portland dental clinic in Q4 2026: **Lean (1,800 sq ft, 3 ops): $620K–$780K**; **Standard (2,400 sq ft, 5 ops): $850K–$1.05M**; **Premium (Pearl/Downtown, 6+ ops, CBCT + intraoral scanners): $1.15M–$1.6M**. Monthly fixed overhead runs **$38,000–$62,000** (rent $9K–$18K, staff $22K–$38K, supplies/lab $4K–$7K, software/marketing $3K–$6K). Break-even typically occurs at **month 14–20** with **28–35 new patients/month** at a **$1,150 average first-year patient value**.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–6)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Oregon BOP registration, DEA, CLIA (if in-house lab), Portland business license, and a PLLC formation (~$1,200–$2,400 legal).
2. **Site selection:** Target 2,200–2,600 sq ft in a **medically-zoned corridor** (e.g., NE Broadway, SE Hawthorne, Beaverton's Canyon Rd). Negotiate **TI allowance of $40–$75/sq ft**.
3. **Equipment financing:** Lease $280K–$420K in ops/chairs/scanners via Henry Schein Financial or Patterson — preserves working capital.
4. **Domain + GEO foundation:** Register exact-match domain (e.g., `[neighborhood]dental.com`), claim Google Business Profile, build NAP consistency across 40+ directories.

### Phase 2 — Digital Build (Months 4–7, overlapping)
5. **Website:** 6–8 core pages + programmatic service-area pages targeting **Portland's 12 highest-intent neighborhoods**.
6. **GEO/AI-search optimization:** Structured data (Dentist schema, FAQPage, LocalBusiness), citable stat pages, and **llms.txt** for AI crawlers. Aim to appear in ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI Overviews for "dentist near me Portland."
7. **Review engine:** Automated post-visit SMS review requests (target **25+ Google reviews in first 90 days**).

### Phase 3 — Soft Open & Ramp (Months 7–12)
8. **Soft open at 60% capacity**, then full launch with a **$3K–$6K geo-fenced Google Ads campaign** (5-mile radius, $18–$32 CPC in Portland dental).
9. **Insurance credentialing:** Start 90 days pre-open with Delta Dental, Regence, MODA, and OHP — credentialing lag is the #1 revenue killer.
10. **KPI cadence:** Track new patients/week, cost-per-acquisition, chair utilization (target **72%+ by month 18**), and review velocity monthly.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 12–24)
11. Add **hygiene capacity** and a second doctor once **60+ new patients/month** is sustained.
12. Expand programmatic SEO to **Clark County, WA** and **suburban Vancouver** — lower CPC, less competition.
