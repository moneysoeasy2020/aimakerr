## 2026 Q4 Houston Market Analysis

Houston is the largest city in Texas and the 4th-largest in the U.S., with a metro population exceeding **7.5 million** (Harris County alone at ~4.8 million). The metro's housing stock is roughly **2.4 million housing units**, and approximately **63% are owner-occupied** — the core demand pool for handyman and contractor services. Houston's "no-zoning" land-use model, aging inner-loop housing (median home age in many ZIPs exceeds 40 years), and high insurance-driven repair demand (post-hurricane and freeze events) create steady, recurring service volume. The Houston metro handyman/home-improvement services market is estimated at **$1.4–1.8 billion annually** in 2026, growing at **4–6% year-over-year**.

**Realistic 2026 Q4 Houston pricing benchmarks** (what you can charge):

| Service | Typical Houston Range |
|---|---|
| Handyman hourly rate | **$65–$110/hr** |
| Minimum service call | **$95–$150** |
| Drywall patch (small) | **$150–$350** |
| Interior door replacement (installed) | **$250–$450** |
| Ceiling fan install | **$125–$250** |
| Deck repair (per sq ft) | **$8–$18/sq ft** |
| Bathroom remodel (full) | **$9,000–$22,000** |
| Kitchen remodel (mid-range) | **$25,000–$65,000** |

**Startup capital reality:** A lean solo handyman launch in Houston runs **$4,500–$12,000** (tools, LLC, insurance, vehicle wrap, initial ads). A licensed general contractor launch with crew and office runs **$35,000–$90,000**. Texas does **not** require a state license for general handyman work under $50,000 per job, but **HVAC, electrical, and plumbing require TDLR licensing** — plan your service menu around this.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Operational Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
1. **Form Texas LLC** via Texas Secretary of State — **$300** filing fee.
2. **Register for Texas Sales & Use Tax Permit** (free) and **Houston business license** if applicable.
3. **General liability insurance** — Houston handyman policies run **$600–$1,800/yr**; add workers' comp if hiring.
4. **Verify TDLR licensing** for any electrical/plumbing/HVAC work — subcontract these to licensed pros.
5. **Open business bank account** and set up QuickBooks/Wave.

### Phase 2 — Digital Asset Build (Weeks 2–4)
6. **Domain + hosting** ($15–$40/mo) — choose a `.com` with "Houston" or service keyword.
7. **Build 6–8 page site** with LocalBusiness + Service schema markup (critical for GEO).
8. **Claim and optimize Google Business Profile** — add Houston service-area ZIPs, photos, Q&A, and 10+ service categories.
9. **Publish 10 GEO-optimized service pages** — each must include Houston-specific pricing, ZIP-level service areas, and FAQ schema.
10. **Build 20+ programmatic location pages** for suburbs: Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Pearland, Cypress, Spring, Pasadena, Missouri City, Baytown, Humble.

### Phase 3 — Trust & Visibility (Weeks 4–8)
11. **Launch review generation system** — target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (critical GEO trust signal).
12. **Submit to 40+ local citations** (Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, Nextdoor, BBB, Houston Chamber).
13. **Set up Google Local Services Ads** — Houston LSA cost-per-lead runs **$25–$85** for handyman categories.
14. **Launch Meta ads** ($500–$1,500/mo test budget) targeting Houston ZIPs with before/after creative.

### Phase 4 — GEO Content Moat (Weeks 8–16)
15. **Publish 4 Houston-specific guides per month** — e.g., "Houston Hurricane Repair Checklist," "Cost to Remodel a Katy Bathroom in 2026."
16. **Add structured data** to every page (FAQPage, HowTo, LocalBusiness, AggregateRating).
17. **Build backlinks** from Houston HOA sites, local news, and trade associations.
18. **Track AI citation share** — use tools like Profound or Otterly to monitor how often generative engines cite your site.

### Phase 5 — Scale (Month 4+)
19. **Hire first helper** at **$18–$24/hr** in Houston market.
20. **Add second service vertical** (e.g., deck building or painting) once first is profitable.
21. **Reinvest 8–12% of revenue** into GEO content and paid ads.
