## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Register LLC with Texas SOS (~$300), get Lubbock business permit, general liability insurance ($400–$900/yr), and IICRC certification ($500–$1,200).
2. **Equipment:** Used truckmount ($8K–$18K) or high-end portable ($2K–$4K); chemicals + hoses ($800–$1,500).
3. **Van wrap / magnetic signage:** $300–$2,500 depending on scope.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
4. **Google Business Profile:** Claim, verify, add 10+ photos, set service area (Lubbock, Wolfforth, Slaton, Levelland).
5. **Website:** 5–7 pages with **service-area pages** (e.g., "Carpet Cleaning in Wolfforth TX") — this is your programmatic SEO backbone.
6. **Citations:** 30+ directories (Yelp, Angi, BBB, Nextdoor, Thumbtack).

### Phase 3 — GEO / AI-Search Optimization (Weeks 4–8)
7. **Structured data:** Add LocalBusiness + Service schema to every page.
8. **Answer-first content:** Write 8–10 FAQ-style posts targeting queries like *"How much does carpet cleaning cost in Lubbock?"* and *"Best carpet cleaner near Texas Tech?"* — AI engines cite clear, factual, locally-specific answers.
9. **Review velocity:** Automate SMS review requests; target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days** (critical for AI trust signals).

### Phase 4 — Demand Capture (Weeks 6–12)
10. **Google Ads:** $500–$1,000/mo on "carpet cleaning Lubbock" + "move out cleaning Lubbock."
11. **Partnerships:** 5–10 property managers near Texas Tech (your highest-ROI channel).
12. **Seasonal push:** Pre-sell December move-out packages in November.
