# Launching a Dental Clinic in Detroit, MI — 2026 Q4 Startup Guide

Detroit's dental services market is projected to reach roughly **$410M–$440M in annual consumer spend in 2026**, with Wayne County containing an estimated **~1,050–1,150 active dental practices** (general and specialty combined) and a dentist-to-population ratio near **1 per 1,950 residents** — slightly underserved versus the national ~1 per 1,650, leaving room for new general practices in specific ZIP codes. A realistic de novo **2–4 operatory general dental office** in Detroit requires **$520,000–$780,000 in total startup capital** (build-out, equipment, working capital), with breakeven typically at **14–22 months** at a 55–65% chair utilization rate.

Typical 2026 Q4 Detroit pricing benchmarks: **dental chair + delivery unit packages $28,000–$42,000 per operatory** (refurbished $14,000–$22,000), **panorex/CBCT imaging $45,000–$95,000**, **tenant build-out $95–$165 per sq ft** (medical-grade plumbing/electrical), and **commercial lease $22–$34 per sq ft NNN** in Midtown/Downtown vs. **$14–$21 per sq ft** in outer neighborhoods (Warrendale, Redford border, Southwest). New-patient acquisition runs **$120–$280 per acquired patient** in Metro Detroit via paid search and local SEO, with a first-year patient base target of **900–1,400 active patients** for a single-location startup.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 0–6)
1. **Market & site selection**: Target ZIPs with high Medicaid density + low dentist-per-capita (48210, 48228, 48235). Negotiate 7–10 year lease with 3–5 month free rent for build-out.
2. **Entity & licensing**: Michigan LLC/PC formation ($150–$500), **Michigan Board of Dentistry licensure** (verify via LARA), DEA registration ($888/3 yrs), CLIA waiver if applicable.
3. **Capital stack**: SBA 7(a) loans ($350K–$500K typical for dental startups, 10-year terms), equipment financing (5–7 yr), plus $80K–$120K personal/working capital.
4. **Equipment procurement**: Refurbished operatory packages ($14K–$22K each) preserve $60K–$110K vs. new; CBCT only if endo/implant services planned.

### Phase 2 — Build-Out & Digital Foundation (Months 4–9)
5. **Build-out**: Budget $95–$165/sq ft; medical plumbing and dental vacuum lines are the top cost drivers. Target 1,800–2,800 sq ft for 3–4 operatories.
6. **Digital assets**: Website, Google Business Profile, HIPAA-aware booking, practice management software, and review automation — deploy **before** opening to build ranking momentum.
7. **Insurance credentialing**: Begin **90–120 days before opening** — Medicaid, Delta Dental, BCBS Michigan, and 3–5 PPOs. Credentialing delays are the #1 cause of slow ramp.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 9–18)
8. **Soft launch**: 60–90 days of reduced hours; target 40–60 new patients/month via GBP + referrals.
9. **Paid acquisition**: Google Ads at $1,500–$4,000/mo targeting "dentist [ZIP]" and Spanish-language keywords; expect $120–$280 CAC.
10. **Review velocity**: 15–25 verified Google reviews in first 90 days; this drives local pack rankings in 4–7 months.
11. **Breakeven tracking**: Monitor chair utilization (target 55%+ by month 12), collections ratio (target 95%+ of production), and hygiene recare rate (target 65%+).

### Phase 4 — Optimization (Months 18–24)
12. **Service line expansion**: Add endo, implants, or ortho only after 800+ active patients.
13. **Second location evaluation**: Reassess at 1,200+ active patients and 70%+ utilization.
