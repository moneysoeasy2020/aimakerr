## Executive Snapshot: The Numbers First

Launching a dental clinic in Madison, WI in Q4 2026 requires an estimated **$485,000–$720,000** in total startup capital for a 3–4 operatory general practice, with **$310,000–$450,000** of that consumed by buildout, equipment (chairs, digital radiography, CBCT, sterilization), and permitting. Madison's dental market supports roughly **1 dentist per 1,650 residents** (Dane County ≈ 575,000 population, ~348 active dentists), which is *slightly under-served* relative to the Wisconsin statewide ratio of 1:1,480 — a favorable entry signal for a new patient-accepting practice. Average Madison-area dental insurance reimbursement runs **8–14% above rural Wisconsin**, and the median household income in Dane County (~$84,600 in 2025 ACS estimates) supports a **$1,150–$1,450** average first-year patient value.

Patient acquisition cost (PAC) in Madison is projected at **$180–$340 per new patient** in Q4 2026 via Google Ads + local SEO, with dental-specific keywords ("dentist near me Madison," "emergency dentist Madison") commanding **$9–$22 CPC**. A clinic needs **28–42 new patients per month** to break even on a $1.9M–$2.4M annual revenue target at typical 22–28% EBITDA margins. Digital marketing setup — website, GBP (Google Business Profile), local citations, review infrastructure — ranges **$4,800–$14,500** depending on whether you use a Madison brick-and-mortar agency or a verified freelance specialist.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan (Q4 2026 → Q1 2027)

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Weeks 1–6, October–mid-November 2026)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Form WI LLC/SCorp (~$130), obtain Wisconsin Dentistry Examining Board license (4–8 weeks), DEA registration, NPI, malpractice insurance ($2,400–$4,800/yr).
2. **Site selection:** Target 1,800–2,600 sq ft; negotiate TI allowance ($30–$55/sq ft typical in Madison).
3. **Secure digital assets:** domain, GBP verification (allow 2–3 weeks), HIPAA-compliant hosting.
4. **Hire freelancer team:** website ($3,200–$4,800), local SEO ($1,500–$2,400), photographer ($800).

### Phase 2 — Buildout & Digital Launch (Weeks 7–14, mid-November–December 2026)
5. **Construction & equipment:** chairs ($28K–$45K each), CBCT ($65K–$110K), sterilization ($18K–$30K).
6. **Website + GBP go-live** with 8–12 service pages (emergency, pediatric, cosmetic, implants).
7. **Review engine:** seed 15–25 reviews from soft-launch patients; target 4.8★ on Google by January.
8. **Google Ads soft launch:** $1,200–$2,000/mo budget, geo-fenced to 8-mile radius.

### Phase 3 — January Patient Surge Capture (Weeks 15–20)
9. **Insurance credentialing** with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna (start 90 days before opening — this is the #1 launch delay).
10. **Community outreach:** Madison Chamber of Commerce, UW–Madison employee benefits fair, neighborhood associations.
11. **Content ramp:** 4 blogs/mo targeting "dentist [neighborhood] Madison" long-tail queries.

### Phase 4 — Optimization (Weeks 21–26, Q1 2027)
12. **Measure PAC** monthly; kill ad sets above $340/patient.
13. **Add second hygienist** once 180 active patients reached.
14. **Reinvest 6–9% of revenue** into marketing — the Madison competitive benchmark.
