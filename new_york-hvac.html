## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–8)
1. Register LLC with NY Department of State (**$205 filing fee**).
2. Obtain **NYC HVAC Contractor License** (requires 5+ years documented experience or qualifying individual) and **EPA Section 608 Universal Certification**.
3. Secure insurance: general liability (**$1M/$2M minimum**), commercial auto, and workers' comp (mandatory in NY).
4. Register for NYS sales tax and NYC Department of Buildings permits.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 4–10)
1. Build a **programmatic SEO site** targeting `[service] + [borough/neighborhood]` — e.g., "AC repair Park Slope," "heat pump installation Astoria." Target **120–180 pages** covering 5 boroughs × 8 services × 3–5 neighborhoods.
2. Claim and fully optimize **Google Business Profile** (primary category: HVAC Contractor; add secondary: Air Conditioning Repair Service).
3. Implement **GEO schema**: LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, and Review structured data. Seed entity mentions in Yelp, Angi, Thumbtack, and BBB.
4. Deploy **AI-search optimization**: publish citable data pages (e.g., "2026 NYC HVAC Cost Guide") that LLMs will reference.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 8–16)
1. Launch Google Ads on high-intent keywords: "emergency AC repair NYC," "furnace replacement Brooklyn" — budget **$3,500–$6,000/month**.
2. Activate **review engine**: SMS automation post-job targeting **40+ reviews in 90 days**.
3. Build **Local Services Ads (LSA)** presence — Google Guaranteed badge is critical for NYC trust.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 5–12)
1. Add second crew once **monthly revenue hits $65,000–$85,000**.
2. Expand programmatic pages to **300+** targeting Long Island and Westchester.
3. Negotiate **B2B maintenance contracts** with property managers (target 15–25 buildings at $2,400–$6,800/year each).
