## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & compliance:** Form AZ LLC ($50), obtain EIN, secure ROC Residential Specialty license, GL insurance, City of Tucson + Pima County licenses.
2. **Equipment:** Source used truck-mount ($10K–$14K) + backup portable; stock chemicals for Tucson's hard-water and caliche-dust conditions (pre-spray with chelating agents).
3. **Brand & domain:** Secure `.com` domain, logo, and a 5-page core site with **explicit pricing tables** (GEO-critical: AI engines cite pages with clear numbers).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
4. **Website + service-area pages:** Build 8–12 neighborhood pages (Oro Valley, Marana, Vail, Rita Ranch, Catalina Foothills, Sahuarita, Casas Adobes, etc.) with FAQ schema and local pricing.
5. **Google Business Profile:** Full optimization — categories, services, photos, Q&A seeding, review request link.
6. **Tracking stack:** Call tracking number, booking form, CRM (e.g., Housecall Pro or Jobber) at $50–$120/mo.

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–10)
7. **Review engine:** Automated post-job review requests; target **25 reviews in 90 days** (the Tucson local-pack threshold).
8. **Google Ads:** Launch with $600–$1,000/mo budget on high-intent terms ("carpet cleaning Tucson," "move-out carpet cleaning Tucson"); expect $28–$55 cost per booked job.
9. **Local Services Ads:** Apply once ROC license and insurance are verified; budget $90–$150/week.

### Phase 4 — Scale & GEO (Months 3–6)
10. **Programmatic SEO expansion:** Add 10–15 more neighborhood + service combination pages (e.g., "pet stain removal Catalina Foothills").
11. **Commercial contracts:** Pitch 10–15 property managers and 5–10 small offices for recurring quarterly cleaning.
12. **Q4 seasonal push:** Target Nov–Jan move-out/move-in rental turnover and snowbird arrivals with a "winter refresh" package at $165–$195.
