- **Saturation risk ZIPs:** 55414 (Dinkytown — student churn), 55401 (North Loop — high rent, low household density)

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–6)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Form MN PLLC ($600–$1,200 legal), obtain MN dental license, DEA, and Minneapolis business license. Budget **$3,500–$6,000** in legal/accounting.
2. **Site selection:** Target ZIPs 55413, 55418, 55408. Negotiate **TI allowance of $35–$65/sq ft** — standard in Q4 2026 Minneapolis medical leasing.
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) at **10.5–11.5%** (Q4 2026 prime + spread) or dental-specific lenders (Bank of America Practice Solutions, Live Oak). Expect **10–15% down**.
4. **Equipment financing:** Lease 4 operatories + CBCT at **$4,200–$6,800/mo** over 60 months.

### Phase 2 — Digital Build (Months 4–7, parallel)
5. **Website + GBP:** Launch 8–10 weeks before opening. HIPAA-compliant hosting with signed BAA. Target **Core Web Vitals LCP < 2.2s**.
6. **Programmatic SEO:** Build **60–120 service × neighborhood pages** (e.g., "emergency dentist North Loop Minneapolis," "Invisalign Uptown Minneapolis"). Target **top-3 local pack** for 8–12 primary keywords within 6 months.
7. **Review engine:** Automate post-visit review requests; target **25+ Google reviews by month 3**, **75+ by month 9**.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 7–18)
8. **Paid acquisition:** Google Ads **$2,500–$4,500/mo**; cost-per-new-patient target **$85–$145** in Minneapolis dental (below national ~$180).
9. **Recall & reactivation:** Implement recall automation by month 2 of operations — drives **18–24%** of mature-practice revenue.
10. **Hire hygienists early:** Minneapolis hygienist market is tight; budget **$42–$52/hr** and post 90 days pre-opening.

### Phase 4 — Optimization (Months 18–36)
11. **Add specialty services** (Invisalign, implants, sedation) once 400+ active patients.
12. **Track to valuation:** 4.5–6.0x EBITDA exit range requires **3+ years of clean financials** and **$1.2M+ revenue** for premium multiple.

## Key Minneapolis-Specific Risks (2026 Q4)
- **Hygienist shortage:** Twin Cities hygienist vacancy rate ~11%; plan **2 hires for every 1 chair**.
- **DSO encroachment:** Park Dental and Metro Dentalcare expanding into Northeast and South Minneapolis — differentiate on **same-day emergency + extended hours**.
- **Winter attrition:** Expect **8–12% seasonal no-show spike Nov–Feb**; build reminder cadence accordingly.
- **MN Medicaid reimbursement lag:** If targeting MA patients, model **30–45 day claim cycles** and budget accordingly.
