Laredo's HVAC demand is driven by extreme heat (100°F+ summer peaks, 240+ cooling-degree days annually) and a fast-growing population of ~267,000 metro residents across Webb County. A realistic startup budget for a licensed, insured residential HVAC contractor in Laredo runs **$38,000–$72,000** in Year 1 (vehicles, tools, EPA 608 certification, TDLR licensing, insurance, and marketing), with break-even typically at **55–90 service calls/month** at an average ticket of **$285–$420** for diagnostics + repair and **$6,500–$11,500** for full system replacements.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Compliance & Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
1. **TDLR Air Conditioning Contractor License** (Texas Dept. of Licensing & Regulation) — required for contracts >$50k or advertising as contractor. Budget **$300–$600**.
2. **EPA Section 608 certification** (all techs handling refrigerant) — **$150–$400/tech**.
3. **General liability + commercial auto insurance** — **$2,800–$5,500/yr** for 1–2 trucks.
4. **Business registration** (Texas SOS LLC, EIN, Webb County DBA) — **$300–$500**.
5. **Bond** (if required for commercial work) — **$100–$300/yr**.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–8)
6. **Google Business Profile** — verify in Laredo, add service categories (AC repair, furnace repair, HVAC contractor), upload 15+ geo-tagged photos, enable messaging.
7. **Website** with programmatic ZIP pages: `/ac-repair-laredo-tx-78040`, `/hvac-installation-78041`, etc. — target 12–18 service-area pages.
8. **Booking + CRM** (Jobber recommended for <5 trucks, ~$169–$399/mo).
9. **Review engine** — automated SMS review requests post-service; target 40+ Google reviews in 90 days.

### Phase 3 — Demand Capture (Weeks 6–12)
10. **Google Local Services Ads** — pay-per-lead, ~$35–$90/lead in Laredo HVAC vertical. Best ROI for launch.
11. **Google Ads** — $1,200–$2,500/mo starting budget, geo-fenced to Webb County + 25-mile radius.
12. **Spanish-language ad sets** — separate campaigns, ~30–40% lower CPC historically in Laredo.
13. **Programmatic SEO** — publish 30+ location/service pages by month 6 (e.g., "AC repair in [neighborhood] Laredo").

### Phase 4 — Operations & Retention (Ongoing)
14. **Maintenance agreements** — $149–$249/yr, target 15% attach rate; recurring revenue stabilizes cash flow.
15. **Fleet branding** — 2 wrapped trucks ($2,800–$4,500 each) → dominant local visibility in a drive-heavy market.
16. **Bilingual dispatch** — non-negotiable for Laredo; expect 40–55% of calls in Spanish.
