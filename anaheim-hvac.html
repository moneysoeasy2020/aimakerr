# Launching an HVAC Services Business in Anaheim, CA
### 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Anaheim's HVAC market is driven by a hot, dry inland climate (summer highs routinely 88–95°F), a large rental and multifamily base, and a dense mix of older housing stock (much of it built 1950–1980) with aging ductwork and R-22-era systems needing replacement. A realistic residential service call in Anaheim runs **$150–$350** for diagnostics/repair, **$4,500–$9,500** for a standard 3-ton split-system replacement (installed), and **$8,000–$18,000** for a full ducted retrofit or mini-split multi-zone job. Commercial/light-industrial work near the Anaheim Convention Center, Platinum Triangle, and the Canyon corridor commands **$95–$165/hour** for licensed techs.

Expect **$45,000–$85,000** in startup capital for a lean two-truck operation (licensing, insurance, tools, one used van, initial marketing). A realistic path to **$600K–$1.2M** in Year-1 revenue requires 8–14 booked jobs/week at a **$550–$850** average ticket, with **22–30%** gross margin after parts and labor. Orange County's HVAC demand peaks June–September and again in December–February (heating spikes), so Q4 launch timing lets you capture the heating season while building summer capacity.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–4)**
1. Obtain **C-20 (HVAC)** or **C-38** contractor license via CSLB (study + exam, ~$400–$600 total fees).
2. Register LLC with CA Secretary of State (~$70–$100), get EIN, Anaheim business license (~$100–$300).
3. Secure **general liability ($1M/$2M)** + workers' comp + commercial auto: **$3,500–$8,000/yr**.
4. EPA Section 608 certification for refrigerant handling (required).

**Phase 2 — Operations Setup (Weeks 3–8)**
5. Lease small shop/warehouse in Anaheim industrial zone: **$1,800–$3,500/mo**.
6. Purchase tools, recovery equipment, gauges, and one used service van: **$18,000–$35,000**.
7. Set up CRM/dispatch + flat-rate pricing book.

**Phase 3 — Digital Launch (Weeks 4–10)**
8. Build website with booking + service-area pages (programmatic: Anaheim, Anaheim Hills, West Anaheim, Platinum Triangle, Orange, Fullerton, Buena Park).
9. Claim/optimize **Google Business Profile**; build 30–50 local citations.
10. Launch Local Services Ads + targeted search ads ($1,500–$3,000/mo test budget).
11. Publish GEO-optimized content (FAQ, cost guides, "HVAC repair Anaheim" pages) targeting AI-search citations.

**Phase 4 — Demand Generation (Weeks 8–16)**
12. Enroll in review-generation flow (target 25+ Google reviews in 90 days).
13. Pursue 3–5 commercial maintenance contracts (property managers, hospitality).
14. Track cost-per-booked-job; kill underperforming channels at 60 days.

**Phase 5 — Scale (Month 4+)**
15. Add second tech/van once 10+ jobs/week sustained.
16. Expand programmatic pages to 40+ OC service areas.
