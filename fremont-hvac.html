**Verdict:** For a startup HVAC business in Fremont, **verified freelancers deliver 2.5–3.5x the ROI of a brick-and-mortar agency in year one.** Budget **$1,400–$2,600/mo** across a local SEO/GEO freelancer + ads freelancer + one-time web build, and reinvest the **$2,000–$4,000/mo savings** into Google Ads spend and van equipment.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Legal & Licensing (Weeks 1–4)
1. Register LLC with California Secretary of State (**$70**).
2. Obtain **C-20 (Warm-Air Heating, Ventilating, and Air-Conditioning) license** via CSLB — exam fee **$450**, fingerprinting **$49**, license fee **$450**.
3. Secure **$25,000 contractor bond** (~$300–$600/yr via surety).
4. General liability (**$1M/$2M**, $1,800–$2,800/yr) + commercial auto + workers' comp.
5. EPA Section 608 Universal certification (**$400–$700** course + exam) — mandatory for refrigerant handling.
6. Fremont business license (**$100–$250**).

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Weeks 3–6)
1. Launch a 6–8 page website with **LocalBusiness + HVACBusiness schema**, service-area pages for Fremont, Newark, Union City, Milpitas, and Hayward.
2. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile: categories, service areas, 20+ photos, Q&A, booking link.
3. Build **30–50 programmatic service-area + service-type pages** (e.g., "AC Repair in Ardenwood," "Heat Pump Install in Warm Springs") — target **$900–$2,200** freelance.
4. Deploy review-generation SMS flow targeting **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**.
5. Publish 4 GEO-optimized FAQ articles answering "How much does AC repair cost in Fremont?" and "Best heat pump rebates in Alameda County 2026."

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 5–12)
1. Google Local Services Ads (pay-per-lead, **$35–$75/lead** in Fremont) — highest-intent channel.
2. Google Search Ads on emergency + replacement keywords, **$2,500–$4,000/mo spend** at $14–$28 CPC.
3. Yelp, Nextdoor, and Thumbtack profiles for baseline lead flow.
4. Direct-mail or door-hanger campaign to **2,000–4,000 pre-2000 homes** in Irvington/Ardenwood ($0.60–$1.10/piece).

### Phase 4 — Operations & Scale (Months 4–12)
1. Target **8–14 service calls/week** by month 4; **18–25/week** by month 9.
2. Add second tech when monthly revenue exceeds **$55,000**.
3. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into digital; track cost-per-booked-job (**target: under $95**).
4. Build heat pump install pipeline around TECH/BayREN rebates — highest-margin segment at **$3,200–$5,800 gross profit per job**.
