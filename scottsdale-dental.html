## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–6)
1. **Entity & licensing:** Form PLLC in Arizona, obtain AZ Dental Board license, DEA registration, and Scottsdale business license.
2. **Site selection:** Target 2,000–2,800 sq ft in a 3-mile radius of your ideal patient demographic; negotiate TI allowance ($40–$90/sq ft typical).
3. **Financing:** SBA 7(a) or conventional practice loan; expect 10–20% down, $650K–$1.1M total.
4. **Build-out & equipment:** 4–6 operatories, CBCT, digital scanners, sterilization; budget 5–7 months.

### Phase 2 — Digital Foundation (Months 4–6, parallel)
5. **Website:** Dental-specific build with LocalBusiness + Dentist schema, service pages per procedure, and AI-crawlable FAQ blocks.
6. **Google Business Profile:** Verify early; complete all fields; seed 10–15 reviews from soft-launch patients.
7. **Citations & NAP:** Consistent listings on Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Yelp, ADA Find-a-Dentist, and 40+ aggregators.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Ramp (Months 6–12)
8. **Paid acquisition:** Google Ads (procedure-specific campaigns: implants, Invisalign, emergency) at $3K–$8K/mo; track cost-per-new-patient against $180–$420 benchmark.
9. **Review engine:** Automate post-visit review requests; target 60+ Google reviews by month 12.
10. **Content moat:** Publish 4–8 GEO-optimized pages/month targeting "dentist near [Scottsdale neighborhood]" and procedure-intent queries.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 12–24)
11. **Membership plan:** Launch in-house plan ($29–$49/mo) to capture the 9–12% uninsured segment.
12. **Specialist partnerships:** Referral network for ortho, oral surgery, endo.
13. **Analytics review:** Quarterly audit of CAC, LTV, and AI-citation visibility (test queries in ChatGPT/Perplexity monthly).
