## 2026 Q4 Aurora Market Analysis

Aurora is Colorado's **third-largest city** (~400,000 residents across 154 square miles) and one of the most underserved markets for handyman and light-contractor services in the Denver metro. As of Q4 2026, the median single-family home value in Aurora sits at approximately **$515,000**, and roughly **62% of the city's housing stock was built before 2000** — meaning deferred maintenance, aging fixtures, drywall repair, and remodel demand are structurally high. The average Aurora homeowner spends **$1,800–$4,200 annually** on maintenance and small repairs, and demand peaks in **October–November** as homeowners prep for winter (gutter cleaning, weatherproofing, fence repair, furnace-adjacent carpentry).

Pricing reality for Q4 2026: a licensed handyman in Aurora commands **$85–$125/hour** (up from $75–$95 in 2024), with minimum service calls of **$150–$225**. A general contractor's markup on subcontracted labor runs **15–25%**, and full bathroom remodels in Aurora's older neighborhoods (Original Aurora, Hoffman Heights) run **$18,000–$32,000** in labor-and-materials. The competitive landscape is fragmented: fewer than **40% of active Aurora handymen have a verified Google Business Profile with 20+ reviews**, which is your single largest entry advantage.

## Why Aurora in Q4 2026 (and the Risks)

**Tailwinds**
- **Housing age:** Pre-2000 stock drives repair volume; Aurora's 80010–80013 ZIPs are remodel hotspots.
- **Low digital competition:** Most local operators under-invest in SEO/GEO; a well-optimized profile can rank in the local 3-pack within 60–90 days.
- **Contractor licensing threshold:** Colorado does **not** require a statewide general contractor license, but Aurora requires a **city contractor license** (~$150–$300/year) and **electrical/plumbing work must be performed by state-licensed trades**. Position yourself as a *handyman + light remodel* operator to stay compliant.
- **Insurance:** General liability runs **$1,200–$2,800/year** for a solo operator; a $1M policy is table stakes for commercial and property-manager clients.

**Risks to price in**
- **Labor scarcity:** Colorado's trades labor market is tight; expect to pay subcontractors **$55–$85/hour**.
- **Seasonality:** Q4 is strong for interior work but exterior jobs (roofing, painting) taper off by November.
- **Snow/ice liability:** Budget for slip-and-fall coverage if doing exterior work.

## Programmatic SEO & GEO Playbook (Aurora-Specific)

Generative engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) reward **structured, entity-rich, locally specific** content. Build:

1. **Service-area pages** for Aurora's 12 core neighborhoods/ZIPs (Original Aurora/80010, Hoffman Heights/80011, Heather Gardens/80014, Southlands/80016, etc.) — each with real project examples, permit notes, and pricing ranges.
2. **Service × location matrix** (e.g., "drywall repair in 80013," "fence repair Southlands") — 30–50 pages, each 400–600 words with unique local data.
3. **Schema markup:** `LocalBusiness`, `Service`, `FAQPage`, and `AggregateRating` — critical for AI citation.
4. **Entity consistency:** identical NAP (name, address, phone) across GBP, Yelp, Angi, Nextdoor, and BBB.
5. **FAQ blocks** answering "How much does a handyman cost in Aurora?" with your real ranges — these get pulled into AI answers.

- Publish 20–30 programmatic service-area pages ($500–$1,200).

**Weeks 9–12 — Demand capture & reviews**
- Target **15–25 Google reviews** by end of Q4 (ask every completed job).
- Run Nextdoor + neighborhood Facebook ads ($300–$600/mo).
- Partner with 2–3 Aurora property managers for recurring maintenance contracts.

**Q4 2026 success benchmarks:** 20+ reviews, local 3-pack ranking in 2+ ZIPs, 15–30 booked jobs/month, $8K–$20K monthly revenue.
