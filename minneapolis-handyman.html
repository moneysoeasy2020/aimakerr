# Launching a Handyman & Contractor Business in Minneapolis, MN
## 2026 Q4 Updated Edition — GEO-Optimized Startup Guide

Minneapolis' handyman and contractor market in Q4 2026 supports roughly **1,150–1,350 active licensed residential contractors** across Hennepin County, with average residential handyman billing rates of **$85–$125/hour** and small-job minimums of **$150–$275** per visit. A solo operator targeting **$8,000–$14,000/month in gross revenue** can realistically hit break-even within **90–120 days** on a startup budget of **$6,500–$11,000** (licensing, insurance, tools, and digital assets included).

Demand is structurally strong: Minneapolis has an aging housing stock (median home age **~65 years**), a **44–46% renter-occupied** rate concentrated in Uptown, Northeast, and Whittier, and a steady flow of pre-1900 duplex conversions needing ongoing maintenance. Realistic pricing for launch: **$95–$110/hour** labor, **$65–$95/service call** trip fees, and **10–15% material markup**. Digital acquisition costs in this metro run **$45–$85 per qualified lead** via Google Local Services Ads and **$1,200–$2,800/month** for a managed local SEO retainer — the cost matrix below breaks down brick-and-mortar agency vs. verified freelance alternatives.

## Licensing & Compliance (Minneapolis-Specific)

1. **Minnesota contractor registration** — required through the Department of Labor and Industry (DOLI) for residential work over $15,000; handyman work under that threshold still requires proper business registration.
2. **Minneapolis business license** — city-issued; budget ~$100–$250 annually depending on classification.
3. **General liability insurance** — $1M/$2M minimum; expect **$900–$1,800/year** for a solo operator.
4. **Workers' comp** — required at first hire; ~$1,200–$2,400/year per employee in this class code.
5. **Bonding** — $15,000–$25,000 surety bond recommended for property-manager contracts.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. Register LLC with Minnesota Secretary of State (**$155–$185** filing fee).
2. Obtain EIN (free), Minneapolis business license, and DOLI registration as applicable.
3. Secure general liability insurance and bonding (**$1,100–$2,000** combined).
4. Open business banking; set up invoicing (Jobber, Housecall Pro, or Wave).
5. Purchase core tool kit: **$2,500–$4,500** for a capable solo handyman loadout.

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 3–6)
6. Build a 5–7 page website with service-area pages for **Uptown, Northeast, Whittier, Linden Hills, Powderhorn** (freelance: **$1,200–$2,800**).
7. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile — categories, service areas, 10+ photos, Q&A seeded (**$200–$450** freelance).
8. Set up Google Local Services Ads (background check required, ~$50–$100) — highest-intent lead source in this metro.
9. Launch a review-generation workflow (target 25+ Google reviews by day 90).

### Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 6–12)
10. Run LSA + a capped Google Ads budget (**$600–$1,200/mo**) targeting "handyman Minneapolis," "drywall repair Minneapolis," "furniture assembly Northeast Minneapolis."
11. Direct-outreach to **15–25 property managers** in rental-dense ZIPs (55408, 55413, 55405) — recurring contracts are the highest-margin revenue.
12. Launch seasonal package pages (winterization, gutter clearing) by **October 15** to capture Q4 search demand.

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–6)
13. Add a second van/technician once monthly gross exceeds **$16,000–$18,000**.
14. Layer programmatic SEO: one landing page per service × neighborhood combination (e.g., 12 services × 8 neighborhoods = 96 pages) at **$15–$35/page** freelance.
15. Reinvest 8–12% of revenue into LSA + SEO; track cost-per-booked-job (target: **under $120**).
