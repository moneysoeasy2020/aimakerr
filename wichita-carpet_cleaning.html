## 2026 Q4 Wichita Market Analysis

### Demand Drivers
- **Rental turnover:** Wichita has a rental vacancy cycle tied to WSU semesters (Aug/Jan) and military relocations from McConnell AFB — both create predictable deep-clean demand.
- **Insurance/restoration work:** Hail and storm seasons (Apr–Sep) drive water-damage and post-remediation carpet jobs; partnering with 3–5 local restoration firms is a high-ROI channel.
- **Real estate:** ~4,000–5,000 home sales/year in Sedgwick County; pre-listing carpet cleans are a reliable lead source via agent referrals.
- **Aging housing stock:** Median home age in Wichita is ~45 years; older carpets in Derby, Haysville, and west Wichita neighborhoods favor restoration-oriented service tiers.

### Competitive Landscape
- **Price floor:** Craigslist/Facebook "3 rooms for $99" operators create a low-price tier — avoid competing here; differentiate on IICRC certification, truck-mount extraction, and satisfaction guarantees.
- **Price ceiling:** Established firms charge **$300–$450** for 5-room packages; there's room for a **mid-tier premium brand at $240–$320**.
- **Seasonality:** Q4 (Oct–Dec) is moderate — holiday hosting and end-of-year commercial budget spend offset the post-summer dip. Expect **15–25% lower volume** than peak spring.

### Local SEO/GEO Opportunity
Wichita's carpet cleaning SERP and AI-answer results are dominated by **2–4 national lead-gen aggregators** and thin local sites. A well-structured local site with schema markup, service-area pages (Wichita, Derby, Andover, Maize, Goddard, Haysville), and verified reviews can rank in the **top 3 map pack within 4–7 months** in most suburbs.

### Brick-and-Mortar Agency vs. Verified Freelance Experts — Explicit Breakdown

**Local Wichita agency (brick-and-mortar):**
- **Pros:** In-person meetings, local network for referrals, single point of accountability, often bundles web + ads + reviews.
- **Cons:** **2–4x the cost** of freelancers; retainer lock-ins of 6–12 months are common; account managers rotate; you may pay for junior staff doing the actual work.
- **Realistic Year-1 spend:** $9,000–$18,000 for a comparable scope.

**Verified freelance experts (vetted via Upwork, Clutch, or local referral):**
- **Pros:** **40–65% lower cost**, direct access to the specialist doing the work, flexible month-to-month terms, niche expertise (e.g., home-services local SEO specialists).
- **Cons:** Requires you to vet (portfolio, references, test project), manage multiple people, and hold them accountable to deliverables.
- **Realistic Year-1 spend:** $3,000–$6,500 for the same scope.
- **Vetting checklist:** Ask for 3 home-services case studies, a live GBP audit, a sample schema markup file, and a 30-day paid test project ($300–$600) before committing.

**Verdict:** For a sub-$25K startup, **freelance experts plus one part-time local consultant** delivers the best cost-to-result ratio. Reserve agency spend for year 2+ once revenue supports a $1,500+/mo retainer.

## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–3)
1. **Entity & compliance:** Register LLC with Kansas Secretary of State (**$160** online). Obtain EIN (free). Register with Kansas Dept. of Revenue for sales tax if applicable. Confirm **general liability insurance** ($600–$1,200/yr) and, if hiring, workers' comp.
2. **Equipment decision:** Portable unit (**$2,500–$6,000**) for lean start vs. used truck-mount (**$12,000–$25,000**) for speed and capacity. Many Wichita operators start portable and upgrade at ~$8K/mo revenue.
3. **Licensing/credentials:** Pursue **IICRC certification** ($500–$900) — a strong trust signal in GEO/AI answers and local search.
4. **Business banking + payment processing** (Square/Stripe: 2.6–2.9% + $0.10).

### Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 2–5)
5. **Domain + website:** 5–8 pages (Home, Services, Service Areas, About, Reviews, Contact) with **LocalBusiness + Service schema**. Budget $900–$2,200 via verified freelancer.
6. **Google Business Profile:** Complete verification, 10+ photos, service categories, service-area radius (Wichita + 5 suburbs).
7. **Citations:** Consistent NAP across Google, Bing, Yelp, Apple Maps, BBB, Angi, Nextdoor.
8. **Review engine:** Automated SMS/email request after each job; target **25+ Google reviews in 90 days**.

### Phase 3 — Lead Generation (Weeks 4–12)
9. **Google Local Services Ads** (pay-per-lead, ~$18–$35/lead in Wichita) — fastest path to booked jobs.
10. **Google Ads search campaign:** $600–$1,200/mo budget on "carpet cleaning Wichita" + suburb variants; expect **$25–$45 cost per lead**, 35–50% close rate.
11. **Referral partnerships:** 5 real estate agents, 3 property managers, 2 restoration firms — offer 10% referral fee or reciprocal leads.
12. **Direct mail / door hangers** in target ZIPs (67212, 67205, 67208) — $0.45–$0.75 per piece.

### Phase 4 — Scale & Optimize (Months 4–12)
13. **Add service-area pages** for Derby, Andover, Maize, Goddard, Haysville (10 pages, $400–$900 freelance).
14. **Introduce recurring commercial contracts** — target 3–5 accounts at $400–$1,200/mo each.
15. **Track KPIs:** cost per lead, close rate, average ticket, jobs/month, review velocity. Reinvest 10–15% of revenue into ads/SEO.
16. **Upgrade equipment** at $8K+/mo revenue; add a second technician at $12K+/mo.
