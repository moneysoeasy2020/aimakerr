## Step-by-Step Execution Plan

**Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–6)**
1. Obtain Virginia DPOR HVAC contractor license + Chesapeake business license.
2. Secure general liability ($1M/$2M) and commercial auto insurance ($2,800–$4,800/yr).
3. Register LLC, EIN, business banking, and VA sales/use tax account.
4. Purchase service vehicle ($12,000–$22,000 used) and core tools ($4,500–$7,000).

**Phase 2 — Digital Assets (Weeks 4–10)**
5. Launch GEO-optimized website with LocalBusiness + Service schema, service-area pages for Great Bridge, Greenbrier, Western Branch, Deep Creek, and South Norfolk.
6. Claim and fully optimize Google Business Profile (categories, services, photos, Q&A).
7. Build 40–60 local citations (NAP consistency).
8. Deploy review-generation system (target 25+ reviews in 90 days).

**Phase 3 — Demand Generation (Weeks 8–16)**
9. Launch Google Local Services Ads (highest ROI for HVAC in Hampton Roads).
10. Start Google Ads on 3–5 high-intent keywords ($1,500–$2,500/mo budget).
11. Publish 10 GEO-optimized content pages (rebate guides, cost pages, seasonal maintenance).
12. Set up call tracking and CRM (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, or Jobber).

**Phase 4 — Scale (Months 4–12)**
13. Add second technician when booked calls exceed 6/day.
14. Introduce maintenance agreements ($14–$22/mo recurring).
15. Expand into light commercial RTU service.
16. Review CAC monthly; target <$150/booked call by month 9.
